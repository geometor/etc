About¶

Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge & Repurposing It for Exact Symbolic Reasoning

About This Edition¶

geometor.etc is a digital reconstruction, semantic index, and symbolic modeling framework for Clark Kimberling’s monumental Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC) at the University of Evansville. Spanning more than 53,000 distinct triangle centers—from antiquity’s incenter X1, centroid X2, circumcenter X3, and orthocenter X4 to modern central discoveries—this edition ingests archival HTML volumes and transforms unstructured mathematical prose into structured Python objects, computable dependency chains, and exact symbolic coordinates.

Every center entry is systematized into a synchronized two-track Book Codex: an Overview track equipped with formal .. enunciation:: directives, .. signature:: center definitions for functional trilinear (\(\alpha : \beta : \gamma\)) and barycentric (\(u : v : w\)) coordinates, comprehensive cross-references to Kimberling’s thematic preambles, and calibrated multi-triangle vector diagrams rendered across Acute, Right, Kepler, and Kimberling reference geometries; and a Code track providing standalone model.py geometric construction scripts ready for execution and algebraic inspection.

By parsing historical definitions, collineations, central lines, central triangles, and geometric transformations into explicit semantic links, geometor.etc bridges centuries of synthetic and analytic triangle geometry with modern symbolic computation. It connects directly with sister GEOMETOR repositories—including geometor.model for algebraic construction verification, geometor.explorer for dynamic SVG laboratories, and historical codices such as geometor.elements and geometor.pappus—establishing a computable foundation for classical and contemporary geometry.

The GEOMETOR Mission¶

The GEOMETOR initiative, conceived and directed by phi ARCHITECT, is dedicated to the systematic recovery, symbolic formalization, and creative re-articulation of humanity’s geometric heritage. GEOMETOR unites classical synthesis with modern algebraic rigor across three core pillars:

Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge

Moving beyond passive page scans, static facsimile bitmaps, and flat optical character recognition (OCR), GEOMETOR recovers the living deductive architecture of 2,300+ years of geometric, axiomatic, and philosophical treatises. Primary sources—spanning Euclid, Apollonius, Archimedes, Pappus, Johannes Kepler, Felix Klein, and Clark Kimberling—are analyzed to elevate enunciations, deductive steps, synthetic justifications, marginal citations, primary-source folios (via publish folio), and calibrated Ink-to-Vector .euclid-graphic diagrams into structured, machine-addressable semantic graphs.

Exact Symbolic Grounding (geometor.model)

Geometric constructions must not depend on floating-point approximations or visual artifacts. GEOMETOR anchors geometric entities in 100% exact symbolic algebra via SymPy and the principles of Rational Trigonometry (quadrance, spread, cross-ratio). Points, lines, circles, and conic loci are evaluated over exact algebraic fields, ensuring that tangencies, incidences, harmonic conjugates, and collinearities represent absolute mathematical truths rather than numerical estimates.

Repurposing Knowledge Across Mediums

Extracted semantic architectures and exact symbolic models are re-deployed across a unified digital ecosystem: synchronized multi-track Book Codices and EPUB 3 editions powered by photon-platform/publish, computable deductive canons (geometor.elements, geometor.etc, geometor.pappus, geometor.divine), interactive SVG exploration laboratories (geometor.explorer), ultra-high-definition visual broadcasts, and neuro-symbolic program synthesis (geometor.seer) across geometor.com.

Sources, Provenance & Methodology¶

  • Primary Corpus: Clark Kimberling, Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC), University of Evansville (established 1994, continuously expanded to 53,144+ centers).

  • Glossary & Preambles: Kimberling’s Glossary of Terms in ETC and catalog of 925 inter-center preambles across Parts 1–37.

  • Biographical Register: Historical and contemporary attribution to over 150 classical and modern mathematicians curated in the People Register.

  • Digital Scriptorium Methodology: Developed and maintained by phi ARCHITECT using Sphinx, Python, geometor.model, and photon-platform/publish. Raw HTML sources are scraped, normalized, parsed into typed dataclasses, linked with the comprehensive glossary, and typeset into responsive web and codex formats.
