.. _persons:

Index Nominum (Geometers & Contributors)
========================================

Biographical register of classical geometers and modern contributors referenced across the *Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers*.

.. _person-henri-brocard:
.. _henri-brocard:

Henri Brocard (1845–1922)
-------------------------

French mathematician and co-founder of modern triangle geometry; namesake of the Brocard points, Brocard angle ω, Brocard axis, Brocard circle, and Brocard triangles.

.. _person-john-horton-conway:
.. _john-horton-conway:

John Horton Conway (1937–2020)
------------------------------

Mathematician who introduced Conway triangle notation (S, S_A, S_B, S_C, S_ω) and the Conway circle in triangle geometry.

.. _person-gaston-darboux:
.. _gaston-darboux:

Jean-Gaston Darboux (1842–1917)
-------------------------------

French geometer after whom the Darboux cubic (K004) and Darboux point :ref:`X(20) <X(20)>` (with De Longchamps) are named.

.. _person-leonhard-euler:
.. _leonhard-euler:

Leonhard Euler (1707–1783)
--------------------------

Swiss mathematician who proved in 1765 that the orthocenter :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>`, circumcenter :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>`, centroid :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>`, and nine-point center :ref:`X(5) <X(5)>` are collinear on the Euler line.

.. _person-pierre-de-fermat:
.. _pierre-de-fermat:

Pierre de Fermat (1607–1665)
----------------------------

French mathematician who posed the problem of finding the point :ref:`X(13) <X(13)>` minimizing the sum of distances \|AX\| + \|BX\| + \|CX\| to the vertices of ABC.

.. _person-karl-wilhelm-feuerbach:
.. _karl-wilhelm-feuerbach:

Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach (1800–1834)
----------------------------------

German geometer who proved in 1822 that the nine-point circle is tangent to the incircle at the Feuerbach point :ref:`X(11) <X(11)>` and to the three excircles.

.. _person-joseph-diaz-gergonne:
.. _joseph-diaz-gergonne:

Joseph Diaz Gergonne (1771–1859)
--------------------------------

French geometer after whom the Gergonne point :ref:`X(7) <X(7)>` (perspector of the intouch triangle) and Gergonne line are named.

.. _person-bernard-gibert:
.. _bernard-gibert:

Bernard Gibert (fl. 1998–present)
---------------------------------

Creator of Cubics in the Triangle Plane (CTC), cataloging and analyzing over 1,200 triangle cubics (K001–K1200+) referenced throughout the ETC.

.. _person-ernst-wilhelm-grebe:
.. _ernst-wilhelm-grebe:

Ernst Wilhelm Grebe (1804–1874)
-------------------------------

German mathematician who investigated the symmedian point :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>` (Grebe point) in 1847.

.. _person-darij-grinberg:
.. _darij-grinberg:

Darij Grinberg (b. 1988)
------------------------

Mathematician and early contributor to Hyacinthos and the ETC on barycentric coordinates, pedal triangles, and circle power identities.

.. _person-antreas-hatzipolakis:
.. _antreas-hatzipolakis:

Antreas P. Hatzipolakis (fl. 1998–present)
------------------------------------------

Founder of the Hyacinthos, Anopolis, and Euclid geometry research forums; proposer of hundreds of geometric configurations in the ETC.

.. _person-kurt-hofstadter:
.. _kurt-hofstadter:

Douglas Richard Hofstadter (b. 1945)
------------------------------------

Cognitive scientist and geometer who discovered the Hofstadter points, including the Hofstadter zero point :ref:`X(359) <X(359)>` and one point :ref:`X(360) <X(360)>`.

.. _person-randy-hutson:
.. _randy-hutson:

Randy Hutson (fl. 2011–present)
-------------------------------

Prolific contributor to the ETC, specializing in synthetic constructions, perspective triangles, radical centers, and conjugate relationships.

.. _person-clark-kimberling:
.. _clark-kimberling:

Clark Kimberling (b. 1942)
--------------------------

Mathematician at the University of Evansville; founder and creator of the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC).

.. _person-dasari-naga-vijay-krishna:
.. _dasari-naga-vijay-krishna:

Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna (fl. 2018–present)
--------------------------------------------

Indian mathematician contributing synthetic reflection and circle constructions in the ETC.

.. _person-floor-van-lamoen:
.. _floor-van-lamoen:

Floor van Lamoen (b. 1966)
--------------------------

Dutch mathematician and triangle geometer, discoverer of the van Lamoen circle and numerous cevapoint and perspectivity constructions in the ETC.

.. _person-emile-lemoine:
.. _emile-lemoine:

Émile Michel Hyacinthe Lemoine (1840–1912)
------------------------------------------

French mathematician and co-founder of modern triangle geometry; studied the symmedian point :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>` (Lemoine point) and Lemoine circles.

.. _person-gohier-de-longchamps:
.. _gohier-de-longchamps:

Gaston Albert Gohier de Longchamps (1842–1906)
----------------------------------------------

French mathematician who studied the De Longchamps point :ref:`X(20) <X(20)>` (reflection of the orthocenter in the circumcenter) and De Longchamps line.

.. _person-cesar-lozada:
.. _cesar-lozada:

César Eliud Lozada (fl. 2012–present)
-------------------------------------

Author of the Extended Glossary, Alphabetical Index of Terms, and Index of Triangles Referenced in ETC; contributor of thousands of triangle centers.

.. _person-angel-montesdeoca:
.. _angel-montesdeoca:

Angel Montesdeoca (fl. 2010–present)
------------------------------------

Spanish geometer and contributor of numerous triangle centers, perspectors, and conic constructions in the ETC.

.. _person-peter-moses:
.. _peter-moses:

Peter J. C. Moses (fl. 2000–present)
------------------------------------

Computational geometer and principal collaborator on the ETC, discoverer of thousands of barycentric combinations, coordinates, and circle/cubic properties.

.. _person-christian-heinrich-von-nagel:
.. _christian-heinrich-von-nagel:

Christian Heinrich von Nagel (1803–1882)
----------------------------------------

German geometer who studied the Nagel point :ref:`X(8) <X(8)>` (perspector of the extouch triangle) and Nagel line in 1836.

.. _person-joseph-neuberg:
.. _joseph-neuberg:

Joseph Jean Baptiste Neuberg (1840–1926)
----------------------------------------

Luxembourger-Belgian mathematician, co-founder of modern triangle geometry, discoverer of the Neuberg cubic (K001) and Neuberg circles.

.. _person-dao-thanh-oai:
.. _dao-thanh-oai:

Dao Thanh Oai (fl. 2013–present)
--------------------------------

Geometer and discoverer of generalizations of the Simson line, Napoleon configuration, and perspective triangles in the ETC.

.. _person-cyril-parry:
.. _cyril-parry:

Cyril F. Parry (1915–1999)
--------------------------

British geometer who discovered the Parry point :ref:`X(111) <X(111)>`, the Parry circle, and the Parry reflection property.

.. _person-stanley-rabinowitz:
.. _stanley-rabinowitz:

Stanley Rabinowitz (b. 1947)
----------------------------

Mathematician and author of 'Location of Triangle Centers Relative to the Incircle and Circumcircle' in the ETC.

.. _person-dan-reznik:
.. _dan-reznik:

Dan Reznik (fl. 2018–present)
-----------------------------

Researcher on Poncelet porisms, locus invariants, and dynamic triangle center loci in the ETC.

.. _person-kiminari-shinagawa:
.. _kiminari-shinagawa:

Kiminari Shinagawa (fl. 2015–present)
-------------------------------------

Geometer who introduced the Shinagawa coefficients (G, H) for triangle centers on the Euler line.

.. _person-theodor-spieker:
.. _theodor-spieker:

Theodor Spieker (1823–1913)
---------------------------

German geometer who discovered the Spieker center :ref:`X(10) <X(10)>` (incenter of the medial triangle and center of mass of the perimeter of ABC).

.. _person-evangelista-torricelli:
.. _evangelista-torricelli:

Evangelista Torricelli (1608–1647)
----------------------------------

Italian physicist and mathematician who solved Fermat's problem via equilateral triangles erected on the sides of ABC, locating :ref:`X(13) <X(13)>` and :ref:`X(14) <X(14)>`.

.. _person-eric-weisstein:
.. _eric-weisstein:

Eric W. Weisstein (b. 1969)
---------------------------

Creator of MathWorld, providing extensive cross-linked articles and illustrations for triangle centers and central circles.

.. _person-peter-yff:
.. _peter-yff:

Peter Yff (1924–2016)
---------------------

Geometer who discovered the Yff center of congruence :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`, Yff parabolic point :ref:`X(190) <X(190)>`, and Yff central triangles.

.. _person-paul-yiu:
.. _paul-yiu:

Paul Yiu (b. 1952)
------------------

Professor of Mathematics at Florida Atlantic University, founder of Forum Geometricorum, and pioneer of modern barycentric triangle geometry.
