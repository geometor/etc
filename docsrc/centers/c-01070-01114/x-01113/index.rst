X_1113
======

.. rubric:: 1st EULER-LINE-CIRCUMCIRCLE INTERSECTION

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_1113 <X_1113>` is a point of intersection of the Euler line and the circumcircle. The other is :ref:`X_1114 <X_1114>`. Of the two, :ref:`X_1113 <X_1113>` is the one closer to :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`.
