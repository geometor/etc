.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`I_aI_bI_c << I_a I_b I_c >>` be the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the centroid of triangle :g:`AI_bI_c << A I_b I_c >>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then the lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_37 <X_37>`. (Eric Danneels, :cite:`Hyacinthos <hyacinthos>` 7892, 9/13/03)

.. container:: note-item

   A simple construction of :ref:`X_37 <X_37>` as a crosspoint can be generalized as follows: let :g:`DEF << D E F >>` be the medial triangle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the cevian triangle of a point U other than the centroid, :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`. The crosspoint of :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` and U is then the point of concurrence of lines LD,ME,NF, where L,M,N are the respective midpoints of :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>`. If :g:`U <U>`\ =\ :math:`u : v : w` (trilinears), then crosspoint(:ref:`X_2 <X_2>`,U) = b/w+c/v : c/u+a/w : a/v+b/u, assuming that uvw is nonzero. In particular, if :g:`U <U>`\ =\ :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, then the crosspoint is :ref:`X_37 <X_37>`. (Seiichi Kirikami, July 10, 2011)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the trilinear pole of the tangent to the Apollonius circle where it meets the :g:`A <A>`-excircle, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_37 <X_37>`. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`O_{a} <O_a>` be the A-extraversion of the Conway circle (the circle centered at the :g:`A <A>`-excenter and passing through :g:`A <A>`, with radius :math:`\sqrt{r_{a}^{2} + s^{2}}`, where :math:`r_{a}` is the A-exradius). Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the radical axis of the circumcircle and :g:`O_{a} <O_a>`. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_37 <X_37>`. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)
