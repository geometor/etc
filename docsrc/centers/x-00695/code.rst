X(695) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(384) — Code
============================================

.. enunciation::

   Let A'B'C' be the cross-triangle of the 1st and 2nd Neuberg triangles. Let A″ be the :term:`crossdifference <crossdifference>` of B' and C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in :ref:`X(695) <X(695)>`. Let La be the :term:`trilinear polar <trilinear-polar>` of A', and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A\* = Lb∩Lc, and define B\* and C\* cyclically. The lines AA\*, BB\*, CC\* concur in :ref:`X(695) <X(695)>`. Also, :ref:`X(695) <X(695)>` is the orthologic center of ABC to A'B'C'. The reciprocal orthologic center is :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 31 2018)
