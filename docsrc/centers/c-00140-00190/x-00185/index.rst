.. container:: note-item

The locus of the orthocenter of :g:`P_{a}P_{b}P_{c} << P_a P_b P_c >>` as P varies is an ellipse centered at :ref:`X_185 <X_185>`. See also X_9730. (Randy Hutson, March 25, 2016)
