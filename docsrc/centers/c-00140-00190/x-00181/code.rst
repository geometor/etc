X_181
=====

.. rubric:: APOLLONIUS POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let O(A),O(B),O(C) be the excircles. Apollonius's Problem includes the construction of the circle :g:`O <O>` tangent to the three excircles and encompassing them. (The circle is called the Apollonius circle.) Let :g:`A' <A'>` = O∩O(A), :g:`B' <B'>`\ =O∩O(B), :g:`C' <C'>`\ =O∩O(C). The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`,\ :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`,\ :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_181 <X_181>`. Yff derived trilinears in 1992.
