.. enunciation::

   Let Ha be the hyperbola passing through A, and with foci at B and C. This is called the A-Soddy hyperbola in (:person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>`, Introduction to the Geometry of the Triangle, 2002; 12.4 The Soddy hyperbolas, p. 143). Let La be the polar of :ref:`X(20) <X(20)>` with respect to Ha. Define Lb, Lc cyclically. La, Lb, Lc concur in :ref:`X(347) <X(347)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 30, 2018)
