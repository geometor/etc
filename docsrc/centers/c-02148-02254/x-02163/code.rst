X_2163
======

.. rubric:: X_2-ISOCONJUGATE OF X_45

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`J_aJ_bJ_c << J_a J_b J_c >>` be the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>` of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`A_{2} <A_2>` and :g:`A_{3} <A_3>` be the points where the perpendicular bisector of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` meets the internal angle bisector of angle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and the internal angle bisector of angle :g:`BCA << B C A >>`, respectively. Let :g:`A'_{2} <A'_2>` and :g:`A'_{3} <A'_3>` be the points where the perpendicular bisector of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` meets the external angle bisector of angle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and the external angle bisector of angle :g:`BCA << B C A >>`, respectively. Let Oab, Oac be the circumcenters of triangles :g:`J_bA_2A'_3 << J_b A_2 A'_3 >>`, :g:`J_cA_3A'_2 << J_c A_3 A'_2 >>`, respectively. Define Obc, Oba, Oca, Ocb cyclically. Then the triangle having sidelines OabOac, ObcOba, OcaOcb is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_2163 <X_2163>`. See :person:`Angel Montesdeoca <angel-montesdeoca>`, `HG090218: El centro del triángulo X(2163) <http://amontes.webs.ull.es/otrashtm/HGT2018.htm#HG090218>`__.
