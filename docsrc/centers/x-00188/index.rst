X(188) = 2nd MID-ARC POINT OF ANTICOMPLEMENTARY TRIANGLE
========================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>` of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, so that :g:`A' <A'>` = :math:`-1 : 1 : 1` (trilinears). Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the point where the bisector of angle :g:`BA'C << B A' C >>` meets the line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`. Define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X(188) <X(188)>`. (Seiichi Kirikami, February 14, 2010)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` be the ellipse with :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>` as foci and passing through the :g:`A <A>`-excenter, and define :g:`E_{b} <E_b>` and :g:`E_{c} <E_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the line tangent to :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` at the :g:`A <A>`-excenter, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`. Then :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` are collinear, and the trilinear pole of line :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` = :ref:`X(188) <X(188)>`. Note: The triangle formed by :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`, :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` is also the excentral triangle of the excentral triangle. Alternately, let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the trilinear pole of line :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>` and :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur at :ref:`X(188) <X(188)>`; see also :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`.
