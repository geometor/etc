X_3568
======

.. rubric:: BELTRAMI-EULER POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let P(2) and U(2) be the 1st and 2nd Beltrami points (as indexed at Bicentric Pairs, accessible using the Tables button at the top of ETC), and let P(40) and U(40) be the :term:`isogonal conjugates <isogonal-conjugate>` of P(2) and U(2), respectively. Then :ref:`X_3568 <X_3568>` is the point of intersection of the lines P(2)P(40) and U(2)U(40). The name "Beltrami-Euler Point" signifies the fact that :ref:`X_3568 <X_3568>` lies on the Euler line. See also :ref:`X_2966 <X_2966>` and :ref:`X_3569 <X_3569>`. Contributed by Chris van Tienhoven, June 7, 2009.
