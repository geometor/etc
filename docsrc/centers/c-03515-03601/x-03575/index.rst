.. container:: note-item

   Let KH denote the hyperbola discussed at :ref:`X_1112 <X_1112>`. As noted in the paper cited at :ref:`X_1112 <X_1112>`, KH is the :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`-Ceva conjugate of the Euler line. Since :ref:`X_3574 <X_3574>` lies on KH, its :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`-Ceva conjugate, which is :ref:`X_3575 <X_3575>`, lies on the Euler line. (:person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, July 7, 2009)
