X_3571
======

.. rubric:: INTERSECTION OF LINES P(6)P(8) AND U(6)U(8)

.. enunciation::

   The points given by trilinears P(6) =\ :math:`b : c : a` and U(6) =\ :math:`c : a : b` are indexed at Bicentric Pairs (accessible using the Tables button at the top of ETC), and likewise for their :term:`isogonal conjugates <isogonal-conjugate>` P(8) =\ :math:`1/b : 1/c : 1/a` and U(8) =\ :math:`1/c : 1/a : 1/b`. The bicentric pair of lines P(6)P(8) and U(6)U(8) concur in :ref:`X_3571 <X_3571>`. Contributed by :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, July 7, 2009.
