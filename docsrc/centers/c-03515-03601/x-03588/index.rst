Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   In 2008, Alexei Myakishev gave the following construction of a conic. Let :g:`C_{A} <C_A>` and :g:`C_{B} <C_B>` be points on the line :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` satisfying \|\ :g:`BC_{A} <[ B C_A ]>`\ \| = \|\ :g:`CB <[ C B ]>`\ \| and \|\ :g:`AC_{B} <[ A C_B ]>`\ \| = \|\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`\ \| and arranged in this order: :g:`C_{A} <C_A>`, B, A, and :g:`C_{B} <C_B>`. Define points :g:`A_{B} <A_B>`, :g:`C_{B} <C_B>`, :g:`B_{C} <B_C>`, :g:`A_{C} <A_C>` cyclically. The six points :g:`C_{A} <C_A>`, :g:`B_{A} <B_A>`, :g:`A_{B} <A_B>`, :g:`C_{B} <C_B>`, :g:`B_{C} <B_C>`, :g:`A_{C} <A_C>` lie on a conic. Myakishev's proof is by Carnot's theorem, since

      [c/(c+a)][(a+b)/b][a/(a+b)][(b+c)/c][b/(b+c)][(c+a)/a] = 1.

.. container:: note-item

   For details, in Russian, visit Item 4 at Geometry.ru,

   :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>` found that :ref:`X_3588 <X_3588>` is the point of concurrence of three lines constructed from points numbered 1,3,4,6,8,9,10,11, as follows:

   The six points :g:`C_{a} <C_a>`, :g:`B_{a} <B_a>`, :g:`A_{b} <A_b>`, :g:`C_{b} <C_b>`, :g:`B_{c} <B_c>`, :g:`A_{c} <A_c>` can be constructed from the :term:`extangents triangle <extangents-triangle>` as follows. Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the extangents triangle. Then :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` = :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`C'A' <[ C' A' ]>`, :g:`A_{c} <A_c>` = :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`A'B' <[ A' B' ]>`, and :g:`B_{c} <B_c>`, :g:`B_{a} <B_a>`, :g:`C_{a} <C_a>`, :g:`C_{b} <C_b>` are defined cyclically. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, January 29, 2018)
