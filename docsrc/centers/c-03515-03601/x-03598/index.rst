X_3598
======

.. rubric:: 1st LIU POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`O_{A} <O_A>` be the circle passing through :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>`, internally tangent to the incircle, and likewise for :g:`O_{B} <O_B>` and :g:`O_{C} <O_C>`. Let :g:`P_{A} <P_A>` be the point where :g:`O_{A} <O_A>` meets the incircle, and likewise for :g:`P_{B} <P_B>` and :g:`P_{C} <P_C>`. Let :g:`Q_{A} <Q_A>` be the point of intersection of the tangents to the incircle at :g:`P_{B} <P_B>` and :g:`P_{C} <P_C>`, and likewise for :g:`P_{B} <P_B>` and :g:`P_{C} <P_C>`. Then :ref:`X_3598 <X_3598>` is the perspector of the triangles :g:`P_{A}P_{B}P_{C} << P_A P_B P_C >>` and :g:`Q_{A}Q_{B}Q_{C} << Q_A Q_B Q_C >>`; this point is also :ref:`X_7 <X_7>`-of-:g:`Q_{A}Q_{B}Q_{C} << Q_A Q_B Q_C >>`. This point and :ref:`X_3599 <X_3599>` were discovered by Kang-Ying Liu of St. Andrew's Priory School, Honolulu, Hawaii, during 2010.
