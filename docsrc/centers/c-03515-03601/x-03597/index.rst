X_3597

.. rubric:: 2nd ODEHNAL POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`M_{A} <M_A>`, :g:`M_{B} <M_B>`, :g:`M_{C} <M_C>` be the centers of the circles :g:`A″ <A″>`, :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` used to construct :ref:`X_3596 <X_3596>`, respectively. Then the lines A-to-:g:`M_{A} <M_A>`, B-to-:g:`M_{B} <M_B>`, C-to-:g:`M_{C} <M_C>` concur in :ref:`X_3597 <X_3597>`. For more, see the reference and sketch at :ref:`X_3596 <X_3596>`.
