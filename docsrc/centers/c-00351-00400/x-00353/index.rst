X_353
=====

.. rubric:: INVERSE-IN-BROCARD-CIRCLE OF X_111

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A_{a}B_{a}C_{a} << A_a B_a C_a >>`, :g:`A_{b}B_{b}C_{b} << A_b B_b C_b >>`, :g:`A_{c}B_{c}C_{c} << A_c B_c C_c >>` be the A-, B- and C-anti-altimedial triangles. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the trilinear product :g:`A_{a} <A_a>`\ \*\ :g:`A_{b} <A_b>`\ \*\ :g:`A_{c} <A_c>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is the :term:`anticomplementary triangle <anticomplementary-triangle>` of the :term:`1st Brocard triangle <1st-brocard-triangle>`, and is similar to the anti-orthocentroidal triangle, with similitude center :ref:`X_353 <X_353>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 2, 2017)
