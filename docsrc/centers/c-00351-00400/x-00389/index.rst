Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   If :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is acute then :ref:`X_389 <X_389>` is the Spieker center of the orthic triangle. :person:`Peter Yff <peter-yff>` reports (Sept. 19, 2001) that since :ref:`X_389 <X_389>` is on the Brocard axis, there must exist T for which :ref:`X_389 <X_389>` is :math:`\sin(A+T) : \sin(B+T) : \sin(C+T)`, and that tan(T) = - cot A cot B cot C.

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`H_{A} <H_A>` be the :g:`A <A>`-altitude of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and let :g:`A' <A'>` be the midpoint of segment :g:`AH_{A} <[ A H_A ]>`. Let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the line through :g:`A' <A'>` parallel to :g:`AO <[ A X_3 ]>`, where O denotes the circumcenter. Define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. The lines :g:`L_{A} <L_A>`, :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`, :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` concur in :ref:`X_389 <X_389>`. (Construction by Alexei Myakishev, March 24, 2010.)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`O_{A} <O_A>` be the circle with center A tangent to line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`O_{B} <O_B>` and :g:`O_{C} <O_C>` cyclically. :ref:`X_389 <X_389>` is the radical center of the three circles. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 9/23/2011)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << H_a H_b H_c >>` be the orthic triangle, let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the orthocenter of :g:`AB'C' << A H_b H_c >>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is the :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>` of the circumorthic triangle. The triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is homothetic to :g:`A'B'C' << H_a H_b H_c >>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_389 <X_389>`. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << H_a H_b H_c >>` be the orthic triangle. Let :g:`O_{a} <O_a>` be the circle with center A tangent to line :g:`B'C' <[ H_b H_c ]>`, and define :g:`O_{b} <O_b>` and :g:`O_{c} <O_c>` cyclically. The radical circle of :g:`O_{a} <O_a>`, :g:`O_{b} <O_b>`, :g:`O_{c} <O_c>` is the Taylor circle, which is also the Spieker radical circle of the orthic triangle if :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is acute. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << H_a H_b H_c >>` be the orthic triangle. :ref:`X_389 <X_389>` is the radical center of the polar circles of :g:`AB'C' << A H_b H_c >>`, :g:`BC'A' << B H_c H_a >>`, :g:`CA'B' << C H_a H_b >>`. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   A construction of :ref:`X_389 <X_389>` is given at `24162 <http://www.hyacinthos.epizy.com/message.php?msg=24162>`__. (:person:`Antreas Hatzipolakis <antreas-hatzipolakis>`, August 29, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the midheight triangle. Let :g:`A_{B} <A_B>`, :g:`A_{C} <A_C>` be the orthogonal projections of :g:`A' <A'>` on :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, resp. Define :g:`B_{C} <B_C>`, :g:`B_{A} <B_A>`, :g:`C_{A} <C_A>`, :g:`C_{B} <C_B>` cyclically. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` = :g:`C_{A}A_{C} <[ C_A A_C ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`A_{B}B_{A} <[ A_B B_A ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`. The lines :g:`A'A″ <[ A' A″ ]>`, :g:`B'B″ <[ B' B″ ]>`, :g:`C'C″ <[ C' C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_389 <X_389>`, which is also :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`-of-:g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>`. (Randy Hutson, March 29, 2020)
