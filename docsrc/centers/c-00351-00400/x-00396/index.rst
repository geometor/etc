.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_396 <X_396>` is the centroid of the points :g:`A_{b} <A_b>`, :g:`A_{c} <A_c>`, :g:`B_{c} <B_c>`, :g:`B_{a} <B_a>`, :g:`C_{a} <C_a>`, :g:`C_{b} <C_b>` in the construction of the 3rd Fermat-Dao equilateral triangle; see X_16267. Also, :ref:`X_396 <X_396>` is the center of the ellipse passing through these six points. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, March 14, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`F <F>` = :ref:`X_13 <X_13>`, the 1st Fermat point. Let :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` be the point on :g:`CA <[ C A ]>` and :g:`A_{c} <A_c>` the point on :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` such that FAbAc is an equilateral triangle. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the midpoint of FAbAc, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`, here named the **7th Fermat-Dao equilateral triangle**, is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The circumcircle of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is the pedal circle of :ref:`X_13 <X_13>` and (X15), and the centroid of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is :ref:`X_396 <X_396>`. The perspector of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`, and the centroid of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is :ref:`X_396 <X_396>`.

.. container:: note-item
