.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`J_{a} <J_a>`, :g:`J_{b} <J_b>`, :g:`J_{c} <J_c>` be the excenters and I the incenter. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the centroid of JbJcI, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is also the cross-triangle of the excentral and 2nd circumperp triangles. :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to the :term:`reflection triangle <reflection-triangle>` of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` at :ref:`X_3612 <X_3612>`.
