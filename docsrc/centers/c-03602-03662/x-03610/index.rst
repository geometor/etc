X_3610
======

.. rubric:: 1st AYME-MOSES PERSPECTOR

.. enunciation::

   Moses found that the locus of X such that the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :g:`X <X>` is perspective to the Ayme triangle is a cubic which passes through the points X_i for i = 1, 2, 19, 75, 279, 304, 346, 2184. A barycentric equation for this Ayme-Moses cubic follows:

      (Cyclic sum of ayz[:math:`by(a^{2}` + :math:`b^{2}` - :math:`c^{2})` - :math:`cz(a^{2}` - :math:`b^{2}` + :math:`c^{2}`] ) = 0.

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   In a :cite:`Hyacinthos <hyacinthos>` message dated January 10, 2011, Jean-Louis Ayme introduced a triangle as follows. Let :math:`R_{A}` be the radical axis of the circumcircle and the :g:`A <A>`-excircle, and define :math:`R_{B}` and :math:`R_{C}` cyclically. Let :g:`T_{A} <T_A>` = :math:`R_{B}`\ ∩\ :math:`R_{C}`, and define :g:`T_{B} <T_B>` and :g:`T_{C} <T_C>` cyclically. (:g:`T_{A} <T_A>` is also the radical center of the circumcircle and the B- and C- excircles.) The Ayme triangle :g:`T_{A}T_{B}T_{C} << T_A T_B T_C >>` is perspective to triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and also perspective to many other triangles. :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>` found that its perspector with the cevian triangle of :ref:`X_346 <X_346>` is :ref:`X_3610 <X_3610>`. He also found that the :g:`A <A>`-vertex of the Ayme triangle has first barycentric as follows:

      - (b + c)(:math:`a^{2}` + :math:`b^{2}` + :math:`c^{2}` + 2bc) : :math:`b(a^{2}` + :math:`b^{2}` - :math:`c^{2})` : :math:`c(a^{2}` - :math:`b^{2}` + :math:`c^{2})`,

   from which the other two vertices are easily obtained. The Ayme triangle is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` with perspector :ref:`X_19 <X_19>`.

.. container:: note-item

   The Ayme triangle is homothetic to the :term:`incentral triangle <incentral-triangle>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_612 <X_612>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, September 14, 2016)
