X_3649
======

.. rubric:: KS(INTOUCH TRIANGLE)

.. enunciation::

   Two proofs of Dao's theorem about cyclic hexagons were published in 2014: Nikolaos Dergiades's `Dao's Theorem on Six Circumcenters Associated with a Cyclic Hexagon <http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2014volume14/FG201424.pdf>`__ and Telv Cohl's `A Purely Synthetic Proof of Dao's Theorem on Six Circumcenters Associated with a Cyclic Hexagon <http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2014volume14/FG201429.pdf>`__. In the degenerate case that the hexagon is the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>` :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`, the triangle of the circumcenters of :g:`AB'C' << A B' C' >>`, :g:`A'BC' << A' B C' >>`, :g:`A'B'C << A' B' C >>` is perspective to :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` with perspector :ref:`X_3649 <X_3649>`. (:person:`Dao Thanh Oai <dao-thanh-oai>`, Francisco Javier Garcia Capitan, `ADGEOM #1718 <https://faculty.evansville.edu/ck6/encyclopedia/www.adgeom.epizy.com/message.php?msg=1717>`__, September 16, 2014)
