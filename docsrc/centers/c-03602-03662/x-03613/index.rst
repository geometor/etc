.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the point of intersection of the tangents to the nine-point circle at the points where the circle meets line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then the lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3613 <X_3613>`. Also, :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is the side-triangle of the :term:`tangential triangle <tangential-triangle>` of the :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>` and the tangential triangle of the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, August 30, 2011.)
