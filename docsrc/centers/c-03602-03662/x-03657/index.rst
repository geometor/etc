Let :g:`P <P>` be the point of intersection of the lines :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`\ \ :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` and :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and let :g:`P' <P'>` be the point where the line through :g:`P <P>` perpendicular to line A\ :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` meets that line. Let :g:`A″ <A″>`\ =\ :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3657 <X_3657>`. (Jean-Louis Ayme, :cite:`Hyacinthos #16676 <hyacinthos>`, August 21, 2008)
