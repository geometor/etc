.. rubric:: H(X_1; M, 1/2)

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`O_{A} <O_A>` be the circle tangent to side :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` at its midpoint and to the circumcircle on the side of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` opposite A. Define :g:`O_{B} <O_B>` and :g:`O_{C} <O_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the external tangent to circles :g:`O_{B} <O_B>` and :g:`O_{C} <O_C>` that is the nearer of the two to :g:`O_{A} <O_A>`. Define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_3616 <X_3616>`. See the reference at :ref:`X_1001 <X_1001>`.
