.. rubric:: ANTICOMPLEMENT OF NAGEL POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`O_{A} <O_A>` be the circle tangent to side :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` at its midpoint and to the circumcircle on the same side of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` as A. Define :g:`O_{B} <O_B>`, :g:`O_{C} <O_C>` cyclically. Then :ref:`X_145 <X_145>` is the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of the line of the exsimilicenters (the Monge line) of :g:`O_{A} <O_A>`, :g:`O_{B} <O_B>`, :g:`O_{C} <O_C>`. See the reference at X_1001.
