X(52) = ORTHOCENTER OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE
======================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`H_{a} <H_a>` be the foot of the :g:`A <A>`-altitude. Let :g:`B_{a} <B_a>` be the foot of the perpendicular from :g:`H_{a} <H_a>` to :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, and define :g:`C_{b} <C_b>` and :g:`A_{c} <A_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`C_{a} <C_a>` be the foot of the perpendicular from :g:`H_{a} <H_a>` to :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, and define :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` and :g:`B_{c} <B_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the orthocenter of :g:`H_{a}B_{a}C_{a} << H_a B_a C_a >>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`H_{a}A' <[ H_a A' ]>`, :g:`H_{b}B' <[ H_b B' ]>`, :g:`H_{c}C' <[ H_c C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X(52) <X(52)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)
