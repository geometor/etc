X_2023
======

.. rubric:: RADICAL TRACE OF GALLATLY AND NINE-POINT CIRCLES

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :term:`1st Brocard triangle <1st-brocard-triangle>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the orthogonal projection of :g:`A <A>` on line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is perpsective to the :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>` at :ref:`X_2023 <X_2023>`. (Note that the :g:`A <A>`-vertex of the medial triangle is the orthogonal projection of :g:`A' <A'>` on line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and cyclically for :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>`.) (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 11, 2019)
