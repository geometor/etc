.. _preamble-0029:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Gallatly Circle, etc., 2007-2040
   :class: note

   In his book The Modern Geometry of the Triangle, 2nd edition (Francis Hodgson, London, 1913), William Gallatly, on page 117, introduces the pedal cicle of the 1st and 2nd Brocard points. The circle is here named the Gallatly circle. Its center is the Brocard midpoint, :ref:`X_39 <X_39>`, and the radius, R sin ω, where ω denotes the Brocard angle, given by

   tan ω = 4[area(:g:`ABC << A B C >>`)]/(:math:`a^{2}` + :math:`b^{2}` + :math:`c^{2})`.

   Other special symbols used in this section are identified just before :ref:`X_1662 <X_1662>`.

   Centers :ref:`X_2007 <X_2007>`-:ref:`X_2040 <X_2040>` were contributed with coordinates by Peter J. C. Moses during September, 2003. For centers of similitude of the Gallatly circle and the circumcircle, see :ref:`X_1689 <X_1689>` and :ref:`X_1690 <X_1690>`; for those of the Gallatly circle and the 2nd Lemoine circle, see :ref:`X_1671 <X_1671>` and :ref:`X_1670 <X_1670>`.
