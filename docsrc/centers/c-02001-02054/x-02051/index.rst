X_2051
======

.. rubric:: EXSIMILICENTER(NINE-POINT CIRCLE, APOLLONIUS CIRCLE)

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_2051 <X_2051>` is the external center of similitude of the nine-point and Apollonius circles. Trilinears for the internal center, :ref:`X_10 <X_10>`, result from g(a,b,c) by changing the "-" just after "cos :g:`A″ <A″>` to "+". (The two circles are described just before :ref:`X_1662 <X_1662>`.)
