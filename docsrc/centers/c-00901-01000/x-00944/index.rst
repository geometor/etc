   Let :g:`H_{a} <H_a>` be the hyperbola passing through :g:`A <A>`, and with foci at :g:`B <B>` and C. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the polar of :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` with respect to :g:`H_{a} <H_a>`. Define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` ∩ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` ∩ :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` ∩ :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`. Triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and its orthocenter is :ref:`X_944 <X_944>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, January 29, 2018)
