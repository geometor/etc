.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Points Associated with Equilateral Triangles (are associated with six equilateral triangles)
   :class: note

   The points :ref:`X_3272 <X_3272>` to :ref:`X_3283 <X_3283>` are associated with six equilateral triangles related to the (1st) Morley triangle; trilinears for vertices of these triangles are given just below. Received from Milorad R. Stevanovic, Nov. 24, 2007 and Dec. 25, 2007.

   | **1st Morley triangle,** :g:`M_{1}M_{2}M_{3} << M_1 M_2 M_3 >>`, where
   | :g:`M_{1} <M_1>` = 1 : 2 cos C/3 : 2 cos B/3
   | :g:`M_{2} <M_2>` = 2 cos :math:`C/3 : 1 : 2` cos A/3
   | :g:`M_{3} <M_3>` = 2 cos B/3 : 2 cos A/3 : 1

   | **2nd Morley triangle,** :g:`P_{1}P_{2}P_{3} << P_1 P_2 P_3 >>`, where
   | :g:`P_{1} <P_1>` = 1 : 2 cos(C/3 - 2π/3) : 2 cos(B/3 - 2π/3)
   | :g:`P_{2} <P_2>` = 2 :math:`\cos(C/3 - 2\pi /3) : 1 : 2` cos(A/3 - 2π/3)
   | :g:`P_{3} <P_3>` = 2 cos(B/3 - 2π/3) : 2 cos(A/3 - 2π/3) : 1

   | **3rd Morley triangle,** :math:`S_{1}`\ :math:`S_{2}`\ :math:`S_{3}`, where
   | :math:`S_{1}` = 1 : 2 cos(C/3 - 4π/3) : 2 cos(B/3 - 4π/3)
   | :math:`S_{2}` = 2 :math:`\cos(C/3 - 4\pi /3) : 1 : 2` cos(A/3 - 4π/3)
   | :math:`S_{3}` = 2 cos(B/3 - 4π/3) : 2 cos(A/3 - 4π/3) : 1

   | The triangles :g:`M_{1}M_{2}M_{3} << M_1 M_2 M_3 >>`, :g:`P_{1}P_{2}P_{3} << P_1 P_2 P_3 >>`, :math:`S_{1}`\ :math:`S_{2}`\ :math:`S_{3}` are discussed in :cite:`TCCT <tcct>`, page 165-166. The three are pairwise homothetic. See also
   | `1st Morley triangle at MathWorld <http://mathworld.wolfram.com/FirstMorleyTriangle.html>`__
   | `2nd Morley triangle at MathWorld <http://mathworld.wolfram.com/SecondMorleyTriangle.html>`__
   | `3rd Morley triangle at MathWorld <http://mathworld.wolfram.com/ThirdMorleyTriangle.html>`__

   | **Unique equilateral triangle inscribed in ABC and homothetic to the 1st Morley triangle**, :g:`J_{1}J_{2}J_{3} << J_1 J_2 J_3 >>`, where
   | :g:`J_{1} <J_1>` =\ :math:`0 : \sin(A/3 - C/3 + \pi /3) : \sin(A/3 - B/3 + \pi /3)`
   | :g:`J_{2} <J_2>` =\ :math:`\sin(B/3 - C/3 + \pi /3) : 0 : \sin(B/3 - A/3 + \pi /3)`
   | :g:`J_{3} <J_3>` =\ :math:`\sin(C/3 - B/3 + \pi /3) : \sin(C/3 - A/3 + \pi /3) : 0`

   **Circumtangential triangle,** inscribed in the circumcircle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and homothetic to the 1st Morley triangle, :g:`T_{1}T_{2}T_{3} << T_1 T_2 T_3 >>`. For trilinears, of the vertices, see :cite:`TCCT <tcct>`, page 166, or MathWorld. Trilinears found by M. Stevanovic:

   | :g:`T_{1} <T_1>` = sin(B/3 - A/3 + π/3) sin(C/3 - A/3 + π/3) : sin(C/3 - B/3) sin(B/3 - A/3 + π/3) : sin(B/3 - C/3) sin(C/3 - A/3 + π/3)
   | :g:`T_{2} <T_2>` = sin(C/3 - A/3) sin(A/3 - B/3 + π/3) : sin(C/3 - B/3 + π/3) sin(A/3 - B/3 + π/3) : sin(A/3 - C/3) sin(C/3 - B/3 + π/3)
   | :g:`T_{3} <T_3>` = sin(B/3 - A/3) sin(A/3 - C/3 + π/3) : sin(A/3 - B/3) sin(B/3 - C/3 + π/3) : sin(A/3 - C/3 + π/3) sin(B/3 - C/3 + π/3)

   **Circumnormal triangle** inscribed in the circumcircle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and homothetic to the 1st Morley triangle, :g:`N_{1}N_{2}N_{3} << N_1 N_2 N_3 >>`. For trilinears, of the vertices, see :cite:`TCCT <tcct>`, page 166, or MathWorld. Trilinears found by M. Stevanovic:

   | :g:`N_{1} <N_1>` = - cos(B/3 - A/3 + π/3) cos(C/3 - A/3 + π/3) : cos(B/3 - C/3) cos(B/3 - A/3 + π/3) : cos(B/3 - C/3) cos(C/3 - A/3 + π/3)
   | :g:`N_{2} <N_2>` = cos(C/3 - A/3) cos(A/3 - B/3 + π/3) : - cos(C/3 - B/3 + π/3) cos(A/3 - B/3 + π/3) : cos(A/3 - A/3) cos(C/3 - B/3 + π/3)
   | :g:`N_{3} <N_3>` = cos(A/3 - B/3) cos(A/3 - C/3 + π/3) : cos(A/3 - B/3) cos(B/3 - C/3 + π/3) : - cos(A/3 - C/3 + π/3) cos(B/3 - C/3 + π/3)

   See also :ref:`X_3602 <X_3602>`-:ref:`X_3609 <X_3609>` and
