X(118) = MIDPOINT OF X(4) AND X(101)
====================================

.. enunciation::

   Let A'B'C' be the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`. Let La be the Gergonne line of triangle AB'C', and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A″ = Lb∩Lc, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines A'A″, B'B″, C'C″ concur in :ref:`X(118) <X(118)>`, which is :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>`-of-A″B″C″. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 31 2018)
