X(549) = MIDPOINT OF X(2) AND X(3) — Code
=========================================

.. enunciation::

   Let Pa be the parabola with focus A and directrix BC. Let La be the polar of :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>` wrt Pa. Define Lb, Lc cyclically. Let A' = Lb∩Lc, B' = Lc∩La, C' = La∩Lb. :ref:`X(141) <X(141)>` of triangle A'B'C' is :ref:`X(549) <X(549)>`. :ref:`X(549) <X(549)>` is also the centroid of the six points of tangency of lines from :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>` to Pa, Pb, and Pc. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 20, 2016)
