X(549) = MIDPOINT OF X(2) AND X(3) — Code
=========================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`P_{a} <P_a>` be the parabola with focus A and directrix :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the polar of :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>` wrt :g:`P_{a} <P_a>`. Define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`, :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`. :ref:`X(141) <X(141)>` of triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is :ref:`X(549) <X(549)>`. :ref:`X(549) <X(549)>` is also the centroid of the six points of tangency of lines from :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>` to :g:`P_{a} <P_a>`, :g:`P_{b} <P_b>`, and :g:`P_{c} <P_c>`.
