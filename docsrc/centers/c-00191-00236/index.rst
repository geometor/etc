X_191–X_236
===========

.. rubric:: Ceva Conjugates

.. enunciation::

   Perspectors of the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :g:`P <P>` and the :term:`anticevian triangle <anticevian-triangle>` of Q (P-:term:`Ceva conjugates <ceva-conjugate>` of :g:`Q <Q>`) for classical :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>`.

.. figure:: c-00191-00236.svg
   :alt: X_191–X_236 — Ceva Conjugates
   :align: center

   **X_191–X_236** (*Ceva Conjugates*) — 15 representative centers (from 46 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0005:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers X_191-X_236 (Ceva conjugates)
   :class: note

   Centers :ref:`X_191 <X_191>`-:ref:`X_236 <X_236>` are Ceva conjugates. (The P-Ceva conjugate of :g:`Q <Q>` is the perspector of the cevian triangle of :g:`P <P>` and the anticevian triangle of :g:`Q <Q>`.)
