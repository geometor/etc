Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the homothetic center of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>` of the A-:term:`extouch triangle <extouch-triangle>` and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to the 3rd extouch triangle at :ref:`X_223 <X_223>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, September 14, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`V_{a} <V_a>`, :g:`V_{b} <V_b>`, :g:`V_{c} <V_c>` be the antipodes of V=\ :ref:`X_40 <X_40>` in the circles (:g:`VBC << V B C >>`), (:g:`VCA << V C A >>`), (:g:`VAB << V A B >>`), respectively. The triangle :g:`V_{a}V_{b}V_{c} << V_a V_b V_c >>` is here named the **Bevan antipodal triangle** (:cite:`Hyacinthos #29638 <hyacinthos>`; however, see next note). The point :ref:`X_223 <X_223>` is the unique finite fixed point of the affine transformation that maps the reference triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` onto :g:`V_{a}V_{b}V_{c} << V_a V_b V_c >>`. (:person:`Angel Montesdeoca <angel-montesdeoca>`, October 15, 2019)

.. container:: note-item

   The construction of the triangle :g:`V_{a}V_{b}V_{c} << V_a V_b V_c >>` can be generalized for an arbitrary point P (instead of V=\ :ref:`X_40 <X_40>`), and is, in fact, the antipedal triangle of P (Randy Hutson, message in :cite:`Anopolis <adgeom>` Group). Thus, "Bevan antipodal triangle" = antipedal triangle of :ref:`X_40 <X_40>`. (Angel Montesdeoca, October 16, 2019)
