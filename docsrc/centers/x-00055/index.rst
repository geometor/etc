X(55) = INSIMILICENTER(CIRCUMCIRCLE, INCIRCLE)
==============================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` be the second points of intersection of the angle bisectors of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` with its incircle. Let :g:`O_{a} <O_a>` be the circle externally tangent to the incircle at :g:`A' <A'>`, and internally tangent to the circumcircle; define :g:`O_{b} <O_b>` and :g:`O_{c} <O_c>` cyclically. Then :ref:`X(55) <X(55)>` is the radical center of circles :g:`O_{a} <O_a>`, :g:`O_{b} <O_b>`, :g:`O_{c} <O_c>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the touchpoint of :g:`O_{a} <O_a>` and the circumcircle, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`A'A″ <[ A' A″ ]>`, :g:`B'B″ <[ B' B″ ]>`, :g:`C'C″ <[ C' C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X(55) <X(55)>`. Let :g:`B_{a} <B_a>`, :g:`C_{a} <C_a>` be the intersections of lines :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, respectively, and the antiparallel to :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` through a point P. Define :g:`C_{b} <C_b>`, :g:`A_{b} <A_b>`, :g:`A_{c} <A_c>`, :g:`B_{c} <B_c>` cyclically. Triangles :g:`AB_{a}C_{a} << A B_a C_a >>`, :g:`A_{b}BC_{b} << A_b B C_b >>`, :g:`A_{c}B_{c}C << A_c B_c C >>` are congruent only when :g:`P <P>` = :ref:`X(55) <X(55)>` or one of its 3 extraversions. Let :g:`A*B*C* << A* B* C* >>` be the :term:`incentral triangle <incentral-triangle>`. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the reflection of line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` in line :g:`AA* <[ A A* ]>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let A''' = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, and define B''' and C'''. The lines A\*A''', B\*B''', C\*C''' concur in :ref:`X(55) <X(55)>`.
