X_1286–X_1311
=============

.. rubric:: Collings Transforms

.. enunciation::

   Concurrence points on the circumcircle of the circumcircles of :g:`AB'C' << A B' C' >>`, :g:`A'BC' << A' B C' >>`, and :g:`A'B'C << A' B' C >>`, where :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is the reflection of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` in :g:`P <P>`.

.. figure:: c-01286-01311.svg
   :alt: X_1286–X_1311 — Collings Transforms
   :align: center

   **X_1286–X_1311** (*Collings Transforms*) — 14 representative centers (from 26 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0018:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Collings Transforms: 1286-1311
   :class: note

   If :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is a triangle, P is a point, and :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` are the reflections of :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>` in :g:`P <P>`, then the circumcircles of triangles :g:`AB'C' << A B' C' >>`, :g:`A'BC' << A' B C' >>` and :g:`A'B'C << A' B' C >>` concur at a point :g:`Q <Q>` on the circumcircle of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The transformation T given by :g:`Q <Q>` = T(P) was described by

   **S. N. Collings,** "Reflections on reflections 2", *Mathematical Gazette* 1974, page 264.

   It was further discussed by :person:`Floor van Lamoen <floor-van-lamoen>` and :person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>`, and coordinates were found by Barry Wolk; see :cite:`Hyacinthos #4547 <hyacinthos>`, #4548, #6469, #6538, #6546, #6560. :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>` noted that T(P) is the point, other than :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`, in which the circumconic centered at P meets the circumcircle of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` (#4548).

   If :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` (trilinears), then :g:`Q <Q>` =\ :math:`f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)`, where

   :math:`f(a,b,c) = 1/[bz(ax + by - cz) - cy(ax + cz - by)]`.

   For given Q, the set of points P satisfying T(P) = Q is a conic. Examples follow:

   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`, the conic passes through X_i for I = 125;
   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_98 <X_98>`, the conic passes through X_i for I = 115, 868;
   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_99 <X_99>`, the conic passes through X_i for I = 2, 39, 114, 618, 619, 629, 630, 641, 642, 1125;
   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_100 <X_100>`, the conic passes through X_i for I = 1, 9, 10, 119, 142, 214, 442, 1145;
   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_107 <X_107>`, the conic passes through X_i for I = 4, 133, 800, 1249;
   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_110 <X_110>`, the conic passes through X_i for I = 5, 6, 113, 141, 206, 942, 960, 1147, 1209;
   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_476 <X_476>`, the conic passes through X_i for I = 30.

   :person:`Bernard Gibert <bernard-gibert>` (4/02/03) identified T(P) as the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of the line of :ref:`X_6 <X_6>` and the :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`-:term:`Ceva conjugate <ceva-conjugate>` of P. He identified the locus of P as the rectangular hyperbola that circumscribes the :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>` and has center W given by the vector equation 4\ :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`\ W = :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`\ Q. The :term:`anticomplement <anticomplement>` of this hyperbola is the rectangular :g:`ABC << A B C >>`-circumhyperbola whose center is the :term:`complement <complement>` of Q. Thus, referring to examples given above:

   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_99 <X_99>`, the conic is the Kiepert hyperbola of the medial triangle;
   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_100 <X_100>`, the conic is the Feuerbach hyperbola of the medial triangle;
   | if :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_110 <X_110>`, the conic is the Jerabek hyperbola of the medial triangle.
