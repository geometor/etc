   The first of ten sections is an Introduction quoted, in part, here:

   We will treat two remarkable triangles: the triangle bounded by the perpendicular bisectors of the internal angle bisectors of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the triangle bounded by the perpendicular bisectors of the external angle bisectors of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. These two triangles and the triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` are three perspective triangles, having a common perspectrix: the Lemoine axis of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The mutual perspectors of the three triangles will be called the first, second and third Sharygin points of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` (after a problem of Igor Sharygin - see Section 10). The report introduces fifteen Sharygin points, of which the 1st, 2nd, 4th, and 6th are :ref:`X_256 <X_256>`, :ref:`X_291 <X_291>`, :ref:`X_846 <X_846>`,

      * and :ref:`X_1054 <X_1054>`, respectively. :ref:`X_1281 <X_1281>` is the 3rd Sharygin point. See also :cite:`Hyacinthos #6293 <hyacinthos>` and #6315.
