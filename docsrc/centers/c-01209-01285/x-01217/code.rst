.. rubric:: CRETAN PERSPECTOR

.. enunciation::

   Using barycentric coordinates, let :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`p : q : r` be a point not on a sideline of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and let :g:`P' <P'>` be the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of P. Let :g:`DEF << D E F >>` and :g:`D'E'F' << D' E' F' >>` be the :term:`pedal triangles <pedal-triangle>` of :g:`P <P>` and :g:`P' <P'>`, respectively. Let
