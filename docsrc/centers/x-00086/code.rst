.. enunciation::

Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the tangent to the circumcircle at :g:`A' <A'>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A* <A*>` = :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>`, and define :g:`B* <B*>` and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically.
