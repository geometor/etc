Notes & Constructions
---------------------

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``L_A`` be the line through :g:`A <A>` parallel to :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`\ \ :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`, and define ``L_B`` and ``L_C`` cyclically. Let ``M_A`` be the reflection of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` in ``L_A``, and define ``M_B`` and ``M_C`` cyclically. Let ``A'`` = ``M_B``\ ∩\ ``M_C``, and define cyclically ``B'`` and ``C'`` cyclically. The triangle ``A'B'C'`` is inversely similar to, and 3 times the size of, :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let ``A″B″C″`` be the reflection of ``A'B'C'`` in line :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`\ \ :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`. The triangle ``A″B″C″`` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, with center of homothety :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`; see :cite:`Hyacinthos #16741 <hyacinthos>`/16782, Sep 2008. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, March 25, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   The circumcircle of the :term:`incentral triangle <incentral-triangle>` intersects the incircle at 2 points, :ref:`X_11 <X_11>` and :ref:`X_3024 <X_3024>`, and the nine-point circle at 2 points, :ref:`X_11 <X_11>` and :ref:`X_115 <X_115>`. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``N_a`` = :ref:`X_5 <X_5>` of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, ``N_b`` = :ref:`X_5 <X_5>` of :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`\ :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, ``N_c`` = :ref:`X_5 <X_5>` of :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`\ :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`. Then :ref:`X_11 <X_11>` = :ref:`X_186 <X_186>` of ``N_aN_bN_c``. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``J_aJ_bJ_c`` be the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>` and ``F_aF_bF_c`` be the :term:`Feuerbach triangle <feuerbach-triangle>`. Let ``F_a'`` = {:ref:`X_5 <X_5>`,\ ``J_a``}-harmonic conjugate of ``F_a``, and define ``F_b'``, ``F_c'`` cyclically. The lines ``AF_a'``, ``BF_b'``, ``CF_c'`` concur in :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``H_aH_bH_c`` be the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`. Let ``L_a`` be the antiorthic axis of ``AH_bH_c``, and define ``L_b`` and ``L_c`` cyclically. Let ``A″`` = ``L_b``\ ∩\ ``L_c``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The triangle ``A″B″C″`` is inversely similar to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, with similitude center :ref:`X_9 <X_9>`, and :ref:`X_11 <X_11>` = :ref:`X_55 <X_55>`-of-``A″B″C″``. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``H_aH_bH_c`` be the orthic triangle. Let ``N_a`` be the Nagel line of ``AH_bH_c``, and define ``N_b`` and ``N_c`` cyclically. Let ``A″`` = ``N_b``\ ∩\ ``N_c``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The triangle ``A″B″C″`` is inversely similar to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and :ref:`X_11 <X_11>` = :ref:`X_36 <X_36>`-of-``A″B″C″``. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``H_aH_bH_c`` be the orthic triangle. The lines ``IO`` of ``AH_bH_c``, ``BH_cH_a``, ``CH_aH_b`` concur in :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``H_aH_bH_c`` be the orthic triangle. The lines ``IO`` of ``AH_bH_c``, ``BH_cH_a``, ``CH_aH_b`` concur in :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`. (Randy Hutson, June 27, 2018)
