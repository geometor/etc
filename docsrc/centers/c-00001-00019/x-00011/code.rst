X_11
====

.. rubric:: FEUERBACH POINT

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_11 <X_11>` is the point of tangency of the nine-point circle and the incircle. The nine-point circle is the circumcenter of the :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>`, as well as the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`. Feuerbach's famous theorem states that the nine-point circle is tangent to the incircle and the three excircles.
