Four-Triangle Symbolic Invariance
---------------------------------

.. figure:: 05_four_triangles.svg
   :alt: X_8 — Invariance Across Four Canonical Symbolic Reference Triangles
   :align: center

   **Four-Triangle Invariance of X_8** across :g:`Heronian (15, 13, 14) <panel-heronian>`, :g:`Right (3, 4, 5) <panel-right>`, :g:`Kepler (1, √φ, φ) <panel-kepler>`, and :g:`Kimberling (6, 9, 13) <panel-kimberling>`.

Across the four canonical algebraic number fields — :g:`Heronian (15, 13, 14) <heronian-X_8>` over :math:`\mathbb{Q}` (:math:`X_8 = \left(7,\; 4\right)`), :g:`Right (3, 4, 5) <right-X_8>` over :math:`\mathbb{Q}` (:math:`X_8 = \left(\frac{11}{5},\; \frac{2}{5}\right)`), :g:`Kepler (1, √φ, φ) <kepler-X_8>` over :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\phi})`, and :g:`Kimberling (6, 9, 13) <kimberling-X_8>` over :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{35})` (:math:`X_8 = \left(\frac{68}{13},\; \frac{4 \sqrt{35}}{91}\right)`) — **X_8** remains strictly interior (:math:`\Delta_a, \Delta_b, \Delta_c > 0`) across all four acute, Pythagorean right, golden right, and obtuse scalene triangles, preserving its exact homogeneous law :math:`u : v : w` without floating-point approximation.
