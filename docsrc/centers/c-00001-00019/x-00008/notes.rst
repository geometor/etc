Notes & Constructions
---------------------

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A_b``, ``A_c``, ``B_c``, ``B_a``, ``C_a``, ``C_b`` be as in the construction of the Conway circle (http://mathworld.wolfram.com/ConwayCircle.html). Let ``O_a`` be the circumcircle of ``AB_cC_b``, and define ``O_b``, ``O_c`` cyclically. :ref:`X_8 <X_8>` is the radical center of ``O_a``, ``O_b``, ``O_c``. see also :ref:`X_21 <X_21>` and :ref:`X_274 <X_274>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A'B'C'`` be Triangle T(-2,1). Let ``A″`` be the trilinear product ``B'``\ \*\ ``C'``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The lines ``AA″``, ``BB″``, ``CC″`` concur in :ref:`X_519 <X_519>`. The lines ``A'A″``, ``B'B″``, ``C'C″`` concur in :ref:`X_8 <X_8>`. (Randy Hutson, November 18, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``I_a`` be the reflection of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` in the perpendicular bisector of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define ``I_b`` and ``I_c`` cyclically. :ref:`X_8 <X_8>` = :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`-of-``I_aI_bI_c``. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A_28B_28C_28`` be the Gemini triangle 28. Let ``L_A`` be the line through ``A_28`` parallel to :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define ``L_B`` and ``L_C`` cyclically. Let ``A'_28`` = ``L_B``\ ∩\ ``L_C``, and define ``B'_28``, ``C'_28`` cyclically. Triangle ``A'_28B'_28C'_28`` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_8 <X_8>`. (Randy Hutson, November 30, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   For yet another construction of :ref:`X_8 <X_8>`, see :person:`Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna <dasari-naga-vijay-krishna>`, "On A Simple Construction of :term:`Triangle Centers <triangle-center>` :ref:`X_8 <X_8>`, :ref:`X_197 <X_197>`, X(K) & :ref:`X_594 <X_594>`", `Scientific Inquiry and Review, Vol. 2, Issue 3, July 2018. <https://admin.umt.edu.pk/Media/Site/SSC/SubSites/SIR/FileManager/Sir2Three/Article-3%20(1).pdf>`__

.. container:: note-item

   Another construction is given by Xavier Dussau: **Elementary construction of the Nagel point.** (``NagelPointDussauConstruction.pdf``) (April 29, 2020)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``T`` be any one of these trianges: {Ascella, 1st circumperp, 2nd circumperp}. Let ``O_A`` be the circle centered at the :g:`A <A>`-vertex of ``T`` and passing through :g:`A <A>`; define ``O_B`` and ``O_C`` cyclically. :ref:`X_8 <X_8>` is the radical center of ``O_A``, ``O_B``, ``O_C``. (Randy Hutson, August 30, 2020)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_8 <X_8>` is the {:ref:`X_69 <X_69>`,\ :ref:`X_75 <X_75>`}-harmonic conjugate of :ref:`X_7 <X_7>`.
