X_5
====

.. rubric:: NINE-POINT CENTER

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_5 <X_5>` is the center of the nine-point circle. Euler showed in that this circle passes through the midpoints of the sides of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and the feet of the altitudes of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, hence six of the nine points.
