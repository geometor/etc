Notes & Constructions
---------------------

.. container:: note-item

   If, however, A> 2π/3, then the Fermat point, defined geometrically as the minimizer of \|\ ``AX``\ \| + \|\ ``BX``\ \| + \|\ ``CX``\ \|, is not the 1st isogonic center (which is defined by the above trilinears). Trilinears for the Fermat point when A> 2π/3 are simply :math:`1:0:0`. To represent the Fermat point in the form :math:`f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)`, one must use Boolean variables, as shown at `Fermat point. <http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_point>`__

.. container:: note-item

   The Evans conic is introduced in **Evans, Lawrence S.,** "A Conic Through Six :term:`Triangle Centers <triangle-center>`," `Forum Geometricorum <http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2002volume2/FG200211index.html>`__ 2 (2002) 89-92.

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``N_aN_bN_c``, ``N_a'N_b'N_c'`` be the outer and :term:`inner Napoleon triangles <inner-napoleon-triangle>`, respectively. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of ``N_a'``, wrt ``N_aN_bN_c``, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines ``N_aA'``, ``N_bB'``, ``N_cC'`` concur in :ref:`X_13 <X_13>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, January 29, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``P`` be a point inside triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` such that the line ``AP`` bisects angle ``BPC``, and NBP bisets ``CPA``, and ``CP`` bisects ``APB``. Then ``P`` = :ref:`X_13 <X_13>`. The locus of P such that ``AP`` bisects ``BPC`` is the circumcubic given by the barycentric equation :math:`c^{2}`\ :math:`xy^{2}` - :math:`b^{2}`\ :math:`xz^{2}` + (:math:`a^{2}` - :math:`b^{2}` + :math:`c^{2})`\ :math:`y^{2}`\ z - (:math:`a^{2}` + :math:`b^{2}` - :math:`c^{2})`\ :math:`yz^{2}` = 0, and the other two cubics are given cyclically. :person:`Bernard Gibert <bernard-gibert>` discusses these cubics as K053A, K053B, K053C; see `Apollonian strophoids. <https://bernard-gibert.pagesperso-orange.fr/Exemples/k053.html>`__ (Paul Hanna and :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, August 6, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   The line :ref:`X_13 <X_13>`\ \ :ref:`X_15 <X_15>` is parallel to the Euler line, and the distance between the two lines is :math:`(S_{B} - S_{C})(S_{C} - S_{A})(S_{A} - S_{B})/|(\cot(\omega ) + 3^{1/2})|*((E - 8F)S^{2})^{1/2}`. (:person:`Kiminari Shinagawa <kiminari-shinagawa>`, February 20, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``Nap_aNap_bNap_c`` be the :term:`outer Napoleon triangle <outer-napoleon-triangle>` and ``Nap'_aNap'_bNap'_c`` the inner Napoleon triangle. Let ``A*`` be the isogonal conjugate, wrt ``Nap_aNap_bNap_c``, of ``Nap'_a``, and define ``B*`` and ``C*`` cyclically. The lines ``Nap_aA*``, ``Nap_bB*``, ``Nap_cC*`` concur in :ref:`X_13 <X_13>`. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``F`` be :ref:`X_13 <X_13>` or :ref:`X_14 <X_14>`. Let ``L`` and ``L'`` be lines through F such that the angle between them is π/3. Let ``L_BC`` = L∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define ``L_CA`` and ``L_AB`` cyclically. Let ``L'_BC`` = ``L'``\ ∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define ``L'_CA`` and ``L'_AB`` cyclically. The lines ``L_BCL'_CA``, ``L_CAL'_AB``, ``L_ABL'_BC`` concur. (:person:`Dao Thanh Oai <dao-thanh-oai>`, 2014)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``F`` be :ref:`X_13 <X_13>` or :ref:`X_14 <X_14>`. Let ``A_0``, ``B_0``, ``C_0`` be points on :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, respectively, such that the directed angles ``FA_0``-to-``FC_0`` = π/3 and ``FC_0``-to-``FB_0`` = π/3. The points ``A_0``, ``B_0``, ``C_0`` are collinear. (Dao Thanh Oai, 2014)
