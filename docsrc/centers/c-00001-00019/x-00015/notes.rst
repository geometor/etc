Notes & Constructions
---------------------

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A'B'C'`` be the 4th Brocard triangle and ``A″B″C″`` be the 4th anti-Brocard triangle. The circumcircles of ``AA'A″``, ``BB'B″``, ``CC'C″`` concur in two points, :ref:`X_15 <X_15>` and :ref:`X_16 <X_16>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 20, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   The line :ref:`X_13 <X_13>`\ \ :ref:`X_15 <X_15>` is parallel to the Euler line, and the distance between the two lines is :math:`(S_{B} - S_{C})(S_{C} - S_{A})(S_{A} - S_{B})/(\cot(\omega ) + 3^{1/2})|*((E - 8F)S^{2})^{1/2}`. (:person:`Kiminari Shinagawa <kiminari-shinagawa>`, February 20, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   The :term:`pedal triangle <pedal-triangle>` of :ref:`X_15 <X_15>` is equilateral.

   This list of circles was contributed by :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, April 17, 2021, with the following notes. Starting with a circle :ref:`X_15 <X_15>`, :ref:`X_16 <X_16>`, and a point ``P`` =\ :math:`p : q : r`, the center of the circle is given by

      a^2\*(c^2\*(a^2 + b^2 - c^2)\*p\*q + a^2\*c^2\*q^2 - b^2\*(a^2 - b^2 + c^2)\*p\*r - a^2\*b^2\*r^2) : : ,

   and the power of vertex :g:`A <A>` with repect to the circle is

      (b^2\*c^2\*(-(c^2\*(a^2 + b^2 - c^2)\*p\*q) - a^2\*c^2\*q^2 + b^2\*(a^2 - b^2 + c^2)\*p\*r + a^2\*b^2\*r^2))/((p + q + r)\*(-(b^2\*c^2\*(b^2 - c^2)\*p) + a^2\*c^2\*(a^2 - c^2)\*q - a^2\*b^2\*(a^2 - b^2)\*r)).

.. container:: note-item

   If P lies on the Lemoine axis, then the power of :g:`A <A>` with respect to to the circle is

      -b^2\*c^2\*(c^2\*q + b^2\*r)/(c^2\*(a^2 - b^2)\*q + b^2\*(a^2 - c^2)\*r)).

.. container:: note-item

   Consider this `experiment <https://youtu.be/e3MkijszDEA>`__, in which 3 regular hexagons, ``HA``, ``H_b``, ``HC`` are erected on the sides of ``T`` = :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let the hexagon vertices be labeled as

      * ``H_a`` = {B,\ ``A_1``,\ ``A_2``,\ ``A_3``,\ ``A_4``,\ :g:`C <C>`), ``H_b`` = {C,\ ``B_1``,\ ``B_2``,\ ``B_3``,\ ``B_4``,\ :g:`A <A>`}, ``H_c``\ ={A,\ ``C_1``,\ ``C_2``,\ ``C_3``,\ ``C_4``,\ :g:`B <B>`},
      * and the vertices of the 3 "flank-triangles", by ``F_a`` = {A,\ ``C_1``,\ ``B_4``}, ``F_b`` = "{B,\ ``A_1``,\ ``C_4``}, ``F_c`` = {C,\ ``B_1``,\ ``A_4``}.

   Between each pair of consecutive hexagons, define 3 "flank" triangles ``F_a`` = {A,\ ``C_1``,\ ``B_4``}, ``F_b`` = {B,\ ``A_1``,\ ``C_4``}, ``F_c``\ ={C,\ ``B_a``,\ ``A_4``}. Let ``T'`` be the triangle with vertices :ref:`X_15 <X_15>`-of-``F_a``, :ref:`X_15 <X_15>`-of-``F_b``, :ref:`X_15 <X_15>`-of-``F_c``. Claim (1): ``T'`` is perspective to ``T``, and the perspector is :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`. Claim (2): :ref:`X_16 <X_16>`-of-``F_a`` = :ref:`X_16 <X_16>`-of-``F_b`` = :ref:`X_15 <X_15>`-of-``F_c`` . (Dan Reznick, November 11, 2021)
