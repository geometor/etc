Notes & Constructions
---------------------

.. container:: note-item

   Although **John Clawson** (``../bstud/clawson.html``) studied this point in 1925, it was studied earlier by Lemoine:

   **Emile Lemoine,** "Quelques questions se rapportant à l'étude des antiparallèles des côtes d'un triangle", *Bulletin de la S. M. F.,* tome 14 (1886), p. 107-128, specifically, on page 114. This article is available online at `Numdam <http://www.numdam.org/>`__.

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A'B'C'`` be the 4th Brocard triangle. Let ``A″`` be the trilinear product ``B'``\ \*\ ``C'``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The lines ``AA″``, ``BB″``, ``CC″`` concur in :ref:`X_19 <X_19>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 18, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`H_aH_bH_c << H_a H_b H_c >>` be the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`. Let ``A″`` be the trilinear product of the (real or imaginary) circumcircle intercepts of line ``H_bH_c``. Define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The lines ``AA″``, ``BB″``, ``CC″`` concur in :ref:`X_19 <X_19>`. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``J_aJ_bJ_c`` be the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>`. Let ``A″`` be the pole, wrt the polar circle, of line ``J_bJ_c``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The lines ``J_aA″``, ``J_bB″``, ``J_cC″`` concur in :ref:`X_19 <X_19>`. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A'B'C'`` be the :term:`hexyl triangle <hexyl-triangle>`. Let ``L_a`` be the :term:`trilinear polar <trilinear-polar>` of ``A'``, and define ``L_b`` and ``L_c`` cyclically. Let ``A″`` = ``L_b``\ ∩\ ``L_c``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The lines ``AA″``, ``BB″``, ``CC″`` concur in :ref:`X_19 <X_19>`. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A'B'C'`` be the hexyl triangle. Let ``A_b`` = :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ ∩\ ``C'A'``, ``A_c`` = :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ ∩\ ``A'B'``, and define ``B_c``, ``B_a``, ``C_a``, ``C_b`` cyclically. Then ``A_b``, ``A_c``, ``B_c``, ``B_a``, ``C_a``, ``C_b`` lie on an ellipse. Let ``A″`` be the intersection of the tangents to the ellipse at ``B_a`` and ``C_a``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The lines ``AA″``, ``BB″``, ``CC″`` concur in :ref:`X_19 <X_19>`. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A'B'C'`` be the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>` of the :term:`extangents triangle <extangents-triangle>`, if :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is acute. Then ``A'B'C'`` is homothetic to the anti-Ara triangle at :ref:`X_19 <X_19>`. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``L_a`` be the A-extraversion of line :ref:`X_650 <X_650>`\ \ :ref:`X_663 <X_663>` (the trilinear polar of :ref:`X_9 <X_9>`), and define ``L_b`` and ``L_c`` cyclically. Let ``A'`` = ``L_b``\ ∩\ ``L_c``, and define ``B'`` and ``C'`` cyclically. The lines ``AA'``, ``BB'``, ``CC'`` concur in :ref:`X_19 <X_19>`. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)
