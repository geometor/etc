Notes & Constructions
---------------------

.. container:: note-item

   As a point on the Euler line, :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` has Shinagawa coefficients :math:`(1, 0)`.

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` is the centroid of the set of 3 vertices, the centroid of the triangle including its interior, but not the centroid of the triangle without its interior; that centroid is :ref:`X_10 <X_10>`.

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` is the identity of the group of :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>` under "barycentric multiplication" defined by

   :math:`(x : y : z) \cdot (u : v : w) = xu : yv : zw`.

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` is the unique point X (as a function of a,b,c) for which the vector-sum ``XA`` + ``XB`` + ``XC`` is the zero vector.

.. container:: note-item

   The **Parry isodynamic circle** is here introduced as the circle centered at :ref:`X_2502 <X_2502>` that passes through the isodynamic points, :ref:`X_15 <X_15>` and :ref:`X_16 <X_16>`. This circle is orthogonal to both the circumcircle and Parry circle. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, February 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A'`` be the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of the perpendicular bisector of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define ``B'`` and ``C'`` cyclically. ``A'B'C'`` is also the :term:`anticomplement <anticomplement>` of the anticomplement of the midheight triangle. ``A'B'C'`` is homothetic to the midheight triangle at :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`. (Randy Hutson, January 29, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``J_aJ_bJ_c`` be the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>`. Let ``O_a`` be the ``J_a``-McCay circle of triangle ``J_aBC``, and define ``O_b``, ``O_c`` cyclically. :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` is the radical center of ``O_a``, ``O_b``, ``O_c``. (Randy Hutson, June 27, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` is the unique point that is the symmedian point of its :term:`antipedal triangle <antipedal-triangle>`. (Randy Hutson, August 19, 2019)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``A'B'C'`` be the midheight triangle. Let ``A″`` be the :term:`cevapoint <cevapoint>` of ``B'`` and ``C'``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically. The lines ``AA″``, ``BB″``, ``CC″`` concur in :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`. (Randy Hutson, October 8, 2019)

.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let

      * ``O_a`` = circle with diameter :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define ``O_b`` and ``O_c`` cyclically;
      * ``A'`` = the reflection of :g:`A <A>` with in ``O_a``, and define ``B'`` and ``C'`` cyclically.

   The triangle ``A'B'C'`` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`. (:person:`Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna <dasari-naga-vijay-krishna>`, April 19, 2021)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``N_a`` = reflection of ninepoint center(N) wrt to :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, define ``N_b``, ``N_c`` cyclically; then ``N_aN_bN_c`` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`. (Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna, June 8, 2021)

.. container:: note-item

   Let ``O`` be a point(not necessarily :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`), and let ``AOB`` be a fixed-angle sector of a circle :g:`C <C>`\ =(O,R), rigidly rotating about center O. Let ``P`` be an arbitrary point. The locus of :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`-of-``PAB`` is a circle ``C'``\ =(``O'``,\ ``R'``) whose center ``O'`` lies on ``OP``. The radius ``R'``, independent of ``P``, is given by ``R'``\ =\ :math:`\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2}}`\ (R/3). `Figure <https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/16231062/145676530-859098b9-0002-4eb5-bc07-b10fabcfb9db.png>`__. (:person:`Dan Reznik <dan-reznik>`, December 11, 2021)

.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let ``P`` be a point, and let

      * ``A'B'C'`` = :term:`pedal triangle <pedal-triangle>` of ``P``;
      * ``O'`` = circumcenter of ``A'B'C'``;
      * ``A″`` = reflection of ``A'`` in ``O'``, and define ``B″`` and ``C″`` cyclically.

   The triangles :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and ``A″B″C″`` are perspective, and their pespector is named here the **pedal antipodal perspector of P**. (Randy Hutson, `Hyacinthos 20403 <http://hyacinthos.epizy.com/message.php?msg=20403>`__, Nov. 21, 2011).

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` is the {:ref:`X_3 <X_3>`,\ :ref:`X_5 <X_5>`}-harmonic conjugate of :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`.
