X_2594–X_2676
=============

.. rubric:: Bicentric Pairs PU(68)–PU(85) & Golden Arbelos Points

.. enunciation::

   Sums, differences, :term:`crosssums <crosssum>`, :term:`crossdifferences <crossdifference>`, :term:`trilinear poles <trilinear-pole>`, and midpoints of bicentric pairs PU(68) through PU(85) (:ref:`X_2594 <X_2594>`\ –\ :ref:`X_2670 <X_2670>`), together with the 1st and 2nd Golden Arbelos points (:ref:`X_2671 <X_2671>`\ –\ :ref:`X_2676 <X_2676>`).

.. figure:: c-02594-02676.svg
   :alt: X_2594–X_2676 — Bicentric Pairs PU(68)–PU(85) & Golden Arbelos Points
   :align: center

   **X_2594–X_2676** (*Bicentric Pairs PU(68)–PU(85) & Golden Arbelos Points*) — 16 representative centers (from 83 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers Related to Bicentric Pairs
   :class: note

   Most of the centers :ref:`X_2594 <X_2594>` - :ref:`X_2670 <X_2670>` are defined in terms of bicentric pairs of points (e.g., the 1st and 2nd Brocard points).
