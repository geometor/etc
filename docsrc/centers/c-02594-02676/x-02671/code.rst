X_2671
======

.. rubric:: 1st GOLDEN ARBELOS POINT

.. enunciation::

   Suppose :g:`UV <[ U V ]>` is a segment and 1/2 < s < 1. Let :g:`P <P>` be the point on :g:`UV <[ U V ]>` satisfying \|UP\|/\|VP\| = s. Let :g:`O <O>` be a semicircle having diameter :g:`UV <[ U V ]>`. Let :g:`H <H>` be the semicircle having diameter VP, on the same side of :g:`UV <[ U V ]>` as O. Let :g:`K <K>` be the semicircle having diameter UP, on the same side of UP as O. The three circular arcs form an *arbelos*, R.
