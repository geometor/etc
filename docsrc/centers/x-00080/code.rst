X(80) = REFLECTION OF INCENTER IN FEUERBACH POINT — Code
========================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let A' be the reflection in BC of the A-vertex of the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>`, and define B' and C' cyclically. The circumcircles of A'BC, B'CA, and C'AB concur in :ref:`X(80) <X(80)>`. Also, the lines AA', BB', CC' concur in :ref:`X(80) <X(80)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)
