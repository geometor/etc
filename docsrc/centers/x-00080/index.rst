=================================================

.. enunciation::

Also, the lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X(80) <X(80)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)
