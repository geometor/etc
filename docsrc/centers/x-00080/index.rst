The lines :g:`A'A″ <[ A' A″ ]>`, :g:`B'B″ <[ B' B″ ]>`, :g:`C'C″ <[ C' C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_80 <X_80>`. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)
