X_476
=====

.. rubric:: TIXIER POINT

.. enunciation::

   The reflection of :ref:`X_110 <X_110>` in the Euler line; :ref:`X_476 <X_476>` is on the circumcircle. (Michel Tixier, 5/9/98). Also, :ref:`X_476 <X_476>` is the center of the polar conic of :ref:`X_30 <X_30>` with respect to the Neuberg cubic; this conic is a rectangular hyperbola passing through the incenter, the excenters, and :ref:`X_30 <X_30>`. (:person:`Peter Yff <peter-yff>`, 5/23/99)
