X(113) = JERABEK ANTIPODE
=========================

.. _preamble-0003:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers X(113)-X(139)
   :class: note

   Centers :ref:`X(113) <X(113)>`-:ref:`X(139) <X(139)>` lie on the nine-point circle.

   Suppose that X is a point on the nine-point circle, and let X' be the reflection of X in the orthocenter, H. Then X is the anticenter of the cyclic quadrilateral ABCX'. Let H\ :sub:`A` be the orthocenter of triangle BCX, Let H\ :sub:`B` be the orthocenter of CAX, and let H\ :sub:`C` be the orthocenter of triangle ABX. Then the quadrilateral HH\ :sub:`A`\ H\ :sub:`B`\ H\ :sub:`C` is homothetic to and congruent to the cyclic quadrilateral ABCX', and X is the center of homothety. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 9/23/2011)
