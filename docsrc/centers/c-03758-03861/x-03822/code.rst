X_3822
======

.. rubric:: X_2099com(3rd EULER TRIANGLE)

.. enunciation::

   Suppose that :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is an acute triangle. Let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the circle that is externally tangent to the nine-point circle and to the sidelines :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` and :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`. Define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. The three circles are here named the **Odehnal tritangent circles**. Their radical center is :ref:`X_3822 <X_3822>`. The center of the Apollonian circle of :g:`L_{A} <L_A>`, :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`, :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` is X_6167. (Boris Odehnal, "A Triad of Tritangent Circles, *Journal for Geometry and Graphics* 18 (2014) no. 1, 61-71)
