X(353) = INVERSE-IN-BROCARD-CIRCLE OF X(111) — Code
===================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let AaBaCa, AbBbCb, AcBcCc be the A-, B- and C-anti-altimedial triangles. Let A' be the trilinear product Aa\*Ab\*Ac, and define B' and C' cyclically. Triangle A'B'C' is the :term:`anticomplementary triangle <anticomplementary-triangle>` of the 1st Brocard triangle, and is similar to the anti-orthocentroidal triangle, with similitude center :ref:`X(353) <X(353)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 2, 2017)
