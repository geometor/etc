.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << N_a N_b N_c >>` be the :term:`extouch triangle <extouch-triangle>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the pole, wrt the polar circle, of line :g:`B'C' <[ N_b N_c ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_278 <X_278>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, September 14, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << N_a N_b N_c >>` be the 2nd extouch triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the pole, wrt the polar circle, of line :g:`B'C' <[ N_b N_c ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << N_a N_b N_c >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_278 <X_278>`. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`H_{a} <H_a>` be the hyperbola passing through :g:`A <A>`, and with foci at :g:`B <B>` and C. This is called the A-Soddy hyperbola in (:person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>`, Introduction to the Geometry of the Triangle, 2002; 12.4 The Soddy hyperbolas, p. 143). Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the polar of :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` with respect to :g:`H_{a} <H_a>`. Define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`. Triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_278 <X_278>`. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)
