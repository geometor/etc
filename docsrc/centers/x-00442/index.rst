X(442) = COMPLEMENT OF SCHIFFLER POINT
======================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`I_{A} <I_A>`, :g:`I_{B} <I_B>`, :g:`I_{C} <I_C>` be the excenters, let :g:`A_{B} <A_B>`, :g:`A_{C} <A_C>` be the projections of A onto :g:`I_{A}I_{B} <[ I_A I_B ]>` and :g:`I_{A}I_{C} <[ I_A I_C ]>`, respectively, and define :g:`B_{C} <B_C>`, :g:`B_{A} <B_A>` and :g:`C_{A} <C_A>`, :g:`C_{B} <C_B>` cyclically. The Euler lines of the four triangles :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, :g:`AA_{B}A_{C} << A A_B A_C >>`, :g:`BB_{C}B_{A} << B B_C B_A >>`, :g:`CC_{A}C_{B} << C C_A C_B >>` concur in :ref:`X(442) <X(442)>`. (Jean-Pierre Ehrmann, 11/24/01)
