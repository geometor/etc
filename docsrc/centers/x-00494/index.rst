X(494) = 2nd VAN LAMOEN HOMOTHETIC CENTER
=========================================

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X(494) <X(494)>` is a homothetic center of triangles associated with squares that circumscribe :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. For details and reference, see :ref:`X(493) <X(493)>`. (:person:`Floor van Lamoen <floor-van-lamoen>`, 4/27/98)
