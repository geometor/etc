X_2120–X_2147
=============

.. rubric:: Eigencenters & Eigentransforms

.. enunciation::

   Eigentransforms ET(X) — :term:`eigencenters <eigencenter>` of the :term:`cevian triangles <cevian-triangle>` of classical centers — paired with their :term:`isogonal conjugates <isogonal-conjugate>` on the self-isogonal cubic Z(X) (:ref:`X_2120 <X_2120>`\ –\ :ref:`X_2143 <X_2143>`), followed by :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`-eigentransforms (:ref:`X_2144 <X_2144>`\ –\ :ref:`X_2147 <X_2147>`).

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Eigencenters I, 2120 -2143
   :class: note

   The eigencenter of a triangle is defined in the Glossary and in :cite:`TCCT <tcct>`, p. 192. In case the triangle is the cevian triangle of a point :g:`U <U>` =\ :math:`u : v : w`, the eigencenter is given by

   :math:`f(u,v,w) : f(v,w,u) : f(w,u,v)`, where :math:`f(u,v,w) = vw(u^{2}v^{2} + u^{2}w^{2} - v^{2}w^{2})`.

   This point is now named the **eigentransform** of :g:`U <U>`, denoted by **ET(U)**. Pairs (i,j) such that X_j = ET(X_i) include

   (1,1), (2,3), (3,1075), (4,155), (6,194), (7,218), (13,62), (14,61), (63,1712), (88,1), (92,47), (94,49), (99,39), (100,1), (110,5), (162,1), (174,266), (190,1), (648,185), (651,1), (653,1), (655,1), (658,1), (660,1), (662,1), (664,2082), (673,1), (694,384), (771,1), (799,1), (811,2083), (823,1), (897,1), (1113,3), (1114,3), (1156,1), (1492,1), (1821,1), (1942,1941)

   For any point P not on a sideline of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let :math:`P ^{-1}` denote the isogonal conjugate of P. Easily verified properties of eigentransforms include the following.

   **1.** ET(U) = U-:term:`Ceva conjugate <ceva-conjugate>` of :math:`U ^{-1}`.

   **2.** ET(U) = :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` if and only if :g:`U <U>` = :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` or U lies on the Steiner circumellipse: yz + zx + xy = 0.

   **3.** ET(U) = eigencenter of the :term:`anticevian triangle <anticevian-triangle>` of :math:`U ^{-1}`.

   **4.** ET(U) is on the self-isogonal cubic Z(U). That is, if :math:`x : y : z` denotes a trilinear variable point, then ET(U) is on the cubic

   :math:`ux(y^{2}` - :math:`z^{2})` + :math:`vy(z^{2}` - :math:`x^{2})` + :math:`wz(x^{2}` - :math:`y^{2})` = 0.

   **5.** The U-Ceva conjugate of every X on Z(U) is on Z(U); thus, the U-Ceva conjugate, V, of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, lies on Z(U); trilinears for :math:`V ^{-1}` are

   1/(1/v + 1/w - 1/u) : 1/(1/w + 1/u - 1/v) : 1/(1/u + 1/v - 1/w);

   ET(U) is the third point of intersection of the line :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`-to-:math:`V ^{-1}` and the cubic Z(U).

   **6.** As U is the pivot of Z(U), the following points on Z(U) are collinear: U, ET(U), [ET(U)] :sup:`-1` .

   For discussions and generalizations using barycentric coordinates, see Section 1.4 of "Special Isocubics in the Triangle Plane," downloadable from :person:`Bernard Gibert <bernard-gibert>`'s Cubics in the Triangle Plane. The cubic Z(U) is there denoted by pK(X6,U), and ET(U) is = (:term:`cevian <cevian>` quotient of :g:`U <U>` and :math:`U^{*})` = (U-Ceva conjugate of :math:`U ^{-1})`; ET(U) is the tangential of :math:`U^{*}` in pK(X6,U).
