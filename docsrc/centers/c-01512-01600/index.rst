.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Orthojoins: X_1512-X_1568
   :class: note

   The orthojoin of a point :g:`X <X>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` other than :ref:`X_6 <X_6>` is defined in the Glossary algebraically in terms of variables a,b,c. When these are sidelengths of a triangle, orthojoin(X) is the orthopole of the trilinear polar of the isogonal conjugate of :g:`X <X>`. (Added to ETC 6/18/03.) Let

   | D(a,b,c) = bc[2abcx + :math:`c^{3}`\ y + :math:`b^{3}`\ z - bc(by + cz) - :math:`a^{2}(cy + bz)`],
   | E(a,b,c) = [(:math:`a^{4}` - (:math:`b^{2}` - :math:`c^{2})`\ :sup:`2`]x - :math:`4a^{2}`\ bc(y cos B + z cos C),
   | :math:`f(a,b,c) = D(a`,b,c)E(a,b,c).

   Then orthojoin(X) =\ :math:`f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)`. Below, orthojoin(X) is written as H(X).

   | Suppose X is not :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` and does not lie on a sideline of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The :term:`crossdifference <crossdifference>` of :g:`X <X>` and :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` has first trilinear
   | a(b-c)x', where x' = (by - cz)/(b-c).

   | Let :math:`X ^{-1}` denote the isogonal conjugate of X. Then
   | :math:`H(X ^{-1})` = (by - cz) cos A : (cz - ax) cos B : (ax - by) cos C.

   In other words, if X lies on a line PG through the centroid G, then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` lies on the line HQ, where H denotes the orthocenter and Q is a point that can be determined from the above formula. Examples:

   | If X is on the Euler line, L(2,3), then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on the line L(4,6);
   | If X is on L(2,6,), then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on the Euler line;
   | If X is on L(1,2), then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on L(4,9);
   | If X is on L(2,7), then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on L(1,4).

   Suppose P is not :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, and let

   S = crossdifference(P, :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`) (S lies on the line at infinity) :g:`S' <S'>` = :term:`orthopoint <orthopoint>`(S) (:g:`S' <S'>` lies on the line at infinity) :g:`S″ <S″>` = :term:`complementary conjugate <complementary-conjugate>` of :g:`S' <S'>` (:g:`S″ <S″>` lies on the nine-point circle)

   Let :g:`X <X>` be a point on line P\ :ref:`X_6 <X_6>` and not on a sideline of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on line :g:`S″S' <[ S″ S' ]>`. Examples:

   | If X is a center on L(1,6) and X is not :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on L(119,517).
   | If X is a center on L(2,6) and X is not :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on L(114,511).
   | If X is a center on L(3,6) and X is not :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on L(113,30).
   | If X is a center on L(6,31) and X is not :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on L(118,516).
   | If X is a center on L(6,44) and X is not :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, then :math:`H(X ^{-1})` is on L(117,515).

   Further,
