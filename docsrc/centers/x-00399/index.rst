X(399) = PARRY REFLECTION POINT
===============================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`L <L>`, M, N be lines through :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`, respectively, parallel to the Euler line. Let :g:`L' <L'>` be the reflection of :g:`L <L>` in sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, let :g:`M' <M'>` be the reflection of M in sideline :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, and let :g:`N' <N'>` be the reflection of :g:`N <N>` in sideline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`. The lines :g:`L' <L'>`, :g:`M' <M'>`, :g:`N' <N'>` concur in :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`, as proved in

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   **Cyril Parry,** Problem 10637, *American Mathematical Monthly* 105 (1998) 68.

.. container:: note-item

   In **Cosmin Pohoata,** "On the Parry reflection point," *:cite:`Forum Geometricorum <forum-geometricorum>`* 8 (2008), 43-48 `here <http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2008volume8/FG200806.pdf>`__, the following is proved:

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the reflection of vertex :g:`A <A>` in line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Let :g:`A_{t}B_{t}C_{t} << A_t B_t C_t >>` be the :term:`tangential triangle <tangential-triangle>` of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The circumcircles of the triangles :g:`A_{t}B'C' << A_t B' C' >>`, :g:`A'B_{t}C' << A' B_t C' >>`, :g:`A'B'C_{t} << A' B' C_t >>` concur in :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`. Moreover, the circumcircles of triangles :g:`A'B_{t}C_{t} << A' B_t C_t >>`, :g:`A_{t}B'C_{t} << A_t B' C_t >>`, :g:`A_{t}B_{t}C' << A_t B_t C' >>` concur in a point :g:`Q <Q>` = X8157), here named the **Parry-Pohoata point**. Barycentric coordinates for :g:`Q <Q>`, of degree 22 in a,b,c, were found by J. F. Garcia Captitán (:cite:`Hyacinthos #15827 <hyacinthos>`, Nov. 19, 2007) and are included in Pohoata's article. Pohoata notes that the point Q lies on the circumcircle of the points :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>`, :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>`, :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`I <I>`, :g:`I_{A} <I_A>`, :g:`I_{B} <I_B>`, :g:`I_{C} <I_C>`, denote the incenter and excenters of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Lawrence Evans (:cite:`Hyacinthos #6878 <hyacinthos>`) found that the circumcircles of the triangles :g:`IA'I_{A} << I A' I_A >>`, :g:`IB'I_{B} << I B' I_B >>`, :g:`IC'_{C} <[ I C'_C ]>` concur in :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`.

.. container:: note-item

   The Pohoata article includes a proof that the circumcircles of the triangles :g:`A'I_{B}I_{C} << A' I_B I_C >>`, :g:`I_{A}B'I_{C} << I_A B' I_C >>`, :g:`I_{A}I_{B}C' << I_A I_B C' >>` also pass through :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`. Similar results involving the Fermat points, :ref:`X(13) <X(13)>` and :ref:`X(14) <X(14)>`, are proved.
