X(399) = PARRY REFLECTION POINT
===============================

.. enunciation::

   Let L, M, N be lines through A, B, C, respectively, parallel to the Euler line. Let L' be the reflection of L in sideline BC, let M' be the reflection of M in sideline CA, and let N' be the reflection of N in sideline AB. The lines L', M', N' concur in :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`, as proved in

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   **Cyril Parry,** Problem 10637, *American Mathematical Monthly* 105 (1998) 68.

.. container:: note-item

   In **Cosmin Pohoata,** "On the Parry reflection point," *:cite:`Forum Geometricorum <forum-geometricorum>`* 8 (2008), 43-48 `here <http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2008volume8/FG200806.pdf>`__, the following is proved:

   Let A' be the reflection of vertex A in line BC, and define B', C' cyclically. Let A\ :sub:`t`\ B\ :sub:`t`\ C\ :sub:`t` be the :term:`tangential triangle <tangential-triangle>` of ABC. The circumcircles of the triangles A\ :sub:`t`\ B'C', A'B\ :sub:`t`\ C', A'B'C\ :sub:`t` concur in :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`. Moreover, the circumcircles of triangles A'B\ :sub:`t`\ C\ :sub:`t`, A\ :sub:`t`\ B'C\ :sub:`t`, A\ :sub:`t`\ B\ :sub:`t`\ C' concur in a point Q = X8157), here named the **Parry-Pohoata point**. Barycentric coordinates for Q, of degree 22 in a,b,c, were found by J. F. Garcia Captitán (:cite:`Hyacinthos #15827 <hyacinthos>`, Nov. 19, 2007) and are included in Pohoata's article. Pohoata notes that the point Q lies on the circumcircle of the points :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>`, :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>`, :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Let I, I\ :sub:`A`, I\ :sub:`B`, I\ :sub:`C`, denote the incenter and excenters of ABC. Lawrence Evans (:cite:`Hyacinthos #6878 <hyacinthos>`) found that the circumcircles of the triangles IA'I\ :sub:`A`, IB'I\ :sub:`B`, IC':sub:`C` concur in :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`.

.. container:: note-item

   The Pohoata article includes a proof that the circumcircles of the triangles A'I\ :sub:`B`\ I\ :sub:`C`, I\ :sub:`A`\ B'I\ :sub:`C`, I\ :sub:`A`\ I\ :sub:`B`\ C' also pass through :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`. Similar results involving the Fermat points, :ref:`X(13) <X(13)>` and :ref:`X(14) <X(14)>`, are proved.

.. container:: note-item

   Pohoata reports that the following points are concyclic: :ref:`X(13) <X(13)>`, :ref:`X(16) <X(16)>`, :ref:`X(110) <X(110)>`, :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`, X(1338), as are the points :ref:`X(14) <X(14)>`, :ref:`X(15) <X(15)>`, :ref:`X(110) <X(110)>`, :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`, X(1337). :person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>` adds (Aug. 13, 2012) that the first of these circles also passes through X(2381), and the second, through X(2380).

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>` is the point P on the line :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>`\ \ :ref:`X(74) <X(74)>` for which the P-Brocard triangle is perspective to ABC. (Randy Hutson, August 26, 2014)

.. container:: note-item

   Let AaBaCa, AbBbCb, AcBcCc be the A-, B- and C-anti-altimedial triangles. Let La be the line through Aa parallel to the Euler line of AaBaCa. Define Lb and Lc cyclically. The lines La, Lb, Lc concur in :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>`. (Randy Hutson, November 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   Let O\ :sub:`A` be the circle centered at the A-vertex of the Walsmith triangle and passing through A; define O\ :sub:`B` and O\ :sub:`C` cyclically. :ref:`X(399) <X(399)>` is the radical center of O\ :sub:`A`, O\ :sub:`B`, O\ :sub:`C`. (Randy Hutson, August 30, 2020)
