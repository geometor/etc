X_849
=====

.. rubric:: 4th HATZIPOLAKIS-YIU POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`D <D>` denote the circumcircle of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`D_{A} <D_A>` be the circle tangent to sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` and tangent to :g:`D <D>` at A. Let :g:`B_{A} <B_A>` = :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`D_{A} <D_A>` and :g:`C_{A} <C_A>` = :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`D_{A} <D_A>`, and define :g:`C_{B} <C_B>`, :g:`C_{B} <C_B>` and :g:`A_{C} <A_C>`, :g:`A_{B} <A_B>` cyclically. Define :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`C_{B}A_{B} <[ C_B A_B ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`A_{C}B_{C} <[ A_C B_C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and :ref:`X_849 <X_849>` is the center of homothety. See A. Hatzipolakis and P. Yiu, :cite:`Hyacinthos #2056 <hyacinthos>`-2070, December, 2000.
