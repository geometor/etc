X(690) = CROSSDIFFERENCE OF LINE X(6) AND X(110) — Code
=======================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let NaNbNc and Na'Nb'Nc' be the outer and inner Napoleon triangles, respectively. Let A' be the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of Na wrt Na'Nb'Nc', and define B' and C' cyclically. Let A″ be the isogonal conjugate of Na' wrt NaNbNc, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines A'A″, B'B″, C'C″ concur in :ref:`X(690) <X(690)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 30, 2015)
