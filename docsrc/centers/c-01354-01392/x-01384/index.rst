.. rubric:: 2nd GRINBERG HOMOTHETIC CENTER

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`G_{A} <G_A>` be the circumcenter of triangle :g:`BCG << B C G >>`, where G - centroid(:g:`ABC << A B C >>`). Define :g:`G_{B} <G_B>` and :g:`G_{C} <G_C>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`G_{A}G_{B}G_{C} << G_A G_B G_C >>` is homothetic to the :term:`pedal triangle <pedal-triangle>` of :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, and :ref:`X_1384 <X_1384>` is the center of the homothety.
