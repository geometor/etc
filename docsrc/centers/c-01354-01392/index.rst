.. _preamble-0019:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Brisse Transforms 1354-1367
   :class: note

   Suppose that P is a point on the circumcircle Γ of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`U <U>` and :g:`V <V>` be the lines through :g:`P <P>` tangent to the incircle. Line U meets Γ in a point :g:`U' <U'>` other than P, and line V meets Γ in a point :g:`V' <V'>` other than P. The line :g:`U'V' <[ U' V' ]>` is tangent to the incircle. The touchpoint, denoted by T(P), is the Brisse transform of P. Suppose P is given by barycentrics :math:`u : v : w`. **Barycentrics for T(P)** are found in

   Edward Brisse, `Perspective Poristic Triangles <http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2001volume1/FG200103.pdf>`__: :math:`a^{4}`/[(b + c - a)\ :math:`u^{2}`] : :math:`b^{4}`/[(c + a - b)\ :math:`v^{2}`] : :math:`c^{4}`/[(a + b - c)\ :math:`w^{2}`].

   If X is given by trilinears :math:`x : y : z`, then T(X) has trilinears a/[(b + c - a)\ :math:`x^{2}`] : b/[(c + a - b)\ :math:`y^{2}`] : c/[(a + b - c)\ :math:`z^{2}`].

   | Examples: :ref:`X_11 <X_11>` = Feuerbach point = T(:ref:`X_109 <X_109>`)
   | :ref:`X_1317 <X_1317>` = incircle-antipode of :ref:`X_11 <X_11>` = T(:ref:`X_106 <X_106>`)

   **Still open** is the question posed in :cite:`Hyacinthos #6832 <hyacinthos>`: to list *all* polynomial centers on the incircle having low degree and to prove that there are no others. Here, "degree" of :g:`X <X>` =\ :math:`p(a,b,c) : p(b,c,a) : p(c,a,b)` [barycentrics] refers to the degree of homogeneity of p(a,b,c), and "low" means less than 6. (The Feuerbach point, :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`, has degree 3.)

   In :cite:`Hyacinthos #6835 <hyacinthos>`, :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>` gives two methods for constructing polynomial centers on the incircle:

   (1) If X is a polynomial center on the incircle and W is any other polynomial center, then the line XW meets the incircle in another point that is a polynomial center.

   (2) If W is on the line at infinity, then the barycentric square :math:`W^{2}` is on the Steiner inscribed ellipse, and the barycentric product :ref:`X_7 <X_7>`\ \*\ :math:`W^{2}` is on the incircle.
