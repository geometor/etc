Notes

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the circumcevian triangle of :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`, and let P(A) be the line through :g:`A' <A'>` parallel to line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`. Define P(B) and P(C) cyclically. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` = P(B)∩P(C), and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and :ref:`X_1383 <X_1383>` is the center of the homothety.

.. container:: note-item

   The center of the homothety is also :ref:`X_1383 <X_1383>` if "circumcevian triangle of :ref:`X_6 <X_6>` (circumsymmedial triangle)" or "4th Brocard triangle" is substituted for "circumcevian triangle of :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`". (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, March 21, 2019)
