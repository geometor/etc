Notes

.. container:: note-item

   The centroid of four points :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`,\ :g:`P <P>` is the complement of the complement of :g:`P <P>` with respect to triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. As an example, :ref:`X_1125 <X_1125>` is the centroid of {:g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`,\ :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`}. (:person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>`, 12/28/02)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` the midpoint of segment :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` and let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the midpoint of segment :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`. Define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_1125 <X_1125>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`I <X_1>` be the incenter of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and :g:`A' <A'>` the centroid of triangle :g:`BCI << B C X_1 >>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_1125 <X_1125>`.

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_1125 <X_1125>` is the center of the ellipse which is the locus of centers of the conics passing through :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`, and :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`. This ellipse is also the locus of crosssums of the intersections of the circumcircle and lines through :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`. Furthmore, this ellipse is the bicevian conic of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` and :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` (i.e. the conic which passes through the vertices of the incentral and medial triangles). The ellipse passes through :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`, :ref:`X_214 <X_214>`, :ref:`X_244 <X_244>`, :ref:`X_1015 <X_1015>` and the midpoints of the sides of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 8/13/2011, :cite:`Hyacinthos #20179 <hyacinthos>`; see also #20181, by Chris van Tienhoven.)
