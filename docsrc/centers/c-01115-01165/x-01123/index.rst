X_1123
======

.. rubric:: PAASCHE POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`D <D>` and E be the congruent circles each tangent to the other and to line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, with D also tangent to line :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` and E also tangent to line :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, meeting in a point :g:`A' <A'>` lying outside triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_1123 <X_1123>`. See

Notes

.. container:: note-item

   **Ivan Paasche,** Aufgabe P 933, *Praxis der Mathematik* 1 (1990), page 40.

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`P_{A} <P_A>` be the parabola with focus A and directrix :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the line of the points of intersection of :g:`P_{A} <P_A>` with the segments :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` and :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`. Define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1123 <X_1123>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 9/23/2011)

.. container:: note-item

   Suppose that :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is an acute triangle. Let :math:`o_{A}` be the circle with diameter :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`. Let :math:`w_{A}` be the circle tangent to segments :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` and :g:`AC <[ A C ]>` and also externally tangent to :math:`o_{A}`, in point :g:`X_{A} <X_A>`. Define :g:`X_{B} <X_B>` and :g:`X_{C} <X_C>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AX_{A} <[ A X_A ]>`, :g:`BX_{B} <[ B X_B ]>`, :g:`CX_{C} <[ C X_C ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1123 <X_1123>`. (Tomasz Cieśla, 19 January 2013) For a related construction, see :ref:`X_1336 <X_1336>`.

.. container:: note-item

   In Hutson's construction of :ref:`X_1123 <X_1123>` given above, the parabola :g:`P_{A} <P_A>` meets segments :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` and :g:`AC <[ A C ]>` in two points, here denoted by :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` and :g:`A_{c} <A_c>`. Cyclically, the parabolas :g:`P_{B} <P_B>` and :g:`P_{C} <P_C>` determine four more points; the six points are then :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` =\ :math:`0 : 2R : c`, :g:`A_{c} <A_c>` =\ :math:`0 : b : 2R`, :g:`B_{c} <B_c>` =\ :math:`a : 0 : 2R`, :g:`B_{a} <B_a>` =\ :math:`2R : 0 : c`, :g:`C_{a} <C_a>` =\ :math:`2R : b : 0`, :g:`C_{b} <C_b>` =\ :math:`a : 2R : 0`. These points lie on a conic, named the Paasche conic at X_37861. (:person:`Vijay Krishna <dasari-naga-vijay-krishna>`, April 14, 2020)
