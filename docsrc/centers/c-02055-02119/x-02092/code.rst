X_2092
======

.. rubric:: DANNEELS-APOLLONIUS PERSPECTOR

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the point common to Apollonius circle and the :g:`A <A>`-excircle, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_2092 <X_2092>`. (Eric Danneels, :cite:`Hyacinthos <hyacinthos>`, #8070, 10/01/03). (Triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is also perspective to the cevian triangle of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`; see :ref:`X_1682 <X_1682>`.)
