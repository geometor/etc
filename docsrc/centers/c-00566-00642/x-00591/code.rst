X_591
=====

.. rubric:: 1st VAN LAMOEN PERPENDICULAR BISECTORS POINT

.. enunciation::

   Erect squares outwardly on the sides of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Two edges emanate from :g:`A <A>`; let :g:`P <P>` and :g:`Q <Q>` be their endpoints. Let a' be the perpendicular bisector of :g:`PQ <[ P Q ]>`, and define b' and c' cyclically. Then a', b', c' concur in :ref:`X_591 <X_591>`. See also :ref:`X_1991 <X_1991>`. (:person:`Floor van Lamoen <floor-van-lamoen>`, 1/4/2001, :cite:`Hyacinthos #2123 <hyacinthos>`)
