.. container:: note-item

   A simple construction of :ref:`X(37) <X(37)>` as a :term:`crosspoint <crosspoint>` can be generalized as follows: let DEF be the :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>` of ABC and let A'B'C' be the cevian triangle of a point U other than the centroid, :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>`. The crosspoint of :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>` and U is then the point of concurrence of lines LD,ME,NF, where L,M,N are the respective midpoints of AA', BB', CC'. If U=u : v : w (trilinears), then crosspoint(:ref:`X(2) <X(2)>`,U) = b/w+c/v : c/u+a/w : a/v+b/u, assuming that uvw is nonzero. In particular, if U=\ :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, then the crosspoint is :ref:`X(37) <X(37)>`. (Seiichi Kirikami, July 10, 2011)

.. container:: note-item

   Let A' be the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of the tangent to the Apollonius circle where it meets the A-excircle, and define B' and C' cyclically. The triangle A'B'C' is homothetic to ABC at :ref:`X(37) <X(37)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

Let La be the radical axis of the circumcircle and Oa. Let A' = Lb∩Lc, B' = Lc∩La, C' = La∩Lb. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in :ref:`X(37) <X(37)>`. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)
