.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Vertex Conjugates
   :class: note

   Suppose that :g:`U <U>` =\ :math:`u : v : w` and :g:`X <X>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` (trilinears) are points not on a sideline of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let

   :math:`f(a,b,c) = a/[a^{2}vwyz - ux(bw + cv)(bz + cy)]`.

   The :g:`U <U>`-vertex conjugate of :g:`X <X>` is the point

   :math:`f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)`.

   For a geometric interpretation, let :g:`T <T>` be the vertex triangle of the circumcevian triangles, :g:`A_{U}B_{U}C_{U} << A_U B_U C_U >>` and :g:`A_{X}B_{X}C_{X} << A_X B_X C_X >>`, of :g:`U <U>` and X; viz., the sidelines of :g:`T <T>` are :g:`A_{U}A_{X} <[ A_U A_X ]>`, :g:`B_{U}B_{X} <[ B_U B_X ]>`, :g:`C_{U}C_{X} <[ C_U C_X ]>`. Then T is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is the :g:`U <U>`-vertex conjugate of :g:`X <X>`.

   The definition of vertex conjugate allows :g:`X <X>` = U. To extend the geometric interpretation to the case that :g:`X <X>` = U, as X approaches U, the vertex triangle approaches a limiting triangle which we call the :term:`tangential triangle <tangential-triangle>` of :g:`U <U>`, a triangle perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` with perspector :g:`U <U>`-vertex conjugate of :g:`U <U>`.

   Among properties of vertex conjugation are these:

   1. :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`-vertex conjugation maps the Darboux cubic to the Darboux cubic. The appearance of (i,j) in the following list means that X_i is on the Darboux cubic and that X_j = :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`-vertex conjugate of X_i:

   (1,84), (3,64), (4,4), (20,3346), (40,3345), (1490,3347), (1498,3348), (2131,3183), (3182,3354)

   2. The fixed point of :g:`U <U>`-vertex conjugation is the 1st Saragossa point of :g:`U <U>`. (Saragossa points are defined just before :ref:`X_1166 <X_1166>`.) The appearance of (i,j) in the following list means that the 1st Saragossa point of X_i is X_j:

   (1,58), (2,251), (3,4), (4,54), (5,1166), (6,6), (7,3449), (8,3450), (9,3451), (21,961), (55,57)

   If :g:`U' <U'>` and :g:`X' <X'>` are the :term:`isogonal conjugates <isogonal-conjugate>` of :g:`U <U>` and X, resp., then the isogonal conjugate of the :g:`U <U>`-vertex conjugate of :g:`X <X>` is the :term:`anticomplement <anticomplement>` of the midpoint of :g:`U' <U'>` and :g:`X' <X'>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 11, 2019)

   The :g:`U <U>`-vertex conjugate of :g:`U <U>` is the isogonal conjugate of the anticomplement of the isogonal conjugate of :g:`U <U>`. (Randy Hutson, January 17, 2020)
