X_1995
======

.. rubric:: ORTHOCORRESPONDENT OF X_6

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`P <P>` be a point in the plane of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and not on the circumcircle. Let (:g:`O_{A} <O_A>`) be the circle tangent to the circumcircle at :g:`A <A>` and passing through P. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the antipode of :g:`A <A>` in (:g:`O_{A} <O_A>`). Let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the tangent to (:g:`O_{A} <O_A>`) at :g:`A' <A'>`. Define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`T_{A} <T_A>` = :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`, and define :g:`T_{B} <T_B>` and :g:`T_{C} <T_C>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`T_{A}T_{B}T_{C} << T_A T_B T_C >>` is homothetic to the :term:`tangential triangle <tangential-triangle>`. When :g:`P <P>` = :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, the center of homothety is :ref:`X_1995 <X_1995>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, June 7, 2019)
