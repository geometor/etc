.. rubric:: (DE LONGCHAMPS LINE)∩(LEMOINE AXIS)

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`L <L>` denote the line at infinity. Then (Lemoine axis) = (:term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of :term:`isotomic conjugate <isotomic-conjugate>` of :g:`L <L>`) and (de Longchamps line) = (isotomic conjugate of isogonal conjugate of :g:`L <L>`), and :ref:`X_3005 <X_3005>` = (Lemoine axis)∩(de Longchamps line). (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, February 10, 2016)
