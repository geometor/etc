X(476) = TIXIER POINT — Code
============================

.. enunciation::

   The reflection of :ref:`X(110) <X(110)>` in the Euler line; :ref:`X(476) <X(476)>` is on the circumcircle. (Michel Tixier, 5/9/98). Also, :ref:`X(476) <X(476)>` is the center of the polar conic of :ref:`X(30) <X(30)>` with respect to the Neuberg cubic; this conic is a rectangular hyperbola passing through the incenter, the excenters, and :ref:`X(30) <X(30)>`. (:person:`Peter Yff <peter-yff>`, 5/23/99)
