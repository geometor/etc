.. container:: note-item

   The term ":g:`AC <[ A C ]>`-incircle" is introduced for "anticomplement of the incircle" by :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>` (Dec. 2, 2004). Thus, the :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`-incircle is the incircle of the :term:`anticomplementary triangle <anticomplementary-triangle>`; the circle has center :ref:`X_8 <X_8>` and radius 2r. The exsimilicenter of the circumcircle and :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`-incircle is :ref:`X_100 <X_100>`, their touchpoint, and the anticomplement of :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`.
