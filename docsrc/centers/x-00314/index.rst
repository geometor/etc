X(314) = ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF X(65)
====================================

.. enunciation::

   Let D and E be the intersections of line :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`\ \ :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>` with lines PU(3) and PU(6), respectively. Let D' and E' be the :term:`isogonal conjugates <isogonal-conjugate>` of D and E, respectively. Let D" and E" be the :term:`isotomic conjugates <isotomic-conjugate>` of D and E, respectively. Then :ref:`X(314) <X(314)>` = D'D" ∩E'E". (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 26, 2015)
