.. container:: note-item

.. container:: note-item

Triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to the :term:`symmedial triangle <symmedial-triangle>` at :ref:`X_1691 <X_1691>`. (Randy Hutson, July 11, 2019)
