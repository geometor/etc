.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the pole of the Gergonne line wrt the circle with :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` as diameter, and define :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. :ref:`X_1699 <X_1699>` is the centroid of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, June 27, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the orthocenter of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is also the anticevian triangle, wrt intouch triangle, of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, and :ref:`X_1699 <X_1699>` is the centroid of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`; see also :ref:`X_3680 <X_3680>`. (Randy Hutson, June 27, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_1699 <X_1699>` is the centroid of triangle formed by reflecting vertices of 1st circumperp triangle in corresponding side of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. (If 2nd circumperp triangle is substituted, for 1st, the resulting triangle is the :term:`Fuhrmann triangle <fuhrmann-triangle>`.) (Randy Hutson, June 27, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`H_aH_bH_c << H_a H_b H_c >>` be the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the :g:`A <A>`-excenter of :g:`AH_bH_c << A H_b H_c >>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The centroid of triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is :ref:`X_1699 <X_1699>`. (Randy Hutson, July 31 2018)
