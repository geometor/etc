.. admonition:: Group Preamble — (Mostly) Circle-related Points, X_2446 - X_2573
   :class: note

   Centers :ref:`X_2446 <X_2446>` to :ref:`X_2573 <X_2573>` were contributed by Peter J. C. Moses during October to November, 2003. See the notes just before :ref:`X_1662 <X_1662>` for an introduction and notation.

   Included in this section are centers of similitude of several pairs of circles. However, for some pairs of central circles, the two centers of similitudes are not :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>` but are instead a pair of :term:`bicentric points <bicentric-points>`. Specifically, Moses found that the insimilicenter (i.e., internal center of similitude) and exsimilicenter of the following pairs are bicentric points; as such they are listed in the Bicentric Pairs section (accessed by the **Tables** button atop this page):

   | {Parry circle and 2nd Lemoine circle}; see P(62) in Bicentric Pairs
   | {Parry circle and circumcircle}; P(63)
   | {Parry circle and incircle}; P(64)
   | {Parry circle and nine-point circle}; P(65)
   | {Parry circle and Spieker circle}; P(66)
   | {Parry circle and Moses circle}; P(67)

   The 2nd Brocard circle, defined in the aforementioned Bicentric Pairs, is mentioned several times [from :ref:`X_2554 <X_2554>` to :ref:`X_2573 <X_2573>`]. A summary of its properties follows.

   **Definition:** The *2nd Brocard circle* is the circle having center :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` and radius eR, where e = (1 - 4 :math:`\sin^{2}`\ ω)\ :sup:`1/2` and R = circumradius. The 2nd Brocard circle and the Brocard circle meet in the 1st and 2nd Brocard points. Peter J. C. Moses found that the 2nd Brocard circle also passes through the points X_i for I = 1670, 1671, 2554, 2555, 2556, 2557. The square of the radius of the 2nd Brocard circle can be written out as

   :math:`a^{2}`\ :math:`b^{2}`\ :math:`c^{2}(a^{4} + b^{4} + c ^{4} - b^{2}c^{2} - c^{2}a^{2} - a^{2}b^{2})`/[4(:math:`b^{2}`\ :math:`c^{2}` + :math:`c^{2}`\ :math:`a^{2}` + :math:`a^{2}`\ :math:`b^{2})`\ (:math:`a^{2}` + :math:`b^{2}` + :math:`c^{2})`]

   X_i is the inverse-in-the-2nd-Brocard-circle of X_j for these (i,j): (6,39), (76,99), (1666,2563), (1667,2562), (1689,1690), (2026,2561), (2027,2560)

   The power of the 2nd Brocard circle with respect to each of the vertices :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>` is :math:`a^{2}`\ :math:`b^{2}`\ :math:`c^{2}`/(:math:`b^{2}`\ :math:`c^{2}` + :math:`c^{2}`\ :math:`a^{2}` + :math:`a^{2}`\ :math:`b^{2})`
