.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the orthic axis of triangle :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`, and define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`, :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{A} <L_A>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{A} <L_A>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`. Triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is here named the **orthic axes triangle**. :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is also the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :ref:`X_4 <X_4>` wrt the orthic triangle. :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`, and homothetic to the polar triangle of the nine-point circle at :ref:`X_427 <X_427>`. The orthic axes of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, the orthic triangle, and the orthic axes triangle concur in :ref:`X_2501 <X_2501>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, August 19, 2019)
