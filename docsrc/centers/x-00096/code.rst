X(96) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(52) — Code
==========================================

.. enunciation::

   Let A'B'C' be the reflection triangle. Let B\ :sub:`A` and C\ :sub:`A` be the orthogonal projections of B' and C' on line BC, resp. Let (O\ :sub:`A`) be the circle with segment B\ :sub:`A`\ C\ :sub:`A` as diameter. Define (O\ :sub:`B`) and (O\ :sub:`C`) cyclically. :ref:`X(96) <X(96)>` is the radical center of circles (O\ :sub:`A`), (O\ :sub:`B`), (O\ :sub:`C`). (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, June 7, 2019)
