X(174) = YFF CENTER OF CONGRUENCE
=================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` be the ellipse with :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>` as foci and passing through :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, and define :g:`E_{b} <E_b>` and :g:`E_{c} <E_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the line tangent to :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` at :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`. Then :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` are collinear, and :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>` = :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. The line :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` meets the line at infinity at the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of X(3659). Alternately, let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the trilinear pole of line :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>` and :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur at :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`; see also :ref:`X(188) <X(188)>`. The points :g:`A″ <A″>`, :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` lie on the circumconic centered at :ref:`X(9) <X(9)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the excentral triangle. :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>` is the trilinear pole of the Monge line of the incircles of :g:`BCA' << B C A' >>`, :g:`CAB' << C A B' >>`, :g:`ABC' << A B C' >>`. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   In notes dated 1987, Yff raises this question concerning certain triangles lying within :g:`ABC << A B C >>`: can :term:`isoscelizers <isoscelizer>` (defined at :ref:`X(173) <X(173)>`), :g:`P_{B}Q_{C} <[ P_B Q_C ]>`, :g:`P_{C}Q_{A} <[ P_C Q_A ]>`, :g:`P_{A}Q_{B} <[ P_A Q_B ]>`, be constructed so that, on putting

.. container:: note-item

   :math:`R_{A}` = :g:`P_{A}Q_{B} <[ P_A Q_B ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`P_{B}Q_{C} <[ P_B Q_C ]>`, :math:`R_{B}` = :g:`P_{B}Q_{C} <[ P_B Q_C ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`P_{C}Q_{A} <[ P_C Q_A ]>`, :math:`R_{C}` = :g:`P_{C}Q_{A} <[ P_C Q_A ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`P_{A}Q_{B} <[ P_A Q_B ]>`,

   the following four triangles are congruent:

   :g:`P_{A} <P_A>`\ :g:`Q_{A} <Q_A>`\ :math:`R_{A}`, :g:`P_{B} <P_B>`\ :g:`Q_{B} <Q_B>`\ :math:`R_{B}`, :g:`P_{C} <P_C>`\ :g:`Q_{C} <Q_C>`\ :math:`R_{C}`, :math:`R_{A}`\ :math:`R_{B}`\ :math:`R_{C}` ?

.. container:: note-item

   After proving that the answer is yes, Yff moves the three isoscelizers in such a way that the three outer triangles, stay congruent and the inner triangle (called the Yff central triangle), :math:`R_{A}`\ :math:`R_{B}`\ :math:`R_{C}`, shrinks to :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`D <D>` be the point on side :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` such that (angle BID) = (angle DIC), and likewise for point :g:`E <E>` on side :g:`CA <[ C A ]>` and point :g:`F <F>` on side :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`. The lines :g:`AD <[ A D ]>`, :g:`BE <[ B E ]>`, :g:`CF <[ C F ]>` concur in :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`. (Seiichi Kirikami, Jan. 29, 2010)

.. container:: note-item

   Generalization: if I is replaced by an arbitrary point :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`p : q : r` (trilinears), then the lines :g:`AD <[ A D ]>`, :g:`BE <[ B E ]>`, :g:`CF <[ C F ]>` concur in the point K(P) =\ :math:`f(p,q,r,A) : f(q,r,p,B) : f(r,p,q,C)`, where :math:`f(p,q,r,A) = (q^{2} + r^{2} + 2qr \cos A)^{-1/2}`. Moreover, if :g:`P* <P*>` is the inverse of :g:`P <P>` in the circumcircle, then K(:g:`P* <P*>`) = K(P). (:person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, Feb. 1, 2010, based on Seiichi Kirikami's construction of :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`)
