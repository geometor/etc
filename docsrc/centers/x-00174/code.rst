X(174) = YFF CENTER OF CONGRUENCE — Code
========================================

.. enunciation::

   Let Ea be the ellipse with B and C as foci and passing through :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, and define Eb and Ec cyclically. Let La be the line tangent to Ea at :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A' = La∩BC, B' = Lb∩CA, C' = Lc∩AB. Then A', B', C' are collinear, and :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>` = :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line A'B'C'. The line A'B'C' meets the line at infinity at the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of X(3659). Alternately, let A″ be the trilinear pole of line La, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″ and CC″ concur at :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`; see also :ref:`X(188) <X(188)>`. The points A″, B″, C″ lie on the circumconic centered at :ref:`X(9) <X(9)>`.
