X(174) = YFF CENTER OF CONGRUENCE — Code
========================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` be the ellipse with :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>` as foci and passing through :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, and define :g:`E_{b} <E_b>` and :g:`E_{c} <E_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the line tangent to :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` at :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`. Then :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` are collinear, and :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>` = :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. The line :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` meets the line at infinity at the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of X(3659). Alternately, let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the trilinear pole of line :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>` and :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur at :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`; see also :ref:`X(188) <X(188)>`. The points :g:`A″ <A″>`, :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` lie on the circumconic centered at :ref:`X(9) <X(9)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)
