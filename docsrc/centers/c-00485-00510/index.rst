X_485–X_510
===========

.. rubric:: Vecten Square Centers & Isoscelizer Centers

.. enunciation::

   Inner and outer Vecten points and square-associated centers (Vierkanten in een driehoek, :ref:`X_485 <X_485>`\ –\ :ref:`X_495 <X_495>`), harmonic conjugates, and :term:`isoscelizer <isoscelizer>`-associated centers (:ref:`X_503 <X_503>`\ –\ :ref:`X_510 <X_510>`).

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers X_485-X_495

   Centers :ref:`X_485 <X_485>`-:ref:`X_495 <X_495>`, and also :ref:`X_371 <X_371>` and :ref:`X_372 <X_372>`, are *Vierkanen in een driehoek*: :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>` associated with squares.

.. _preamble-0011:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers X_503-X_510
   :class: note

   Centers :ref:`X_503 <X_503>`-:ref:`X_510 <X_510>`, and also centers :ref:`X_173 <X_173>`, :ref:`X_174 <X_174>`, :ref:`X_258 <X_258>`, and :ref:`X_351 <X_351>`-:ref:`X_364 <X_364>` are associated with isoscelizers.

   A line :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` perpendicular to the internal bisector line of :g:`A <A>` is an *A-isoscelizer.*. Suppose X is a point not on a sideline of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and let

   L(A,X) = the A-isoscelizer passing through :g:`X <X>`; E(A,X) = L(A,X)∩\ :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`; :math:`F(A,X) =L(A`,X)∩\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`; T(A,X) = the triangle wth vertices :g:`A <A>`, :g:`E <E>`\ (A,X), F(A,X); :math:`H(A,X) =A-altitude of T(A`,X); D(A,X) = distance between E(A,X) and F(A,X); X(A) = distance between E(A,X) and F(A,X);

   Cyclically define L(B,X), E(B,X), . . . , X(B) and L(C,X), E(C,X), . . . , X(C).

   Each center, :ref:`X_503 <X_503>` to :ref:`X_510 <X_510>`, is defined by :person:`Peter Yff <peter-yff>` as the point :g:`X <X>` of concurrence of isoscelers satisfying certain conditions.

   Geometer's Sketchpad sketches for centers :ref:`X_503 <X_503>`-:ref:`X_510 <X_510>` were contributed by :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, May 7, 2005.
