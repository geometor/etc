.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`I_{a} <I_a>` be the reflection of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` in the perpendicular bisector of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`I_{b} <I_b>` and :g:`I_{c} <I_c>` cyclically. Then :ref:`X_2771 <X_2771>` = :ref:`X_30 <X_30>`-of-:g:`I_{a}I_{b}I_{c} << I_a I_b I_c >>`; :g:`I_{a}I_{b}I_{c} << I_a I_b I_c >>` is the inner Garcia triangle. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, February 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let AeBeCe and AiBiCi be the Ae and Ai triangles (aka K798e and K798i triangles). Let Ae' be the Ai-isogonal conjugate of Ae, and define Be' and Ce' cyclically. Let Ai' be the Ae-isogonal conjugate of Ai, and define Bi' and Ci' cyclically. Triangles Ae'Be'Ce' and Ai'Bi'Ci' are inversely congruent, with similitude center :ref:`X_2771 <X_2771>`. Triangle Ai'Bi'Ci' is also the cross-triangle of the Ae and Ai triangles. (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
