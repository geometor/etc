X(531) = DIRECTION OF VECTOR AX + BX + CX, where X = X(14)
==========================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let A' be the nine-point center of BC\ :ref:`X(13) <X(13)>`, and define B' and C' cyclically. A'B'C' is an equilateral triangle concyclic with :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>` and :ref:`X(13) <X(13)>` (inscribed in the 1st Hutson circle). The isogonal (and isotomic) conjugate of :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>` wrt A'B'C' is :ref:`X(531) <X(531)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, February 10, 2016)
