X(393) = X(25)-CROSS CONJUGATE OF X(4)
======================================

.. enunciation::

   Let Ha be the foot of the A-altitude of ABC. Let Pa be the foot of the altitude from Ha to AB, and Qa the foot of the altitude from Ha to CA. Define Hb, Hc, Pb, Pc, Qb, Qc cyclically. Let A' be the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line PaQa, and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in :ref:`X(393) <X(393)>`. Lines PaQa, PbQb, PcQc are the antiparallels in the construction of the Taylor circle. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, January 29, 2018)
