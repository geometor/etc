X_530
=====

.. rubric:: DIRECTION OF VECTOR AX + BX + CX, where X = X_13

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the nine-point center of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ :ref:`X_14 <X_14>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is an equilateral triangle concyclic with :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` and :ref:`X_14 <X_14>` (inscribed in the 2nd Hutson circle). The isogonal (and isotomic) conjugate of :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` wrt :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is :ref:`X_530 <X_530>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, February 10, 2016)
