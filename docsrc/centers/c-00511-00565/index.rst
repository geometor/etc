.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers on the line at infinity: X_511-X_526 (Centers on the lines at infinity: X_511-X_526)
   :class: note

   In barycentric coordinates, the line at infinity, L, is the set of points :g:`X <X>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` satisfying :math:`x + y + z = 0`. The isogonal conjugate of :g:`L <L>`, given by a^2 y z + b^2 z x + c^2 x y = 0, is the circumcircle (e.g., :ref:`X_98 <X_98>`-:ref:`X_112 <X_112>`).

   The collection of collinearities reported below for each of the centers on L comprises a family of parallel lines.

   Barycentrics :math:`x: y : z` (and trilinears) for :ref:`X_30 <X_30>`, :ref:`X_511 <X_511>`-:ref:`X_526 <X_526>`, and many other :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>` on :g:`L <L>`, are polynomials in (a,b,c). For example, barycentrics for :ref:`X_511 <X_511>` are :math:`p(a,b,c) : p(b,c,a) : p(c,a,b)`, where

   p(a,b,c) = :math:`a^{2}(a^{2}b^{2} + a^{2}c^{2} - b^{4} - c^{4})`.

   Such polynomials comprise a family that can be partitioned into two subfamilies: even and odd. Each subfamily can be generated as linear combination of polynomials in certain bases. For example, a basis for the even polynomials of degrees 1 to 4 follows:

   | degree 1: 2a - b - c
   | degree 2: :math:`2a^{2}` - :math:`b^{2}` - :math:`c^{2}`, 2bc - ca - ab
   | degree 3: :math:`2a^{3}` - :math:`b^{3}` - :math:`c^{3}`, :math:`a^{2}`\ b + :math:`a^{2}`\ c - :math:`b^{2}`\ c - :math:`b^{2}`\ c, :math:`a^{2}`\ b + :math:`a^{2}`\ c - :math:`b^{2}`\ c - :math:`b^{2}`\ c
   | degree 4: :math:`2a^{4}` - :math:`b^{4}` - :math:`c^{4}`, :math:`a^{3}`\ b + :math:`a^{3}`\ c - :math:`b^{3}`\ c - :math:`b^{3}`\ c, :math:`a^{3}`\ b + :math:`a^{3}`\ c - :math:`b^{3}`\ c - :math:`b^{3}`\ c, :math:`2b^{2}`\ :math:`c^{2}` - :math:`a^{2}`\ :math:`b^{2}` - :math:`a^{2}`\ :math:`c^{2}`, :math:`2a^{2}`\ bc - :math:`ab^{2}`\ c - :math:`abc^{2}`

   A basis for the odd polynomials of degrees 1 to 2 follows:

   | degree 1: b - c
   | degree 2: (b - c)(a + b + c), (b - c)a

   See `Clark Kimberling, "A combinatorial classification of triangle centers on the line at infinity," Journal of Integer Sequences 22 (2019) Article 19.5.4. <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL22/Kimberling/kimb9.pdf>`__
