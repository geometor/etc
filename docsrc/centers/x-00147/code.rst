X(147) = TARRY POINT OF ANTICOMPLEMENTARY TRIANGLE — Code
=========================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let T be the P-Brocard triangle for any P on the Brocard axis (including the 1st Brocard triangle, for P = :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>`). Let O\ :sub:`A` be the circle centered at the A-vertex of T and passing through A; define O\ :sub:`B` and O\ :sub:`C` cyclically. :ref:`X(147) <X(147)>` is the radical center of O\ :sub:`A`, O\ :sub:`B`, O\ :sub:`C`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, August 30, 2020)
