Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (PONCELET, BRIANCHON & FEUERBACH (1821–1822)):** ``Nine-Point Circle (Center X_5, Radius R/2) • 3 Midpoints + 3 Altitude Feet + 3 Euler Points`` — The Nine-Point Circle: Centered at X_5, the nine-point circle circumscribes both the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c and the orthic triangle. (*Dilation of the circumcircle by −1/2 at X_2 or +1/2 at X_4 produces the exact same nine-point circle.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (FEUERBACH TANGENCY):** ``Midpoint of X_3 and X_4 • Tangency with Incircle at Feuerbach Point X_11`` — Dual Homothety: X_5 is the complement of X_3 and midpoint of X_3 X_4, tangent to the incircle at X_11. (*|X_1 X_5| = |R/2 − r|, proving internal tangency of the incircle and nine-point circle at X_11.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (NINE-POINT NEXUS):** ``Euler Line {2, 3, 4, 5} ∩ Feuerbach Line {1, 5, 11, 12}`` — Intersection at X_5: The Euler line {2, 3, 4, 5} and Feuerbach line {1, 5, 11, 12} cross at X_5. (*X_11 always lies strictly on the nine-point circle.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (ORTHIC & MEDIAL CIRCUMCIRCLE):** ``X_5 • X_2 • X_3 • X_4 • X_1 • X_11 • X_12`` — Nine-Point Family: X_5 unites X_2, X_3, X_4 with X_1, X_11, X_12. (*Because the medial and orthic triangles share the same circumcircle, their circumcenters coincide at X_5.*)
