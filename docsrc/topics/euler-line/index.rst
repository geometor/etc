Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (EULER (1765) • NOVI COMMENTARII):** ``Euler Line L(2, 3, 4, 5, 20, 30) • |X_3 X_2| : |X_2 X_5| : |X_5 X_4| = 2 : 1 : 3`` — The Euler Line: Connects X_2, X_3, X_4, and X_5 across the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c and nine-point circle. (*On an equilateral triangle, X_2, X_3, X_4, and X_5 coincide and the Euler line is indeterminate.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (EULER INFINITY POINT X_30):** ``Trilinear Equation: sin(2A) sin(B−C) α + sin(2B) sin(C−A) β + sin(2C) sin(A−B) γ = 0`` — Shinagawa Coefficients: Every Euler center is a linear combination p·X_4 + q·X_3 with X_2 = (1, 2) and X_5 = (1, 1). (*X_30 is the point at infinity of the Euler line; X_20 (de Longchamps) is the reflection of X_4 in X_3.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (2 : 1 : 3 SEGMENT RATIOS):** ``Collinearity {2, 3, 4, 5, 20}`` — Central Line Spine: The Euler line {2, 3, 4, 5, 20} links the circumcenter, centroid, nine-point center, orthocenter, and de Longchamps point. (*X_2 trisects X_3 X_4, while X_5 bisects X_3 X_4.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (COLLECTION 1 ANCHOR):** ``X_3 • X_2 • X_5 • X_4 • X_20`` — Ordered Euler Sequence: X_2, X_3, X_4, X_5, and X_20 lie in exact rational ratio along the Euler axis. (*Four of the first five centers in Kimberling's Encyclopedia lie on the Euler line.*)
