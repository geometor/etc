Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (APOLLONIUS OF PERGA • LOCUS OF CONSTANT RATIO):** ``Three Apollonian Circles: PB/PC = c/b • PC/PA = a/c • PA/PB = b/a`` — The Apollonian Circles: Intersect on the Brocard axis at the 1st Isodynamic Point X_15 and 2nd Isodynamic Point X_16, orthogonal to the circumcircle. (*Diameter of the A-Apollonian circle is the harmonic segment between interior and exterior bisector feet on BC.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (EQUILATERAL PEDAL TRIANGLES):** ``Coaxial Pencil Centered on the Lemoine Axis • Radical Axis = Brocard Axis`` — Equidistant Pedal Property: At X_15 and X_16, vertex distances satisfy a·|PA| = b·|PB| = c·|PC| and the pedal triangle is equilateral. (*X_15 and X_16 are the unique points in the plane whose pedal triangles on △ABC are equilateral.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (RADICAL CHORD):** ``Brocard Axis {3, 6, 15, 16} & Symmedian Crossings {6, 13, 16} • {6, 14, 15}`` — Apollonian Axes: Radical axis {3, 6, 15, 16} meets Fermat–Isodynamic lines {6, 13, 16} and {6, 14, 15} at X_6. (*All three Apollonian circles intersect orthogonally with the circumcircle of △ABC.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (MOBIUS EQUILATERAL INVARIANCE):** ``X_15 • X_16 • X_3 • X_6 • X_13 • X_14`` — Isodynamic Network: X_15 and X_16 pair isogonally with Fermat points X_13 and X_14 across X_3, X_6. (*A Möbius transformation sending X_15 to 0 and X_16 to ∞ maps △ABC to an equilateral triangle.*)
