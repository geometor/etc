Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (ORTHOCENTRIC SYSTEM {X_1, J_a, J_b, J_c}):** ``Incentral Triangle I_a I_b I_c (Cevian of X_1) • Excentral Triangle J_a J_b J_c (Anticevian of X_1)`` — Bisector Triangles: Interior angle bisectors through X_1 form the incentral feet I_a, I_b, I_c, while the incircle and circumcircle govern the excentral duality. (*X_1 and the three excenters form an orthocentric system: each point is the orthocenter of the other three.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (BEVAN CIRCLE (RADIUS 2R)):** ``△ABC is the Orthic Triangle of the Excentral Triangle • Mittenpunkt X_9 is its Symmedian Point`` — Excentral Shift: X_1 is the orthocenter of the excentral triangle, the circumcircle is its nine-point circle, and X_9 is its symmedian point. (*Since △ABC is the orthic triangle of J_a J_b J_c, the circumcircle of J_a J_b J_c (the Bevan circle) has radius 2R.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (MITTENPUNKT CONCURRENCE):** ``Incenter–Symmedian–Mittenpunkt Line {1, 6, 9} • OI Line {1, 3} • Fuhrmann Axis {4, 9}`` — Excentral Axes: Symmedian–Mittenpunkt line {1, 6, 9}, OI line {1, 3}, and orthocenter–Mittenpunkt axis {4, 9}. (*Lines from the excenters to the side midpoints M_a, M_b, M_c concur at Mittenpunkt X_9.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (EXCENTRAL ↔ ORTHIC ISOMORPHISM):** ``X_1 • X_9 • X_6 • X_3 • X_4`` — Excentral Network: X_1 and X_9 link X_6, X_3, and X_4. (*Every theorem about the orthic triangle of △ABC has a dual theorem about △ABC inside the excentral triangle.*)
