Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (NAPOLEON'S THEOREM):** ``Isogonic Triangles (Perspectors X_13, X_14) • Napoleon Triangles (Perspectors X_17, X_18)`` — Equilateral Constructions: Outer and inner equilateral apexes yield Fermat points X_13, X_14, while their centroids form equilateral triangles with perspectors X_17, X_18 on the circumcircle stage. (*For any triangle ABC, the centers of outer (or inner) equilateral triangles on its sides form an equilateral triangle.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (TORRICELLI CIRCLES):** ``Area(Outer Napoleon) − Area(Inner Napoleon) = Area(△ABC)  •  Shared Centroid X_2`` — Area & Centroid Invariance: Both Napoleon triangles have centroid X_2, while X_13, X_14 and X_17, X_18 correspond to Kiepert angles ±60° and ±30°. (*The circumcircles of the three outer equilateral triangles concur at the 1st Fermat point X_13.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (COLLINEAR EQUILATERAL CENTERS):** ``Kiepert Chords {2, 13, 17} • {4, 13, 18} • {3, 17, 18}`` — Napoleon Central Lines: Centroid axis {2, 13, 17}, orthocenter axis {4, 13, 18}, and circumcenter axis {3, 17, 18}. (*X_17 and X_18 are isogonal conjugates of each other's pedal/circumcircle partners on the Kiepert hyperbola.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (COLLECTION 1 CAPSTONE):** ``X_17 • X_18 • X_13 • X_14 • X_15 • X_16`` — Equilateral Six-Center Family: Napoleon points X_17, X_18, Fermat points X_13, X_14, and Isodynamic points X_15, X_16. (*Completes the equilateral geometry of Collection 1 before Clawson point X_19.*)
