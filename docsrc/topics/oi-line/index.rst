Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (EULER'S INEQUALITY R ≥ 2r):** ``OI Line L(1, 3, 40, 55, 56, 57) • |X_1 X_3|² = R(R − 2r)`` — The OI Line: Connects Incenter X_1 and Circumcenter X_3 as the line of centers of the incircle and circumcircle. (*Since |X_1 X_3|² = R(R − 2r) ≥ 0, the circumradius R is at least twice the inradius r.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (PONCELET PORISM AXIS):** ``Similicenters X_55 (Internal) & X_56 (External) • Bevan Point X_40`` — Similitude & Reflection: X_3 is the midpoint of X_1 and Bevan point X_40; X_55, X_56 divide X_1 X_3 in ratio r : R. (*The OI line is the axis of symmetry for Poncelet's bicentric triangle family between the incircle and circumcircle.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (CIRCUMCIRCLE–INCIRCLE AXIS):** ``Collinearity {1, 3, 40, 55, 56}`` — OI Central Axis: The line {1, 3, 40, 55, 56} governs the metric separation between inscribed and circumscribed circles. (*Contains X_1, X_3, X_36 (circumcircle inverse of X_1), X_40, X_55, X_56, and X_57.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (ISOGONAL LINKS TO X_7 & X_8):** ``X_1 • X_3 • X_40 • X_55 • X_56`` — OI Line Centers: X_1, X_3, X_40, X_55, and X_56 align along the OI axis. (*X_55 is the isogonal conjugate of Gergonne X_7; X_56 is the isogonal conjugate of X_55's harmonic partner.*)
