Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (CEVIAN & ANTICEVIAN TRIANGLES OF X_2):** ``Medial Triangle M_a M_b M_c (Scale −1/2) • Anticomplementary Triangle (Scale −2)`` — Medial & Anticomplementary Hierarchy: Centered at X_2, the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c has circumcircle equal to the nine-point circle and incircle equal to the Spieker circle. (*Area(Medial) = 1/4 Area(△ABC); Area(Anticomplementary) = 4 Area(△ABC).*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (THREE-GENERATION LADDER):** ``Center Mapping: X_4 (Anticomp) ↦ X_3 (△ABC) ↦ X_5 (Medial)  •  X_8 ↦ X_1 ↦ X_10`` — Shift of Centers: Passing from anticomplementary to △ABC to medial maps X_4 ↦ X_3 ↦ X_5 and X_8 ↦ X_1 ↦ X_10. (*△ABC is simultaneously the medial triangle of its anticomplementary triangle.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (SHARED CENTROID X_2):** ``Euler Line {2, 3, 4, 5} & Nagel Line {1, 2, 8, 10}`` — Dual Homothetic Axes: The Euler line {2, 3, 4, 5} and Nagel line {1, 2, 8, 10} cross at the common centroid X_2. (*The medial, reference, and anticomplementary triangles all share the exact same centroid X_2.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (UNIVERSAL TEMPLATE):** ``X_2 • (X_4, X_3, X_5) • (X_8, X_1, X_10)`` — Homothetic Triads: X_2 governs the Euler triad X_3, X_4, X_5 and the Nagel triad X_1, X_8, X_10. (*Every triangle center X_n has a medial counterpart (complement) and anticomplementary counterpart (anticomplement).*)
