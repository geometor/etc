Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (HOMOTHETIC SCALING):** ``Complement h(X_2, −1/2) • Anticomplement h(X_2, −2)`` — Centroid Homothety: Dilation at X_2 by ratio −1/2 maps △ABC to the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c and the circumcircle to the nine-point circle. (*Every center of the medial triangle is the exact complement of the corresponding center of △ABC.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (COLLINEAR TRIPLETS):** ``u : v : w  ↦  v+w : w+u : u+v  ⟷  −u+v+w : u−v+w : u+v−w`` — Barycentric Shift: Complement maps u : v : w to v+w : w+u : u+v; anticomplement maps to −u+v+w : u−v+w : u+v−w. (*For any point P, its anticomplement P', P, X_2, and complement P'' form a 6 : 2 : 1 segment ratio.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (PARALLEL GEOMETRIES):** ``Euler Line {2, 3, 4, 5} & Nagel Line {1, 2, 8, 10}`` — Collection 1 Action: Centroid X_2 is the intersection of the Nagel homothety axis {1, 2, 8, 10} and the Euler homothety axis {2, 3, 4, 5}. (*The Euler line of △ABC and the Nagel line of △ABC are linked by a 1 : −2 dilation about X_2.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (MIDPOINT THEOREM):** ``(X_8 → X_1 → X_10) • (X_4 → X_3 → X_5)`` — Homothetic Chains: On the Nagel axis X_8 → X_1 → X_10; on the Euler axis X_4 → X_3 → X_5. (*X_10 is the midpoint of X_1 X_8, and X_5 is the midpoint of X_3 X_4.*)
