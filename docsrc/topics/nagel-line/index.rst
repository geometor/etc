Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (CHRISTIAN HEINRICH VON NAGEL (1836)):** ``Nagel Line L(1, 2, 8, 10) • |X_1 X_2| : |X_2 X_10| : |X_10 X_8| = 2 : 1 : 3`` — The Nagel Line: Connects X_1, X_2, X_8, and X_10 across the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c and incircle. (*The Spieker circle of the medial triangle is the −1/2 homothetic image of the incircle at X_2.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (PERIMETER CENTER X_10):** ``Homothety at X_2: X_8 ↦ X_1 ↦ X_10`` — Barycentric Progression: X_1 = a:b:c, X_2 = 1:1:1, X_8 = s−a:s−b:s−c, and X_10 = b+c:c+a:a+b. (*X_10 is the center of mass of the uniform wire perimeter of triangle ABC.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (2 : 1 : 3 SEGMENT RATIOS):** ``Collinearity {1, 2, 8, 10}`` — Nagel Axis: The central line {1, 2, 8, 10} intersects the Euler line at the Centroid X_2. (*X_10 is the exact midpoint of segment X_1 X_8, and X_2 trisects segment X_1 X_8.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (COLLECTION 1 QUARTET):** ``X_1 • X_2 • X_10 • X_8`` — Ordered Nagel Sequence: X_1, X_2, X_10, and X_8 mirror the Euler quartet under inscribed geometry. (*Links four fundamental centers of Collection 1 along a single homothetic axis.*)
