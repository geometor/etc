Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (ORTHOGONAL CIRCLES):** ``OP · OP' = R²  •  X_15 ↔ X_16 in Circumcircle`` — Circumcircle Inversion: Radial inversion across the circumcircle centered at X_3 exchanges the 1st Isodynamic point X_15 and 2nd Isodynamic point X_16. (*Any circle passing through a pair of inverse points is strictly orthogonal to the circle of inversion.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (CONFORMAL INVOLUTION):** ``Radial Product |X_3 X_15| · |X_3 X_16| = R²`` — Algebraic Formula: Inversion of P in the circumcircle reflects along ray from X_3 with radial product OP · OP' = R². (*Circle inversion preserves angles between curves and maps generalized circles to generalized circles.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (RADIAL AXES THROUGH X_3):** ``Brocard Axis {3, 6, 15, 16} & Euler Line {3, 4}`` — Collection 1 Inversion Axes: The Brocard axis {3, 6, 15, 16} carries the inverse pair X_15, X_16 while {3, 4} carries the orthocenter inverse X_186. (*Every line through the circumcenter X_3 is invariant as a set under circumcircle inversion.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (INVERSION CATALOG):** ``X_15 ↔ X_16 on Brocard Axis {3, 6, 15, 16}`` — Inverse Partners: On the Brocard axis through X_3 and X_6, X_15 and X_16 are exact circumcircle inverses. (*Incenter X_1 inverts in the circumcircle to X_36; Orthocenter X_4 inverts to X_186.*)
