Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (CEVIAN REFLECTION • ∠ACP = ∠BCP*):** ``P = α : β : γ  ⟷  P* = 1/α : 1/β : 1/γ`` — Isogonal Conjugacy: Reflecting the cevians of X_2 across the angle bisectors through X_1 produces the symmedians concurring at X_6, while incircle and circumcircle anchor the metric duality. (*Isogonal lines make equal angles with the interior angle bisectors at vertices A, B, and C.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (ALGEBRAIC INVOLUTION):** ``(X_2, X_6) • (X_3, X_4) • (X_13, X_15)`` — Reciprocal Trilinears: α : β : γ maps to 1/α : 1/β : 1/γ and barycentrics u : v : w map to a²/u : b²/v : c²/w. (*Points on the circumcircle map to the line at infinity; the sidelines of △ABC are singular.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (ISOGONAL PAIRS):** ``Classic Isogonal Pairs in Collection 1`` — Collection 1 Action: Medians and symmedians pair {2, 6}, circumcenter and orthocenter pair {3, 4}, and Fermat and Isodynamic centers pair {13, 15}. (*Self-isogonal fixed points are the incenter X_1 and the three excenters I_a, I_b, I_c.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (GLOBAL DUALITY):** ``X_1 Fixed • X_2 ↔ X_6 • X_3 ↔ X_4 • X_13 ↔ X_15`` — Conjugate Network: X_1 is fixed; X_2 conjugates with X_6; X_3 conjugates with X_4; and X_13 conjugates with X_15. (*Also pairs Gergonne X_7 ↔ X_55, Nagel X_8 ↔ X_57, and Napoleon X_17 ↔ X_18.*)
