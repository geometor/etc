Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (TORRICELLI, FERMAT, APOLLONIUS & NAPOLEON):** ``Equilateral Triad: Fermat (X_13, X_14) • Isodynamic (X_15, X_16) • Napoleon (X_17, X_18)`` — Equilateral Central Lines: Fermat points X_13, X_14, Isodynamic points X_15, X_16, and Napoleon points X_17, X_18 on the circumcircle stage. (*All six centers arise from the π/3 and −π/3 Kiepert equilateral constructions on sides BC, CA, AB.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (60° & 30° KIEPERT PARAMETERS):** ``Kiepert Hyperbola {2, 4, 13, 14, 17, 18} ↔ Brocard Axis {3, 6, 15, 16}`` — Kiepert–Brocard Duality: X_13, X_14, X_17, X_18 lie on the Kiepert hyperbola with X_2, X_4, while their isogonal partners X_15, X_16 lie on the Brocard axis. (*Kiepert hyperbola K(θ) gives X_13, X_14 at θ = ±60° and X_17, X_18 at θ = ±30°.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (CROSS-ALIGNMENT OF TRIADS):** ``Collinearities {3, 6, 15, 16} • {2, 13, 17} • {4, 13, 18}`` — Equilateral Axes: Brocard axis {3, 6, 15, 16}, centroid–Fermat–Napoleon line {2, 13, 17}, and orthocenter–Fermat–Napoleon line {4, 13, 18}. (*Line {6, 13, 16} and {6, 14, 15} also cross at the Symmedian point X_6.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (COMPLETE EQUILATERAL SUBSYSTEM):** ``X_13 ↔ X_15 • X_14 ↔ X_16 • X_17 ↔ X_18`` — Isogonal Pairs: X_13 and X_15, X_14 and X_16, and X_17 and X_18. (*Together X_13–X_18 form a closed subsystem under isogonal conjugation and circumcircle inversion.*)
