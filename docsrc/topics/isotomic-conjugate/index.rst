Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (MIDPOINT SYMMETRY • BD = CE):** ``P = u : v : w  ⟷  P† = 1/u : 1/v : 1/w`` — Isotomic Conjugacy: Reflecting cevian feet across the side midpoints of the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c fixes the centroid X_2 and swaps touch-point cevians of the incircle. (*By Ceva's theorem, reflecting cevian feet across M_a, M_b, M_c preserves concurrence.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (AFFINE INVOLUTION):** ``Barycentrics u : v : w ↦ 1/u : 1/v : 1/w`` — Reciprocal Barycentrics: Sub-area weights u : v : w invert to 1/u : 1/v : 1/w while trilinears map to (bc α)⁻¹ : (ca β)⁻¹ : (ab γ)⁻¹. (*Isotomic conjugation commutes with every affine transformation of triangle ABC.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (TOUCH-POINT SYMMETRY):** ``Gergonne–Nagel Isotomic Axis {7, 8}`` — Collection 1 Action: The Soddy line {1, 7, 8} and centroid axes {2, 7} and {2, 8} frame the Gergonne–Nagel isotomic pair. (*Incircle and excircle tangency points lie at distance s − b and s − c from opposite vertices.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (ISOTOMIC PAIRS):** ``X_2 Self-Isotomic • X_7 ↔ X_8`` — Conjugate Network: X_2 is self-isotomic, while Gergonne point X_7 and Nagel point X_8 are mutual isotomic conjugates. (*Also pairs Incenter X_1 ↔ X_75 (isotomic conjugate of incenter) and Orthocenter X_4 ↔ X_69.*)
