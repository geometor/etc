T_9 • The Feuerbach Line¶

Category: Named Central Lines

The line of centers passing through the Incenter X1, Nine-Point Center X5, Feuerbach Point X11, and its harmonic conjugate X12 at the tangency of the incircle and nine-point circle.

Primary Construction & Central Lines¶

T_9 • The Feuerbach Line

Key Properties & Sequenced Operations¶

  1. Definition (FEUERBACH’S THEOREM (1822)): Feuerbach Line L(1, 5, 11, 12) • |X_1 X_5| = |R/2 − r| — The Feuerbach Line: Joins Incenter X1 and Nine-Point Center X5 through tangency point X11 and X12 of the incircle and nine-point circle. (Internal tangency requires the distance between centers |X1 X5| to equal the difference of radii |R/2 − r|.)

  2. Coordinate & Algebraic Law (HARMONIC RANGE (X1, X5; X11, X12) = −1): Harmonic Similicenters: X_11 (Internal) & X_12 (External) — Tangency & Similitude: X11 is the internal tangency point and X12 is the harmonic conjugate dividing X1 X5 in ratio r : R/2. (X11 and X12 are the internal and external centers of similitude of the incircle and nine-point circle.)

  3. Central Line Incidences (LINE OF CENTERS): Collinearity {1, 5, 11, 12} — Feuerbach Axis: The central line {1, 5, 11, 12} connects two tangent circles and four Collection 1 centers. (Perpendicular to the common tangent of the incircle and nine-point circle at X11.)

  4. Collection 1 Network (COLLECTION 1 HARMONIC PAIR): X_1 • X_5 • X_11 • X_12 — Feuerbach Quartet: X1, X5, X11, and X12 form a harmonic quartet on the Feuerbach line. (X11 and X12 are also perspectors of the orthic and intouch configurations.)

Collection 1 Centers Involved¶

X1, X5, X11, X12
