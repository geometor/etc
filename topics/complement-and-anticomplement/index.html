T_3 • Complement & Anticomplement¶

Category: Transformations & Conjugacies

Homothety centered at the Centroid X2 with ratio −1/2 (complement) and ratio −2 (anticomplement), mapping every triangle element to its medial or anticomplementary counterpart.

Primary Construction & Central Lines¶

T_3 • Complement & Anticomplement

Key Properties & Sequenced Operations¶

  1. Definition (HOMOTHETIC SCALING): Complement h(X_2, −1/2) • Anticomplement h(X_2, −2) — Centroid Homothety: Dilation at X2 by ratio −1/2 maps △ABC to the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c and the circumcircle to the nine-point circle. (Every center of the medial triangle is the exact complement of the corresponding center of △ABC.)

  2. Coordinate & Algebraic Law (COLLINEAR TRIPLETS): u : v : w  ↦  v+w : w+u : u+v  ⟷  −u+v+w : u−v+w : u+v−w — Barycentric Shift: Complement maps u : v : w to v+w : w+u : u+v; anticomplement maps to −u+v+w : u−v+w : u+v−w. (For any point P, its anticomplement P’, P, X2, and complement P’’ form a 6 : 2 : 1 segment ratio.)

  3. Central Line Incidences (PARALLEL GEOMETRIES): Euler Line {2, 3, 4, 5} & Nagel Line {1, 2, 8, 10} — Collection 1 Action: Centroid X2 is the intersection of the Nagel homothety axis {1, 2, 8, 10} and the Euler homothety axis {2, 3, 4, 5}. (The Euler line of △ABC and the Nagel line of △ABC are linked by a 1 : −2 dilation about X2.)

  4. Collection 1 Network (MIDPOINT THEOREM): (X_8 → X_1 → X_10) • (X_4 → X_3 → X_5) — Homothetic Chains: On the Nagel axis X8 → X1 → X10; on the Euler axis X4 → X3 → X5. (X10 is the midpoint of X1 X8, and X5 is the midpoint of X3 X4.)

Collection 1 Centers Involved¶

X2, X1, X8, X10, X3, X4, X5
