T_24 • Equilateral, Isogonic & Napoleon Triangles¶

Category: Derived Central Triangles

The outer and inner equilateral triangles erected on sides BC, CA, AB, whose apexes form the Isogonic Triangles (perspectors X13, X14) and whose centers form the equilateral Napoleon Triangles (perspectors X17, X18).

Primary Construction & Central Lines¶

T_24 • Equilateral, Isogonic & Napoleon Triangles

Key Properties & Sequenced Operations¶

  1. Definition (NAPOLEON’S THEOREM): Isogonic Triangles (Perspectors X_13, X_14) • Napoleon Triangles (Perspectors X_17, X_18) — Equilateral Constructions: Outer and inner equilateral apexes yield Fermat points X13, X14, while their centroids form equilateral triangles with perspectors X17, X18 on the circumcircle stage. (For any triangle ABC, the centers of outer (or inner) equilateral triangles on its sides form an equilateral triangle.)

  2. Coordinate & Algebraic Law (TORRICELLI CIRCLES): Area(Outer Napoleon) − Area(Inner Napoleon) = Area(△ABC)  •  Shared Centroid X_2 — Area & Centroid Invariance: Both Napoleon triangles have centroid X2, while X13, X14 and X17, X18 correspond to Kiepert angles ±60° and ±30°. (The circumcircles of the three outer equilateral triangles concur at the 1st Fermat point X13.)

  3. Central Line Incidences (COLLINEAR EQUILATERAL CENTERS): Kiepert Chords {2, 13, 17} • {4, 13, 18} • {3, 17, 18} — Napoleon Central Lines: Centroid axis {2, 13, 17}, orthocenter axis {4, 13, 18}, and circumcenter axis {3, 17, 18}. (X17 and X18 are isogonal conjugates of each other’s pedal/circumcircle partners on the Kiepert hyperbola.)

  4. Collection 1 Network (COLLECTION 1 CAPSTONE): X_17 • X_18 • X_13 • X_14 • X_15 • X_16 — Equilateral Six-Center Family: Napoleon points X17, X18, Fermat points X13, X14, and Isodynamic points X15, X16. (Completes the equilateral geometry of Collection 1 before Clawson point X19.)

Collection 1 Centers Involved¶

X13, X14, X15, X16, X17, X18, X2, X3, X4
