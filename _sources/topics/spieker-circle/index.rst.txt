Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (THEODOR SPIEKER (1888)):** ``Spieker Circle (Center X_10, Radius r/2) • Incircle of Medial Triangle M_a M_b M_c`` — The Spieker Circle: Centered at X_10, the Spieker circle is inscribed in the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c. (*Dilation of the incircle at X_2 by factor −1/2 produces the Spieker circle of radius r/2.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (RADICAL CENTER OF EXCIRCLES):** ``Midpoint of X_1 and X_8 on the Nagel Line • Barycentrics b+c : c+a : a+b`` — Perimeter Centroid: X_10 = b+c : c+a : a+b is the complement of X_1 and midpoint of X_1 X_8. (*Equal tangents can be drawn from X_10 to all three excircles of △ABC.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (MEDIAL INCIRCLE GEOMETRY):** ``Nagel Line {1, 2, 8, 10} ∩ Fuhrmann–Spieker Line {4, 9, 10}`` — Spieker Axes: The Nagel line {1, 2, 8, 10} and the Fuhrmann–Spieker axis {4, 9, 10} meet at X_10. (*Every property of X_1 in △ABC translates to X_10 inside the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (COLLECTION 1 ANCHOR):** ``X_10 • X_1 • X_2 • X_8 • X_4 • X_9`` — Spieker Network: X_10 bisects X_1 and X_8 through X_2, and aligns with X_4 and X_9. (*Connects the incenter, centroid, orthocenter, Nagel point, and Mittenpunkt.*)
