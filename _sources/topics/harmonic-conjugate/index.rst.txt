======================================
T_4 • Harmonic Conjugate & Cross-Ratio
======================================

**Category:** Transformations & Conjugacies

Four collinear centers (A, B; C, D) form a harmonic range when C and D divide segment AB internally and externally in the same ratio, yielding cross-ratio −1.

Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (PROJECTIVE INVARIANCE):** ``(A, B; C, D) = (AC / CB) : (AD / DB) = −1`` — Harmonic Range: On the Euler line through X_5, internal and external division of X_3 and X_4 with X_2 links the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c and nine-point circle. (*Cross-ratio (A, B; C, D) = −1 is preserved under every projective collineation.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (HARMONIC MEAN):** ``Euler Range (X_3, X_4; X_2, X_5) = −1`` — Homogeneous Linear Combination: If C = p·A + q·B, then its harmonic conjugate with respect to A and B is D = p·A − q·B, satisfying 2/AB = 1/AC + 1/AD. (*On the Euler line: X_2 : X_5 divide X_3 X_4 in ratios 1 : 2 and 1 : −1.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (SIMILICENTER DUALITY):** ``Euler Line {2, 3, 4, 5} & Feuerbach Line {1, 5, 11, 12}`` — Collection 1 Harmonic Axes: The Euler line {2, 3, 4, 5} and Feuerbach line {1, 5, 11, 12} meet at the Nine-Point Center X_5. (*Internal and external centers of similitude of any two circles are harmonic conjugates with respect to their centers.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (APOLLONIAN HARMONICS):** ``(X_2, X_4; X_3, X_5) = −1 • (X_1, X_5; X_11, X_12) = −1`` — Harmonic Quartets: Euler centers X_2, X_3, X_4, X_5 and Feuerbach centers X_1, X_5, X_11, X_12. (*On the Brocard axis, (X_3, X_6; X_15, X_16) also forms a classical harmonic structure.*)
