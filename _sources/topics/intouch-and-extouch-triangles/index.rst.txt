Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (JOSEPH DIAZ GERGONNE (1818) & CHRISTIAN VON NAGEL (1836)):** ``Intouch Triangle T_a T_b T_c (Perspector X_7) • Extouch Triangle E_a E_b E_c (Perspector X_8)`` — Contact Triangles: The intouch triangle T_a T_b T_c inscribed in the incircle has perspector X_7, while the extouch triangle has perspector X_8. (*Intouch triangle is the pedal triangle of X_1 and cevian triangle of X_7.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (EQUAL AREA PROPERTY):** ``Symmetric Touch Segments: BT_a = CE_a = s−b  •  CT_a = BE_a = s−c`` — Isotomic Touch Feet: Intouch perspector X_7 = 1/(s−a) : 1/(s−b) : 1/(s−c) and extouch perspector X_8 = s−a : s−b : s−c are reciprocal in barycentrics. (*The intouch triangle T_a T_b T_c and extouch triangle E_a E_b E_c always have the exact same area: 2Δ²r / (abc).*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (COMPLEMENT OF GERGONNE POINT IS MITTENPUNKT X_9):** ``Soddy Line {1, 7} • Nagel Line {1, 2, 8} • Mittenpunkt Line {2, 7, 9}`` — Contact Central Lines: Soddy axis {1, 7}, Nagel axis {1, 2, 8}, and Gergonne–Centroid–Mittenpunkt line {2, 7, 9}. (*Under homothety at X_2, X_1 maps to X_8 (anticomplement), while X_9 is the complement of X_7.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (SPLITTER SEGMENTS):** ``X_7 ↔ X_8 • X_1 • X_2 • X_9`` — Contact Quartet: Gergonne X_7 and Nagel X_8 pair across X_1, X_2, and X_9. (*The cevians through X_8 bisect the perimeter of △ABC and are called the splitters of the triangle.*)
