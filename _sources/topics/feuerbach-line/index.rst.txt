========================
T_9 • The Feuerbach Line
========================

**Category:** Named Central Lines

The line of centers passing through the Incenter X_1, Nine-Point Center X_5, Feuerbach Point X_11, and its harmonic conjugate X_12 at the tangency of the incircle and nine-point circle.

Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (FEUERBACH'S THEOREM (1822)):** ``Feuerbach Line L(1, 5, 11, 12) • |X_1 X_5| = |R/2 − r|`` — The Feuerbach Line: Joins Incenter X_1 and Nine-Point Center X_5 through tangency point X_11 and X_12 of the incircle and nine-point circle. (*Internal tangency requires the distance between centers |X_1 X_5| to equal the difference of radii |R/2 − r|.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (HARMONIC RANGE (X_1, X_5; X_11, X_12) = −1):** ``Harmonic Similicenters: X_11 (Internal) & X_12 (External)`` — Tangency & Similitude: X_11 is the internal tangency point and X_12 is the harmonic conjugate dividing X_1 X_5 in ratio r : R/2. (*X_11 and X_12 are the internal and external centers of similitude of the incircle and nine-point circle.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (LINE OF CENTERS):** ``Collinearity {1, 5, 11, 12}`` — Feuerbach Axis: The central line {1, 5, 11, 12} connects two tangent circles and four Collection 1 centers. (*Perpendicular to the common tangent of the incircle and nine-point circle at X_11.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (COLLECTION 1 HARMONIC PAIR):** ``X_1 • X_5 • X_11 • X_12`` — Feuerbach Quartet: X_1, X_5, X_11, and X_12 form a harmonic quartet on the Feuerbach line. (*X_11 and X_12 are also perspectors of the orthic and intouch configurations.*)
