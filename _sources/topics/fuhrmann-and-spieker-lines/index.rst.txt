Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (CHRISTIAN VON NAGEL & WILHELM FUHRMANN):** ``Fuhrmann–Spieker Axis L(4, 9, 10) • Mittenpunkt Pencils L(1, 6, 9) & L(2, 7, 9)`` — Mittenpunkt & Spieker Web: X_9 and X_10 align with Orthocenter X_4 across the medial triangle M_a M_b M_c and incircle. (*X_9 is the symmedian point of the excentral triangle; X_10 is the incenter of the medial triangle.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (HOMOTHETIC & ISOGONAL BRIDGES):** ``Three Concurrent Central Lines at Mittenpunkt X_9`` — Concurrence at X_9: {4, 9, 10}, {1, 6, 9}, and {2, 7, 9} all concur at Mittenpunkt X_9. (*X_9 is the complement of the Gergonne point X_7 (since X_2 divides X_7 X_9 in 2 : 1 ratio).*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (CLAWSON & FEUERBACH CROSSINGS):** ``Collinearities {4, 9, 10} • {1, 6, 9} • {2, 7, 9}`` — Central Line Pencils: {4, 9, 10} meets {1, 6, 9} and {2, 7, 9} at X_9. (*Line {2, 11, 19} and {3, 12, 19} similarly cross at the Clawson point X_19.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (COLLECTION 1 SYNTHESIS):** ``X_4 • X_9 • X_10 • X_1 • X_6 • X_2 • X_7`` — Connected Centers: X_9 links X_4 and X_10 with X_1, X_6 and X_2, X_7. (*Nine of the nineteen centers in Collection 1 meet along the Mittenpunkt and Spieker pencils.*)
