===============================
T_14 • The Incircle & Excircles
===============================

**Category:** Named Circles

The four tritangent circles tangent to all three sidelines of triangle ABC: the interior Incircle centered at X_1 (radius r = Δ/s) and the three exterior Excircles.

Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (EUCLID BOOK IV • PROPOSITION 4):** ``Incircle (Center X_1, Radius r = Δ/s) • Excircles (Radii r_a, r_b, r_c)`` — The Incircle & Excircles: Centered at X_1, the incircle touches the sidelines at the intouch triangle T_a T_b T_c. (*1/r = 1/r_a + 1/r_b + 1/r_c and Area(△ABC) = √(r · r_a · r_b · r_c).*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (SEVEN COLLECTION 1 CENTERS):** ``Touch-Point Distances: AT_b = AT_c = s−a • BT_c = BT_a = s−b • CT_a = CT_b = s−c`` — Tritangent Metrics: Inradius r = Δ/s, exradii r_a = Δ/(s−a), and touch segments s−a : s−b : s−c. (*Generates X_1 (Incenter), X_7 (Gergonne), X_8 (Nagel), X_9 (Mittenpunkt), X_10 (Spieker), X_11, X_12.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (RADICAL & HOMOTHETIC AXES):** ``Tritangent Axes {1, 7} • {1, 8} • {1, 11, 12}`` — Tritangent Pencils: X_1 anchors the Soddy axis {1, 7}, Nagel axis {1, 8}, and Feuerbach axis {1, 11, 12}. (*The Spieker center X_10 is the radical center of the three excircles.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (TRITANGENT HARMONY):** ``X_1 • X_7 • X_8 • X_9 • X_11 • X_12`` — Tritangent Family: X_1, X_7, X_8, X_9, X_11, and X_12. (*Replacing (a, b, c) with (−a, b, c) transforms the incenter into the A-excenter.*)
