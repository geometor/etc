Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (HENRI BROCARD & ÉMILE LEMOINE):** ``Brocard Axis L(3, 6, 15, 16) ⊥ Lemoine Axis`` — The Brocard Axis: Connects Circumcenter X_3 and Symmedian Point X_6 with Isodynamic Points X_15, X_16 across the circumcircle. (*Isogonal conjugate of the Brocard axis is the Kiepert hyperbola.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (ISOGONAL TRANSFORM TO KIEPERT HYPERBOLA):** ``Trilinear Equation: bc(b²−c²)α + ca(c²−a²)β + ab(a²−b²)γ = 0`` — Radical Chord of Apollonian Circles: X_3 = cos A : cos B : cos C and X_6 = a : b : c span the axis containing X_15, X_16. (*Every point on the Kiepert hyperbola has its isogonal conjugate on the Brocard axis.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (DIAMETER OF BROCARD CIRCLE):** ``Collinearity {3, 6, 15, 16}`` — Brocard Collinearity: The Brocard axis {3, 6, 15, 16} is the common radical line of the three Apollonian circles. (*Segment X_3 X_6 is the diameter of the Brocard circle passing through the two Brocard points.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (KIEPERT DUALITY):** ``X_3 • X_6 • X_15 • X_16`` — Brocard Quartet: X_3, X_6, X_15, and X_16 align on the Brocard axis. (*Isogonal conjugation maps the Kiepert centers {X_2, X_4, X_13, X_14} onto the Brocard axis {X_6, X_3, X_15, X_16}.*)
