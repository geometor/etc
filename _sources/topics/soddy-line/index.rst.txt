Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (FREDERICK SODDY (1936) • DESCARTES CIRCLE THEOREM):** ``Soddy Line L(1, 7, 20, 175, 176)`` — The Soddy Line: Connects Incenter X_1 and Gergonne Point X_7 across the intouch triangle T_a T_b T_c and incircle. (*The three mutually tangent vertex circles of radii s−a, s−b, s−c meet at T_a, T_b, T_c.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (RADICAL & PERSPECTION AXIS):** ``Intersection with Euler Line at de Longchamps Point X_20`` — Euler–Soddy Intersection: The Soddy line through X_1 and X_7 meets the Euler line {2, 3, 4, 20} at X_20. (*Perpendicular to the Gergonne line (trilinear polar of X_7).*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (DE LONGCHAMPS INTERSECTION):** ``Soddy Line {1, 7, 20} ∩ Euler Line {2, 3, 4, 20}`` — Crossing Central Axes: The Soddy line {1, 7, 20} and the Euler line {2, 3, 4, 20} intersect at X_20. (*X_20 is the anticomplement of the orthocenter X_4 and reflection of X_4 in X_3.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (SODDY CENTERS X_175 & X_176):** ``X_1 • X_7 • X_20`` — Soddy Collinearity: X_1, X_7, and X_20 anchor the Soddy axis on △ABC. (*The inner and outer Soddy centers divide X_1 and X_7 harmonically with the Descartes curvatures.*)
