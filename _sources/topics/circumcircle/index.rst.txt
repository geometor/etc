Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (EUCLID BOOK IV • PROPOSITION 5):** ``Circumcircle (Center X_3, Radius R = abc / 4Δ) • 2R = a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C`` — The Circumcircle: Passes through A, B, C with center X_3 on the perpendicular bisectors of △ABC. (*Barycentric equation: a²vw + b²wu + c²uv = 0; trilinear equation: aβγ + bγα + cαβ = 0.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (SIMSON LINE LOCUS):** ``Isogonal Transform of the Line at Infinity (u + v + w = 0)`` — Projective & Metric Role: X_3 = cos A : cos B : cos C anchors the circumcircle of radius R = abc / (4Δ). (*The pedal feet of a point P are collinear (forming a Simson line) if and only if P lies on the circumcircle.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (RADIAL INVERSION CENTER):** ``Euler Line {2, 3, 4, 5} & Brocard Axis {3, 6, 15, 16}`` — Orthogonal Axes at X_3: X_3 is the origin of the Euler line {2, 3, 4, 5} and the Brocard axis {3, 6, 15, 16}. (*X_15 and X_16 are mutual inverses in the circumcircle along the Brocard axis.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (TANGENTIAL POLARITY):** ``X_3 • X_2 • X_4 • X_5 • X_6 • X_15 • X_16`` — Circumcircle Network: X_3 links X_2, X_4, X_5 on the Euler line and X_6, X_15, X_16 on the Brocard axis. (*The tangents to the circumcircle at A, B, C form the tangential triangle, whose perspector is X_6.*)
