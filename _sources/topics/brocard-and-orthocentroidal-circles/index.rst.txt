Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (BROCARD (1881) & GUINAND (1984)):** ``Brocard Circle (Diameter X_3 X_6) • Orthocentroidal Circle (Diameter X_2 X_4)`` — Diameter Circles: The Brocard circle on diameter X_3 X_6 and the orthocentroidal circle on diameter X_2 X_4. (*Every pedal of X_6 onto the perpendicular bisectors lies on the Brocard circle.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (GUINAND'S THEOREM):** ``Brocard Circle Center X_182 (Midpoint of X_3 X_6) • Orthocentroidal Center X_381 (Midpoint of X_2 X_4)`` — Midpoint Centers: Brocard diameter X_3 X_6 and Euler diameter X_2 X_4 bound the interior locus of X_1 and X_6. (*In any non-equilateral triangle, X_1 lies inside the orthocentroidal disk and never on the Euler line unless isosceles.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (RADICAL AXIS):** ``Brocard Axis {3, 6} & Euler Line {2, 3, 4, 5}`` — Diameter Axes: The Brocard axis {3, 6} and Euler line {2, 3, 4, 5} form the perpendicular diameters of the two circles at X_3. (*The radical axis of the circumcircle and Brocard circle is the Lemoine line.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (CLAWSON & SYMMEDIAN GEOMETRY):** ``X_2 • X_3 • X_4 • X_5 • X_6`` — Five Classical Centers: X_2, X_3, X_4, X_5 on the Euler line and X_6 on the Brocard circle. (*The orthocentroidal and Brocard circles are linked by pedal projections and isogonal inversion.*)
