Key Properties & Sequenced Operations
-------------------------------------

1. **Definition (FAGNANO'S MINIMUM PERIMETER TRIANGLE (1775)):** ``Orthic Triangle H_a H_b H_c (Perspector X_4) • Tangential Triangle (Perspector X_6)`` — Orthic & Tangential Duality: The orthic triangle H_a H_b H_c of X_4 is inscribed in the nine-point circle and homothetic to the tangential triangle of X_6. (*In any acute triangle, the orthic triangle has the minimum perimeter of all inscribed triangles.*)
2. **Coordinate & Algebraic Law (ORTHIC INCENTER X_4):** ``Parallel Corresponding Sides: H_b H_c ∥ Tangent at A  (since OA ⊥ H_b H_c)`` — Parallel Side Theorem: Circumradii from X_3 are perpendicular to the orthic sides H_a H_b H_c, making them parallel to the tangents of X_6. (*Altitudes AD, BE, CF bisect the interior angles of the orthic triangle H_a H_b H_c.*)
3. **Central Line Incidences (CLAWSON POINT X_19):** ``Euler Line {3, 4, 5} • Brocard Axis {3, 6} • Clawson Axis {4, 19}`` — Orthic–Tangential Axes: Euler line {3, 4, 5}, Brocard axis {3, 6}, and Clawson line {4, 19}. (*X_19 is the second center of similitude of the orthic and excentral configurations and perspector of the orthic axis.*)
4. **Collection 1 Network (POLAR TRIANGLE RESPECT TO CIRCUMCIRCLE):** ``X_4 • X_3 • X_5 • X_6 • X_19`` — Orthic Network: X_4, X_3, X_5, X_6, and Clawson point X_19. (*The tangential triangle is the polar triangle of △ABC with respect to the circumcircle, and anticevian of X_6.*)
