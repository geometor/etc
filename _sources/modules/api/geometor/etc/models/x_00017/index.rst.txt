.. py:function:: construct_x_00017(model: geometor.model.Model, A: sympy.geometry.Point, B: sympy.geometry.Point, C: sympy.geometry.Point) -> sympy.geometry.Point

   Construct ``X_17 = 1ST NAPOLEON POINT`` and its classical incident central lines on ``ABC``.

   Constructed elements include:

   - Outer equilateral triangles and outer Napoleon triangle ``< Nap_a Nap_b Nap_c >``
     with perspectivity lines ``[ A Nap_a ]``, ``[ B Nap_b ]``, ``[ C Nap_c ]``
     concurring at ``X_17``.
   - Incident central lines: ``{ 3, 13 }``, ``{ 4, 15 }``, ``{ 5, 14 }``, and
     Napoleon line ``{ 6, 18 }``.
