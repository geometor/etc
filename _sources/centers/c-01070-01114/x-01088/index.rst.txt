X_1088
======

.. rubric:: TRILINEAR SQUARE OF X_7

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_1088 <X_1088>` is the Brianchon point (perspector) of the inellipse that is the trilinear square of the Gergonne line. The center of this inellipse is X_11019. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, October 15, 2018)
