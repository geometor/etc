X(690) = CROSSDIFFERENCE OF LINE X(6) AND X(110) — Code
=======================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`N_{a}N_{b}N_{c} << N_a N_b N_c >>` and :g:`N_{a}'N_{b}'N_{c}' << N_a' N_b' N_c' >>` be the outer and inner Napoleon triangles, respectively. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of :g:`N_{a} <N_a>` wrt :g:`N_{a}'N_{b}'N_{c}' << N_a' N_b' N_c' >>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the isogonal conjugate of :g:`N_{a}' <N_a'>` wrt :g:`N_{a}N_{b}N_{c} << N_a N_b N_c >>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`A'A″ <[ A' A″ ]>`, :g:`B'B″ <[ B' B″ ]>`, :g:`C'C″ <[ C' C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X(690) <X(690)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 30, 2015)
