X(695) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(384)
=====================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the cross-triangle of the 1st and 2nd Neuberg triangles. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the :term:`crossdifference <crossdifference>` of :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X(695) <X(695)>`. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the :term:`trilinear polar <trilinear-polar>` of :g:`A' <A'>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A* <A*>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, and define :g:`B* <B*>` and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA* <[ A A* ]>`, :g:`BB* <[ B B* ]>`, :g:`CC* <[ C C* ]>` concur in :ref:`X(695) <X(695)>`. Also, :ref:`X(695) <X(695)>` is the orthologic center of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` to :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. The reciprocal orthologic center is :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>`.
