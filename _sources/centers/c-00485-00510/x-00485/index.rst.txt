Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Erect a square outwardly from each side of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the triangle formed by the respective centers of the squares. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_485 <X_485>`. For details, visit :person:`Floor van Lamoen <floor-van-lamoen>`'s site, `Vierkanten in een driehoek: 1. Omgeschreven vierkanten <http://home.wxs.nl/~lamoen/wiskunde/vierkant.html>`__ (van Lamoen, 4/26/98) and his article "Friendship Among :term:`Triangle Centers <triangle-center>`," *:cite:`Forum Geometricorum <forum-geometricorum>`,* 1 (2001) 1-6. See also :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>`'s papers, `"Squares Erected on the Sides of a Triangle" <http://www.math.fau.edu/yiu/bottema38.pdf>`__, and `"On the Squares Erected Externally on the Sides of a Triangle" <http://www.math.fau.edu/yiu/square.pdf>`__.
