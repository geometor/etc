.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`P <P>` be a point on the circumcircle, and let

   (Oab) = circle through :g:`A <A>` tangent to line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` at B

   (Oac) = circle through :g:`A <A>` tangent to line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` at C

   :g:`B_{a} <B_a>` = point other than A where the line :g:`PB <[ P B ]>` meets (Oab)

   :g:`C_{a} <C_a>` = point other than A where the line :g:`PC <[ P C ]>` meets (Oac)

   Hab = orthocenter of APBa

   Hac = orthocenter of APCa
