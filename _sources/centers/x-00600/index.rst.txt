X(600) = 3rd HATZIPOLAKIS-YIU POINT
===================================

.. enunciation::

   Let O\ :sub:`A` be the circle with center A and radius R, the circumradius of triangle ABC. Let B\ :sub:`A` be the point where O\ :sub:`A` meets line AB farthest from B. Define C\ :sub:`B` and A\ :sub:`C` cyclically. Let C\ :sub:`A` be the point where O\ :sub:`A` meets line AC farthest from C. Define A\ :sub:`B` and B\ :sub:`C` cyclically. :ref:`X(600) <X(600)>` is the radical center of the circles AB\ :sub:`A`\ C\ :sub:`A`, BC\ :sub:`B`\ A\ :sub:`B`, CA\ :sub:`C`\ B\ :sub:`C`. If "farthest from" is changed to "nearest to" in the construction, the resulting point is X(5507). (:person:`Antreas Hatzipolakis <antreas-hatzipolakis>`, :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>`, 1/5/01, :cite:`Hyacinthos #2344 <hyacinthos>`, #2346-8; for a correction in coordinates, see Paul Yiu, Adgeom #202.)
