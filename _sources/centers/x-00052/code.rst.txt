X(52) = ORTHOCENTER OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE — Code
=============================================

.. enunciation::

   Let Ha be the foot of the A-altitude. Let Ba be the foot of the perpendicular from Ha to CA, and define Cb and Ac cyclically. Let Ca be the foot of the perpendicular from Ha to AB, and define Ab and Bc cyclically. Let A' be the orthocenter of HaBaCa, and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines HaA', HbB', HcC' concur in :ref:`X(52) <X(52)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)
