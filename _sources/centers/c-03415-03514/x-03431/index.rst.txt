Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the orthocentroidal triangle. :ref:`X_3431 <X_3431>` is the radical center of the circumcircles of :g:`A'BC << A' B C >>`, :g:`B'CA << B' C A >>`, :g:`C'AB << C' A B >>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, June 27, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let

      * :g:`O_{a} <O_a>` = circle with diameter :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`O_{b} <O_b>` and :g:`O_{c} <O_c>` cyclically;
      * :g:`A' <A'>` = reflection of :g:`A <A>` in :g:`O_{a} <O_a>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically;
      * :g:`P_{a} <P_a>` = polar of :g:`A' <A'>` with respect to :g:`O_{a} <O_a>`, and define :g:`P_{b} <P_b>` and :g:`P_{c} <P_c>` cyclically;
      * :g:`A″ <A″>` = :g:`P_{c} <P_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`P_{b} <P_b>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically.

   The triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_3431 <X_3431>`. (:person:`Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna <dasari-naga-vijay-krishna>`, June 8, 2021)
