.. rubric:: INTERSECTION OF LINES X_1X_4 AND X_3X_7

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the midpoint of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` and the A-intouch point. Define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to the 2nd :term:`extouch triangle <extouch-triangle>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_3487 <X_3487>`.
