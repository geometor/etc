   Let :g:`P <P>` = :ref:`X_477 <X_477>` and H=\ :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`; let :g:`H_{A} <H_A>` be the orthocenter of the triangle :g:`BCP << B C P >>`. Define :g:`H_{B} <H_B>` and :g:`H_{C} <H_C>` cyclically. Then H, :g:`H_{A} <H_A>`, :g:`H_{B} <H_B>`, :g:`H_{C} <H_C>` are the vertices of a quadrilateral that is homothetic to the cyclic quadrilateral having vertices :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`, P. The center of homothety is :ref:`X_3258 <X_3258>`. Moreover, :ref:`X_3258 <X_3258>` is the anticenter of the quadrilateral ABCP. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 9/23/2011)
