   * **Trilinears:** for :ref:`X_3221 <X_3221>` are obtained by applying the :term:`symbolic substitution <symbolic-substitution>` (a,b,c) → (bc,ca,ab) to trilinear coordinates of :ref:`X_647 <X_647>`. As a symbolic substitution, this mapping takes lines onto lines. In particular, points on the Euler line, :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`\ \ :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`, with coefficients given by :ref:`X_647 <X_647>`, are mapped to the line :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`\ \ :ref:`X_194 <X_194>`, with coefficients given by :ref:`X_3221 <X_3221>`.
