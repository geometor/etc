Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   As a point on the Euler line, :ref:`X_21 <X_21>` has Shinagawa coefficients :math:`(\$aS_{A}\$, abc - \$aS_{A}\$)`.

.. container:: note-item

   The name of this point honors **Kurt Schiffler** (``../bstud/schiffler.html``).

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << I_a I_b I_c >>` be the :term:`incentral triangle <incentral-triangle>` of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the reflection of line :g:`B'C' <[ I_b I_c ]>` in line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`; define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. The triangle formed by the lines :g:`L_{A} <L_A>`, :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`, :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_21 <X_21>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 9/23/2011)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 2nd circumperp triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the cevapoint of :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, and :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_21 <X_21>`. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << Eu_a Eu_b Eu_c >>` be the :term:`Feuerbach triangle <feuerbach-triangle>`. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the tangent to the nine-point circle at :g:`A' <Eu_a>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the isogonal conjugate of the trilinear pole of :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Let :g:`A*B*C* << A* B* C* >>` be the circumcevian triangle, wrt :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>`, of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`. The lines :g:`AA* <[ A A* ]>`, :g:`BB* <[ B B* ]>`, :g:`CC* <[ C C* ]>` concur in :ref:`X_21 <X_21>`. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 2nd circumperp triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the trilinear product :g:`B' <B'>`\ \*\ :g:`C' <C'>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then :g:`A″ <A″>`, :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` are collinear on line :ref:`X_36 <X_36>`\ \ :ref:`X_238 <X_238>` (the :term:`trilinear polar <trilinear-polar>` of :ref:`X_81 <X_81>`). The lines :g:`A'A″ <[ A' A″ ]>`, :g:`B'B″ <[ B' B″ ]>`, :g:`C'C″ <[ C' C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_21 <X_21>`. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)
