X_30
====

.. rubric:: EULER INFINITY POINT

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_30 <X_30>` is the point of intersection of the Euler line and the line at infinity. Thus, each of the lines listed below is parallel to the Euler line.

.. figure:: x-00030.svg
   :alt: X_30 — EULER INFINITY POINT
   :align: center

   **X_30** (*EULER INFINITY POINT*) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

   :class: note

   * **Central Lines:** :g:`{2, 3} <{ 2, 3 }>` (:term:`Euler line <euler line>`), :g:`{13, 15} <{ 13, 15 }>`
   * **Related Centers:** :g:`X_74 <X_74>` (:ref:`ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF EULER INFINITY POINT <X_74>`), :g:`X_2 <X_2>` (:ref:`CENTROID <X_2>`), :g:`X_3 <X_3>` (:ref:`CIRCUMCENTER <X_3>`), :g:`X_13 <X_13>` (:ref:`1st ISOGONIC CENTER <X_13>`), :g:`X_15 <X_15>` (:ref:`1st ISODYNAMIC POINT <X_15>`)
