X_20–X_30
=========

.. rubric:: Euler Line Centers

.. enunciation::

   Classical centers lying on the Euler line :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`, including the :person:`De Longchamps <gohier-de-longchamps>` point, Schiffler point, Exeter point, far-out point, homothetic center of the orthic and :term:`tangential triangles <tangential-triangle>`, and the Euler infinity point.
