X_20–X_30
=========

.. rubric:: Euler Line Centers

.. enunciation::

   Classical centers lying on the Euler line :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`, including the :person:`De Longchamps <gohier-de-longchamps>` point, Schiffler point, Exeter point, far-out point, homothetic center of the orthic and :term:`tangential triangles <tangential-triangle>`, and the Euler infinity point.

.. figure:: c-00020-00030.svg
   :alt: X_20–X_30 — Euler Line Centers
   :align: center

   **X_20–X_30** (*Euler Line Centers*) — 10 representative centers (from 11 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0001:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers 20-30 (Points on the Euler line)
   :class: note

   | 2- 5, 140, 186, 199, 235, 237, 297, 376- 379,381- 384,
   | 401- 475, 546- 550, 631, 632 (and others) lie on the Euler line.
