.. enunciation::

Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the insimilicenter of (:g:`O_{B} <O_B>`) and (:g:`O_{C} <O_C>`), and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`A'A″ <[ A' A″ ]>`, :g:`B'B″ <[ B' B″ ]>`, :g:`C'C″ <[ C' C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3082 <X_3082>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 11, 2019)
