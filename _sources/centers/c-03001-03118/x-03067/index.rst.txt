X_3067
======

.. rubric:: HOFSTADTER ELLIPSE INTERSECTION

.. enunciation::

   The Hofstadter ellipse E(0) is described at :ref:`X_359 <X_359>`. The point other than :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, and :g:`C <C>` in which E(0) meets the circumcircle is :ref:`X_3067 <X_3067>`. As with :ref:`X_359 <X_359>` and :ref:`X_360 <X_360>`, this is a :term:`transcendental center <transcendental-center>`, with "exposed angles" :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>` in its coordinates.
