X_3102

.. rubric:: INNER VERTEX OF THE BROCARD SQUARE

.. enunciation::

   The 1st Brocard point (trilinears :math:`c/b : a/c : b/a)` and 2nd Brocard point (:math:`b/c : c/a : a/b)` are opposing vertices of a square here called the Brocard square. The other two vertices are :ref:`X_3102 <X_3102>` and :ref:`X_3103 <X_3103>`. Both are on the Brocard axis :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`\ \ :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, and :ref:`X_3102 <X_3102>` is the closer to :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`.
