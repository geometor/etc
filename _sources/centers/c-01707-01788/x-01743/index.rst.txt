.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the center of the conic through the contact points of the B- and :g:`C <C>`-excircles with the sidelines of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to the excentral triangle at :ref:`X_1743 <X_1743>`. See also :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`, :ref:`X_25 <X_25>`, :ref:`X_218 <X_218>`, :ref:`X_222 <X_222>`, :ref:`X_940 <X_940>`. (Randy Hutson, July 23, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let a' be the external bisector of :g:`A <A>` and a" its reflection in :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`; define b" and c" cyclically. Then :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and the triangle bounded by a", b" and c" are perspective with perspector X_15446. (:person:`César Lozada <cesar-lozada>`, October 10, 2018)
