X_494
=====

.. rubric:: 2nd VAN LAMOEN HOMOTHETIC CENTER

.. enunciation::

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_494 <X_494>` is a homothetic center of triangles associated with squares that circumscribe :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. For details and reference, see :ref:`X_493 <X_493>`. (:person:`Floor van Lamoen <floor-van-lamoen>`, 4/27/98)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the intersection of line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` and the common tangent to the B- and C-Lucas(-1) circles (at their touchpoint). Define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :ref:`X_494 <X_494>`. Also, :ref:`X_494 <X_494>` is the point of intersection of the tangents at :ref:`X_372 <X_372>` and :ref:`X_486 <X_486>` to the orthocubic K006. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 23, 2015)
