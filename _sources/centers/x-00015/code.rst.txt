X(15) = 1st ISODYNAMIC POINT — Code
===================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`U <U>` and V be the points on sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` met by the interior and exterior bisectors of angle A. The circle having diameter :g:`UV <[ U V ]>` is the A-Apollonian circle. The B- and C- Apollonian circles are similarly constructed. Each circle passes through one vertex and both isodynamic points. (This configuration, based on the :term:`cevians <cevian>` of :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, generalizes to arbitrary cevians; see :cite:`TCCT <tcct>`, p. 98, problem 8.)
