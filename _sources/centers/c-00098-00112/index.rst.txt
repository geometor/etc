X_98–X_112
==========

.. rubric:: Centers on the Circumcircle

.. enunciation::

   Notable points on the circumcircle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, including the Tarry point, Steiner point, anticomplementary, Feuerbach, Kiepert, and Jerabek antipodes, and centers derived from circumcircle mappings.

.. figure:: c-00098-00112.svg
   :alt: X_98–X_112 — Centers on the Circumcircle
   :align: center

   **X_98–X_112** (*Centers on the Circumcircle*) — 15 centers constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0002:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers on the circumcircle: X_74, X_98-X_112
   :class: note

   Centers :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`, :ref:`X_98 <X_98>`-:ref:`X_112 <X_112>`, and many others in ETC lie on the circumcircle. Mappings **Λ** and **Ψ** derived from such a point :g:`P <P>` for application to points :g:`X <X>`, are defined here:

   Λ(P,X) = :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of the point where line :g:`PX <[ P X ]>` meets the line at infinity.

   | Let Y = Λ(P,X), let :g:`Q <Q>` = isogonal conjugate of :g:`P <P>`, and let Y and Z be the points where line YQ meets the circumcircle;
   | then **Ψ(P,X) = Z.**
