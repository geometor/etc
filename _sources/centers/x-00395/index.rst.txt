.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`F <F>` = :ref:`X_14 <X_14>`, the 2nd Fermat point. Let :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` be the point on :g:`CA <[ C A ]>` and :g:`A_{c} <A_c>` the point on :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` such that FAbAc is an equilateral triangle. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the midpoint of FAbAc, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`, here named the **8th Fermat-Dao equilateral triangle**, is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The circumcircle of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is the pedal circle of :ref:`X_14 <X_14>` and (X16), and the centroid of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is :ref:`X_395 <X_395>`. The perspector of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`, and the centroid of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is :ref:`X_395 <X_395>`. See X_16247, X_16267, and X_16459. (Based on notes from Dao Thanh Dao, March 15, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_395 <X_395>` is the pole of the Euler line wrt every conic passing through :ref:`X_14 <X_14>`, :ref:`X_16 <X_16>`, :ref:`X_18 <X_18>` and :ref:`X_62 <X_62>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, January 17, 2020)
