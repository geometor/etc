X_190
=====

.. rubric:: YFF PARABOLIC POINT

.. enunciation::

   In unpublished notes, Yff has studied the parabola tangent to sidelines :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` and having focus :ref:`X_101 <X_101>`. If :g:`A' <A'>`,\ :g:`B' <B'>`,\ :g:`C' <C'>` are the respective points of tangency, then the lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_190 <X_190>`.

.. container:: note-item

   The line :ref:`X_100 <X_100>`\ \ :ref:`X_190 <X_190>` is tangent to the Steiner circumellipse at :ref:`X_190 <X_190>` and to the circumcircle at :ref:`X_100 <X_100>`. (:person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, July 7, 2009)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`H_{a} <H_a>` be the hyperbola passing through :g:`A <A>`, and with foci at :g:`B <B>` and C. This is called the A-Soddy hyperbola in (:person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>`, *Introduction to the Geometry of the Triangle,* 2002; 12.4 The Soddy hyperbolas, p. 143). Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the polar of :ref:`X_8 <X_8>` with respect to :g:`H_{a} <H_a>`. Define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_190 <X_190>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 26, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`I_{a} <I_a>` be the reflection of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` in the perpendicular bisector of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`I_{b} <I_b>` and :g:`I_{c} <I_c>` cyclically. :ref:`X_190 <X_190>` = :ref:`X_238 <X_238>` of :g:`I_{a}I_{b}I_{c} << I_a I_b I_c >>`. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A_{5}B_{5}C_{5} << A_5 B_5 C_5 >>` and :g:`A_{6}B_{6}C_{6} << A_6 B_6 C_6 >>` be Gemini triangles 5 and 6, resp. Let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the tangent at :g:`A <A>` to conic {A,\ :g:`B_{5} <B_5>`,\ :g:`C_{5} <C_5>`,\ :g:`B_{6} <B_6>`,\ :g:`C_{6} <C_6>`}}, and define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_190 <X_190>`. (Randy Hutson, January 15, 2019)
