X_181
=====

.. rubric:: APOLLONIUS POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let O(A),O(B),O(C) be the excircles. Apollonius's Problem includes the construction of the circle :g:`O <O>` tangent to the three excircles and encompassing them. (The circle is called the Apollonius circle.) Let :g:`A' <A'>` = O∩O(A), :g:`B' <B'>`\ =O∩O(B), :g:`C' <C'>`\ =O∩O(C). The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`,\ :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`,\ :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_181 <X_181>`. Yff derived trilinears in 1992.

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_181 <X_181>` is the external center of similitude (or exsimilicenter) of the incircle and Apollonius circle. The internal center is :ref:`X_1682 <X_1682>`. (Peter J. C. Moses, 8/22/2003)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_181 <X_181>` is the isogonal conjugate of the :term:`isotomic conjugate <isotomic-conjugate>` of :ref:`X_12 <X_12>`; also, :ref:`X_181 <X_181>` is the {X_i ,X_j }-harmonic conjugate of X_k for these (i,j,k)): (31,51,3271), (42,1400,1402), (57,1401,1357), (57,1469,1401). (Peter J. C. Moses, 6/20/2014)

.. container:: note-item

   A proof of the the concurrence of lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`,\ :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`,\ :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` follows.

   :g:`A <A>` = exsimilicenter(incircle, :g:`A <A>`-excircle)

   :g:`A' <A'>` = exsimilicenter(:g:`A <A>`-excircle, Apollonius circle)

   Let J = exsimilicenter(incircle, Apollonius circle).

   By Monge's theorem, the points :g:`A <A>`, :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`J <J>` are collinear. In particular, J lies on line :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, and cyclically, J lies on lines :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>` and :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>`. Therefore, J = :ref:`X_181 <X_181>`. (:person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>`, :cite:`Hyacinthos <hyacinthos>`, 7461, 8/10/03)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the inverse-in-excircles-radical-circle of the midpoint of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is inscribed in the Apollonius circle and homothetic to the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>` at :ref:`X_181 <X_181>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, March 21, 2019)

   See also **Clark Kimberling, Shiko Iwata, and Hidetosi Fukagawa,** Problem 1091 and Solution, *Crux Mathematicorum* 13 (1987) 128-129; 217-218. [proposed 1985].
