X_176
=====

.. rubric:: EQUAL DETOUR POINT

.. enunciation::

   A point :g:`X <X>` is defined as a point of equal detour of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` if \|\ :g:`XB <[ X B ]>`\ \| + \|\ :g:`XC <[ X C ]>`\ \| - \|\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ \| = \|\ :g:`XC <[ X C ]>`\ \| + \|\ :g:`XA <[ X A ]>`\ \| - \|\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`\ \| = \|\ :g:`XA <[ X A ]>`\ \| + \|\ :g:`XB <[ X B ]>`\ \| - \|\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`\ \|. Veldkamp established that :g:`X <X>` = :ref:`X_176 <X_176>` for some triangles :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, but the conditions he gives are not correct. Hajja and Yff proved that the condition tan(A/2) + tan(B/2) + tan(C/2) < 2 is necessary and sufficient for the existence of exactly two points of equal detour and that the condition tan(A/2) + tan(B/2) + tan(C/2) = 2 is necessary and sufficient for the existence of exactly one point of equal detour. Yff found that :ref:`X_176 <X_176>` is also is the center of the inner Soddy circle. See also :ref:`X_175 <X_175>` and the 1st and 2nd Eppstein points, :ref:`X_481 <X_481>`, :ref:`X_482 <X_482>`.

Notes

.. container:: note-item

   The points :ref:`X_175 <X_175>` and :ref:`X_176 <X_176>` are discussed in an 1890 article by :person:`Emile Lemoine <emile-lemoine>`, accessible at `Gallica <http://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k201173h>`__. The article begins on page 111, and the two points are considered beginning on page 128.

.. container:: note-item

   The following construction was found by Elkies: call two circles within :g:`ABC << A B C >>` *companion circles* if they are the incircles of two triangles formed by dividing :g:`ABC << A B C >>` into two smaller triangles by passing a line through one of the vertices and some point on the opposite side; chain of circles O(1), O(2), ... such that O(n),O(n+1) are companion incircles for every n consists of at most six distinct circles; there is a unique chain consisting of only three distinct circles; and for this chain, the three subdividing lines concur in :ref:`X_176 <X_176>`.

.. container:: note-item

   **G. R. Veldkamp**, "The isoperimetric point and the point(s) of equal detour," *American Mathematical Monthly* 92 (1985) 546-558.

.. container:: note-item

   **Noam D. Elkies and Jiro Fukuta**, Problem E 3236 and Solution, *American Mathematical Monthly* 97 (1990) 529-531 [proposed 1987].

.. container:: note-item

   Mowaffaq Hajja and :person:`Peter Yff <peter-yff>`, "The isoperimetric point and the point(s) of equal detour in a triangle," *Journal of Geometry* 87 (2007) 76-82.
