   Let :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` be the ellipse with :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>` as foci and passing through :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, and define :g:`E_{b} <E_b>` and :g:`E_{c} <E_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the line tangent to :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` at :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`. Then :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` are collinear, and :ref:`X_174 <X_174>` = :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. The line :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` meets the line at infinity at the isogonal conjugate of X_3659. Alternately, let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the trilinear pole of line :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>` and :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur at :ref:`X_174 <X_174>`; see also :ref:`X_188 <X_188>`.    After proving that the answer is yes, Yff moves the three isoscelizers in such a way that the three outer triangles, stay congruent and the inner triangle (called the Yff central triangle), :math:`R_{A}`\ :math:`R_{B}`\ :math:`R_{C}`, shrinks to :ref:`X_174 <X_174>`.
