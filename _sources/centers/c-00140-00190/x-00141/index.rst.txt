Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`P <P>` be a point on the circumcircle, and let :g:`L <L>` be the line tangent to the circumcircle at P. Let :g:`P' <P'>` be the trilinear pole of :g:`L <L>`, and let :g:`P″ <P″>` be the isotomic conjugate of :g:`P' <P'>`. As P traces the circumcircle, :g:`P″ <P″>` traces an ellipse inscribed in :g:`ABC << A B C >>` with center at :ref:`X_141 <X_141>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 26, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 2nd Brocard triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the :term:`cevapoint <cevapoint>` of :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_141 <X_141>`. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)
