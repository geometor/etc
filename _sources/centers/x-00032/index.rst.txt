Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   The 5th Brocard triangle is here introduced as the vertex triangle of the circumcevian triangles of the 1st and 2nd Brocard points. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 26, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   The 5th Brocard triangle is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_32 <X_32>`, homothetic to the medial triangle at X_3096, homothetic to the :term:`anticomplementary triangle <anticomplementary-triangle>` at X_2896, perspective to the 1st Brocard triangle at X_2896, and perspective to the 3rd Brocard triangle at :ref:`X_32 <X_32>`.(Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 1st Brocard triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>`, :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` be inverse-in-circumcircle of :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` resp. :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_32 <X_32>`. (Randy Hutson, July 20, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 1st Brocard triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the :term:`cevapoint <cevapoint>`, wrt :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`, of :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. :g:`A'A″ <[ A' A″ ]>`, :g:`B'B″ <[ B' B″ ]>`, :g:`C'C″ <[ C' C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_32 <X_32>`. (Randy Hutson, July 20, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_32 <X_32>` is the Brianchon point (perspector) of the inellipse that is the barycentric square of the Lemoine axis. The center of this inellipse is X_8265. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_32 <X_32>` is the {:ref:`X_3 <X_3>`,\ :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`}-harmonic conjugate of :ref:`X_39 <X_39>`.
