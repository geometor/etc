X(314) = ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF X(65) — Code
===========================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`D <D>` and E be the intersections of line :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`\ \ :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>` with lines PU(3) and PU(6), respectively. Let :g:`D' <D'>` and :g:`E' <E'>` be the :term:`isogonal conjugates <isogonal-conjugate>` of :g:`D <D>` and E, respectively. Let :g:`D″ <D″>` and :g:`E″ <E″>` be the :term:`isotomic conjugates <isotomic-conjugate>` of :g:`D <D>` and E, respectively. Then :ref:`X(314) <X(314)>` = :g:`D'D″ <[ D' D″ ]>` ∩\ :g:`E'E″ <[ E' E″ ]>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 26, 2015)
