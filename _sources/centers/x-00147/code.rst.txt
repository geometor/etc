X(147) = TARRY POINT OF ANTICOMPLEMENTARY TRIANGLE — Code
=========================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`T <T>` be the :g:`P <P>`-Brocard triangle for any P on the Brocard axis (including the 1st Brocard triangle, for :g:`P <P>` = :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>`). Let :g:`O_{A} <O_A>` be the circle centered at the :g:`A <A>`-vertex of :g:`T <T>` and passing through :g:`A <A>`; define :g:`O_{B} <O_B>` and :g:`O_{C} <O_C>` cyclically. :ref:`X(147) <X(147)>` is the radical center of :g:`O_{A} <O_A>`, :g:`O_{B} <O_B>`, :g:`O_{C} <O_C>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, August 30, 2020)
