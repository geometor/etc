X_1166–X_1208
=============

.. rubric:: Saragossa Points

.. enunciation::

   1st, 2nd, and 3rd Saragossa points (Q, :g:`Q' <Q'>`, :g:`Q″ <Q″>`) constructed from the :term:`cevian <cevian>` and circumcevian triangles of classical centers.

.. figure:: c-01166-01208.svg
   :alt: X_1166–X_1208 — Saragossa Points
   :align: center

   **X_1166–X_1208** (*Saragossa Points*) — 12 representative centers (from 43 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0017:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Saragossa Points 1166-1208 (Saragossa points (1st, 2nd, 3rd))
   :class: note

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of a point :g:`P <P>`, and let :g:`A″ <A″>`, :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` be the respective intersections of lines :g:`PA <[ P A ]>`, :g:`PB <[ P B ]>`, :g:`PC <[ P C ]>` with the circumcircle of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let

   :g:`U <U>` = :g:`B'C″ <[ B' C″ ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`B″C' <[ B″ C' ]>` V = :g:`C'A″ <[ C' A″ ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`C″A' <[ C″ A' ]>` W = :g:`A'B″ <[ A' B″ ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`A″B' <[ A″ B' ]>`.

   | Lines :g:`AU <[ A U ]>`, :g:`BV <[ B V ]>`, CW concur in the 1st Saragossa point of :g:`P <P>`;
   | lines :g:`A'U <[ A' U ]>`, :g:`B'V <[ B' V ]>`, :g:`C'W <[ C' W ]>` concur in the 2nd Saragossa point of :g:`P <P>`;
   | lines :g:`A″U <[ A″ U ]>`, :g:`B″V <[ B″ V ]>`, :g:`C″W <[ C″ W ]>` concur in the 3rd Saragossa point of :g:`P <P>`.

   | These concurrences were presented by :person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>` (:cite:`Hyacinthos #6531 <hyacinthos>`, February 14, 2003),
   | with coordinates as follows. Let :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` (trilinears), and abbreviate the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Saragossa points
   | as Q, :g:`Q' <Q'>`, :g:`Q″ <Q″>`, respectively; then first trilinears are

   | for :g:`Q <Q>`: :math:`f(a,b,c) = a/[x(bz + cy)]`,
   | for :g:`Q' <Q'>`: :math:`f(a,b,c) = ax[(b^{2}z^{2} + c^{2}y^{2})x + xyzbc + ayz(bz + cy)]`,
   | for :g:`Q″ <Q″>`: :math:`f(a,b,c) = ax[(b^{2}z^{2} + c^{2}y^{2})x + ayz(bz + cy)]`.

   The name *Saragossa* refers to the king who proved Ceva's theorem before Ceva did. See

   **J. B. Hogendijk,** "Al-Mu'taman ibn Hud [bar over u], 11th century king of Saragossa and brilliant mathematician," *Historia Mathematica,* 22 (1995) 1-18.

   The points :g:`P <P>`, :g:`Q' <Q'>`, :g:`Q″ <Q″>` are collinear.

   | The 1st Saragossa point of X_i is X_j for these (i,j):
   | (1,58) (2,251) (3,4) (4,54) (6,6) (19,284) (21,961) (24,847) (25,2) (28,943) (31,81)
   | (32,83) (51,288) (55,57) (56,1) (58,1126) (64,3) (84,947) (154,1073) (184,275)
   | (198,282) (512,249) (513,59) (667,1016) (939,937) (1036,959).

   | The 2nd Saragossa point of X_i is X_j for these (i,j): (1,386) (6,6)
   | The 3rd Saragossa point of X_i is X_j for these (i,j): (3,185) (4,389) (6,6)
