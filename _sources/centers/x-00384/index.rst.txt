.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 1st Brocard triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the cevapoint of :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_384 <X_384>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 11, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the line through the 2nd Brocard circle intercepts (other than PU(1)) of lines AP(1) and AU(1); define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`. The triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is here introduced as the 6th Brocard triangle; :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to the 1st Brocard triangle at :ref:`X_384 <X_384>`, homothetic to the 1st anti-Brocard triangle at :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`, and perspective to the 3rd Brocard triangle at :ref:`X_384 <X_384>`. The 2nd Brocard circle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is the 1st Lemoine circle of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)
