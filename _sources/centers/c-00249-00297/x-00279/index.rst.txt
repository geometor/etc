Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_279 <X_279>` is the Brianchon point (perspector) of the inellipse that is the barycentric square of the Gergonne line. The center of the inellipse is :ref:`X_4000 <X_4000>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, October 15, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A_{1}B_{1}C_{1} << A_1 B_1 C_1 >>` and :g:`A_{2}B_{2}C_{2} << A_2 B_2 C_2 >>` be the 1st and 2nd Conway triangles. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the barycentric product :g:`A_{1} <A_1>`\ \*\ :g:`A_{2} <A_2>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_279 <X_279>`. (Randy Hutson, July 11, 2019)

.. container:: note-item

   An **exarc circle** is a circle tangent to two sides of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and externally tangent to the circumcircle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The point whose distance to the sides :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` are proportional to the respective radii of the exarc circles is :ref:`X_279 <X_279>`. The point having distances to the sides proportional to the radii of the inarc circles is :ref:`X_7 <X_7>`. See **Martin Lukarevski**, "Exarc radii and the Finsler-Hadwiger inequality", *The Mathematical Gazette* 106, issue 565, March 2022, pp. 138-143.
