Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Let P(2) and U(2) be the 1st and 2nd Beltrami points (as indexed at Bicentric Pairs, accessible using the Tables button at the top of ETC), and let P(40) and U(40) be the isogonal conjugates of P(2) and U(2), respectively. Then :ref:`X_3569 <X_3569>` is the point of intersection of the lines P(2)U(2) and P(40)U(40). The name "Beltrami-Parry Point" signifies the fact that :ref:`X_3569 <X_3569>` lies on the line :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`\ \ :ref:`X_111 <X_111>`; where :ref:`X_111 <X_111>` is the Parry point.and :ref:`X_74 <X_74>` is the isogonal conjugate of the Euler infinity point. See also :ref:`X_3568 <X_3568>`. Contributed by Chris van Tienhoven, June 7, 2009..
