X_3590
======

.. rubric:: 1st DIXIT POINT

.. enunciation::

   The radius of circle :g:`T_{A} <T_A>` is \|\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ \|/6, and the Kiepert angle (e.g., B-to-C-to-:ref:`X_3890 <X_3890>`) is arctan(2/3).

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   On Dec. 22, 2000, Atul Dixit constructed this point in :cite:`Hyacinthos <hyacinthos>` message #2183, as follows. Let :g:`ABC << A B C >>` be a triangle with medians :g:`AD <[ A D ]>`, :g:`BE <[ B E ]>`, :g:`FC <[ F C ]>` and centroid G. Construct semicircles with diameters :g:`BD <[ B D ]>`, :g:`DC <[ D C ]>`, :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` outwardly. Let :g:`T_{A} <T_A>` be the circle tangent to the three semicircles :g:`BD <[ B D ]>`, :g:`DC <[ D C ]>`, :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and let :g:`A' <A'>` be the center of :g:`T_{A} <T_A>`. Define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then the lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3590 <X_3590>`. Barycentric coordinates were found by :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>` (2000), and further properties and related points, by :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>` (2010).
