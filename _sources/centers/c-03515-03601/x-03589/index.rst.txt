.. container:: note-item

   In 2003, :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>` gave a general construction as follows: Suppose that :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`u : v : w` (barycentrics). The centroids of the triangles :g:`BCP << B C P >>`, :g:`CAP << C A P >>`, :g:`ABP << A B P >>` form a triangle homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, with ratio -1/3 and center

.. container:: note-item

   In 2010, Seiichi Kirikami gave another construction for :g:`P' <P'>`: let

   :g:`D <D>` = :g:`AP <[ A P ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`E <E>` = :g:`BP <[ B P ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`F <F>` = :g:`CP <[ C P ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Then the Newton lines of the quadrilaterals PEAF, PFBD, PDCE concur in :g:`P' <P'>`. (The Newton line of a quadrilateral is the line of the midpoints of the two diagonals of the quadrilateral.)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`E <E>` be the bicevian conic of :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` and :ref:`X_6 <X_6>`; i.e., the ellipse that passes through the vertices of the medial and symmedial triangles. Let :g:`L <L>` be a line through :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`, and let :g:`P <P>` and :g:`P' <P'>` be the points of intersection of :g:`L <L>` and the circumcircle.
