=====

.. rubric:: HARMONIC OF X_187

.. enunciation::

   "Each one of the three circles of Apollonius of the reference triangle cuts the circumcircle in a second point. These three points form a triangle which is perspective with the base triangle through the symmedian point, the co-symmedian triangle. This triangle also possesses a Neuberg cubic, which has three points in common with the cubic of the first triangle in the circumcenter and the two isodynamic centers. Hence the remaining points of intersection must lie on a conic, which is necessarily a circle, since two of these six points are the circular points :g:`I <I>` and J." Quoted from (`T. W. Moore and J. H. Neelley <https://www.jstor.org/stable/pdf/2299192.pdf>`__). The center of the circle is :ref:`X_574 <X_574>`. (:person:`Bernard Gibert <bernard-gibert>`, `euclid 4332 <https://groups.io/g/euclid/message/4332>`__)
