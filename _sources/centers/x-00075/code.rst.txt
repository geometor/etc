.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A_{2}B_{2}C_{2} << A_2 B_2 C_2 >>` be the 2nd Conway triangle. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line :g:`B_{2}C_{2} <[ B_2 C_2 ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X(75) <X(75)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)
