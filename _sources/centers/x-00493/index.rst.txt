Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_493 <X_493>` is the homothetic center of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and the Lucas homothetic triangle; see :ref:`X_371 <X_371>`. Writing t for the ratio L:W at :ref:`X_371 <X_371>`, let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the line through the intersections, other than A, of the A-Lucas(t) circle and sides :g:`CA <[ C A ]>` and :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`. Define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then the triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`, here introduced as the Lucas(t) homothetic triangle, is homothetic to triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. If t = 1, the center of homothety is :ref:`X_493 <X_493>`; for t = -1, it is :ref:`X_494 <X_494>`; for t = 2, it is :ref:`X_588 <X_588>`; and for t = -2, it is :ref:`X_589 <X_589>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, February 9, 2013)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the intersection of line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` and the common tangent to the B- and C-Lucas circles (at their touchpoint). Define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :ref:`X_493 <X_493>`. Also, :ref:`X_493 <X_493>` is the point of intersection of the tangents at :ref:`X_371 <X_371>` and :ref:`X_485 <X_485>` to the orthocubic K006. (Randy Hutson, July 23, 2015)
