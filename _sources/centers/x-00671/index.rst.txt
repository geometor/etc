.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>`-Fuhrmann triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the reflection of :g:`A <A>` in :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is inversely similar to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, with similitude center :ref:`X(671) <X(671)>`. (Randy Hutson, November 30, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`P_{a} <P_a>` be the perspector of the A-Neuberg circle, and define :g:`P_{b} <P_b>` and :g:`P_{c} <P_c>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AP_{a} <[ A P_a ]>`, :g:`BP_{b} <[ B P_b ]>`, :g:`CP_{c} <[ C P_c ]>` concur in :ref:`X(671) <X(671)>`. (Randy Hutson, November 30, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the reflection in :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` of the :g:`A <A>`-vertex of the :term:`antipedal triangle <antipedal-triangle>` of :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The circumcircles of :g:`A'BC << A' B C >>`, :g:`B'CA << B' C A >>`, and :g:`C'AB << C' A B >>` concur at :ref:`X(671) <X(671)>`. (Randy Hutson, November 30, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 1st Brocard triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the reflection of :g:`A <A>` in line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Let A''' be the reflection of :g:`A' <A'>` in line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define B''' and C''' cyclically. Let :g:`A* <A*>` = B″B'''∩C″C''', and define B\*and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA* <[ A A* ]>`, :g:`BB* <[ B B* ]>`, :g:`CC* <[ C C* ]>` concur in :ref:`X(671) <X(671)>`, which is also :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>`-of-:g:`A*B*C* << A* B* C* >>`. (Randy Hutson, July 11, 2019)
