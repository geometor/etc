X(83) = CEVAPOINT OF CENTROID AND SYMMEDIAN POINT — Code
========================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`K <K>` denote the symmedian point, :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>`. Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of K. Let :g:`K_{A} <K_A>` be K of the triangle :g:`AB'C' << A B' C' >>`; let :g:`K_{B} <K_B>` be K of :g:`A'BC' << A' B C' >>` and let :g:`K_{C} <K_C>` be K of :g:`A'B'C << A' B' C >>`. The lines :g:`AK_{A} <[ A K_A ]>`, :g:`BK_{B} <[ B K_B ]>`, :g:`CK_{C} <[ C K_C ]>` concur in :ref:`X(83) <X(83)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 9/23/2011)
