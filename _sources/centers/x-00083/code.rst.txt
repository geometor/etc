X(83) = CEVAPOINT OF CENTROID AND SYMMEDIAN POINT — Code
========================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let K denote the symmedian point, :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>`. Let A'B'C' be the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of K. Let K\ :sub:`A` be K of the triangle AB'C'; let K\ :sub:`B` be K of A'BC' and let K\ :sub:`C` be K of A'B'C. The lines AK\ :sub:`A`, BK\ :sub:`B`, CK\ :sub:`C` concur in :ref:`X(83) <X(83)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 9/23/2011)
