X_355
=====

.. rubric:: FUHRMANN CENTER

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_355 <X_355>` = the center of the Fuhrmann circle, defined as the circumcircle of the :term:`Fuhrmann triangle <fuhrmann-triangle>` :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>`, where :g:`A″ <A″>` is obtained as follows: let :g:`A' <A'>` be the midpoint of the circumcircle-arc having endpoints :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>` and not containing A; then :g:`A″ <A″>` is the reflection of :g:`A' <A'>` in line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`. Vertices :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` are obtained cyclically. (Other constructions of :g:`A″ <A″>`, and hence the Fuhrmann triangle, follow: (1) Let :g:`I_{A} <I_A>` be the reflection of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` in :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`; then :g:`A″ <A″>` is the circumcenter of :g:`I_{A}BC << I_A B C >>`. (2) Let :g:`J_{A} <J_A>` be the reflection of the :g:`A <A>`-excenter in :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`; then :g:`A″ <A″>` is the circumcenter of :g:`J_{A}BC << J_A B C >>`.
