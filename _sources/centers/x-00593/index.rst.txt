X_593
=====

.. rubric:: 1st HATZIPOLAKIS-YIU POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let O(A) be the circle tangent to line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` and to the circumcircle of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at vertex :g:`A <A>`. Let :g:`A_{B} <A_B>` and :g:`A_{C} <A_C>` be where O(A) meets lines :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` and :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`, respectively. Let L(A) be the line joining :g:`A_{B} <A_B>` and :g:`A_{C} <A_C>`, and define L(B) and L(C) cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be where L(B) and L(C) meet, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is homothetic to triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_593 <X_593>`. See :person:`Antreas Hatzipolakis <antreas-hatzipolakis>`, :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>`, :cite:`Hyacinthos #2070 <hyacinthos>`.
