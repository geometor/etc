X(185) = NAGEL POINT OF THE ORTHIC TRIANGLE
===========================================

.. enunciation::

   Alexei Myakishev has noted that :ref:`X(185) <X(185)>` is the Nagel point of the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>` only is :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is an acute triangle.

.. container:: note-item
