X(113) = JERABEK ANTIPODE
=========================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`. Let :g:`L <L>` be the line through :g:`A' <A'>` parallel to the Euler line, and define M and N cyclically. Let :g:`L' <L'>` be the reflection of :g:`L <L>` in sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`M' <M'>` and :g:`N' <N'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`L' <L'>`, :g:`M' <M'>`, :g:`N' <N'>` concur in :ref:`X(113) <X(113)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, August 26, 2014)

.. _preamble-0003:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers X(113)-X(139)
   :class: note

   Centers :ref:`X(113) <X(113)>`-:ref:`X(139) <X(139)>` lie on the nine-point circle.

   Suppose that X is a point on the nine-point circle, and let :g:`X' <X'>` be the reflection of :g:`X <X>` in the orthocenter, H. Then X is the anticenter of the cyclic quadrilateral ABCX'. Let :g:`H_{A} <H_A>` be the orthocenter of triangle :g:`BCX << B C X >>`, Let :g:`H_{B} <H_B>` be the orthocenter of :g:`CAX << C A X >>`, and let :g:`H_{C} <H_C>` be the orthocenter of triangle :g:`ABX << A B X >>`. Then the quadrilateral :math:`HH_{A}`\ :g:`H_{B}H_{C} <[ H_B H_C ]>` is homothetic to and congruent to the cyclic quadrilateral ABCX', and X is the center of homothety. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)
