X(20) = DE LONGCHAMPS POINT — Code
==================================

.. enunciation::

   Let La be the polar of :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>` wrt the circle centered at A and passing through :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>`, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. (Note: :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>` is the perspector of any circle centered at a vertex of ABC.) Let A″ = Lb∩\Lc, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. Triangle A″B″C″ is homothetic to the :term:`anticomplementary triangle <anticomplementary-triangle>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X(20) <X(20)>`, which is also the orthocenter of A″B″C″. Also, let La be the line through the intersections of the B- and C-Soddy ellipses, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Then La,Lb,Lc concur in :ref:`X(20) <X(20)>`. Also, let A'B'C' be the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :ref:`X(253) <X(253)>`. Let A″ be the orthocenter of AB'C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in :ref:`X(20) <X(20)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 18, 2015)
