X(274) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(213) — Code
============================================

.. enunciation::

   Let Ab, Ac, Bc, Ba, Ca, Cb be as in the construction of the Conway circle; see http://mathworld.wolfram.com/ConwayCircle.html. Let Oa be the circumcircle of ABaCa, and define Ob and Oc cyclically. Then :ref:`X(274) <X(274)>` is the radical center of Oa, Ob, Oc. see also :ref:`X(8) <X(8)>` and :ref:`X(21) <X(21)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, April 9, 2016)
