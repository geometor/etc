.. rubric:: REFLECTION OF X_64 IN X_3

.. enunciation::

   Let (:g:`B_{a} <B_a>`, :g:`C_{a} <C_a>`) be the points where :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` is cut by the parallels through :g:`A <A>` to the b- and c- altitudes, respectively, and define (:g:`C_{b} <C_b>`, :g:`A_{b} <A_b>`), (:g:`A_{c} <A_c>`, :g:`B_{c} <B_c>`) cyclically. Then the perpendicular bisectors of (:g:`B_{a} <B_a>`, :g:`C_{a} <C_a>`), (:g:`C_{b} <C_b>`, :g:`A_{c} <A_c>`) and (:g:`A_{c} <A_c>`, :g:`B_{c} <B_c>`) concur at :ref:`X_1498 <X_1498>`.
