Notes

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_1490 <X_1490>` is :ref:`X_68 <X_68>`-of-the-excentral triangle and the reflection of :ref:`X_84 <X_84>` in :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`. These and other properties itemized here were reported by :person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>`, May 19, 2003.

.. container:: note-item

   Let U(A) be the circle with center A having the radius of the :g:`A <A>`-excircle, and define U(B) and U(C) cyclically. Then :ref:`X_1490 <X_1490>` is the radical center of the three circles. (Hauke Reddmann, :cite:`Hyacinthos <hyacinthos>`, Jan. 8, 2009)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`O_{a} <O_a>` be the circle centered at :g:`A <A>` and passing through the :g:`A <A>`-excenter, and define :g:`O_{b} <O_b>` and :g:`O_{c} <O_c>` cyclically. The radical center of :g:`O_{a} <O_a>`, :g:`O_{b} <O_b>`, :g:`O_{c} <O_c>` = :ref:`X_1490 <X_1490>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, September 14, 2016)
