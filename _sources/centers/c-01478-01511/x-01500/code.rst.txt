X_1500
======

.. rubric:: INSIMILICENTER OF MOSES CIRCLE AND INCIRCLE

.. enunciation::

   The circle having center :ref:`X_39 <X_39>` and radius R tan ω sin 2ω, where R denotes the circumradius of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, is here introduced as the Moses circle. It is tangent to the nine-point circle at :ref:`X_115 <X_115>`, and its internal and external centers of similitude with the incircle are :ref:`X_1500 <X_1500>` and :ref:`X_1015 <X_1015>`, respectively. (Peter J. C. Moses, 5/29/03)
