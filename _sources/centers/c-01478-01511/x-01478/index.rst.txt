X_1478
======

.. rubric:: CENTER OF JOHNSON-YFF CIRCLE

.. enunciation::

   The Johnson Circle Theorem is the fact that if three congruent circles intersect in a point, then the circle passing through the other three intersections is congruent to them. This fourth circle is the Johnson circle of the three given circles. There are three congruent circles each tangent to two sides of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. :person:`Peter Yff <peter-yff>` proved that their Johnson circle has center :ref:`X_1478 <X_1478>`. The circle is here named the Johnson-Yff circle of the triangle.
