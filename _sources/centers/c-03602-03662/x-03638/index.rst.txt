Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let :g:`X <X>`, :g:`Y <Y>`, :g:`Z <Z>` be the points of contact of the incircle with sidelines :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`,\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`,\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, and let :g:`X' <X'>`,\ :g:`Y' <Y'>`,\ :g:`Z' <Z'>` be the harmonic conjugates of :g:`X <X>`, :g:`Y <Y>`, :g:`Z <Z>` with respect to {B,C}, {C,A}, {A,B}, respectively. It is well known that :g:`AX <[ A X ]>`, :g:`BY <[ B Y ]>`, :g:`CZ <[ C Z ]>` concur in the Gergonne point, :ref:`X_7 <X_7>`, and that :g:`X' <X'>`,\ :g:`Y' <Y'>`,\ :g:`Z' <Z'>` lie on the Gergonne line. The circles with centers :g:`X' <X'>`,\ :g:`Y' <Y'>`,\ :g:`Z' <Z'>` passing respectively through :g:`X <X>`, :g:`Y <Y>`, :g:`Z <Z>` are orthogonal to the incircle and to the Gergonne line, forming a coaxal system with the Gergonne line as line of centers. The radical axis is the Soddy line, which passes through :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` and :ref:`X_7 <X_7>` and is the line of centers of the orthogonal coaxal system, which includes the Soddy circles. Among the points on this line are the two limiting points (point-circles of the system): :ref:`X_3638 <X_3638>` and :ref:`X_3639 <X_3639>`. The former lies between :ref:`X_7 <X_7>` and the inner Soddy point, :ref:`X_175 <X_175>`. (Richard Guy, September 2, 2011)
