.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`ABC << A B C >>` be a triangle and Γ\ :sub:`a` a variable circle passing through :g:`A <A>` and the midpoint, :g:`M_{a} <M_a>`, of side :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`. Let :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` and :g:`A_{c} <A_c>` be the points, other than A and :g:`M_{a} <M_a>`, in which the circle Γ\ :sub:`a` meets :g:`AC <[ A C ]>` and :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, respectively. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the vertex of parallelogram with vertices :g:`A <A>`, :g:`A_{b} <A_b>`, :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`A_{c} <A_c>`. When Γ\ :sub:`a` varies, the locus of :g:`A' <A'>` is a line :g:`ℓ_{a} <ℓ_a>`. Define :g:`ℓ_{b} <ℓ_b>` and :g:`ℓ_{c} <ℓ_c>` cyclically. The triangle formed by the lines :g:`ℓ_{a} <ℓ_a>`, :g:`ℓ_{b} <ℓ_b>`, :g:`ℓ_{c} <ℓ_c>` is orthologic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` with orthology center :ref:`X_3627 <X_3627>`. The reciprocal orthologic center of these triangles is :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`. See **X(3627)** (``X(3627).png``). (:person:`Angel Montesdeoca <angel-montesdeoca>`, July 13, 2022)
