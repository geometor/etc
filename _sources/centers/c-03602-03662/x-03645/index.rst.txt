X_3645
======

.. rubric:: POINT ALKAID

.. enunciation::

   Denote the incenter and excenters by I, :g:`I_{A} <I_A>`, :g:`I_{B} <I_B>`, :g:`I_{C} <I_C>`. Let :g:`J_{A} <J_A>` be the incenter of triangle :g:`BCI << B C I >>`, and define :g:`J_{B} <J_B>` and :g:`J_{C} <J_C>` cyclically. The lines :g:`I_{A}J_{A} <[ I_A J_A ]>`, :g:`I_{B}J_{B} <[ I_B J_B ]>`, :g:`I_{C}J_{C} <[ I_C J_C ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3645 <X_3645>`.
