Notes

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`I <X_1>` be the incenter and :g:`I_{A} <I_A>` the :g:`A <A>`-excenter of the triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the line joining the circumcenter of triangle :g:`BCI << B C X_1 >>` and the incenter of triangle :g:`BCI_{A} << B C I_A >>`, and define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. The lines :g:`L_{A} <L_A>`, :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`, :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` concur in :ref:`X_3659 <X_3659>`. (Seiichi Kirikami, April 12, 2010)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` be the ellipse with :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>` as foci and passing through :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, and define :g:`E_{b} <E_b>` and :g:`E_{c} <E_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the line tangent to :g:`E_{a} <E_a>` at :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` = :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`B' <B'>` = :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`C' <C'>` = :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`. Then :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` are collinear, and the line :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` meets the line at infinity at the isogonal conjugate of :ref:`X_3659 <X_3659>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`I_{A}I_{B}I_{C} << I_A I_B I_C >>` be the excentral triangle. The lines :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`\ \ :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` of triangles :g:`BCI_{A} << B C I_A >>`, :g:`CAI_{B} << C A I_B >>`, :g:`ABI_{C} << A B I_C >>` concur in :ref:`X_3659 <X_3659>`. (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
