   For any point :g:`P <P>`, let L(A) be the line through :g:`P <P>` parallel to :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`. Let :g:`U <U>` = :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`\ ∩L(A) and V = :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`\ ∩L(A), and let a' = \|\ :g:`UV <[ U V ]>`\ \|. Define b' and c' cyclically. Let :g:`L <L>` be the line consisting of points P such that a + ta' = b + tb' = c + tc'. Then :ref:`X_3644 <X_3644>` is given by t=1. The points corresponding to t = -1, -1/2, and 0, are :ref:`X_75 <X_75>`, :ref:`X_37 <X_37>`, and :ref:`X_192 <X_192>`, respectively. (Seiichi Kirikami, November 7, 2010)
