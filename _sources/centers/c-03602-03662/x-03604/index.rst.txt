Notes

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_3604 <X_3604>` is {:ref:`X_1134 <X_1134>`, :ref:`X_1135 <X_1135>`}-harmonic conjugate of :ref:`X_3277 <X_3277>`. (:person:`Bernard Gibert <bernard-gibert>`, November 3, 2010)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 1st Morley triangle. Let :g:`B_{a} <B_a>` and :g:`C_{a} <C_a>` be points on :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` such that :g:`A'B_{c}C_{a} << A' B_c C_a >>` is an equilateral triangle; define :g:`C_{b} <C_b>` and :g:`A_{c} <A_c>` cyclically, and define :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` and :g:`B_{c} <B_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the midpoint of :g:`A_{b} <A_b>` and :g:`A_{c} <A_c>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>`, here named the **1st MORLEY-MIDPOINT TRIANGLE,** is an equilateral triangle that is homothetic to :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` and perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, with perspector :ref:`X_3604 <X_3604>`. (:person:`Thanh Oai Dao <dao-thanh-oai>` and :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, March 30, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 3rd Morley triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the trilinear pole of line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3604 <X_3604>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 11, 2019)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 3rd Morley triangle. Let :g:`A* <A*>` be the foot of the perpendicular from :g:`A <A>` to line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B* <B*>` and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically. The lines :g:`A'A* <[ A' A* ]>`, :g:`B'B* <[ B' B* ]>`, :g:`C'C* <[ C' C* ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3604 <X_3604>`. (Randy Hutson, July 11, 2019)
