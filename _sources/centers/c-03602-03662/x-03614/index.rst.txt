X_3614
======

.. rubric:: 1st HUTSON-FEUERBACH POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`Eu_aEu_bEu_c << Eu_a Eu_b Eu_c >>` be the :term:`Feuerbach triangle <feuerbach-triangle>`, L the line through :g:`A <A>` and :ref:`X_5 <X_5>`, and :g:`A″ <A″>` = L∩\ :g:`Eu_bEu_c <[ Eu_b Eu_c ]>`; define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then the lines :g:`Eu_aA″ <[ Eu_a A″ ]>`, :g:`Eu_bB″ <[ Eu_b B″ ]>`, :g:`Eu_cC″ <[ Eu_c C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3614 <X_3614>`. :ref:`X_3614 <X_3614>` = {:ref:`X_5 <X_5>`,\ :ref:`X_12 <X_12>`}-harmonic conjugate of :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`. Further, :ref:`X_3614 <X_3614>` is the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` (with respect to the Feuerbach triangle) of the perspectrix of :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and the Feuerbach triangle. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, August 30, 2011.)
