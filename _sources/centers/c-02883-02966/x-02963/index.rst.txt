.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`Q <Q>` be the pedal curve (a limaçon of Pascal) of the circle with center X_7728 and radius \|OH\|, with respect to :ref:`X_265 <X_265>`. Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the triangle formed by the tangents at :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>` to Q. Then the triangles :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` are perspective, and their perspector is :ref:`X_2963 <X_2963>`. Moreover, X_21975 is the only finite fixed point of the affine transformation that maps a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` onto :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. See **X(21975)** (``X(21975).png``). (:person:`Angel Montesdeoca <angel-montesdeoca>`, September 10, 2019)

.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let

      * N= Nine point circle,
      * :g:`N'_{a} <N'_a>`, :g:`A' <A'>` = reflection of :g:`N <N>`, :g:`A <A>` in :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, define :g:`N'_{b} <N'_b>`, :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`N'_{c} <N'_c>`, :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically
      * :g:`P_{a} <P_a>` = polar of :g:`A' <A'>` with respect to :g:`N'_{a} <N'_a>`, and define :g:`P_{b} <P_b>` and :g:`P_{c} <P_c>` cyclically;
      * :g:`A″ <A″>` = :g:`P_{c} <P_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`P_{b} <P_b>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically.

   The triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_2963 <X_2963>`. (:person:`Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna <dasari-naga-vijay-krishna>`, June 8, 2021)
