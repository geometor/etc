X(116) = MIDPOINT OF X(4) AND X(103)
====================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the Soddy line of triangle :g:`AB'C' << A B' C' >>`, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically.
