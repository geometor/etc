Note: the line :ref:`X(23) <X(23)>`\ \ :ref:`X(385) <X(385)>` is the polar of :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>` wrt the circumcircle. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)
