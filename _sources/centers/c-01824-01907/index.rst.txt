X_1824–X_1907
=============

.. rubric:: Zosma Transforms

.. enunciation::

   :term:`Isogonal conjugates <isogonal-conjugate>` of the inverse Mimosa transforms (Z(X)) of classical :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>`.

.. figure:: c-01824-01907.svg
   :alt: X_1824–X_1907 — Zosma Transforms
   :align: center

   **X_1824–X_1907** (*Zosma Transforms*) — 16 representative centers (from 84 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0026:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Zosma Transforms 1824-1907
   :class: note

   | The Zosma transform of a point :g:`X <X>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` is the isogonal conjugate of the inverse Mimosa transform of :g:`X <X>`, given by trilinears
   | **(y + z) sec A : (z + x) sec B : (x + y)sec C.**

   (Zosma is another star name.)
