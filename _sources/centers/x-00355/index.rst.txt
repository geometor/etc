   Let A'B'C' be the :term:`anticomplementary triangle <anticomplementary-triangle>`. Then :ref:`X(355) <X(355)>` is the radical center of the incircles of A'BC, B'CA, C'AB. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   Let A'B'C' be the outer Garcia triangle and A″B″C″ the inner Garcia triangle. Let A\* be the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>`, wrt A'B'C', of A″, and define B\* and C\* cyclically. The lines A'A\*, B'B\*, C'C\* concur in :ref:`X(355) <X(355)>`; see also :ref:`X(952) <X(952)>`. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

.. container:: note-item

   **Ross Honsberger,** *Episodes in Nineteenth and Twentieth Century Euclidean Geometry,* Mathematical Association of America, 1995. Chapter 6: The Fuhrmann Circle.

.. container:: note-item

   Let O\ :sub:`A` be the circle centered at the A-vertex of the Yff contact triangle and passing through A; define O\ :sub:`B` and O\ :sub:`C` cyclically. :ref:`X(355) <X(355)>` is the radical center of O\ :sub:`A`, O\ :sub:`B`, O\ :sub:`C`. (Randy Hutson, August 30, 2020)
