X_3671
======

.. rubric:: X_958com(INTOUCH TRIANGLE)

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`O* <O*>` be a circle with center :ref:`X_2 <X_2>` and variable radius :g:`R* <R*>`. Let :g:`L_{A} <L_A>` be the radical axis of :g:`O* <O*>` and the :g:`A <A>`-excircle, and define :g:`L_{B} <L_B>` and :g:`L_{C} <L_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>`\ =\ :g:`L_{B} <L_B>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{C} <L_C>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. There is a unique value of :g:`R* <R*>` for which triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_3671 <X_3671>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, January 15, 2019)
