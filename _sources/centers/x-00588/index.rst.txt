X_588
=====

.. rubric:: 1st KENMOTU-VAN LAMOEN POINT

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_588 <X_588>` is the perspector of triangles :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`, where :g:`A' <A'>` is the circumcenter of :ref:`X_371 <X_371>` and the points where the squares in the Kenmotu configuration with center :ref:`X_371 <X_371>` meet sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` are defined cyclically.
