.. container:: note-item

   **Alexei Myakishev,** "The M-Configuration of a Triangle," `Forum Geometricorum 3 (2003) 135-144 <http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2003volume3/FG200315.pdf>`__,

   proves that the lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_92 <X_92>`. He notes that another construction follows from Proposition 2 of the article: let :g:`A_{1} <A_1>` be the midpoint of the arc :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` of the circumcircle that passes through :g:`A <A>`, and let :g:`A_{2} <A_2>` be the point, other than A, in which the :g:`A <A>`-altitude meets the circumcircle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` = :g:`A_{1}A_{2} <[ A_1 A_2 ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`. Define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then the lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_92 <X_92>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Suppose that :g:`T <T>` = :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is a :term:`central triangle <central-triangle>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the pole with respect to the polar circle of the line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The appearance of :g:`T <T>` in the following list means that the lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_92 <X_92>`: Feurerbach, incentral, excentral, extangents, Apollonius, mixtilinear excentral.
