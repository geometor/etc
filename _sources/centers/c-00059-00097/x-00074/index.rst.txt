Notes

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`T <T>` be the triangle formed by reflecting the orthic axis in the sidelines of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`; then T is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the point of intersection of the orthic axis and line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Let :g:`O_{A} <O_A>` be the circumcenter of :g:`AB'C' << A B' C' >>`, and define Let :g:`O_{B} <O_B>` and :g:`O_{C} <O_C>` cyclically; then the lines :g:`AO_{A} <[ A O_A ]>`, :g:`BO_{B} <[ B O_B ]>`, :g:`CO_{C} <[ C O_C ]>` concur in :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, August 26, 2014)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << K_a K_b K_c >>` be the :term:`anticomplementary triangle <anticomplementary-triangle>`. Let :g:`L <L>` be the line through :g:`A' <K_a>` parallel to the Euler line, and define M and N cyclically. Let :g:`L' <L'>` be the reflection of :g:`L <L>` in sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`M' <M'>` and :g:`N' <N'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`L' <L'>`, :g:`M' <M'>`, :g:`N' <N'>` concur in :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`. (Randy Hutson, August 26, 2014)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`-:term:`Fuhrmann triangle <fuhrmann-triangle>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the reflection of :g:`A <A>` in line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is inversely similar to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, with similitude center :ref:`X_265 <X_265>`, and :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`, which is also the orthocenter of :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>`. (Randy Hutson, August 26, 2014)

.. container:: note-item

   In :cite:`Hyacinthos <hyacinthos>` 8129 (10/4/03), :person:`Floor van Lamoen <floor-van-lamoen>` noted that if :ref:`X_74 <X_74>` is denoted by J, then each of the points :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`,\ :g:`J <J>` is J of the other three, in analogy with the well known property of orthocentric systems (that is, each of the points :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`,\ :g:`H <H>` is the orthocenter of the other three).

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the orthocentroidal triangle and :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` the anti-orthocentroidal triangle. Let :g:`A* <A*>` be the reflection of :g:`A″ <A″>` in :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B* <B*>` and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA* <[ A A* ]>`, :g:`BB* <[ B B* ]>`, :g:`CC* <[ C C* ]>` concur in :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

The lines :g:`A'A″ <[ A' A″ ]>`, :g:`B'B″ <[ B' B″ ]>`, :g:`C'C″ <[ C' C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_74 <X_74>`. (Randy Hutson, January 15, 2019)
