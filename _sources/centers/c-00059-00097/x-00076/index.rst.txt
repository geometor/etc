.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the perspector of the A-McCay circle, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_76 <X_76>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, April 9, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_76 <X_76>` is the :term:`vertex conjugate <vertex-conjugate>` of the foci of the inellipse that is the barycentric square of the de Longchamps line. The center of the inellipse is :ref:`X_626 <X_626>` and its Brianchon point (perspector) is :ref:`X_1502 <X_1502>`. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the obverse triangle of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`. Let :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` be the N-obverse triangle of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`. Let :g:`A* <A*>` be the barycentric product :g:`A' <A'>`\ \*\ :g:`A″ <A″>`, and define :g:`B* <B*>` and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA* <[ A A* ]>`, :g:`BB* <[ B B* ]>`, :g:`CC* <[ C C* ]>` concur in :ref:`X_76 <X_76>`. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)

   :ref:`X_76 <X_76>` is the barycentric multiplier for the Steiner circumellipse. (The barycentric product of :ref:`X_76 <X_76>` and the circumcircle is the Steiner circumellipse.) (Randy Hutson, August 19, 2019)
