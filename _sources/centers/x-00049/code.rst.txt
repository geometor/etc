X(49) = CENTER OF SINE-TRIPLE-ANGLE CIRCLE — Code
=================================================

.. enunciation::

   Suppose that P and Q are distinct points in the plane of a triangle ABC . Let P\ :sub:`A` = reflection of P in line AQ, let Q\ :sub:`A` = reflection of Q in line AP, and let M\ :sub:`A` = midpoint of segment P\ :sub:`A`\ Q\ :sub:`A`. Define M\ :sub:`B` and M\ :sub:`C` cyclically. :person:`César Lozada <cesar-lozada>` found that if Q = :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of P, then the locus of P for which M\ :sub:`A`\ M\ :sub:`B`\ M\ :sub:`C` is perspective to ABC is the union of a cubic and 6 circles: specifically, the McCay cubic (K003), the circles {B,C,B',C'}}, {C,A,C',A'}}, {A,B,A',B'}}, and the circles {B,C,A'}}, {C,A,B'}}, {A,B,C'}}, where A',B',C' are the excenters of ABC. Moreover, if P = :ref:`X(3) <X(3)>` and Q = :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>`, then M\ :sub:`A`\ M\ :sub:`B`\ M\ :sub:`C` is not only perspective, but homothetic, to ABC, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X(49) <X(49)>`. See `Hyacinthos 23265, June 1, 2015. <http://www.hyacinthos.epizy.com/message.php?msg=23265>`__
