.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>` is the point of concurrence of the symmedians (i.e., reflections of medians in corresponding angle bisectors). :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>` is the point which, when given by *actual* trilinear distances x,y,z, minimizes x\ :sup:`2` + y\ :sup:`2` + z\ :sup:`2`.

.. container:: note-item

.. container:: note-item

Loginov's "Energy methods for single-position passive radar based on special points of a triangle"**, downloadable in **Russian** (``EnergyMethodsRussian.pdf``) or as an **English translation** (``EnergyMethodsEnglish.pdf``).

.. container:: note-item

   Let A'B'C' be the :term:`pedal triangle <pedal-triangle>` of an arbitrary point X, and let S(X) be the vector sum XA' + XB' + XC'. Then

.. container:: note-item

   S(X) = (0 vector) if and only if X = :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>`.

.. container:: note-item

   The "if" implication is equivalent to the well known fact that :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>` is the centroid of its pedal triangle, and the converse was proved by Barry Wolk (:cite:`Hyacinthos #19 <hyacinthos>`, Dec. 23, 1999).

   Let P and Q be distinct points in a plane, and let T be a triangle rigidly rotating about P. Let X be a triangle center, and let

      * X\ :sub:`T` = X-of-T;
      * O = a circle with center Q';
      * {A,B,C} = vertices of T;
      * A' = inverse of A in O, and define B' and C' cyclically;
      * T' = A'B'C';
      * X':sub:`T` = X-of-T'.
