X_1662–X_1706
=============

.. rubric:: Circle-Related Points

.. enunciation::

   Internal and external centers of similitude among the circumcircle, incircle, nine-point circle, Brocard circle, 1st and 2nd Lemoine circles, Apollonius circle, and Bevan circle.

.. figure:: c-01662-01706.svg
   :alt: X_1662–X_1706 — Circle-Related Points
   :align: center

   **X_1662–X_1706** (*Circle-Related Points*) — 16 representative centers (from 45 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0024:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Circle-related Points 1662-1706
   :class: note

   The next cluster of points were contributed by Peter J. C. Moses during August, 2003. Symbols used to represent functions of a,b,c (or :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`) include

   | σ = area of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`
   | ω = arccot(cot A + cot B + cot C) = arccot[(:math:`a^{2}` + :math:`b^{2}` +\ :math:`c^{2})`/(4σ)]; ω is the Brocard angle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`
   | e = :math:`\sqrt{1 - 4 \sin^{2}\omega }` (as in Gallatly, p. 96, along with other formulas involving ω)
   | s = (a + b + c)/2 = semiperimeter of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`
   | r = σ/s = inradius of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`

   Circles mentioned in this section are the following:
