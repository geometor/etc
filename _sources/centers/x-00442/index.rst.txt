Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   As a point on the Euler line, :ref:`X_442 <X_442>` has Shinagawa coefficients :math:`(\$aS_{A}\$ + abc,\$aS_{A}\$ - abc)`

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`I_{A} <I_A>`, :g:`I_{B} <I_B>`, :g:`I_{C} <I_C>` be the excenters, let :g:`A_{B} <A_B>`, :g:`A_{C} <A_C>` be the projections of A onto :g:`I_{A}I_{B} <[ I_A I_B ]>` and :g:`I_{A}I_{C} <[ I_A I_C ]>`, respectively, and define :g:`B_{C} <B_C>`, :g:`B_{A} <B_A>` and :g:`C_{A} <C_A>`, :g:`C_{B} <C_B>` cyclically. The Euler lines of the four triangles :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, :g:`AA_{B}A_{C} << A A_B A_C >>`, :g:`BB_{C}B_{A} << B B_C B_A >>`, :g:`CC_{A}C_{B} << C C_A C_B >>` concur in :ref:`X_442 <X_442>`. (Jean-Pierre Ehrmann, 11/24/01)

.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let :g:`DEF << D E F >>` denote the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>` of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and let

      * :g:`H_{A} <H_A>` = orthocenter of :g:`IBC << I B C >>`
      * :g:`M_{A} <M_A>` = midpoint of segment :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`
      * :g:`N_{A} <N_A>` = midpoint of the arc :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` which does not include A
      * :math:`S_{A}` = reflection of :g:`I <I>` in line :g:`DE <[ D E ]>`,
      * and define cyclically the points :g:`H_{B} <H_B>`, :g:`H_{C} <H_C>`, :g:`M_{B} <M_B>`, :g:`M_{C} <M_C>`, :g:`N_{B} <N_B>`, :g:`N_{C} <N_C>`, and :math:`S_{B}`, :math:`S_{C}`. The lines :math:`S_{A}`\ :g:`M_{A} <M_A>`, :math:`S_{B}`\ :g:`M_{B} <M_B>`, :math:`S_{CA}`\ :g:`M_{C} <M_C>` concur in :ref:`X_442 <X_442>`, and :ref:`X_442 <X_442>` is the pole, with respect to the incircle, of the perspectrix of the triangles :g:`H_{A}H_{B}H_{C} << H_A H_B H_C >>` and :g:`N_{A}N_{B}N_{C} << N_A N_B N_C >>`. (Dominik Burek, January 18 2012)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << J_a J_b J_c >>` be the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>`. :ref:`X_442 <X_442>` is the radical center of the nine-point circles of :g:`A'BC << J_a B C >>`, :g:`B'CA << J_b C A >>`, :g:`C'AB << J_c A B >>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, September 14, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let (:g:`J_{a} <J_a>`) be the :g:`A <A>`-excircle of the A-altimedial triangle, and define (:g:`J_{b} <J_b>`) and (:g:`J_{c} <J_c>`) cyclically. :ref:`X_442 <X_442>` is the radical center of (:g:`J_{a} <J_a>`), (:g:`J_{b} <J_b>`), (:g:`J_{c} <J_c>`). (Randy Hutson, November 2, 2017)
