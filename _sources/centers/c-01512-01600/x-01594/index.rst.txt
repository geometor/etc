X_1594
======

.. rubric:: RIGBY-LALESCU ORTHOPOLE

.. enunciation::

   Traian Lalescu (Trajan Lalesco) proved in "A Class of Remarcable Triangles," *Gazeta Matematica* 20 (1915) 213 [in Romanian], that if triangles :g:`DEF << D E F >>` and :g:`D'E'F' << D' E' F' >>` are inscribed in a circle and directed arclengths satisfyarc :g:`DD' <[ D D' ]>` + arc :g:`EE' <[ E E' ]>` + arc :g:`FF' <[ F F' ]>` = 0 mod 2π, then the Simson lines of :g:`D <D>`, :g:`E <E>`, :g:`F <F>` with respect to :g:`D' <D'>`,\ :g:`E' <E'>`,\ :g:`F' <F'>` and the Simson lines of :g:`D' <D'>`,\ :g:`E' <E'>`,\ :g:`F' <F'>` with respect to :g:`D <D>`, :g:`E <E>`, :g:`F <F>` concur in the midpoint X of the segment of the orthocenters of :g:`DEF << D E F >>` and :g:`D'E'F' << D' E' F' >>`. Daniel Vacaretu considered triangles :g:`DEF << D E F >>` and :g:`D'E'F' << D' E' F' >>` associated with left and right :term:`isoscelizers <isoscelizer>` and inscribed in the sine-triple-angle circle. He obtained the second set of trilinears shown above for the midpoint X. (See also the bicentric pair PU(61).)

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   As a point on the Euler line, :ref:`X_1594 <X_1594>` has Shinagawa coefficients :math:`(2F, E + 2F)`.

.. container:: note-item

   In *Episodes in Nineteenth and Twentieth Century Euclidean Geometry,*, page 132, Ross Honsberger presents :ref:`X_1594 <X_1594>` as the orthopole of the six sides of two triangles and as the point common to six Simson lines. Honsberger calls this orthopole the Rigby Point. (Notes on Lalescu and Honsberger received from D. Vacaretu, 19/16/03)
