Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   As a point on the Euler line, :ref:`X_1593 <X_1593>` has Shinagawa coefficients :math:`(F, E - F)`.

.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let

      * Γ = circumcircle
      * :g:`DEF << D E F >>` = :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>`
      * :g:`E' <E'>` = Γ-antipode of E
      * (A) = circle that passes through :g:`E <E>`,\ :g:`F <F>`,\ :g:`E' <E'>`
      * :g:`A' <A'>` = center of (A), and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically.

   Then the triangles :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` are homothetic, and their homothetic center is :ref:`X_1593 <X_1593>`. (:person:`Angel Montesdeoca <angel-montesdeoca>`, February 14, 2023)
