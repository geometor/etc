X_2999
======

.. rubric:: DANNEELS-BEVAN HOMOTHETIC CENTER

.. enunciation::

   Let :math:`R_{A}` be the radical axis of the Bevan circle and the :g:`A <A>`-excircle. Define :math:`R_{B}` and :math:`R_{C}` cyclically. The three radical axes form a triangle homothetic to triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and :ref:`X_2999 <X_2999>` is the center of homothety. (Eric Danneels, :cite:`Hyacinthos #10926 <hyacinthos>`, Dec. 5, 2004.
