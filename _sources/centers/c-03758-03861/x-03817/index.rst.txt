.. container:: note-item

   In the plane of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, let I = :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` and

   :g:`L <L>` = line through midpoint of :g:`CA <[ C A ]>` perpendicular to :g:`BI <[ B X_1 ]>`

   :g:`L' <L'>` = line through midpoint of :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` perpendicular to :g:`CI <[ C X_1 ]>`

   :g:`L″ <L″>` = line through midpoint of :g:`AI <[ A X_1 ]>` perpendicular to :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`

   The lines :g:`L <L>`, :g:`L' <L'>`, :g:`L″ <L″>` concur in a point, :g:`A' <A'>`; define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Ten :ref:`X_3817 <X_3817>` = :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`-of-:g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. Also, :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` = complement of the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>`, and :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` = extraversion triangle of :ref:`X_10 <X_10>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, September 14, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`T_aT_bT_c << T_a T_b T_c >>` be the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>`, and :g:`A_{a}A_{b}A_{c} << A_a A_b A_c >>`, :g:`B_{a}B_{b}B_{c} << B_a B_b B_c >>`, :g:`C_{a}C_{b}C_{c} << C_a C_b C_c >>` the A-, B-, and C-:term:`extouch triangles <extouch-triangle>`. Let :g:`A_{b}' <A_b'>` = :g:`T_bT_c <[ T_b T_c ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`B_{c}B_{a} <[ B_c B_a ]>` and :g:`A_{c}' <A_c'>` = :g:`T_bT_c <[ T_b T_c ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`C_{a}C_{b} <[ C_a C_b ]>`. The six points lie on a common ellipse. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)
