X_2672
======

.. rubric:: 2nd GOLDEN ARBELOS POINT

.. enunciation::

   In the construction of :ref:`X_2671 <X_2671>`, if :g:`A' <A'>` is the center of the s-Archimedean circle of the inward arbelos on segment :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` are defined cyclically, then :g:`A'A <[ A' A ]>`, :g:`B'B <[ B' B ]>`, :g:`C'C <[ C' C ]>` concur in :ref:`X_2672 <X_2672>`.
