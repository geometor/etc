.. rubric:: TRILINEAR POLE OF EULER LINE

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_648 <X_648>` is constructed as the pole of the Euler line L as follows: let :g:`A″ <A″>`, :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` be the points where L meets the sidelines :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` be the harmonic conjugates of :g:`A″ <A″>`, :g:`B″ <B″>`, :g:`C″ <C″>` with respect to {B,C}, {C,A}, {A,B}, respectively, The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_648 <X_648>`.
