Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Let Ea be the ellipse with B and C as foci and passing through :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, and define Eb and Ec cyclically. Let La be the line tangent to Ea at :ref:`X(1) <X(1)>`, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A' = La∩BC, B' = Lb∩CA, C' = Lc∩AB. Then A', B', C' are collinear, and :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>` = :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line A'B'C'. The line A'B'C' meets the line at infinity at the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of X(3659). Alternately, let A″ be the trilinear pole of line La, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″ and CC″ concur at :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`; see also :ref:`X(188) <X(188)>`. The points A″, B″, C″ lie on the circumconic centered at :ref:`X(9) <X(9)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let A'B'C' be the excentral triangle. :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>` is the trilinear pole of the Monge line of the incircles of BCA', CAB', ABC'. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   In notes dated 1987, Yff raises this question concerning certain triangles lying within ABC: can :term:`isoscelizers <isoscelizer>` (defined at :ref:`X(173) <X(173)>`), P\ :sub:`B`\ Q\ :sub:`C`, P\ :sub:`C`\ Q\ :sub:`A`, P\ :sub:`A`\ Q\ :sub:`B`, be constructed so that, on putting

.. container:: note-item

   R\ :sub:`A` = P\ :sub:`A`\ Q\ :sub:`B`\ ∩P\ :sub:`B`\ Q\ :sub:`C`, R\ :sub:`B` = P\ :sub:`B`\ Q\ :sub:`C`\ ∩P\ :sub:`C`\ Q\ :sub:`A`, R\ :sub:`C` = P\ :sub:`C`\ Q\ :sub:`A`\ ∩P\ :sub:`A`\ Q\ :sub:`B`,

   the following four triangles are congruent:

   P\ :sub:`A`\ Q\ :sub:`A`\ R\ :sub:`A`, P\ :sub:`B`\ Q\ :sub:`B`\ R\ :sub:`B`, P\ :sub:`C`\ Q\ :sub:`C`\ R\ :sub:`C`, R\ :sub:`A`\ R\ :sub:`B`\ R\ :sub:`C` ?

.. container:: note-item

   After proving that the answer is yes, Yff moves the three isoscelizers in such a way that the three outer triangles, stay congruent and the inner triangle (called the Yff :term:`central triangle <central-triangle>`), R\ :sub:`A`\ R\ :sub:`B`\ R\ :sub:`C`, shrinks to :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Let D be the point on side BC such that (angle BID) = (angle DIC), and likewise for point E on side CA and point F on side AB. The lines AD, BE, CF concur in :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`. (Seiichi Kirikami, Jan. 29, 2010)

.. container:: note-item

   Generalization: if I is replaced by an arbitrary point P = p : q : r (trilinears), then the lines AD, BE, CF concur in the point K(P) = f(p,q,r,A) : f(q,r,p,B) : f(r,p,q,C), where f(p,q,r,A) = (q\ :sup:`2` + r\ :sup:`2` + 2qr cos A)\ :sup:`-1/2`. Moreover, if P\* is the inverse of P in the circumcircle, then K(P\*) = K(P). (:person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, Feb. 1, 2010, based on Seiichi Kirikami's construction of :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>`)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X(174) <X(174)>` is the homothetic center of ABC and the extangents triangle of the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>`. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)
