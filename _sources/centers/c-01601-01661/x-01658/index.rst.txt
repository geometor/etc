X_1658
======

.. rubric:: CIRCUMCENTER OF KOSNITA TRIANGLE

.. enunciation::

   The vertices of the :term:`Kosnita triangle <kosnita-triangle>` are the circumcenters of the triangles :g:`BOC << B O C >>`, :g:`COA << C O A >>`, :g:`AOB << A O B >>`, where O is the circumcenter, :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`. (:person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>`, 8/24/03)
