.. admonition:: Group Preamble — TCC Perspectors 1601-1634
   :class: note

   Suppose P is a point. As noted in :cite:`TCCT <tcct>`, p. 201, the tangential triangle is perspective to the circumcevian triangle of :g:`P <P>`. (The tangential triangle is also perspector to the circum-:term:`anticevian triangle <anticevian-triangle>` of :g:`P <P>`, with the same perspector as for the circumcevian triangle of :g:`P <P>`.) In August 2003, Jean-Pierre Ehrmann noted that if :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` (barycentrics), then the perspector is given by

   :math:`a^{2}(b^{4}/y^{2} + c^{4}/z^{2} - a^{4}/x^{2})` : :math:`b^{2}(c^{4}/z^{2} + a^{4}/x^{2} - b^{4}/y^{2})` : :math:`c^{2}(a^{4}/x^{2} + b^{4}/y^{2} - c^{4}/z^{2})` .

   Denote this perspector by T(P), and call it the TCC-perspector of P. If :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` (trilinears), then

   T(P) =\ :math:`a(b^{2}/y^{2} + c^{2}/z^{2} - a^{2}/x^{2}) : b(c^{2}/z^{2} + a^{2}/x^{2} - b^{2}/y^{2}) : c(a^{2}/x^{2} + b^{2}/y^{2} - c^{2}/z^{2})`.

   The transformation T carries :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>` to triangle centers. .. admonition:: Group Preamble — Tripolar Centroids 1635-1651
   :class: note

   Suppose P, Q, R are collinear points not on the line at infinity. Let M = midpoint{Q, R}. The segment PM has two trisectors. The trisector closer to M here defines the **centroid of {P, Q, R}.**

   Suppose :g:`X <X>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` (trilinears) is a point other than :ref:`X_2 <X_2>`, and define the **tripolar centroid of X** as the point TG(X) given by

   **TG(X) = x(by - cz)(by + cz - 2ax) : y(cz - ax)(cz + ax - 2by) : z(ax - by)(ax + by - 2cz).**

   TG(X) is the centroid of the points :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`C'A' <[ C' A' ]>`, :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`A'B' <[ A' B' ]>`, where :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` denotes the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of X. The notions of centroid and tripolar centroid were contributed by :person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>`, August 24, 2003.
