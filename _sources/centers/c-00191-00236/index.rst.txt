X_191–X_236
===========

.. rubric:: Ceva Conjugates

.. enunciation::

   Perspectors of the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of :g:`P <P>` and the :term:`anticevian triangle <anticevian-triangle>` of Q (P-:term:`Ceva conjugates <ceva-conjugate>` of :g:`Q <Q>`) for classical :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>`.
