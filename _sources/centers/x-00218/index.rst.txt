X(218) = X(7)-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X(55)
=====================================

.. enunciation::

   Let A' be the center of the conic through the contact points of the B- and C- excircles with the sidelines of ABC. Define B' and C' cyclically. The triangle A'B'C' is perspective to the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>` at :ref:`X(218) <X(218)>`. See also :ref:`X(6) <X(6)>`, :ref:`X(25) <X(25)>`, :ref:`X(222) <X(222)>`, :ref:`X(940) <X(940)>`, X(1743). (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, July 23, 2015)
