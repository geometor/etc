X(589) = 2nd KENMOTU-VAN LAMOEN POINT
=====================================

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X(589) <X(589)>` is the perspector of triangles ABC and A'B'C', where A' is the circumcenter of :ref:`X(372) <X(372)>` and the points where the squares in the Kenmotu configuration with center :ref:`X(372) <X(372)>` meet sideline BC, and B' and C' are defined cyclically. See the reference at :ref:`X(588) <X(588)>`.
