X_1283
======

.. rubric:: 7th SHARYGIN POINT

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_1283 <X_1283>` is the center of similitude of the triangles :g:`DEF << D E F >>` and :g:`D'E'F' << D' E' F' >>` constructed at :ref:`X_1281 <X_1281>`. Coordinates were found by Jean-Pierre Ehrmann. See :cite:`Hyacinthos #6293 <hyacinthos>` and #6315.
