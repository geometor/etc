X_1217
======

.. rubric:: CRETAN PERSPECTOR

.. enunciation::

   Using barycentric coordinates, let :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`p : q : r` be a point not on a sideline of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and let :g:`P' <P'>` be the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of P. Let :g:`DEF << D E F >>` and :g:`D'E'F' << D' E' F' >>` be the :term:`pedal triangles <pedal-triangle>` of :g:`P <P>` and :g:`P' <P'>`, respectively. Let

   :g:`X <X>` = :g:`PD' <[ P D' ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`P'D <[ P' D ]>`, :g:`Y <Y>` = :g:`PE' <[ P E' ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`P'E <[ P' E ]>`, :g:`Z <Z>` = :g:`PF' <[ P F' ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`P'F <[ P' F ]>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << H_a H_b H_c >>` be the :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>` of the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>` of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the reflection of :ref:`X_4 <X_4>` in :g:`A' <H_a>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Let :g:`K_{ab} <K_ab>` and :g:`K_{ac} <K_ac>` be the circumcenters of triangles :g:`A″BA << A″ B A >>` and :g:`A″CA << A″ C A >>`, respectively. Let A''' = :g:`BK_{ac} <[ B K_ac ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CK_{ab} <[ C K_ab ]>`, and define B''' and C''' cyclically. The lines AA''', BB''', CC''' concur in :ref:`X_1217 <X_1217>`. (:person:`Antreas Hatzipolakis <antreas-hatzipolakis>`, :cite:`Anopolis #39 <adgeom>`, 3/19/2002)

.. container:: note-item

   Then the triangle :g:`XYZ << X Y Z >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` (Dominik Burek, June 8, 2012) and the perspector is given by barycentric coordinates :math:`k(a,b,c) : k(b,c,a) : k(c,a,b)`, where k = 1/(:math:`pqr(b^{2}` + :math:`c^{2}` - :math:`a^{2})` + 2\ :math:`p(b^{2}`\ :math:`r^{2}` + :math:`c^{2}`\ :math:`q^{2})`) (Peter Moses, Oct. 22, 2012). The point :ref:`X_1217 <X_1217>` results from taking :g:`P <P>` = :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`.
