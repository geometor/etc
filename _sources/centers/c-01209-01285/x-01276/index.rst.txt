X_1276
======

.. rubric:: 2nd EVANS PERSPECTOR

.. enunciation::

   Evans conjectured that :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, :ref:`X_484 <X_484>`, :ref:`X_1276 <X_1276>`, :ref:`X_1277 <X_1277>` are concyclic, and he reported that :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>` confirmed this conjecture and noted that the center of this circle is :ref:`X_1019 <X_1019>`. (Lawrence Evans, 2/24/2003)

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`T <T>` be the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>`, whose vertices are the A-, B-, C- excenters of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`U <U>` be the equilateral triangle having segment :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` as base with vertex :g:`A' <A'>` on the side of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` that does not contain vertex :g:`A <A>`. Define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically, and let :g:`T' <T'>` be the triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. Let V be the equilateral triangle having :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` as base with vertex :g:`A″ <A″>` on the side of :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` that contains A. Define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically, and let :g:`T″ <T″>` = :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>`. Then T and :g:`T' <T'>` are perspective, and :ref:`X_1276 <X_1276>` is their perspector. (Lawrence Evans, 2/4/2003)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_1276 <X_1276>` is the perspector of the excentral triangle and the apices of equilateral triangles constructed outward from the sides, as in the construction of :ref:`X_13 <X_13>`. More generally, the excentral triangle is perspective to every Kiepert triangle. The locus of the perspector :g:`K_{θ} <K_θ>` is the line :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`\ \ :ref:`X_9 <X_9>`. Specifically, :g:`K_{θ} <K_θ>` divides the segment from :ref:`X_75 <X_75>` to :ref:`X_9 <X_9>` in the ratio - ((4R+r)/s) cot θ. (Paul Yiu, 2/27/04).
