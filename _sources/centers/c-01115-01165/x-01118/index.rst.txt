X_1118
======

.. rubric:: 1st HATZIPOLAKIS PERSPECTOR

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << T_a T_b T_c >>` be the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>` of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`C_{A} <C_A>` be the point other than :g:`C' <T_c>` in which the perpendicular to :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` from :g:`C' <T_c>` meets the incircle, let :g:`B_{A} <B_A>` be the point other than :g:`B' <T_b>` in which the perpendicular to :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` from :g:`B' <T_b>` meets the incircle, and let :g:`A_{0} <A_0>` be the point of intersection of lines :g:`BC_{A} <[ B C_A ]>` and :g:`CB_{A} <[ C B_A ]>`. Define :g:`B_{0} <B_0>` and :g:`C_{0} <C_0>` cyclically. Then triangle :g:`A_{0}B_{0}C_{0} << A_0 B_0 C_0 >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_1118 <X_1118>`. (:person:`Antreas Hatzipolakis <antreas-hatzipolakis>`, #5321, 4/30/02)
