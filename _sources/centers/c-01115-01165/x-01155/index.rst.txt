X_1155
======

.. rubric:: SCHRÖDER POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`XYZ << X Y Z >>` be the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>` of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`; i.e., the :term:`pedal triangle <pedal-triangle>` of the incenter, I. The circles AIX, BIY, CIZ concur in two points. One of them is I; the other is :ref:`X_1155 <X_1155>`. This result is obtain by inversion in

.. container:: note-item

   Each vertex of the :term:`tangential triangle <tangential-triangle>` of any triangle :g:`T <T>` is the inverse-in-the-circumcircle-of-T of the midpoints of the sides of T. Applying this to triangle :g:`XYZ << X Y Z >>` shows that :ref:`X_1155 <X_1155>` is the inverse-in-the-incircle of the centroid of :g:`XYZ << X Y Z >>`; i.e., :ref:`X_1155 <X_1155>` is :ref:`X_23 <X_23>`-of-the-intouch-triangle. (:person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>`, #6319, 1/11/03; coordinates by Jean-Pierre Ehrmann, #6320, 1/11/03)
