X_1157
======

.. rubric:: INVERSE-IN-CIRCUMCIRCLE OF X_54

.. enunciation::

   For any point :g:`X <X>`, let :g:`X_{A} <X_A>` be the reflection of :g:`X <X>` in sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`X_{B} <X_B>` and :g:`X_{C} <X_C>` cyclically. Then :ref:`X_1157 <X_1157>` is the unique point :g:`X <X>` for which the lines :g:`AX_{A} <[ A X_A ]>`, :g:`BX_{B} <[ B X_B ]>`, :g:`CX_{C} <[ C X_C ]>` concur on the circumcircle; the point of concurrence is :ref:`X_1141 <X_1141>`.
