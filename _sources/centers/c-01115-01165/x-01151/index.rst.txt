.. container:: note-item

   Fourteen constructions for :ref:`X_1151 <X_1151>` received from Randy Hutson, September 5, 2015:

      (1)-(10): Each pair of the following triangles are perspective, and their perspector is :ref:`X_1151 <X_1151>`: tangential triangle, Lucas tangents triangle, Lucas inner tangential triangle, 1st Lucas secondary tangents triangle, Lucas Brocard triangle.

      (11) Let :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` be the centers of the Kenmotu squares. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the reflection of :g:`A' <A'>` in :ref:`X_371 <X_371>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_1151 <X_1151>`.

      (12) Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the Lucas tangents triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the :term:`cevapoint <cevapoint>` of :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1151 <X_1151>`.

      (13) Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the Lucas central triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the pole, wrt the A-Lucas circle, of line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1151 <X_1151>`. The point :g:`A″ <A″>` is also the intersection of the polars of :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` wrt the A-Lucas circle, and likewise for :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>`.

      (14) Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the Lucas central triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the pole, wrt the A-Lucas circle, of line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1151 <X_1151>`.

The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1151 <X_1151>`. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)
