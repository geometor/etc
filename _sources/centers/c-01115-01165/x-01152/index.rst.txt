Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_1152 <X_1152>` is the perpsector of each pairs of the following five triangles: tangential triangle, Lucas(-1) tangents triangle, Lucas(-1) inner tangential triangle, 1st Lucas(-1) secondary tangents triangle, Lucas(-1) Brocard triangle. Also, :ref:`X_1152 <X_1152>` perspector of Lucas(-8) central triangle and circumsymmedial triangle. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, October 13, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the Lucas(-1) tangents triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the :term:`cevapoint <cevapoint>` of :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1152 <X_1152>`. (Randy Hutson, October 13, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the Lucas(-1) central triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the pole, wrt the A-Lucas(-1) circle, of line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1152 <X_1152>`. The pole :g:`A″ <A″>` can also be constructed as the intersection of the polars of :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` wrt the A-Lucas(-1) circle, and similarly for :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>`. (Randy Hutson, October 13, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the Lucas(-1) central triangle. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the pole, wrt the A-Lucas(-1) circle, of line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1152 <X_1152>`. (Randy Hutson, October 13, 2015)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the triangle whose trilinear vertex matrix is the sum of the matrices for the Lucas(-1) central and Lucas(-1) tangents triangles, so that :g:`A' <A'>` =\ :math:`2a(S_{A} - S) : b(2SB - S) : c(2SC - S)`. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1152 <X_1152>`. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   Let {a'} be the circle through :g:`B <B>` and :g:`C <C>` orthogonal to the circumcircle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and define {b'} and {c'} cyclically. The circle internally tangent to {a'}, {b'}, {c'} has center :ref:`X_1152 <X_1152>`; see :ref:`X_1151 <X_1151>`. (:person:`César Lozada <cesar-lozada>`, July 3, 2019)
