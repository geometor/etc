.. enunciation::

Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_1141 <X_1141>` was first noted (:cite:`Hyacinthos #1498 <hyacinthos>`, September 25, 2000) by :person:`Bernard Gibert <bernard-gibert>` as a point of intersection of the circumcircle and certain cubic, denoted :g:`K_{n} <K_n>`. To define :g:`K_{n} <K_n>`, note first that the Neuberg cubic is the locus of a point M such that the reflections of M in the sidelines of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` are the vertices of a triangle perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The locus of the perspector is the cubic :g:`K_{n} <K_n>`, and :ref:`X_1141 <X_1141>` is the point, other than :g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`, in which :g:`K_{n} <K_n>` meets the circumcircle. Also, :ref:`X_1141 <X_1141>` is the perspector when M = :ref:`X_1157 <X_1157>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the reflection of :g:`A <A>` in line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Let :g:`A_{B} <A_B>` be the reflection of :g:`A' <A'>` in :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, and define :g:`A_{C} <A_C>`, :g:`B_{C} <B_C>`, :g:`B_{A} <B_A>`, :g:`C_{A} <C_A>`, :g:`C_{B} <C_B>` cyclically. Let

   :g:`A_{1} <A_1>` = :g:`BA_{B} <[ B A_B ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CA_{C} <[ C A_C ]>`, and define :g:`B_{1} <B_1>` and :g:`C_{1} <C_1>` cyclically,

      * :g:`A_{2} <A_2>` = :g:`BA_{C} <[ B A_C ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CA_{B} <[ C A_B ]>`, and define :g:`B_{2} <B_2>` and :g:`C_{2} <C_2>` cyclically,
      * :g:`A_{3} <A_3>` = :g:`BB_{A} <[ B B_A ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CC_{A} <[ C C_A ]>`, and define :g:`B_{3} <B_3>` and :g:`C_{3} <C_3>` cyclically,
      * :g:`A_{4} <A_4>` = :g:`BB_{C} <[ B B_C ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CC_{B} <[ C C_B ]>`, and define :g:`B_{4} <B_4>` and :g:`C_{4} <C_4>` cyclically,
      * :g:`A_{5} <A_5>` = :g:`BC_{A} <[ B C_A ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CB_{A} <[ C B_A ]>`, and define :g:`B_{5} <B_5>` and :g:`C_{5} <C_5>` cyclically,
      * :g:`A_{6} <A_6>` = :g:`BC_{B} <[ B C_B ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CB_{C} <[ C B_C ]>`, and define :g:`B_{6} <B_6>` and :g:`C_{6} <C_6>` cyclically.

.. container:: note-item

   Then triangle :g:`A_{n}B_{n}C_{n} << A_n B_n C_n >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, for n = 1,2,3,4,5,6. The six perspectors are :ref:`X_1141 <X_1141>`, :ref:`X_186 <X_186>`, :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`, :ref:`X_54 <X_54>`, :ref:`X_265 <X_265>`, :ref:`X_5 <X_5>`, respectively. (Keith Dean, #4953, 3/12/02; coordinates by :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>`, #4963; summary by Dean, #4971)

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_1141 <X_1141>` is the antipode of :ref:`X_930 <X_930>` on the circumcircle, and :ref:`X_1141 <X_1141>` lies on the line of the nine-point center, :ref:`X_5 <X_5>`, and its isogonal conjugate, :ref:`X_54 <X_54>`.
