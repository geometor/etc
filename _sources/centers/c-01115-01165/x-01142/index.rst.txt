Notes

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` be the respective centers of the three Malfatti circles of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the point of intersection of lines :g:`BC' <[ B C' ]>` and :g:`CB' <[ C B' ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. Then triangle :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_1142 <X_1142>`. (:person:`Stanley Rabinowitz <stanley-rabinowitz>`, #4610, 12/29/01; coordinates by :person:`Paul Yiu <paul-yiu>`, #4614, 12/30/01)
