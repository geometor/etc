X_1145
======

.. rubric:: 3rd EHRMANN POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A' <A'>`,\ :g:`B' <B'>`,\ :g:`C' <C'>` be the respective excenters of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and let :g:`A_{B} <A_B>` be the projection of :g:`A <A>` on :g:`A'B' <[ A' B' ]>`, let :g:`A_{C} <A_C>` be the projection of :g:`A <A>` on :g:`A'C' <[ A' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B_{C} <B_C>`, :g:`B_{A} <B_A>`, :g:`C_{A} <C_A>`, :g:`C_{B} <C_B>` cyclically. The Euler lines of the three triangles :g:`A'A_{B}A_{C} << A' A_B A_C >>`, :g:`B'B_{C}B_{A} << B' B_C B_A >>`, :g:`C'C_{A}C_{B} << C' C_A C_B >>` concur in :ref:`X_1145 <X_1145>`. Also, :ref:`X_1145 <X_1145>` is :ref:`X_974 <X_974>` of the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>`. (Analogously, :ref:`X_442 <X_442>` is :ref:`X_973 <X_973>` of the excentral triangle; see the note at :ref:`X_442 <X_442>`.) Jean-Pierre Ehrmann (#4200, 10/24/01)
