.. rubric:: PAASCHE POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`D <D>` and E be the congruent circles each tangent to the other and to line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, with D also tangent to line :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` and E also tangent to line :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, meeting in a point :g:`A' <A'>` lying outside triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`.
