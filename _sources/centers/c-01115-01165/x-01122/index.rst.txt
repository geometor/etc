X_1122
======

.. rubric:: 1st GRINBERG POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`A_{B} <A_B>` be the touchpoint of the :g:`A <A>`-excircle and line :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, let :g:`A_{C} <A_C>` be the touchpoint of the :g:`A <A>`-excircle and line :g:`AC <[ A C ]>`, and let :g:`M_{A} <M_A>` be the midpoint of segment :g:`A_{B}A_{C} <[ A_B A_C ]>`. Define :g:`M_{B} <M_B>` and :g:`M_{C} <M_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>`, :g:`B' <B'>`, :g:`C' <C'>` be the touchpoints of the incircle with lines :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, :g:`CA <[ C A ]>`, :g:`AB <[ A B ]>`, respectively. The triangles :g:`M_{A}M_{B}M_{C} << M_A M_B M_C >>` and :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` are perspective, and the perspector is :ref:`X_1122 <X_1122>`. (:person:`Darij Grinberg <darij-grinberg>`, 12/28/02)
