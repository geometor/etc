X(93) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(49)
===================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`O_{A}O_{B}O_{C} << O_A O_B O_C >>` be the Kosnita triangle. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the pole wrt polar circle of line :g:`O_{B}O_{C} <[ O_B O_C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X(93) <X(93)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, June 7, 2019)
