Notes

.. container:: note-item

   Let S = :ref:`X_99 <X_99>`. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the centroid of the triangle :g:`BCS << B C S >>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Let :g:`D' <D'>` be the centroid of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. The four centroids form a quadrilateral homothetic to the quadrilateral ABCS. The center of homothety is :ref:`X_620 <X_620>`, which is the centroid of ABCS. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)

.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` and :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` be the (equilateral) :term:`antipedal triangles <antipedal-triangle>` of :ref:`X_13 <X_13>` and :ref:`X_14 <X_14>`, resp. Let :g:`A* <A*>` be the midpoint of :g:`A' <A'>` and :g:`A″ <A″>`, and define :g:`B* <B*>` and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically. The triangle :g:`A*B*C* << A* B* C* >>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_620 <X_620>`. (Randy Hutson, February 10, 2016)

.. container:: note-item
