Let :g:`A' <A'>` be where this ellipse meets sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then triangles :g:`ABC << A B C >>` and :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` are perspective, and their perspector is :ref:`X_598 <X_598>`.
