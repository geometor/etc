Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   The tangent to the nine-point circle at the point where it is tangent to the incircle (:ref:`X_11 <X_11>`, the Feuerbach point) is here named the **Feuerbach tangent line**. It is line :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`\ \ :ref:`X_244 <X_244>`. The inverse of the incircle in the excircles radical circle is a circle tangent to the Apollonius circle at X_3030. The line tangent to the Apollonius circle at X_3030 is here named the **Apollonius tangent line**. It is line :ref:`X_2254 <X_2254>`\ X_3030. :ref:`X_2254 <X_2254>` is the intersection of the Feuerbach tangent line and the Apollonius tangent line. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, January 15, 2019)
