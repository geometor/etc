.. container:: note-item

   Contribution by Angel Montesdeoa, September 10, 2017: (start)

   In the plane of a triangle :g:`BAC << B A C >>`, let

   :g:`O <X_3>` = :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` = circumcenter

   U(O,q) = circle with center O and radius q

   :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` = :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>`

   :g:`A*B*C* << A* B* C* >>` = polar triangle of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` with respect to U(O,q).

   There exists a unique q such that the lines :g:`AA* <[ A A* ]>`, :g:`BB* <[ B B* ]>`, :g:`CC* <[ C C* ]>` concur, and the point of concurrence is :ref:`X_2217 <X_2217>`. Moreover, the radius and a barycentric equation for U(O,q) are given as follows:

      q^2 = (4 r R^2 s(r+R))/(a b c) = (a b c (2 a b c+(a+b-c) (a-b+c)(-a+b+c)))/(2 (a+b-c) (a-b+c) (-a+b+c) (a+b+c));

      abc(x^2+y^2+z^2) + 2a (a+b) (a+c)y z + 2b(b+c)(b+a)z x + 2c(c+a)(c+b)x y = 0. (end)
