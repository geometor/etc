X_237–X_248
===========

.. rubric:: Line Conjugates

.. enunciation::

   Intersections of line :g:`PQ <[ P Q ]>` with the :term:`trilinear polar <trilinear-polar>` of the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of P (P-:term:`line conjugates <line-conjugate>` of :g:`Q <Q>`).

.. figure:: c-00237-00248.svg
   :alt: X_237–X_248 — Line Conjugates
   :align: center

   **X_237–X_248** (*Line Conjugates*) — 11 representative centers (from 12 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0006:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers X_237-X_248 (Line conjugates)
   :class: note

   Centers :ref:`X_237 <X_237>`-:ref:`X_248 <X_248>` are line conjugates. (The :g:`P <P>`-line conjugate of :g:`Q <Q>` is the point where line :g:`PQ <[ P Q ]>` meets the trilinear polar of the isogonal conjugate of :g:`Q <Q>`.)

   See :person:`Clark Kimberling <clark-kimberling>` and Peter J. C. Moses, `Line conjugates in the plane of a triangle, <https://trebuchet.public.springernature.app/get_content/9bf860cd-18a5-4f8a-a5b0-90acefaf051b>`__ *Aequationes Mathematicae*, August 2022.
