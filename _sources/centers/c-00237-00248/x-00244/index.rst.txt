.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`O* <O*>` be a circle with center :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` and variable radius :g:`R* <R*>`. Let :g:`L_{a} <L_a>` be the radical axis of :g:`O* <O*>` and the :g:`A <A>`-excircle, and define :g:`L_{b} <L_b>` and :g:`L_{c} <L_c>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>`\ =\ :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`, :g:`B' <B'>`\ =\ :g:`L_{c} <L_c>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`, :g:`C' <C'>`\ =\ :g:`L_{a} <L_a>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{b} <L_b>`. Then :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the locus of the perspector as :g:`R* <R*>` varies is the hyperbola {:g:`A <A>`, :g:`B <B>`, :g:`C <C>`,\ :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`,\ :ref:`X_10 <X_10>`}}, which has center :ref:`X_244 <X_244>`. Also, :ref:`X_244 <X_244>` lies in the inellipse centered at :ref:`X_10 <X_10>`, as well as the Hofstadter ellpse E(1/2), which is the incentral inellipse. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 26, 2016)

.. container:: note-item

   **Art from triangle centers:** Let :g:`X <X>` = :ref:`X_244 <X_244>`. In a square, suppose that P is a randomly chosen point, and form a triangle using two vertices of the square and P as the third vertex. Then let :g:`P <P>` = X and repeat the procedure, several thousand times. For typical results, see **X(244) art** (``X(244)art.png``) and **X(244) art animated** (``X(244)KageyAnimated.gif``). For results using other :term:`triangle centers <triangle-center>` for :g:`X <X>`, see **X(41) art** (``X(41)art.png``), **X(58) art** (``X(58)art.png``), **X(76) art** (``X(76)art.png``), **X(115) art** (``X(115)art.png``), **X(141) art** (``X(141)art.png``), **X(179) art** (``X(179)art.png``), and **X(255) art** (``X(255)art.png``).
