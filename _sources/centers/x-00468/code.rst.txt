X(468) = X(2)-LINE CONJUGATE OF X(3) — Code
===========================================

.. enunciation::

   Let (:g:`P_{a} <P_a>`) be the A-power circle of the A-altimedial triangle, and define (:g:`P_{b} <P_b>`) and (:g:`P_{c} <P_c>`) cyclically. The radical center of (:g:`P_{a} <P_a>`), (:g:`P_{b} <P_b>`), (:g:`P_{c} <P_c>`) is :ref:`X(4) <X(4)>`, and the harmonic traces of (:g:`P_{a} <P_a>`), (:g:`P_{b} <P_b>`), (:g:`P_{c} <P_c>`) are :ref:`X(2) <X(2)>` and :ref:`X(468) <X(468)>`.
