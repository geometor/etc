X_113–X_139
===========

.. rubric:: Centers on the Nine-Point Circle & Orthic Centers

.. enunciation::

   Points on the nine-point circle (:ref:`X_113 <X_113>`\ –\ :ref:`X_139 <X_139>`), centers of the Kiepert, Feuerbach, and Jerabek rectangular hyperbolas, and centers transferred to the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`.

   :alt: X_113–X_139 — Centers on the Nine-Point Circle & Orthic Centers
   :align: center

   **X_113–X_139** (*Centers on the Nine-Point Circle & Orthic Centers*) — 14 representative centers (from 27 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)` with unit baseline :g:`AB <[ A B ]>` = :math:`1`.

.. _preamble-0003:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Centers X_113-X_139 (Points on the nine-point circle)
   :class: note

   Centers :ref:`X_113 <X_113>`-:ref:`X_139 <X_139>` lie on the nine-point circle.

   Suppose that X is a point on the nine-point circle, and let :g:`X' <X'>` be the reflection of :g:`X <X>` in the orthocenter, H. Then X is the anticenter of the cyclic quadrilateral ABCX'. Let :g:`H_{A} <H_A>` be the orthocenter of triangle :g:`BCX << B C X >>`, Let :g:`H_{B} <H_B>` be the orthocenter of :g:`CAX << C A X >>`, and let :g:`H_{C} <H_C>` be the orthocenter of triangle :g:`ABX << A B X >>`. Then the quadrilateral :math:`HH_{A}`\ :g:`H_{B}H_{C} <[ H_B H_C ]>` is homothetic to and congruent to the cyclic quadrilateral ABCX', and X is the center of homothety. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, 9/23/2011)
