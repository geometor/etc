X(188) = 2nd MID-ARC POINT OF ANTICOMPLEMENTARY TRIANGLE — Code
===============================================================

.. enunciation::

   Let A'B'C' be the :term:`excentral triangle <excentral-triangle>` of ABC, so that A' = -1 : 1 : 1 (trilinears). Let A'' be the point where the bisector of angle BA'C meets the line BC. Define B'' and C'' cyclically. The lines AA'', BB'', CC'' concur in :ref:`X(188) <X(188)>`. (Seiichi Kirikami, February 14, 2010)
