X(927) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(926)
=====================================

.. enunciation::

   Let La, Lb, Lc be the lines through A, B, C, resp. parallel to the Gergonne line. Let Ma, Mb, Mc be the reflections of BC, CA, AB in La, Lb, Lc, resp. Let A' = Mb∩Mc, and define B' and C' cyclically. Triangle A'B'C' is inversely similar to, and 3 times the size of, ABC. The centroid of A'B'C' is :ref:`X(927) <X(927)>`; see :cite:`Hyacinthos #16741 <hyacinthos>`/16782, Sep 2008. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, March 25, 2016)
