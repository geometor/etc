.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the :term:`1st Brocard triangle <1st-brocard-triangle>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the reflection of :g:`A' <A'>` in :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically; then :ref:`X_1916 <X_1916>` is the radical center of the circumcircles of :g:`A″BC << A″ B C >>`, :g:`B″CA << B″ C A >>`, :g:`C″AB << C″ A B >>`. Let :g:`A* <A*>` be the reflection of :g:`A <A>` in :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define :g:`B* <B*>` and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically; then :ref:`X_1916 <X_1916>` is the radical center of the circumcircles of :g:`A*BC << A* B C >>`, :g:`B*CA << B* C A >>`, :g:`C*AB << C* A B >>`. The first set of circles equals the second set. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, February 10, 2016)
