X_1991
======

.. rubric:: 2nd VAN LAMOEN PERPENDICULAR BISECTORS POINT

.. enunciation::

   Erect squares inwardly on the sides of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Two edges emanate from :g:`A <A>`; let :g:`P <P>` and :g:`Q <Q>` be their endpoints. Let a' be the perpendicular bisector of :g:`PQ <[ P Q ]>`, and define b' and c' cyclically. Then a', b', c' concur in :ref:`X_1991 <X_1991>`. See :ref:`X_591 <X_591>` for the 1st Van Lamoen perpendicular bisectors point, constructed from outwardly drawn squares.
