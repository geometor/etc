.. rubric:: ORTHOCORRESPONDENT OF X_5

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`O_{A}O_{B}O_{C} << O_A O_B O_C >>` be the :term:`Kosnita triangle <kosnita-triangle>`. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line :g:`O_{B}O_{C} <[ O_B O_C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_1994 <X_1994>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, March 21, 2019)
