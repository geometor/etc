X_2091
======

.. rubric:: 2nd STEVANOVIC PERSPECTOR

.. enunciation::

   The bisector A\ :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` of angle A meets the :g:`A <A>`-excircle in two points; let :g:`A' <A'>` be the point closer to :g:`I <I>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. Then triangle :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` is perspective to the :term:`intouch triangle <intouch-triangle>`, and the perspector is :ref:`X_2091 <X_2091>`. (Milorad Stevanovic, :cite:`Hyacinthos #8088 <hyacinthos>`, 10/02/03)
