X(311) = ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF X(54) — Code
===========================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`O_{A}O_{B}O_{C} << O_A O_B O_C >>` be the Kosnita triangle. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the :term:`trilinear pole <trilinear-pole>` of line :g:`O_{B}O_{C} <[ O_B O_C ]>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in X(1994). Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the trilinear pole of line :g:`B'C' <[ B' C' ]>`, and define B″and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA″ <[ A A″ ]>`, :g:`BB″ <[ B B″ ]>`, :g:`CC″ <[ C C″ ]>` concur in :ref:`X(311) <X(311)>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 30, 2018)
