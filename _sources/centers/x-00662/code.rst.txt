.. enunciation::

Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the trilinear product :g:`A_{2} <A_2>`\ \*\ :g:`A_{3} <A_3>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_662 <X_662>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, February 10, 2016)
