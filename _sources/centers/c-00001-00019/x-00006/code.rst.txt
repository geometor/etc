X_6
====

.. rubric:: SYMMEDIAN POINT (LEMOINE POINT, GREBE POINT)

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_6 <X_6>` is the point of concurrence of the symmedians (i.e., reflections of medians in corresponding angle bisectors). :ref:`X_6 <X_6>` is the point which, when given by *actual* trilinear distances x,y,z, minimizes :math:`x^{2}` + :math:`y^{2}` + :math:`z^{2}`.

   **X_6** (*SYMMEDIAN POINT (LEMOINE POINT, GREBE POINT)*) constructed at :g:`X_6 <X_6>` = :math:`\left(\frac{1673}{295},\; \frac{1176}{295}\right)` in exact rational coordinates on the Heronian reference triangle :math:`(a, b, c) = (15, 13, 14)` over :math:`\mathbb{Q}` with vertices :g:`A <A>` = :math:`(0, 0)`, :g:`B <B>` = :math:`(14, 0)`, :g:`C <C>` = :math:`(5, 12)`.
