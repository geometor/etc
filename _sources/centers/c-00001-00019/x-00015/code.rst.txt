X_15
====

.. rubric:: 1st ISODYNAMIC POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`U <U>` and :g:`V <V>` be the points on sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` met by the interior and exterior bisectors of angle :g:`A <A>`. The circle having diameter :g:`UV <[ U V ]>` is the A-Apollonian circle. The B- and C- Apollonian circles are similarly constructed. Each circle passes through one vertex and both isodynamic points. (This configuration, based on the :term:`cevians <cevian>` of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, generalizes to arbitrary cevians; see :cite:`TCCT <tcct>`, p. 98, problem 8.)
