X_15
====

.. rubric:: 1st ISODYNAMIC POINT

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`U <U>` and :g:`V <V>` be the points on sideline :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` met by the interior and exterior bisectors of angle :g:`A <A>`. The circle having diameter :g:`UV <[ U V ]>` is the A-Apollonian circle. The B- and C- Apollonian circles are similarly constructed. Each circle passes through one vertex and both isodynamic points. (This configuration, based on the :term:`cevians <cevian>` of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, generalizes to arbitrary cevians; see :cite:`TCCT <tcct>`, p. 98, problem 8.)

   This list of circles was contributed by Peter Moses, April 17, 2021, with the following notes. Starting with a circle :ref:`X_15 <X_15>`, :ref:`X_16 <X_16>`, and a point :g:`P <P>` =\ :math:`p : q : r`, the center of the circle is given by

      a^2\*(c^2\*(a^2 + b^2 - c^2)\*p\*q + a^2\*c^2\*q^2 - b^2\*(a^2 - b^2 + c^2)\*p\*r - a^2\*b^2\*r^2) : : ,

   and the power of vertex :g:`A <A>` with repect to the circle is

      (b^2\*c^2\*(-(c^2\*(a^2 + b^2 - c^2)\*p\*q) - a^2\*c^2\*q^2 + b^2\*(a^2 - b^2 + c^2)\*p\*r + a^2\*b^2\*r^2))/((p + q + r)\*(-(b^2\*c^2\*(b^2 - c^2)\*p) + a^2\*c^2\*(a^2 - c^2)\*q - a^2\*b^2\*(a^2 - b^2)\*r)).

.. container:: note-item

   If P lies on the Lemoine axis, then the power of :g:`A <A>` with respect to to the circle is

      -b^2\*c^2\*(c^2\*q + b^2\*r)/(c^2\*(a^2 - b^2)\*q + b^2\*(a^2 - c^2)\*r)).
