X_10
====

.. rubric:: SPIEKER CENTER

.. enunciation::

   The Spieker circle is the incircle of the :term:`medial triangle <medial-triangle>`; its center, :ref:`X_10 <X_10>`, is the centroid of the perimeter of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`.
