X(50) = X(74)-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X(184) — Code
=============================================

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`DEF << D E F >>` be any equilateral triangle inscribed in the circumcircle of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`D' <D'>` be the barycentric product :g:`E*F <[ E* F ]>`, and define :g:`E' <E'>`, :g:`F' <F'>` cyclically. Then :g:`D' <D'>`,\ :g:`E' <E'>`,\ :g:`F' <F'>` all line on a line passing through :ref:`X(50) <X(50)>`. In the special case that :g:`DEF << D E F >>` is the circumtangential triangle, the points :g:`D' <D'>`,\ :g:`E' <E'>`,\ :g:`F' <F'>` lie on the Brocard axis, and in case :g:`DEF << D E F >>` is the circumnormal triangle, the points :g:`D' <D'>`,\ :g:`E'F' <[ E' F' ]>` lie on the line :ref:`X(50) <X(50)>`\ \ :ref:`X(647) <X(647)>`. See also X(6149). (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, January 29, 2015)
