Notes

.. container:: note-item

   As a point on the Euler line, :ref:`X_3146 <X_3146>` has Shinagawa coefficients :math:`(1, -4)`.

.. container:: note-item

   The point :ref:`X_3146 <X_3146>` exemplifies a theorem based on a discussion in Tran Quang Hung and :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, `Hyacinthos 25668 <http://www.hyacinthos.epizy.com/message.php?msg=25668>`__. The theorem (Peter Moses, April 10, 2017) is as follows.

.. container:: note-item

   Suppose that P is a point on a circumhyperbola H. Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the cevian triangle of :g:`P <P>` and let :g:`O' <O'>` be the circumcircle of :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>`. Let :g:`A″ <A″>` be the point other than :g:`A' <A'>` that lies on both :g:`O' <O'>` and line :g:`BC <[ B C ]>`, and define :g:`B″ <B″>` and :g:`C″ <C″>` cyclically. The orthocenter of :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>` lies on the isogonal conjugate of :g:`H <H>`.

.. container:: note-item

   Writing :g:`P' <P'>` for the orthocenter of of :g:`A″B″C″ << A″ B″ C″ >>`, examples of the mapping P → :g:`P' <P'>` include the following:

      * Jerabek hyperbola → Euler line
      * Feuerbach hyperbola → :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`\ \ :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`
      * Kiepert hyperbola → Brocard axis.
