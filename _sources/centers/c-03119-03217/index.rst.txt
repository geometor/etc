.. _preamble-0049:

.. admonition:: Group Preamble — Danneels Perspectors
   :class: note

   | Suppose :g:`A_{1}B_{1}C_{1} << A_1 B_1 C_1 >>` is the :term:`cevian triangle <cevian-triangle>` of a point :g:`X <X>`.
   | Let :g:`L_{AB} <L_AB>` be the line through :g:`B <B>` parallel to :g:`A_{1}B_{1} <[ A_1 B_1 ]>`, and let :g:`L_{AC} <L_AC>` be the line through :g:`C <C>` parallel to :g:`A_{1}C_{1} <[ A_1 C_1 ]>`.
   | Let :g:`A_{2} <A_2>` = :g:`L_{AB} <L_AB>`\ ∩\ :g:`L_{AC} <L_AC>`, and define :g:`B_{2} <B_2>` and :g:`C_{2} <C_2>` cyclically. Let

   :g:`A_{3} <A_3>` = :g:`BB_{2} <[ B B_2 ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`CC_{2} <[ C C_2 ]>`, :g:`B_{3} <B_3>` = :g:`CC_{2} <[ C C_2 ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`AA_{2} <[ A A_2 ]>`, :g:`C_{3} <C_3>` = :g:`AA_{2} <[ A A_2 ]>`\ ∩\ :g:`BB_{2} <[ B B_2 ]>`.

   Eric Danneels proves in "A Simple Perspectivity," *:cite:`Forum Geometricorum <forum-geometricorum>`* 6 (2006) 199-203, that the triangles :g:`A_{3}B_{3}C_{3} << A_3 B_3 C_3 >>` and :g:`ABC << A B C >>` are perspective. If :g:`X <X>` =\ :math:`x : y : z` (barycentrics), then the Danneels perspector P(X) is given by

   P(X) = x(y - z)\ :sup:`2` : y(z - x)\ :sup:`2` : z(x - y)\ :sup:`2`.

   If :g:`X' <X'>` denotes the :term:`isotomic conjugate <isotomic-conjugate>` of :g:`X <X>`, then P(:g:`X' <X'>`) = P(X). A few examples selected from Danneels's article follow:

   | P(:ref:`X_1 <X_1>`) = P(:ref:`X_75 <X_75>`) = :ref:`X_244 <X_244>`
   | P(:ref:`X_3 <X_3>`) = P(:ref:`X_264 <X_264>`) = :ref:`X_2972 <X_2972>`
   | P(:ref:`X_4 <X_4>`) = P(:ref:`X_69 <X_69>`) = :ref:`X_125 <X_125>`
   | P(:ref:`X_7 <X_7>`) = P(:ref:`X_8 <X_8>`) = :ref:`X_11 <X_11>`.
