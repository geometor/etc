Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   As a point on the Euler line, :ref:`X_1312 <X_1312>` has Shinagawa coefficients :math:`(J - 1, J + 3)`.

.. container:: note-item

   :ref:`X_1312 <X_1312>` is a point of intersection of the Euler line and the nine-point circle. Its antipode on the nine-point circle

   is :ref:`X_1313 <X_1313>`. Of the two points, :ref:`X_1312 <X_1312>` is the one closer to :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`. The asymptotes of the Jerabek hyperbola meet at :ref:`X_125 <X_125>` on the nine-point circle. One of the asymptotes meets the circle again at :ref:`X_1312 <X_1312>`, and the other, at :ref:`X_1313 <X_1313>`. Thus, the points :ref:`X_125 <X_125>`, :ref:`X_1312 <X_1312>`, :ref:`X_1313 <X_1313>` form a right triangle of which the midpoint of the hypotenuse :ref:`X_1312 <X_1312>`-to-:ref:`X_1313 <X_1313>` is :ref:`X_5 <X_5>`. (Peter J. C. Moses, 3/14/2003)

.. container:: note-item

   Of the points other than :ref:`X_125 <X_125>` in which the nine-point circle meets the asymptotes of the Jerabek hyperbola, :ref:`X_1312 <X_1312>` is the one farther from :ref:`X_3 <X_3>`. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, December 2, 2017)
