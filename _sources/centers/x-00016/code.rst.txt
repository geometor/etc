X(16) = 2nd ISODYNAMIC POINT — Code
===================================

.. enunciation::

   Let U and V be the points on sideline BC met by the interior and exterior bisectors of angle A. The circle having diameter UV is the A-Apollonian circle. The B- and C- Apollonian circles are similarly constructed. Each circle passes through a vertex and both isodynamic points. The :term:`pedal triangle <pedal-triangle>` of :ref:`X(16) <X(16)>` is equilateral.
