Let :g:`A* <A*>` be the diagonal :term:`crosspoint <crosspoint>` of trapezoid B':g:`C'C″B″ << C' C″ B″ >>` (i.e., the intersection of lines :g:`B'C″ <[ B' C″ ]>` and :g:`C'B″ <[ C' B″ ]>`); define :g:`B* <B*>` and :g:`C* <C*>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA* <[ A A* ]>`, :g:`BB* <[ B B* ]>`, :g:`CC* <[ C C* ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3407 <X_3407>`. (Randy Hutson, July 20, 2016)
