Notes
-----

.. container:: note-item

   Randy Huston, September 5, 2015, gives three constructions of :ref:`X_3398 <X_3398>`:

      (1) Let :g:`X <X>` be the 2nd Brocard point of :term:`pedal triangle <pedal-triangle>` of 1st Brocard point. Let Y be the 1st Brocard point of pedal triangle of 2nd Brocard point. Then :ref:`X_3398 <X_3398>` is the :term:`vertex conjugate <vertex-conjugate>` of :g:`X <X>` and Y.

      (2) Let :g:`U <U>` be the circle {{:ref:`X_371 <X_371>`,\ :ref:`X_372 <X_372>`,PU(1),PU(39)}} and V the 2nd Brocard circle. Then :ref:`X_3398 <X_3398>` is the center of the inverse-in-U of V.

      (3) Let :g:`A'B'C' << A' B' C' >>` be the 3rd Brocard triangle. Let :g:`O_{a} <O_a>` be the circumcenter of :g:`A'BC << A' B C >>`, and define :g:`O_{b} <O_b>` and :g:`O_{c} <O_c>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AO_{a} <[ A O_a ]>`, :g:`BO_{b} <[ B O_b ]>`, :g:`CO_{c} <[ C O_c ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3398 <X_3398>`.
