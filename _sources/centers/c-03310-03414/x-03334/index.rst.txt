X_3334
======

.. rubric:: PERSPECTOR OF TRIANGLES T1T2T3 AND J1J2J3

.. enunciation::

   The equilateral triangles :g:`T_{1}T_{2}T_{3} << T_1 T_2 T_3 >>` and :g:`J_{1}J_{2}J_{3} << J_1 J_2 J_3 >>` are defined just before :ref:`X_3272 <X_3272>`. The points :ref:`X_3334 <X_3334>` and :ref:`X_3335 <X_3335>`, contributed April 16, 2008 by :person:`Peter Moses <peter-moses>`, complete a list (just before :ref:`X_3272 <X_3272>` of perspectors associated with equilateral triangles.
