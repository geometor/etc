X_3363
======

.. rubric:: X_6 OF PEDAL TRIANGLE OF X_2

.. enunciation::

   :ref:`X_3363 <X_3363>` is the symmedian point of the :term:`pedal triangle <pedal-triangle>` of the centroid. A second construction uses the midpoint :ref:`X_597 <X_597>` of the symmedian point and centroid and the midpoint :ref:`X_115 <X_115>` of the two Fermat points, :ref:`X_13 <X_13>` and :ref:`X_14 <X_14>`; specifically, :ref:`X_3363 <X_3363>` is the point in which the line :ref:`X_115 <X_115>`\ \ :ref:`X_597 <X_597>` meets the Euler line. Contributed by Po-chieh Chen and Shao-cheng Liu, May 29, 2008.
