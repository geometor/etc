.. rubric:: INTERSECTION OF LINES X_1X_3 AND X_7X_1125

.. enunciation::

   Let :g:`O_{A} <O_A>` be the circle tangent to side :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` at its midpoint and to the circumcircle on the side ob :g:`BC <[ B C ]>` opposite A. Define :g:`O_{B} <O_B>` and :g:`O_{C} <O_C>` cyclically. Let :g:`A' <A'>` be the insimilicanter of :g:`O_{B} <O_B>` and :g:`O_{C} <O_C>`, and define :g:`B' <B'>` and :g:`C' <C'>` cyclically. The lines :g:`AA' <[ A A' ]>`, :g:`BB' <[ B B' ]>`, :g:`CC' <[ C C' ]>` concur in :ref:`X_3361 <X_3361>`. See the reference at :ref:`X_1001 <X_1001>`.
