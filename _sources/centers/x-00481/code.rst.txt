.. rubric:: 1st EPPSTEIN POINT

.. enunciation::

Then :ref:`X_481 <X_481>` is the point of concurrence of lines :g:`I_{A} <I_A>`-to-:g:`E_{A} <E_A>`, :g:`I_{B} <I_B>`-to-:g:`E_{B} <E_B>`, :g:`I_{C} <I_C>`-to-:g:`E_{C} <E_C>`.
