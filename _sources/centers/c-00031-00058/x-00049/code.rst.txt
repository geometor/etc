X_49
====

.. rubric:: CENTER OF SINE-TRIPLE-ANGLE CIRCLE

.. enunciation::

   Suppose that :g:`P <P>` and :g:`Q <Q>` are distinct points in the plane of a triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>` . Let :g:`P_{A} <P_A>` = reflection of :g:`P <P>` in line :g:`AQ <[ A Q ]>`, let :g:`Q_{A} <Q_A>` = reflection of :g:`Q <Q>` in line :g:`AP <[ A P ]>`, and let :g:`M_{A} <M_A>` = midpoint of segment :g:`P_{A}Q_{A} <[ P_A Q_A ]>`. Define :g:`M_{B} <M_B>` and :g:`M_{C} <M_C>` cyclically. :person:`César Lozada <cesar-lozada>` found that if :g:`Q <Q>` = :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugate>` of :g:`P <P>`, then the locus of :g:`P <P>` for which :g:`M_{A}M_{B}M_{C} << M_A M_B M_C >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` is the union of a cubic and 6 circles: specifically, the McCay cubic (K003), the circles {B,C,\ :g:`J_b <J_b>`,\ :g:`J_c <J_c>`}}, {C,A,\ :g:`J_c <J_c>`,\ :g:`J_a <J_a>`}}, {A,B,\ :g:`J_a <J_a>`,\ :g:`J_b <J_b>`}}, and the circles {B,C,\ :g:`J_a <J_a>`}}, {C,A,\ :g:`J_b <J_b>`}}, {A,B,\ :g:`J_c <J_c>`}}, where :g:`J_a <J_a>`,\ :g:`J_b <J_b>`,\ :g:`J_c <J_c>` are the excenters of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Moreover, if :g:`P <P>` = :ref:`X_3 <X_3>` and :g:`Q <Q>` = :ref:`X_4 <X_4>`, then :g:`M_{A}M_{B}M_{C} << M_A M_B M_C >>` is not only perspective, but homothetic, to :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, and the center of homothety is :ref:`X_49 <X_49>`. See `Hyacinthos 23265, June 1, 2015. <http://www.hyacinthos.epizy.com/message.php?msg=23265>`__
