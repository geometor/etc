.. container:: note-item

   Let :g:`P <P>` be a point in the plane of triangle :g:`ABC << A B C >>`, not on a sideline of :g:`ABC << A B C >>`. Let :g:`A_{1} <A_1>` be the isogonal conjugate of :g:`A <A>` with respect to triangle :g:`BCP << B C P >>`, and define :g:`B_{1} <B_1>`, :g:`C_{1} <C_1>` cyclically. Call triangle :g:`A_{1}B_{1}C_{1} << A_1 B_1 C_1 >>` the 1st isogonal triangle of :g:`P <P>`. :g:`A_{1}B_{1}C_{1} << A_1 B_1 C_1 >>` is also the reflection triangle of the isogonal conjugate of :g:`P <P>`. :g:`A_{1}B_{1}C_{1} << A_1 B_1 C_1 >>` is perspective to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` iff P lies on the Neuberg cubic. The perspector lies on cubic K060 (pK(X1989, X265), O(X5) orthopivotal cubic). Let :g:`A_{2} <A_2>` be the isogonal conjugate of :g:`A_{1} <A_1>` with respect to triangle B1C1P, and define :g:`B_{2} <B_2>`, :g:`C_{2} <C_2>` cyclically. Call triangle :g:`A_{2}B_{2}C_{2} << A_2 B_2 C_2 >>` the 2nd isogonal triangle of P. Continuing, let An be the isogonal conjugate of A(n-1) with respect to triangle B(n-1)C(n-1)P, and define B(n), C(n) cyclically. Call triangle AnBnCn the nth isogonal triangle of P. For n >= 2, all triangles AnBnCn are perspective to A(n-1)B(n-1)C(n-1). Call the perspector, Pn, the nth isogonal perspector of P. Pn is the orthocenter of A(n-1)B(n-1)C(n-1) and either the incenter or an excenter of AnBnCn. The triangles AnBnCn are all concyclic, with P as center. Call the circle the isogonal circle of P. For :g:`P <P>` = :ref:`X_1 <X_1>`, the 2nd isogonal triangle of :ref:`X_1 <X_1>` is homothetic to :g:`ABC << A B C >>` at :ref:`X_36 <X_36>`; see also :ref:`X_35 <X_35>`, :ref:`X_1478 <X_1478>`, :ref:`X_1479 <X_1479>`, :ref:`X_3583 <X_3583>`, :ref:`X_3585 <X_3585>`, X_5903, X_7741, X_7951. (:person:`Randy Hutson <randy-hutson>`, November 18, 2015)
