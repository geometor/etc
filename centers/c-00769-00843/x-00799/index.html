X799¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X798

X799 is the isogonal conjugate of X798.

Notes¶

Let La be the A-extraversion of line X1X75, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A' = Lb ∩ Lc, and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X799. (Randy Hutson, January 29, 2018)

As the trilinear product of Steiner circumellipse antipodes, X799 lies on conic {A, B, C,X668,X789}} with center X6376 and perspector X75. (Randy Hutson, July 11, 2019)
