X(862) = INTERCEPT OF EULER LINE AND TRILINEAR POLAR OF X(19)¶

As a point on the Euler line, X(862) has Shinagawa coefficients \((\$ab\$F, -\$abS_{C}\$)\).
Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = \cot C \sin 2B \sin(C - A) + \cot B \sin 2C \sin(B - A)\)
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(0.826096539919\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(g(a,b,c) : g(b,c,a) : g(c,a,b)\), where \(g(a,b,c) = (a^{2} - bc)(b + c)/(b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2})\) (M. Iliev, 5/13/07)

▸ Incident Central Lines2 lines

{2, 3}, {661, 663}

▸ Functional Relations1 relation

  • X(862) = crossdifference of every pair of points on line X(63)X(647)
