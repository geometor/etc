Notes¶

Let A' be the center of the conic through the contact points of the B- and C-excircles with the sidelines of ABC. Define B' and C' cyclically. The triangle A'B'C' is perspective to the excentral triangle at X1743. See also X6, X25, X218, X222, X940. (Randy Hutson, July 23, 2015)

In the plane of ABC, let a’ be the external bisector of A and a” its reflection in BC; define b” and c” cyclically. Then ABC and the triangle bounded by a”, b” and c” are perspective with perspector X15446. (César Lozada, October 10, 2018)
