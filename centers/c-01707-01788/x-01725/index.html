X1725¶

MIMOSA TRANSFORM OF X74

Let L denote the line having trilinears of X1725 as coefficients. Then L is the line passing through X3 perpendicular to the Euler line.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {1, 21}, {33, 46}

  • Related Centers: X1 (INCENTER), X21 (SCHIFFLER POINT), X33 (PERSPECTOR OF THE ORTHIC AND INTANGENTS TRIANGLES), X46 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X1)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = - (\cos A)/x + (\cos B)/y + (\cos C)/z\),\(x : y : z\) = X74
Barycentrics: \(af(a,b,c) : bf(b,c,a) : cf(c,a,b)\)
