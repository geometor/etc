X1770¶

MIMOSA TRANSFORM OF X943

X1770 = cevapoint of X46 and X1717

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {1, 7}, {4, 46}

  • Related Centers: X46 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X1), X1717 (MIMOSA TRANSFORM OF X35), X1 (INCENTER), X7 (GERGONNE POINT), X4 (ORTHOCENTER)

Notes¶

Let IaIbIc be the reflection triangle of X1. Let A' be the cevapoint of Ib and Ic, and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X1770. (Randy Hutson, July 20, 2016)
