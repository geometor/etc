X(275) = CEVAPOINT OF ORTHOCENTER AND SYMMEDIAN POINT¶

Let A'B'C' be the circumorthic triangle. Let A″ be the trilinear pole of line B'C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X(275). (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
Trilinears: \(\csc 2A \sec(B - C) : \csc 2B \sec(C - A) : \csc 2C \sec(A - B)\)
Barycentrics: \(\sec A \sec(B - C) : \sec B \sec(C - A) : \sec C \sec(A - B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-6.110630103881\)
