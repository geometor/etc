X2420¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X2394

Centers X2420 to X2445, on the cubic K162, are isogonal conjugates of centers X2394 to X2419, which are on the Simson cubic, K010. For details, see the note just before X2394.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 6}, {110, 112}

  • Related Centers: X476 (TIXIER POINT), X648 (TRILINEAR POLE OF EULER LINE), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X110 (FOCUS OF KIEPERT PARABOLA), X112 (Ψ(ORTHOCENTER, SYMMEDIAN POINT))

Trilinears: \(1/f(a,b,c) : 1/f(b,c,a) : 1/f(c,a,b)\), where f(a,b,c) is as given just before X2394
Barycentrics: \(a/f(a,b,c) : b/f(b,c,a) : c/f(c,a,b)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-0.326744904123\)

X2420 lies on the cubic K162.
