X2393¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X2373

As the isogonal conjugate of a point on the circumcircle, X2393 lies on the line at infinity.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {6, 25}, {66, 69}

  • Related Centers: X2373 (CIR(X67)), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X25 (HOMOTHETIC CENTER OF ORTHIC AND TANGENTIAL TRIANGLES), X66 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X22), X69 (SYMMEDIAN POINT OF THE ANTICOMPLEMENTARY TRIANGLE)

Trilinears: \(1/U : 1/V : 1/W\), where \(U : V : W\) = X2373
Barycentrics: \(a^{2} (a^{4} b^{2} + a^{4} c^{2} - 2 a^{2} b^{2} c^{2} - b^{6} + b^{4} c^{2} + b^{2} c^{4} - c^{6}) : :\)
▸ Incident Central Lines10 lines

{6, 25}, {23, 895}, {30, 511}, {66, 69}, {67, 1205}, {141, 1368}, {157, 577}, {160, 216}, {401, 1632}, {599, 1853}

▸ Functional Relations5 relations

  • X2393 = isogonal conjugate of X2373

  • X2393 = Xi-Ceva conjugate of Xj for these (i,j): (4, 1560), (23, 187), (895, 6), (935, 647)

  • X2393 = crosspoint of Xi and Xj for these (i,j): (4, 111), (6, 67)

  • X2393 = crosssum of Xi and Xj for these (i,j): (2, 23), (3, 524), (669, 1648)

  • X2393 = homothetic center of X3- and X6-Ehrmann triangles; see X25

Notes¶

Let A″B″C″ be the 2nd Ehrmann triangle. Let Pa be the pole of line B″C″ wrt the A-Ehrmann circle, and define Pb and Pc cyclically. The lines A”Pa, B”Pb, C”Pc concur in X2393. (Randy Hutson, November 18, 2015)
