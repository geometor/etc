Notes¶

Five constructions by Randy Hutson, January 29, 2015:

  1. Let SymaSymbSymc be the tangential triangle. Let A″ be the pole, wrt the polar circle, of line SymbSymc, and define B″, C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X264.

  2. Let SymaSymbSymc be the symmedial triangle. Let A″ be the pole, wrt the polar circle, of line SymbSymc, and define B″, C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X264.

  3. Let SymaSymbSymc be the circumsymmedial triangle. Let A″ be the pole, wrt the polar circle, of line SymbSymc, and define B″, C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X264.

  4. Let SymaSymbSymc be the Lucas(t) central triangle (for any t). Let A″ be the pole, wrt the polar circle, of line SymbSymc, and define B″, C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X264.

  5. X264 is the trilinear pole of the line X297X525. This line is the isotomic conjugate of the MacBeath circumconic, which is the isogonal conjugate of the orthic axis. The line is also the polar of X6 wrt the polar circle, and the radical axis of the polar and orthosymmedial circles, and the polar conjugate of the circumcircle)

Let A' be the trilinear product of the vertices of the A-anti-altimedial triangle, and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X264. (Randy Hutson, November 2, 2017)
