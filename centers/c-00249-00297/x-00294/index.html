X294¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X241

X294 is the isogonal conjugate of X241.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {6, 7}, {1, 41}

  • Related Centers: X241 (-LINE CONJUGATE OF X55), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X7 (GERGONNE POINT), X1 (INCENTER), X41 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X31)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = (b + c - a)/(b^{2} + c^{2} - ab - ac)\)
