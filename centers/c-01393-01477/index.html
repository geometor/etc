X1393–X1477¶

Beth Conjugates & Harmonic Conjugates

Fixed-point P-beth conjugates of P and harmonic conjugates along central lines.

Group Preamble — Beth Conjugates 1393-1477

If X is a triangle center then X = P-beth conjugate of P for some triangle center P. Using the definition of beth conjugate (in the Glossary), it is easy to prove that the P-beth conjugate of P is the trilinear product P*X56,
so that P = X*X8. In trilinears,

if P =\(p : q : r\), then (P-beth conjugate of P) =\(up : vq : wr\), where

\(u : v : w\) = a/(b + c - a) : b/(c + a - b) : c/(a + b - c), or equivalently,
\(u : v : w\) = 1 - cos A : 1 - cos B : 1 - cos C.
