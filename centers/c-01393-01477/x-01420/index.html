X1420¶

X145-BETH CONJUGATE OF X145

X1420 = homothetic center of 1st Johnson-Yff triangle and cross-triangle of Aquila and anti-Aquila triangles

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {1, 3}, {34, 106}

  • Related Centers: X145 (ANTICOMPLEMENT OF NAGEL POINT), X1 (INCENTER), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X34 (-BETH CONJUGATE OF X4), X106 (Λ(INCENTER, CENTROID))

Trilinears: \((1 - \cos A) u(a,b,c)\), where \(u :v : w\) = X145
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-0.073173528548\)

Notes¶

Let Ja, Jb, Jc be the excenters and I the incenter. Let A' be the centroid of JbJcI, and define B' and C' cyclically; A'B'C' is also the cross-triangle of the excentral and 2nd circumperp triangles. The triangle A'B'C' is homothetic to the Hutson intouch triangle at X1420. (Randy Hutson, July 31 2018)
