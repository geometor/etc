X4000¶

X346com[T(-a, c)]

As described in the preamble to X3758, the notation T(-a, c) represents the central triangle whose A-vertex is the point \(-a : c : b\). This triangle and seven others are discussed just before X4357.

Constructed Elements

  • Derived Triangle: Medial Triangle △MaMbMc — vertices Ma, Mb, Mc (Formed by the midpoints of sides BC, CA, AB)

  • Central Lines: {2, 346}

  • Related Centers: X2 (CENTROID), X346 (ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF X279)

Barycentrics: \(a^{2} + b^{2} + c^{2} - 2bc : :\)

Notes¶

X4000 is the center of the inellipse that is the barycentric square of the Gergonne line. The Brianchon point (perspector) of the inellipse is X279. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)
