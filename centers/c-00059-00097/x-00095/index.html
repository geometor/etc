X95¶

CEVAPOINT OF CENTROID AND CIRCUMCENTER

X95 is the cevapoint of centroid and circumcenter.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {54, 69}, {2, 97}

  • Related Centers: X2 (CENTROID), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X54 (KOSNITA POINT), X69 (SYMMEDIAN POINT OF THE ANTICOMPLEMENTARY TRIANGLE), X97 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X53)

Trilinears: \(b^{2}c^{2}\sec(B - C) : :\)

Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the symmedial triangle. Let La be the reflection of line B'C' in line BC, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A″ = Lb∩Lc, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X95. (Randy Hutson, August 19, 2015)

Let A' be the intersection, other than A, of the circumcircle and the branch of the Lucas cubic that contains A, and define B' and C' cyclically. The triangle A'B'C' is here introduced as the Lucas triangle (not to be confused with the Lucas central triangle). The vertices A', B', C' lie on the rectangular hyperbola {X2,X20,X54,X69,X110,X2574,X2575,X2979}}. (See https://bernard-gibert.pagesperso-orange.fr/Exemples/k007.html.) Also, X95 is the trilinear product of the vertices of the Lucas triangle. (Randy Hutson, August 19, 2015)
