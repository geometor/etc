X92¶

Notes¶

Alexei Myakishev, “The M-Configuration of a Triangle,” Forum Geometricorum 3 (2003) 135-144,

proves that the lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X92. He notes that another construction follows from Proposition 2 of the article: let A1 be the midpoint of the arc BC of the circumcircle that passes through A, and let A2 be the point, other than A, in which the A-altitude meets the circumcircle. Let A″ = A1A2∩BC. Define B″ and C″ cyclically. Then the lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X92.

Suppose that T = A'B'C' is a central triangle. Let A″ be the pole with respect to the polar circle of the line B'C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The appearance of T in the following list means that the lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X92: Feurerbach, incentral, excentral, extangents, Apollonius, mixtilinear excentral. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)
