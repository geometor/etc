Let A'B'C' be the 1st Brocard triangle. Let A″ be the reflection of A in line B'C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. Let A’’’ be the reflection of A' in line BC, and define B’’’ and C’’’ cyclically. Let A* = B″B’’’∩C″C’’’, and define B*and C* cyclically. The lines AA*, BB*, CC* concur in X671, which is also X2-of-A*B*C*. (Randy Hutson, July 11, 2019)
