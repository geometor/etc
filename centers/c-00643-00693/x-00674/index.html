X674¶

CROSSDIFFERENCE OF X6 AND X514

As the isogonal conjugate of a point on the circumcircle, X674 lies on the line at infinity.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {6, 31}, {51, 210}

  • Related Centers: X675 (TRILINEAR POLE OF LINE X6X514), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X31 (2nd POWER POINT), X51 (CENTROID OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE), X210 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X37)
