Notes¶

Let La be the line through A parallel to the de Longchamps line, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let Ma be the reflection of BC in La, and define Mb and Mc cyclically. Let A' = Mb∩Mc, and define B', C' cyclically. The triangle A'B'C' is inversely similar to, and 3 times the size of, ABC. Let A″B″C″ be the reflection of A'B'C' in the de Longchamps line. The triangle A″B″C″ is homothetic to ABC, with center of homothety X1495. (See Hyacinthos #16741/16782, Sep 2008.) (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)
