X1482¶

REFLECTION OF CIRCUMCENTER IN INCENTER

X1482 is the reflection of circumcenter in incenter.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 1}

  • Related Centers: X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X1 (INCENTER)
