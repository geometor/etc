X1505¶

EXSIMILICENTER OF MOSES AND 2nd LEMOINE CIRCLES

X1505 is the exsimilicenter of moses and 2nd Lemoine circles.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 6}, {2, 589}

  • Related Centers: X1307 (COLLINGS TRANSFORM OF X640), X512 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X99), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X2 (CENTROID), X589 (2nd KENMOTU-VAN LAMOEN POINT)
