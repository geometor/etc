X1502¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1501

X1502 is the isogonal conjugate of X1501.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {76, 141}, {99, 160}

  • Related Centers: X1501 (TRILINEAR 5th POWER POINT), X76 (3rd BROCARD POINT), X141 (COMPLEMENT OF SYMMEDIAN POINT), X99 (STEINER POINT), X160 (-OF-TANGENTIAL-TRIANGLE)

Notes¶

X1502 is the Brianchon point (perspector) of the inellipse that is the barycentric square of the de Longchamps line. The center of this inellipse is X626. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)
