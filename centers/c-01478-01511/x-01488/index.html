X1488¶

2nd STEVANOVIC POINT

X1488 = X1-cross conjugate of X174

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {1, 166}, {57, 173}

  • Related Centers: X1 (INCENTER), X174 (YFF CENTER OF CONGRUENCE), X166 (GERGONNE POINT OF EXCENTRAL TRIANGLE), X57 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X9), X173 (CONGRUENT ISOSCELIZERS POINT)

Trilinears: \(1/[1 + \sin(A/2)] : :\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(2.088904139589\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(\sec^{2}(A'/2) : :\), where A'B'C' is the excentral triangle

▸ Incident Central Lines6 lines

{1, 166}, {7, 2089}, {57, 173}, {145, 188}, {557, 1274}, {558, 1143}

▸ Functional Relations4 relations

  • X1488 = X1-cross conjugate of X174

  • X1488 = SS(A→A') of X7, where A'B'C' is the excentral triangle

  • X1488 = trilinear pole of Monge line of incircles of BCI, CAI, ABI

  • X1488 = trilinear pole of Monge line of I-excircles of BCI, CAI, ABI

Notes¶

Let U be the A-excenter of triangle ABC; let A' be the incenter of triangle UBC, and define B', C' cyclically. Let A″ = IA'∩BC, and define B″, C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X1488. (Milorad R. Stevanovic, Hyacinthos #7185, 5/21/03. See also X1130 and X1489.)
