X118¶

MIDPOINT OF X4 AND X101

X118 is the midpoint of X4 and X101.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {4, 101}

  • Related Centers: X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X101 (Ψ(INCENTER, SYMMEDIAN POINT))

Trilinears: \(g(b,c,a) + g(c,b,a) : :\), where \(g(b,c,a) = b^{3}c/[(b - c) \cot A + (b - a) \cot C]\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-2.178056337287\)

X118 lies on the nine-point circle.

▸ Functional Relations10 relations

  • X118 = complementary conjugate of X516

  • X118 = complement of X103

  • X118 = anticenter of cyclic quadrilateral ABCX101

  • X118 = X103-of-medial triangle.

  • X118 = Λ(X1, X7), wrt orthic triangle

  • X118 = Λ(X4, X9), wrt orthic triangle

Notes¶

Let HaHbHc be the orthic triangle. Let La be the Gergonne line of triangle AHbHc, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A″ = Lb∩Lc, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines HaA″, HbB″, HcC″ concur in X118, which is X3-of-A″B″C″. (Randy Hutson, July 31 2018)
