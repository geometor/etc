Notes¶

Let HaHbHc be the orthic triangle. Let L be the line through Ha parallel to the Euler line, and define M and N cyclically. Let L' be the reflection of L in sideline BC, and define M' and N' cyclically. The lines L', M', N' concur in X113. (Randy Hutson, August 26, 2014)

Let HaHbHc be the orthic triangle. Let MA be the reflection of the orthic axis in line HbHc, and define Let MB and Let MC cyclically. Let A″ = Let MB∩MC, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines HaA″, HbB″ HcC″ concur in X113. (Randy Hutson, August 26, 2014)

Let HaHbHc be the orthic triangle. Let NA be the orthic axis of AHbHc, and define NB and NC cyclically. Let A″ = NB∩NC, B″ = NC∩AC, C″ = NA∩BC. Then triangle A″B″C″ is inversely similar to ABC, with similitude center X6, and the lines HaA″, HbB″, HcC″ concur in X113. Also, X113 = X3-of-A″B″C″. (Randy Hutson, August 26, 2014)
