X(401) = BAILEY POINT¶

Group Preamble

Centers 401- 475, 2- 4, 20- 30, 376, 379, 381- 384 (and others) lie on the Euler line.

Coordinates¶

Trilinears¶

  • \([\sin 2B \sin 2C - \sin^{2}(2A)](\csc A) : :\)

Barycentrics¶

  • \(\sin 2B \sin 2C - \sin^{2}(2A) : :\)

  • \(\tan B + \tan C - \tan A - \cot \omega : :\)

  • \(a^8 - 2 a^6 (b^2 + c^2) + a^4 (b^4 + b^2 c^2 + c^4) + b^2 c^2 (b^2 - c^2)^2 : :\)

  • \(\tan A - \cot \omega ' : :\), where ω’ is the Brocard angle of the orthic triangle

  • Kimberling (6, 9, 13) Search Value: \(3.684283855313\)

Linear Combinations¶

  • X(401) = 3 SA SB SC SW X(2) - S^4 X(20)

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X(401) has Shinagawa coefficients (EF + F2 - S2, 2S2).
