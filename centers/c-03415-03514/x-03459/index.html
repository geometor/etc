X3459¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X195

X3459 is the isogonal conjugate of X195.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {5, 195}, {137, 252}

  • Related Centers: X195 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X3), X5 (NINE-POINT CENTER), X137 (-OF-ORTHIC-TRIANGLE), X252 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X143)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = y(195)z(195)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-0.307549623304\)

X3459 lies on the Napoleon cubic.

Notes¶

Let A' be the reflection of X5 in BC, and define B' and C' cyclically. Let Oa be the circumcenter of A'BC, and define Ob and Oc cyclically. The lines AOa, BOb, COc concur in X3459. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)
