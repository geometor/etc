X1662–X1706¶

Circle-Related Points

Internal and external centers of similitude among the circumcircle, incircle, nine-point circle, Brocard circle, 1st and 2nd Lemoine circles, Apollonius circle, and Bevan circle.

Group Preamble — Circle-related Points 1662-1706

The next cluster of points were contributed by Peter J. C. Moses during August, 2003. Symbols used to represent functions of a,b,c (or A, B, C) include

σ = area of triangle ABC
ω = arccot(cot A + cot B + cot C) = arccot[(\(a^{2}\) + \(b^{2}\) +\(c^{2})\)/(4σ)]; ω is the Brocard angle of ABC
e = \(\sqrt{1 - 4 \sin^{2}\omega }\) (as in Gallatly, p. 96, along with other formulas involving ω)
s = (a + b + c)/2 = semiperimeter of ABC
r = σ/s = inradius of ABC

Circles mentioned in this section are the following:
