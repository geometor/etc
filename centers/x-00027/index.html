X(27) = CEVAPOINT OF ORTHOCENTER AND CLAWSON CENTER¶

Coordinates¶

Trilinears¶

  • \((\sec A)/(b + c) : (\sec B)/(c + a) : (\sec C)/(a + b)\)

Barycentrics¶

  • \((\tan A)/(b + c) : (\tan B)/(c + a) : (\tan C)/(a + b)\)

  • Kimberling (6, 9, 13) Search Value: \(-2.596826036189\)

Linear Combinations¶

  • X(27) = 3 SA SB SC X(2) - 2 S^2 s^2 X(3)

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X(27) has Shinagawa coefficients (F, -E - F - $bc$).

X(27) is the {X(2),X(4)}-harmonic conjugate of X(469).
