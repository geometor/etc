X502¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X501

X502 is the isogonal conjugate of X501.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {10, 191}, {594, 3678}

  • Related Centers: X1 (INCENTER), X10 (SPIEKER CENTER), X191 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X1), X594 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X593), X3678 (com(EXTOUCH TRIANGLE))

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = bc(b + c)/[a^{3} - b^{3} - c^{3} - bc(a + b + c) + ab(a - b) + ac(a - c)]\)
Barycentrics: \(af(a,b,c) : bf(b,c,a) : cf(c,a,b)\)

Notes¶

Let IaIbIc be the incentral triangle. Let BCA″ be the triangle similar to IaIbIc such that the segment AA″ crosses the line BC. Define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X502. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)
