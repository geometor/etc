Notes¶

Let HaHbHc be the orthic triangle of a triangle ABC, and let

  • O(A) = circle with center A and radius AHa, and define O(B) and O(C) cyclically

  • p(A) = polar of X4 wrt O(A), and define p(B) and p(C) cyclically

  • A″ = p(B)∩p(C), and define B″ and C″ cyclically.

Then A″B″C″ is homothetic to ABC, and the center of homothety is X2052. (Angel Montesdeoca, September 30, 2016)
