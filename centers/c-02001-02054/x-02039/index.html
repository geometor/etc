X2039¶

1st NINE-POINT-CIRCLE-KIEPERT-ASYMPTOTES INTERSECTION

Of the points other than X115 in which the nine-point circle meets the asymptotes of the Kiepert hyperbola, X2039 is the one nearer to X3.
