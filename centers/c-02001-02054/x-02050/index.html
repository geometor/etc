X2050¶

{X3,X5}-HARMONIC CONJUGATE OF X2049

X2050 is the {X3,X5}-harmonic conjugate of X2049.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 5} (Euler line)

  • Related Centers: X2049 (EULER LINE INTERCEPT OF LINE X), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X5 (NINE-POINT CENTER)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = (s^{2} - r^{2})\cos A - (s^{2} + r^{2})\cos(B - C)\), where r = inradius, s = semiperimeter
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-5.328603145420\)
▸ Incident Central Lines4 lines

{2, 3}, {11, 1460}, {171, 1699}, {1482, 1999}

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X2050 has Shinagawa coefficients \((r^{2}, s^{2})\).
