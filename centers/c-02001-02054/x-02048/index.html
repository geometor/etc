X2048¶

{X3,X5}-HARMONIC CONJUGATE OF X2047

X2048 is the {X3,X5}-harmonic conjugate of X2047.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 5} (Euler line)

  • Related Centers: X2047 (EULER LINE INTERCEPT OF LINE X), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X5 (NINE-POINT CENTER)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = (s - r)\cos A - (s + r)\cos(B - C)\), where r = inradius, s = semiperimeter
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-3.468071768266\)
▸ Incident Central Lines2 lines

{2, 3}, {486, 1686}

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X2048 has Shinagawa coefficients \((r, s)\).
