X98–X112¶

Centers on the Circumcircle

Notable points on the circumcircle of ABC, including the Tarry point, Steiner point, anticomplementary, Feuerbach, Kiepert, and Jerabek antipodes, and centers derived from circumcircle mappings.

Group Preamble — Centers on the circumcircle: X74, X98-X112

Centers X74, X98-X112, and many others in ETC lie on the circumcircle. Mappings Λ and Ψ derived from such a point P for application to points X, are defined here:

Λ(P,X) = isogonal conjugate of the point where line PX meets the line at infinity.

Let Y = Λ(P,X), let Q = isogonal conjugate of P, and let Y and Z be the points where line YQ meets the circumcircle;
then Ψ(P,X) = Z.
