X68¶

PRASOLOV POINT

Coordinates for X68 can be obtained easily from the Ceva ratios given his Prasolov’s proof of concurrence.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {155, 5}

  • Related Centers: X155 (EIGENCENTER OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE), X5 (NINE-POINT CENTER)

Trilinears: \(\cos A \sec 2A : \cos B \sec 2B : \cos C \sec 2C\)
Barycentrics: \(\tan 2A : \tan 2B : \tan 2C\)

Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the 2nd Euler triangle. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X68, as proved in V. V. Prasolov, Zadachi po planimetrii, Moscow, 4th edition, 2001.

Let Oa be the circle centered at the A-vertex of the orthic triangle and passing through A; define Ob and Oc cyclically. Then X68 is the radical center of Oa, Ob, Oc. (Randy Hutson, November 2, 2017)

The X3-Fuhrmann triangle is inversely similar to ABC, with similitude center X3, and perspective to ABC at X68. (Randy Hutson, November 3, 2017)
