X3350¶

INTERSECTION OF LINES X3X_2131 AND X4X_1073

X3350 = X2-Ceva conjugate of X3344

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {4, 1073}, {3, 2131}

  • Related Centers: X2 (CENTROID), X3344 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X3343), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X1073 (-CROSS CONJUGATE OF X3), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X2131 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X2130)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = bc[x + y + z - 2yz(y + z - x)/(y^{2} + z^{2} - x^{2})]\), where x : y : \(z = 1/(b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2}) : 1/(c^{2} + a^{2} - b^{2}) : 1/(a^{2} + b^{2} - c^{2})\) (M. Iliev, 10/27/08)
Barycentrics: \(af(a,b,c) : bf(b,c,a) : cf(c,a,b)\)
