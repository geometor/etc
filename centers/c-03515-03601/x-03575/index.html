Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X3575 has Shinagawa coefficients \((F, -E - 3F)\).

Let KH denote the hyperbola discussed at X1112. As noted in the paper cited at X1112, KH is the X4-Ceva conjugate of the Euler line. Inversely, the Euler line is the X4-Ceva conjugate of KH. Since X3574 lies on KH, its X4-Ceva conjugate, which is X3575, lies on the Euler line. (Peter Moses, July 7, 2009)
