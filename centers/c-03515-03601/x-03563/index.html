Notes¶

The antipodal pair X3563 to X3565 were described by Peter Moses, Dec. 13, 2004.

Let A' be the reflection in line BC of the A-vertex of the antipedal triangle of X6, and define B' and C' cyclically. Let OA be the circumcenter of AB'C', and define OB and OC cyclically. Let U be the circumcenter of A'BC, and define V and W cyclically. Let O' be the circumcenter of OAOBOC. Let O″ be the circumcenter of UVW. Then X3563 = Λ(O',O″). Also, O'O″∩(infinity line) = X3564. (Randy Hutson, June 19, 2015)
