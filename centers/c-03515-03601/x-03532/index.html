X3532¶

INTERSECTION OF LINES X(6,1204) AND X(64,1620)

Let DEF be the cevian triangle of X69 and A' the center of the circle that passes through D and through points where the line EF cuts to the circumcircle, and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X3532. (Angel Montesdeoca, October 30, 2019)

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {72, 165}, {68, 550}

  • Related Centers: X1151 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1131), X1152 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1132), X72 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X28), X165 (CENTROID OF THE EXCENTRAL TRIANGLE), X68 (PRASOLOV POINT), X550 (MIDPOINT OF X3 AND X20)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = 1/(4 \cos A - 3 \sin B \sin C)\)
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(10.066330179103\)

X3532 lies on the Jerabek hyperbola.
