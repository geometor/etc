X3527¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X631

X3527 is the isogonal conjugate of X631.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 51}, {5, 69}

  • Related Centers: X631 (3/5*OG), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X51 (CENTROID OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE), X5 (NINE-POINT CENTER), X69 (SYMMEDIAN POINT OF THE ANTICOMPLEMENTARY TRIANGLE)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = 1/(\cos A + \sin B \sin C)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-0.759241529748\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates2 forms

  • Trilinears: \(g(A,B,C) : g(B,C,A) : g(C,A,B)\), where \(g(A,B,C) = 1/(\sec A + 2 \sec B \sec C)\)

  • Trilinears: \(1/(2 \cos A + \cos B \cos C) : :\)

▸ Incident Central Lines12 lines

{3, 51}, {5, 69}, {6, 1598}, {24, 3431}, {25, 54}, {64, 389}, {68, 381}, {72, 1482}, {73, 999}, {74, 1112}, {185, 3426}, {1092, 3066}

▸ Functional Relations4 relations

  • X3527 = isogonal conjugate of X631

  • X3527 = isotomic conjugate of polar conjugate of X34818

  • X3527 = X1181-cross conjugate of X3

  • X3527 = perspector of 2nd Lemoine circle

Notes¶

Let HA be the foot of the A-altitude. Let BA and CA be the feet of perpendiculars from HA to CA and AB, resp. Let GA be the centroid of HABACA. Define GB and GC cyclically. The lines AGA, BGB, CGC concur in X3527. (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
