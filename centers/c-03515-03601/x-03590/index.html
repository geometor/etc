X3590¶

1st DIXIT POINT

The radius of circle TA is |BC|/6, and the Kiepert angle (e.g., B-to-C-to-X3890) is arctan(2/3).

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {20, 1327}, {590, 1131}

  • Related Centers: X20 (DE LONGCHAMPS POINT), X1327 (ARCTAN(3) KIEPERT POINT), X590 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X588), X1131 (ARCTAN(2) KIEPERT POINT)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = bc/(b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2} + 6 \operatorname{area}(ABC))\)
Barycentrics: 1/(\(2S_{A}\) + 3S) : 1/(\(2S_{B}\) + 3S) : 1/(\(2S_{C}\) + 3S) [Conway notation]
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(1.253197919441\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(g(A,B,C) : g(B,C,A) : g(C,A,B)\), where \(g(A, B, C) = 1/(2 \cos A + 3 \sin A)\)

X3590 lies on the Kiepert hyperbola.

▸ Incident Central Lines14 lines

{2, 3594}, {4, 6221}, {6, 3591}, {20, 1327}, {76, 3595}, {140, 3316}, {485, 3523}, {486, 5056}, {590, 1131}, {1132, 3068}, {1271, 5490}, {1328, 3091}, {1656, 3317}, {3543, 6482}

Notes¶

On Dec. 22, 2000, Atul Dixit constructed this point in Hyacinthos message #2183, as follows. Let ABC be a triangle with medians AD, BE, FC and centroid G. Construct semicircles with diameters BD, DC, BC outwardly. Let TA be the circle tangent to the three semicircles BD, DC, BC, and let A' be the center of TA. Define B' and C' cyclically. Then the lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X3590. Barycentric coordinates were found by Paul Yiu (2000), and further properties and related points, by Peter Moses (2010).
