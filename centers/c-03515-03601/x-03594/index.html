X3594¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF 2nd DIXIT POINT

X3594 is the isogonal conjugate of 2nd dixit point.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 6}, {486, 546}

  • Related Centers: X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X486 (INNER VECTEN POINT), X546 (MIDPOINT OF X4 AND X5)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = a(b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2} - 6 \operatorname{area}(ABC))\)
Barycentrics: \(a^{2}(2S_{A} - 3S)\) : \(b^{2}(2S_{B} - 3S)\) : \(c^{2}(2S_{C} - 3S)\) [Conway notation]
