X3558¶

2nd PAPPUS POINT

X3558 = crosssum of PU(119)

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 6}, {4, 3413}

  • Related Centers: X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X3413 (1st KIEPERT INFINITY POINT)

Trilinears: \(e \sin A - \sin(A - \omega ) : :\), where e is defined at X1340
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-7.928813637710\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(a(a^{2} - Z)\), where \(Z^{2} = a^{4} + b^{4} + c^{4} - b^{2}c^{2} - c^{2}a^{2} - a^{2}b^{2}\)

▸ Linear Combinations2 combinations

  • X3558 = \(2\) X3 - \(3\) X1341

  • X3558 = \(4\) X39 - \(3\) X1340

X3558 lies on the cubics K028, K289, K704.

▸ Incident Central Lines4 lines

{3, 6}, {4, 3413}, {194, 3414}, {262, 6177}

▸ Functional Relations6 relations

  • X3558 = reflection of Xi in Xj for these (i,j): (1380, 2028), (3557, 3095)

  • X3558 = isogonal conjugate of X6178

  • X3558 = crosssum of PU(119)

  • X3558 = crosspoint of PU(119)

  • X3558 = inverse-in-2nd-Brocard-circle of X1340

  • X3558 = {Xi,Xj}-harmonic conjugate of Xk for these (i,j,k): (3, 576, 3557), (6, 32, 3557), (6, 1380, 1340), (6, 2028, 1341), (61, 62, 3557), (371, 372, 1379), (1670, 1671, 1340), (1687, 1688, 2559), (1689, 1690, 3557), (3104, 3105, 3557)

Notes¶

X3558 is the crosspoint (and crosssum) of the imaginary foci of the Steiner inellipse. (Bernard Gibert, January 4, 2015)

X3558 is the Brocard axis intercept, other than X1341, of the circle {{X1341,PU(1)}}. Also, X3558 is the exsimilicenter of the 2nd Lemoine circle and the circle {{X371,X372,PU(1),PU(39)}}. (Randy Hutson, January 5, 2015)
