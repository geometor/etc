X3557¶

1st PAPPUS POINT

X3557 was submitted and called the Pappus point by Roland Bacher (March 11, 2009); coordinates and the related point X3558 were found by Peter Moses (March 12, 2009).

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 6}, {4, 3414}

  • Related Centers: X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X3414 (2nd KIEPERT INFINITY POINT)

Trilinears: \(e \sin A + \sin(A - \omega ) : :\), where e is defined at X1340
Barycentrics: \(ag(a,b,c) : bg(b,c,a) : cg(c,a,b)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(0.622006435169\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(g(a,b,c) : g(b,c,a) : g(c,a,b)\), where \(g(a,b,c) = a(a^{2} + Z)\), where \(Z^{2} = a^{4} + b^{4} + c^{4} - b^{2}c^{2} - c^{2}a^{2} - a^{2}b^{2}\)

▸ Linear Combinations2 combinations

  • X3557 = \(2\) X3 - \(3\) X1340

  • X3557 = \(4\) X39 - \(3\) X1341

X3557 lies on the cubics K028, K289, K704.

▸ Incident Central Lines4 lines

{3, 6}, {4, 3414}, {194, 3413}, {262, 6178}

▸ Functional Relations6 relations

  • X3557 = reflection of Xi in Xj for these (i,j): (1379, 2029), (3558, 3095)

  • X3557 = isogonal conjugate of X6177

  • X3557 = inverse-in-2nd-Brocard-circle of X1341

  • X3557 = {Xi,Xj}-harmonic conjugate of Xk for these (i,j,k): (3, 576, 3558), (6, 32, 3558), (6, 1379, 1341), (6, 2029, 1340), (61, 62, 3558), (371, 372, 1380), (1670, 1671, 1341), (1687, 1688, 2558), (1689, 1690, 3558), (3104, 3105, 3558)

  • X3557 = crosssum of PU(118)

  • X3557 = crosspoint of PU(118)

Notes¶

X3557 is the crosspoint (and crosssum) of the real foci of the Steiner inellipse. (Bernard Gibert, January 4, 2015)

X3557 is the Brocard axis intercept, other than X1340, of the circle {{X1340,PU(1)}}. Also, X3557 is the insimilicenter of the 2nd Lemoine circle and the circle {{X371,X372,PU(1),PU(39)}}. (Randy Hutson, January 5, 2015)
