Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X3534 has Shinagawa coefficients \((5, -9)\).

Let MaMbMc be the medial triangle. Let A″ be the pole of line MbMc wrt the A-power circle, and define B″, C″ cyclically. X3534 = X2-of-A″B″C″. (Randy Hutson, March 14, 2018)

Let OA be the circle centered at the A-vertex of the Gemini triangle 107 and passing through A; define OB and OC cyclically. X3534 is the radical center of OA, OB, OC. (Randy Hutson, August 28, 2020)
