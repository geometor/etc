Notes¶

Let P(2) and U(2) be the 1st and 2nd Beltrami points (as indexed at Bicentric Pairs, accessible using the Tables button at the top of ETC), and let P(40) and U(40) be the isogonal conjugates of P(2) and U(2), respectively. Then X3569 is the point of intersection of the lines P(2)U(2) and P(40)U(40). The name “Beltrami-Parry Point” signifies the fact that X3569 lies on the line X74X111; where X111 is the Parry point.and X74 is the isogonal conjugate of the Euler infinity point. See also X3568. Contributed by Chris van Tienhoven, June 7, 2009..
