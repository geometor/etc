X3574¶

INTERSECTION OF LINES X4X_54 AND X5X_51

X3574 = midpoint of X4 and X54

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {4, 54}

  • Related Centers: X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X54 (KOSNITA POINT)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = bc[a^{2}(b^{2} + c^{2}) - (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2}](2a^{6} + b^{6} + c^{6} - 3a^{4}b^{2} - 3a^{4}c^{2} - b^{2}c^{4} - b^{4}c^{2})\)
Barycentrics: \(af(a,b,c) : bf(b,c,a) : cf(c,a,b)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-5.932189231698\)

▸ Functional Relations7 relations

  • X3574 = X21-of-the-orthic-triangle if ABC is acute. Let HaHbHc be the orthic triangle, which is the pedal (and cevian) triangle of the orthocenter, H = X4 . The Euler lines of the triangles HHbHc, HHcHa, HHaHb concur in X3574. (Michel Garitte, July 7, 2009)

Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the cevian triangle of X5. Let La be the reflection of line B'C' in line BC, and define Lb, Lc cyclically. Let A″ = Lb ∩ Lc, and define B″, C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in the isogonal conjugate of X3574. (Randy Hutson, January 29, 2018)

Let HaHbHc be the orthic triangle. X3574 is the radical center of the circles O(3,4) of triangles AHbHc, BHcHa, CHaHb. (Randy Hutson, July 31 2018)
