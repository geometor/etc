X3644¶

REFLECTION OF X75 IN X192

X3644 is the reflection of X75 in X192.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {75, 192}

  • Related Centers: X75 (ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF INCENTER), X192 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = bc(3/a - 2/b - 2/c)\)
Barycentrics: \(3/a - 2/b - 2/c : 3/b - 2/c - 2/a : 3/c - 2/a - 2/b\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-2.756085360801\)
▸ Linear Combinations1 combination

  • X3644 = \(9\) X2 - \(10\) X37 = \(6\) X2 - \(5\) X75 = \(3\) X2 - \(5\) X192, \(etc.\)

▸ Incident Central Lines3 lines

{2, 37}, {190, 1743}, {726, 3244}

▸ Functional Relations2 relations

  • X3644 = reflection of Xi in Xj for these (i,j): (75, 192), (1278, 37)

  • X3644 = anticomplement of X4686

Notes¶

For any point P, let L(A) be the line through P parallel to BC. Let U = AB∩L(A) and V = AC∩L(A), and let a’ = |UV|. Define b’ and c’ cyclically. Let L be the line consisting of points P such that a + ta’ = b + tb’ = c + tc’. Then X3644 is given by t=1. The points corresponding to t = -1, -1/2, and 0, are X75, X37, and X192, respectively. (Seiichi Kirikami, November 7, 2010)
