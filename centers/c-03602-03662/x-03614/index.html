X3614¶

1st HUTSON-FEUERBACH POINT

Let EuaEubEuc be the Feuerbach triangle, L the line through A and X5, and A″ = L∩EubEuc; define B″ and C″ cyclically. Then the lines EuaA″, EubB″, EucC″ concur in X3614. X3614 = {X5,X12}-harmonic conjugate of X11. Further, X3614 is the trilinear pole (with respect to the Feuerbach triangle) of the perspectrix of ABC and the Feuerbach triangle. (Randy Hutson, August 30, 2011.)
