X3661¶

1st KIRIKAMI-MOSES POINT

X3661 and X3662 were considered by Seiichi Kirikami and Peter Moses in December, 2011, in connection with combos.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {1, 2}, {75, 141}

  • Related Centers: X1 (INCENTER), X2 (CENTROID), X75 (ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF INCENTER), X141 (COMPLEMENT OF SYMMEDIAN POINT)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = bc(b^{2} + c^{2} + bc)\)
Barycentrics: \(b^{2} + c^{2} + bc : c^{2} + a^{2} + ca : a^{2} + b^{2} + ab\)
