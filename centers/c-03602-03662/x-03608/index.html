X3608¶

1st MORLEY-GIBERT PERSPECTOR

X3608 is the perspector of the Morley triangle and the triangle of the cusps of the Steiner deltoid. (Bernard Gibert, November 3, 2010). See

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {5, 356}, {20, 3278}

  • Related Centers: X5 (NINE-POINT CENTER), X356 (MORLEY CENTER), X20 (DE LONGCHAMPS POINT), X3278 (PERSPECTOR OF TRIANGLES M1M2M3 AND T1T2T3)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = \cos A + 2 \cos B \cos C + 3 \cos(A/3) + 6 \cos(B/3) \cos(C/3)\)
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
