X3646¶

POINT ALMACH

Denote the incenter and excenters by I, IA, IB, IC. Let KA be the centroid of triangle BCI, and define KB and KC cyclically. The lines IAKA, IBKB, ICKC concur in X3646.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {2, 40}, {1, 210}

  • Related Centers: X2 (CENTROID), X40 (BEVAN POINT), X1 (INCENTER), X210 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X37)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) ; f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = 5 + \cos A + (3 + \cos A)(\csc A)(\sin B + \sin C)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(2.801690292702\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates2 forms

  • Trilinears: \(1 + 2(1 + \cos^{2}(A/2)) \cos(B/2) \cos(C/2) \cos(A/2)\)

  • Trilinears: \(a^{3} + a^{2} (b + c) - a (b^{2} + 10 b c + c^{2}) - (b + c) (b^{2} + 6 b c + c^{2}) : :\)

▸ Linear Combinations1 combination

  • X3646 = \(r\) X1 - \(12R\) X2 - \(2r\) X3

▸ Incident Central Lines11 lines

{1, 210}, {2, 40}, {3, 2951}, {9, 1125}, {10, 1058}, {11, 1697}, {57, 191}, {405, 1490}, {748, 1453}, {936, 1001}, {978, 1045}

Notes¶

Of the 2 intersections of the Bevan circle and line BC, let Ab be the one closer to B, and define Bc and Ca cyclically. Let Ac be the one closer to C, and define Ba and Cb cyclically. Let Ab' = {B,C}-harmonic conjugate of Ab, and define Bc' and Ca' cyclically. Let Ac' = {B,C}-harmonic conjugate of Ac, and define Ba' and Cb' cyclically. The points Ab', Ac', Bc', Ba', Ca', Cb' lie on an ellipse centered at X3646. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)
