Notes¶

X239 is the perspector of triangle ABC and the tangential triangle of the conic that passes through A, B, C, and the pair P(6) and U(6) of bicentric points (see the notes just before X1908. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)

X239 is the point of intersection of the following lines:

  • X1X2 = trilinear polar of X190

  • trilinear polar of cevapoint <cevapoint>`{:ref:`X1, X2}, which is X239X514

  • UV, where U = X1-Ceva-conjugate-of-(2) = X192, and V = X2-Ceva-conjugate-of-X1 = X9 (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)
