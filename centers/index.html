Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers¶

Thematic Collections (X1–X3000)

X1–X19Classical & Ancient Centers (19 centers)

The foundational centers of classical triangle geometry: the incenter, centroid, circumcenter, orthocenter, nine-point center, symmedian point, Gergonne, Nagel, Mittenpunkt, Spieker, Feuerbach, Fermat, isodynamic, Napoleon, and Clawson points.

X20–X30Euler Line Centers (11 centers)

Classical centers lying on the Euler line X2 X3, including the De Longchamps point, Schiffler point, Exeter point, far-out point, homothetic center of the orthic and tangential triangles, and the Euler infinity point.

X31–X58Power Points, Circle Similicenters & Named Centers (28 centers)

2nd and 3rd power points, the Bevan point, Brocard midpoint, Kosnita point, and internal and external centers of similitude of the circumcircle, incircle, and nine-point circle.

X59–X97Isogonal & Isotomic Conjugates of Classical Centers (39 centers)

Isogonal conjugates (X59–X74) and isotomic and cross conjugates (X75–X97) of the classical centers X1–X58.

X98–X112Centers on the Circumcircle (15 centers)

Notable points on the circumcircle of ABC, including the Tarry point, Steiner point, anticomplementary, Feuerbach, Kiepert, and Jerabek antipodes, and centers derived from circumcircle mappings.

X113–X139Centers on the Nine-Point Circle & Orthic Centers (27 centers)

Points on the nine-point circle (X113–X139), centers of the Kiepert, Feuerbach, and Jerabek rectangular hyperbolas, and centers transferred to the orthic triangle.

X140–X190Derived Triangle Centers, Isoscelizers & Ajima-Malfatti Points (51 centers)

Complements, anticomplements, and classical centers evaluated on the medial, orthic, tangential, and excentral triangles, together with congruent isoscelizer, equal detour, and Ajima-Malfatti points.

X191–X236Ceva Conjugates (46 centers)

Perspectors of the cevian triangle of P and the anticevian triangle of Q (P-Ceva conjugates of Q) for classical triangle centers.

X237–X248Line Conjugates (12 centers)

Intersections of line PQ with the trilinear polar of the isogonal conjugate of P (P-line conjugates of Q).

X249–X297Isogonal Conjugates II & Hirst Inverses (49 centers)

Isogonal conjugates of centers X115–X248, together with cross conjugates and X2-Hirst inverses.

X298–X350Isotomic Conjugates II (53 centers)

Isotomic conjugates of previously cataloged triangle centers, together with X2-Hirst inverses and radical centers.

X351–X400Parry Circle, Morley/Yff Centers & Pedal Centroids (50 centers)

The Parry circle center, Morley trisector centers, Yff equal-parallelians, trisected-perimeter, and equilateral-cevian points, Kenmotu point, and centroids of pedal and cevian triangles.

X401–X484Euler Line Substitutions & Hirst Inverses (84 centers)

Centers lying on the Euler line generated by Xk-substitutions and Hirst inversions, followed by the Tixier point, Ajima-Malfatti radical center, and Evans perspectors.

X485–X510Vecten Square Centers & Isoscelizer Centers (26 centers)

Inner and outer Vecten points and square-associated centers (Vierkanten in een driehoek, X485–X495), harmonic conjugates, and isoscelizer-associated centers (X503–X510).

X511–X565Centers on the Line at Infinity & Vector Directions (55 centers)

Points on the line at infinity x + y + z = 0 (X511–X526, isogonal conjugates of circumcircle points), unit vector direction centers, and classical midpoints.

X566–X642Brocard Axis Circles & Gibert Centers (77 centers)

Centers on the Brocard axis X3 X6 whose circles cut equal chords on the sidelines of ABC (X566–X584), congruent shrunk insquares points, and centers contributed by Bernard Gibert (X616–X642).

X643–X693Crossdifferences & Trilinear Poles (51 centers)

Trilinear multipliers for the Kiepert and Yff parabolas, crossdifferences of classical centers, and trilinear poles of central lines.

X694–X768Even (p, q) Circumcircle Points (75 centers)

Even trigonometric (p, q) points on the circumcircle and line at infinity, paired with their isogonal conjugates.

X769–X843Odd o-(p, q) Circumcircle Points (75 centers)

Odd trigonometric o-(p, q) points on the circumcircle and line at infinity, paired with their isogonal conjugates.

X844–X900Hatzipolakis-Yiu Centers, Hirst Inverses & Crossdifferences (57 centers)

Hatzipolakis-Yiu points, the Yiu angle point (X848), Kiepert hyperbola multipliers, Hirst inverses, and Euler line intercepts.

X901–X1000Star-Named Centers, Ehrmann Points & Anticomplements (100 centers)

Star-named centers (Agena, Achernar, Algenib, Alcor), Ehrmann points, anticomplements, and midpoints of classical triangle centers.

X1001–X1069Classical Midpoints, Euler Intercepts & Cevapoints (69 centers)

Midpoints of classical centers, Euler line intercepts, collinearity intersections, cevapoints, and pure-angle trilinear centers.

X1070–X1114Cross Conjugates, Trilinear Squares & Euler-Circumcircle Intersections (45 centers)

Cross conjugates, trilinear squares and cubes of classical centers, and the intersections of the Euler line with the circumcircle (X1113, X1114).

X1115–X1165Morley-Taylor-Marr, Hofstadter, Ehrmann & Star Centers (51 centers)

Morley-Taylor-Marr centers, Hofstadter points, the Ehrmann congruent squares point (X1144), Grebe triangle centers, and star-named points (Aldebaran, Algjebbah, Altair, Altais).

X1166–X1208Saragossa Points (43 centers)

1st, 2nd, and 3rd Saragossa points (Q, Q', Q″) constructed from the cevian and circumcevian triangles of classical centers.

X1209–X1285Saragossa Conjugates, Sharygin Points & Lemoine Homothetic Center (77 centers)

Isogonal and isotomic conjugates of the Saragossa points, Sharygin points, and the Lemoine homothetic center (X1285).

X1286–X1311Collings Transforms (26 centers)

Concurrence points on the circumcircle of the circumcircles of AB'C', A'BC', and A'B'C, where A'B'C' is the reflection of ABC in P.

X1312–X1353Moses Intersections, Circumcircle Inverses, Wernau Points & Similicenters (42 centers)

Moses orthocentroidal-Euler intersections, circumcircle inverses, 1st and 2nd Wernau points (X1337, X1338), and similicenters of the circumcircle, nine-point circle, orthocentroidal circle, and Brocard circle.

X1354–X1392Brisse Transforms, Rigby/Griffiths Points & Complementary Conjugates (39 centers)

Polynomial centers on the incircle generated by the Brisse transform (X1354–X1367), Rigby and Griffiths points, and complementary and anticomplementary conjugates.

X1393–X1477Beth Conjugates & Harmonic Conjugates (85 centers)

Fixed-point P-beth conjugates of P and harmonic conjugates along central lines.

X1478–X1511Johnson-Yff Circle, Perspectors & Star-Named Centers (34 centers)

The Johnson-Yff circle center, tangential-of-tangential perspectors, star-named centers (Bellatrix, Betelgeuse, Biham), and Napoleon and Fermat crosssums.

X1512–X1600Orthojoins, Moses Circle Centers & Star-Named Centers (89 centers)

Orthojoins H(X) — orthopoles of the trilinear polars of isogonal conjugates (X1512–X1568) — together with Moses circle similicenters and star-named centers (Capella, Caph, Cebalrai, Celaeno, Chertan, Chort).

X1601–X1661TCC-Perspectors & Tripolar Centroids (61 centers)

Perspectors of the tangential triangle and the circumcevian triangle of P (X1601–X1634), tripolar centroids (X1635–X1651), and Evans and Grinberg perspectors.

X1662–X1706Circle-Related Points (45 centers)

Internal and external centers of similitude among the circumcircle, incircle, nine-point circle, Brocard circle, 1st and 2nd Lemoine circles, Apollonius circle, and Bevan circle.

X1707–X1788Mimosa Transforms (82 centers)

Centers generated by the Mimosa transform M(X) of classical triangle centers.

X1789–X1823Inverse Mimosa Transforms & Aleph Conjugates (35 centers)

Inverse Mimosa transforms of classical triangle centers and aleph conjugates.

X1824–X1907Zosma Transforms (84 centers)

Isogonal conjugates of the inverse Mimosa transforms (Z(X)) of classical triangle centers.

X1908–X2000Bicentric Pairs PU(k) & Orthocorrespondents (93 centers)

Midpoints, crosssums, crossdifferences, trilinear poles, and trilinear products of bicentric pairs PU(k) (X1908–X1982), Vega transforms, and orthocorrespondents (X1992–X2000).

X2001–X2054Orthocorrespondents, Gallatly Circle & Euler-Vecten-Gibert Points (54 centers)

Orthocorrespondents of classical centers (X2001–X2006), similicenters and intersections of the Gallatly circle (X2007–X2040), and the six Euler-Vecten-Gibert points and harmonic conjugates (X2041–X2054).

X2055–X2119Orion Transforms, Circumcircle Inverses, PK/NK Transforms, Reflections & Kepler Points (65 centers)

Orion transforms OT(X) (X2055–X2065), Caroli and Cursa points, circumcircle inverses (X2070–X2080), PK and NK transforms (X2081–X2092), classical reflections (X2093–X2105), and Kepler points and their isotomic conjugates (X2106–X2119).

X2120–X2147Eigencenters & Eigentransforms (28 centers)

Eigentransforms ET(X) — eigencenters of the cevian triangles of classical centers — paired with their isogonal conjugates on the self-isogonal cubic Z(X) (X2120–X2143), followed by X2-eigentransforms (X2144–X2147).

X2148–X2254Isotomic Conjugates (X2-Isoconjugates) I (107 centers)

Isotomic conjugates (X2-isoconjugates) of triangle centers X5 through X892.

X2255–X2364Isotomic Conjugates (X2-Isoconjugates) II (110 centers)

Isotomic conjugates (X2-isoconjugates) of triangle centers X901 through X2099.

X2365–X2445Circumcircle CIR(X) Points & Gibert-Simson Transforms (81 centers)

Circumcevian-eigentransform points CIR(X) on the circumcircle and their isogonal conjugates (X2365–X2393), followed by Gibert-Simson transforms GS(X) on the Simson cubic K010 (X2394–X2419) and their isogonal conjugates on K162 (X2420–X2445).

X2446–X2520Circle & Conic Intersections, Lemoine Inverses & Radical Centers I (75 centers)

Intersections of central lines with the incircle, Bevan circle, Steiner inellipse, and Steiner circumellipse, inverses in the 1st and 2nd Lemoine circles, and circle radical centers (X2446–X2520).

X2521–X2593Circle Similicenters, Radical Centers & Isoconjugates II (73 centers)

Similicenters and intersections involving the orthocentroidal, 2nd Brocard, Parry, and Gallatly circles (X2521–X2573), followed by isogonal and Xk-isoconjugates (X2574–X2593).

X2594–X2676Bicentric Pairs PU(68)–PU(85) & Golden Arbelos Points (83 centers)

Sums, differences, crosssums, crossdifferences, trilinear poles, and midpoints of bicentric pairs PU(68) through PU(85) (X2594–X2670), together with the 1st and 2nd Golden Arbelos points (X2671–X2676).

X2677–X2770Rigby-Simson RS(P, U) & Simson-Rigby SR(P, U) Points (94 centers)

Rigby-Simson orthopoles RS(P, U) where the Simson lines of circumcircle points P and U meet (X2677–X2686), and Simson-Rigby circumcircle points SR(P, U) whose Simson lines pass through RS(P, U) perpendicular to PU (X2687–X2770).

X2771–X2854Isogonal Conjugates of Simson-Rigby SR(P, U) Points (84 centers)

Points on the line at infinity formed as the isogonal conjugates of the Simson-Rigby circumcircle points X2687–X2770.

X2855–X2882Simson-Moses SM(P, U) Points & Conjugates (28 centers)

Simson-Moses circumcircle points SM(P, U) generated by isotomic conjugation of circumcircle pairs (X2855–X2868) and their isogonal conjugates on the line at infinity (X2869–X2882).

X2883–X2966Isogonal Conjugates with Respect to Special Triangles (84 centers)

Complementary (medial-isogonal), anticomplementary-isogonal, tangential-isogonal, and excentral-isogonal conjugates of classical triangle centers, together with trilinear and barycentric products.

X2967–X3000MacBeath Points, Dual Centers DC(X) / CD(X) & Central Line Intersections (34 centers)

The eight MacBeath points (X2967–X2974), circle radical centers (X2975–X2978), dual triangle mappings DC(X) and CD(X) (X2979–X2998), the Danneels-Bevan homothetic center (X2999), and the Soddy-antiorthic intersection (X3000).
