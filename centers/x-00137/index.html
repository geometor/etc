X(137) = X(110)-OF-ORTHIC-TRIANGLE¶

X(137) = X(110)-of-orthic triangle
Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = (\sec A)(\sin 2B + \sin 2C)[(\sin 2B - \sin 2C)^{2}] u(A,B,C)\), \(u(A,B,C) = [\sin^{2}(2A) - \sin^{2}(2B) - \sin^{2}(2C) - \sin 2B \sin 2C]\)
Barycentrics: \(af(A,B,C) : bf(B,C,A) : cf(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-3.125748576905\)

X(137) lies on the nine-point circle

X(137) lies on the cevian circle of X(5).
