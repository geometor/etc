X16¶

2nd ISODYNAMIC POINT

second_isodynamic_point(A, B, C) -> X_16 • Neuberg (1885) • Kimberling X(16) • Search Value \((6, 9, 13)\): \(- \frac{39183 \sqrt{3}}{13729} - \frac{4272 \sqrt{35}}{13729} \approx -6.784211495839\)

Let U and V be the points on sideline BC met by the interior and exterior bisectors of angle A. The circle having diameter UV is the A-Apollonian circle. The B- and C- Apollonian circles are similarly constructed. Each circle passes through a vertex and both isodynamic points. The pedal triangle of X16 is equilateral.

Constructed Elements

  • Derived Triangle: Equilateral Pedal Triangle △PdaPdbPdc — vertices Pda, Pdb, Pdc (Equilateral pedal triangle of isodynamic point)

  • Apollonian Circles: A-Apollonian, B-Apollonian, C-Apollonian — coaxial circles through A, B, C orthogonal to circumcircle

  • Circle: Circumcircle (circumcircle) — Passes through vertices A, B, C; centered at X3 CIRCUMCENTER
