X11¶

FEUERBACH POINT

X11 is the point of tangency of the nine-point circle and the incircle. The nine-point circle is the circumcenter of the medial triangle, as well as the orthic triangle. Feuerbach’s famous theorem states that the nine-point circle is tangent to the incircle and the three excircles.

  • Circle: Incircle (incircle) — Inscribed circle tangent to sidelines at T_a, T_b, T_c; centered at X1 INCENTER

  • Circle: Nine-Point Circle (ninepointcircle) — Passes through side midpoints M_a, M_b, M_c and altitude feet H_a, H_b, H_c; centered at X5

  • Central Lines: {1, 5}

  • Related Centers: X1 (INCENTER), X5 (NINE-POINT CENTER)
