X14¶

2nd ISOGONIC CENTER

second_isogonic_center(A, B, C) -> X_14 • Torricelli • Neuberg • Kimberling X(14) • Search Value \((6, 9, 13)\): \(\frac{153287 \sqrt{3}}{123561} + \frac{15488 \sqrt{35}}{41187} \approx 4.373431772013\)

Construct the equilateral triangle BA'C having base BC and vertex A' on the positive side of BC; similarly construct equilateral triangles CB'A and AC'B based on the other two sides. The lines AA',BB',CC' concur in X14. The antipedal triangle of X14 is equilateral.

Constructed Elements

  • Inward Equilateral Side Triangles: △BCA'', △CAB'', △ABC'' erected inward on sidelines with apexes A'', B'', C''

  • Concurrence Lines (Inner Cevians): A–A'', B–B'', C–C'' concurring at X14
