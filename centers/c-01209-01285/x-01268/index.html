X1268¶

ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF X1125

X1268 is the isotomic conjugate of X1125.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {7, 12}, {10, 86}

  • Related Centers: X1125 (COMPLEMENT OF X10), X7 (GERGONNE POINT), X12 ({X), X10 (SPIEKER CENTER), X86 (CEVAPOINT OF INCENTER AND CENTROID)

Trilinears: \(1/(a^{2}f(a,b,c)) : 1/(b^{2}f(b,c,a)) : 1/(c^{2}f(c,a,b))\), where f(a,b,c) is as in X1125)

Notes¶

Let A22B22C22 be Gemini triangle 22. Let A' be the perspector of conic {{A, B, C,B22,C22}}, and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X1268. (Randy Hutson, January 15, 2019)
