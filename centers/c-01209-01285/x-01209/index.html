X1209¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1166

X1209 is the isogonal conjugate of X1166.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {52, 973}

  • Related Centers: X52 (ORTHOCENTER OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE), X973 (1st EHRMANN POINT)

Barycentrics: \((S^{2} + S_{B} S_{C}) (a^{2} (S_{A}^{2} + S^{2}) + 2 S_{A} (S^{2} + S_{B} S_{C})) : :\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(7.037932714967\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Barycentrics: \((a^{2} (b^{2} + c^{2}) - (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2}) (a^{4} (b^{2} + c^{2}) - 2 a^{2} (b^{4} + b^{2} c^{2} + c^{4}) + (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2} (b^{2} + c^{2})) : :\)

  • X1209 = isogonal conjugate of X1166

  • X1209 = complementary conjugate of X140

  • X1209 = crosspoint of X2 and X311

  • X1209 = perspector of circumconic centered at X570

  • X1209 = X2-Ceva conjugate of X570

  • X1209 = X54 of X5-Brocard triangle

Notes¶

In the plane of a triangle ABC, let

  • A'B'C' = medial triangle;

  • BA = reflection of B' in BC, and define CB and AC cyclically;

  • CA = reflection of C' in BC, and define AB and BC cyclically;

  • OA = circle {{B', C', BA, CA}}, and define OB and OC cyclically.

Then X1209 = radical center of OA, OB, OC. (Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna, September 8, 2021)

X1209 is the {X17,X18}-harmonic conjugate of X231.
