Notes¶

Darij Grinberg, Sharygin Points Report, Hyacinthos #6293 (1/8/03) and #6315 (1/10/03)

The first of ten sections is an Introduction quoted, in part, here:

We will treat two remarkable triangles: the triangle bounded by the perpendicular bisectors of the internal angle bisectors of a triangle ABC, and the triangle bounded by the perpendicular bisectors of the external angle bisectors of triangle ABC. These two triangles and the triangle ABC are three perspective triangles, having a common perspectrix: the Lemoine axis of ABC. The mutual perspectors of the three triangles will be called the first, second and third Sharygin points of ABC (after a problem of Igor Sharygin - see Section 10). The report introduces fifteen Sharygin points, of which the 1st, 2nd, 4th, and 6th are X256, X291, X846,

  • and X1054, respectively. X1281 is the 3rd Sharygin point. See also Hyacinthos #6293 and #6315.
