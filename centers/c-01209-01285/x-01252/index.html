X1252¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1086

X1252 is the isogonal conjugate of X1086.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {59, 672}, {100, 650}

  • Related Centers: X1086 (CENTER OF HYPERBOLA {{A,B,C,X), X59 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X11), X672 (CROSSDIFFERENCE OF X1 AND X514), X100 (ANTICOMPLEMENT OF FEUERBACH POINT), X650 (CROSSDIFFERENCE OF X1 AND X3)

Trilinears: \(1/f(a,b,c) : 1/f(b,c,a) : 1/f(c,a,b)\), where f(a,b,c) is as in X1086
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(0.782521288014\)

Notes¶
