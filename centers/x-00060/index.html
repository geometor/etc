X(60) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(12)¶

Let A’B’C’ be the cevian triangle of X(21). Let A″, B″, C″ be the inverse-in-circumcircle of A’, B’, C’. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X(60). (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)
