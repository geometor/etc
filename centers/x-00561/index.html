X561¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF 4th POWER POINT

X561 is the isogonal conjugate of 4th power point.

Notes¶

X561 is the vertex conjugate of the foci of the inellipse that is the trilinear square of the de Longchamps line (with center X21235 and perspector X1928). (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)

Let A'B'C' be the obverse triangle of X1. Let A″B″C″ be the N-obverse triangle of X1. Let A* be the trilinear product A'*A″, and define B* and C* cyclically. The lines AA*, BB*, CC* concur in X561. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)
