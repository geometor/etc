Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the hexyl triangle. Let A″ be the pole, wrt the polar circle, of line B'C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X158. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)

Let A'B'C' and A″B″C″ be the Euler and anti-Euler triangles, resp. Let A* be the trilinear product A'*A″, and define B* and C* cyclically. The lines AA*, BB*, CC* concur in X158. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)
