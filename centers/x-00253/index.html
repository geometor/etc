X253¶

X4-CROSS CONJUGATE OF X2

X253 is the perspector of ABC and the pedal triangle of X64.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {20, 64}, {7, 280}

  • Related Centers: X154 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X6), X20 (DE LONGCHAMPS POINT), X64 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X20), X7 (GERGONNE POINT), X280 (-CROSS CONJUGATE OF X8)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = (\csc A)/(\tan B + \tan C - \tan A)\)
Barycentrics: \(g(A,B,C) : g(B,C,A) : g(C,A,B)\), where \(g(A,B,C) = 1/(\tan B + \tan C - \tan A)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(6.547843480141\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates2 forms

  • Trilinears: \(g(A,B,C) : g(B,C,A) : g(C,A,B)\), where \(g(A,B,C) = (\csc^{2} A)/(\cos A - \cos B \cos C)\)

  • Barycentrics: \(1/(3 a^{4} - 2 a^{2} (b^{2} + c^{2}) - (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2}) : :\)

X253 lies on the Lucas cubic.
