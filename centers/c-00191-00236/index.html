X191–X236¶

Ceva Conjugates

Perspectors of the cevian triangle of P and the anticevian triangle of Q (P-Ceva conjugates of Q) for classical triangle centers.

Group Preamble — Centers X191-X236 (Ceva conjugates)

Centers X191-X236 are Ceva conjugates. (The P-Ceva conjugate of Q is the perspector of the cevian triangle of P and the anticevian triangle of Q.)
