Notes¶

Let TaTbTc be the intouch triangle. Let

  • AaAbAc = A-extouch triangle, and define BaBbBc and CaCbCc cyclically

  • Ab' = TbTc∩BbBc, and define Bc' and Ca' cyclically

  • Ac' = TbTc∩CbCc, and define Ba' and Cb' cyclically

Then X3742 = centroid of {Ab',Ac',Bc',Ba',Ca',Cb'}. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)
