Notes¶

Let P1 and P2 be the two points on the Gergonne line whose trilinear polars are parallel to the Gergonne line. P1 and P2 lie on the circumconic centered at X1086 (hyperbola {{A, B, C, X2, X7}}), and circle {{X2, X109, X675}}. The midpoint of P1 and P2 is X1638. X3676 is the barycentric product P1*P2. (Randy Hutson, January 15, 2019)
