X3680¶

X2136com(EXTOUCH TRIANGLE)

X3680 = X4-of-Garcia-reflection-triangle

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {40, 104}, {7, 145}

  • Related Centers: X1420 (-BETH CONJUGATE OF X145), X40 (BEVAN POINT), X104 (ANTIPODE OF X100), X7 (GERGONNE POINT), X145 (ANTICOMPLEMENT OF NAGEL POINT)

Barycentrics: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = a(a - b - c)/(3a - b - c)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(16.226961690788\)

X3680 lies on the Feuerbach hyperbola.

Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be Triangle T(-1,3) (aka excenters-reflections triangle). Let A″ be the trilinear product B'*C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines A'A″, B'B″, C'C″ concur in X3680. (Randy Hutson, March 21, 2019)

Let A' be the orthocenter of BCX1, and define B' and C' cyclically. A'B'C' is also the anticevian triangle, wrt intouch triangle, of X1, and also the Garcia reflection triangle (Gemini triangle 8). X3680 is the orthocenter of A'B'C'. (see also X1699) (Randy Hutson, March 21, 2019)
