Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the excentral triangle, and let H be the hyperbola {{X1,X5,X30,A',B',C'}}. Then X2624 is the perspector wrt A'B'C' of H. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)

Let H be the rectangular hyperbola passing through X1, X5, X30 and the excenters (centered at X476); then X2624 is the isogonal conjugate of the perspector of ABC and the tangential triangle, wrt excentral triangle, of H. (Randy Hutson, February 10, 2016)
