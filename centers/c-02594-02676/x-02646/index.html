Notes¶

In the place of a triangle ABC, let I = X1 = incenter, and let

  • A'B'C' = intouch triangle;

  • Ma = midpoint of segment AI;

  • La = MaA', and define Lb and Lc cyclically;

  • \(R_{a}\) = reflection of La in AI, and define Bb and \(R_{c}\) cyclically.

The lines \(R_{a}\), \(R_{b}\), \(R_{c}\) concur in X2646. (Angel Montesdeoca, October 9, 2021)
