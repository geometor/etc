X2804¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X2720

X2804 is the isogonal conjugate of X2720.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {11, 123}, {100, 108}

  • Related Centers: X2720 (SR(X100, X108)), X11 (FEUERBACH POINT), X123 (-OF-MEDIAL-TRIANGLE), X100 (ANTICOMPLEMENT OF FEUERBACH POINT), X108 (Ψ(CIRCUMCENTER, INCENTER))

Trilinears: \(1/f(a,b,c) : 1/f(b,c,a) : 1/f(c,a,b)\), where f(a,b,c) is as at X2720
Barycentrics: \(a/f(a,b,c) : b/f(b,c,a) : c/f(c,a,b)\)
▸ Incident Central Lines6 lines

{11, 123}, {30, 511}, {100, 108}, {104, 1295}, {1145, 1769}, {1317, 1359}

▸ Functional Relations2 relations

  • X2804 = isogonal conjugate of X2720

  • X2804 = Xi-Ceva conjugate of Xj for these (i,j): (100, 119), (655, 9), (901, 1329), (1295, 123), (1320, 11)

Notes¶

Let Ia be the reflection of X1 in the perpendicular bisector of BC, and define Ib and Ic cyclically. X2804 = X521-of-IaIbIc; IaIbIc is the inner Garcia triangle. (Randy Hutson, February 10, 2016)
