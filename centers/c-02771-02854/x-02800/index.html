X2800¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X2716

X2800 is the isogonal conjugate of X2716.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {11, 65}, {4, 80}

  • Related Centers: X2716 (SR(X100, X102)), X11 (FEUERBACH POINT), X65 (ORTHOCENTER OF THE INTOUCH TRIANGLE), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X80 (REFLECTION OF INCENTER IN FEUERBACH POINT)

Trilinears: \(1/f(a,b,c) : 1/f(b,c,a) : 1/f(c,a,b)\), where f(a,b,c) is as at X2716
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(a^{5}(b + c) - a^{4}(b + c)^{2} - a^{3}(b - 2c)(2b - c)(b + c) + a^{2}(b - c)^{2}(2b + c)(b + 2c) + a(b - c)^{2}(b + c)(b^{2} - 3bc + c^{2}) - (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2}(b^{2} - bc + c^{2}) : :\)

▸ Incident Central Lines18 lines

{1, 104}, {3, 214}, {4, 80}, {8, 153}, {10, 119}, {11, 65}, {30, 511}, {40, 78}, {72, 1145}, {84, 1320}, {149, 151}, {942, 1387}, {1012, 2099}, {1317, 1364}, {1457, 1735}, {1519, 1737}, {1765, 1953}, {1862, 1902}

▸ Functional Relations2 relations

  • X2800 = isogonal conjugate of X2716

  • X2800 = crosssum of X55 and X2265

Notes¶

Let Ia be the reflection of X1 in the perpendicular bisector of BC, and define Ib and Ic cyclically; then X2800 = X515-of-IaIbIc; IaIbIc is the inner Garcia triangle. (Randy Hutson, February 10, 2016)
