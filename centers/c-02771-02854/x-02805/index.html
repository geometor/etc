X2805¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X2721

X2805 is the isogonal conjugate of X2721.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {11, 126}, {37, 100}

  • Related Centers: X2721 (SR(X100, X111)), X11 (FEUERBACH POINT), X126 (-OF-MEDIAL-TRIANGLE), X37 (CROSSPOINT OF INCENTER AND CENTROID), X100 (ANTICOMPLEMENT OF FEUERBACH POINT)

Trilinears: \(1/f(a,b,c) : 1/f(b,c,a) : 1/f(c,a,b)\), where f(a,b,c) is as at X2721
Barycentrics: \(a/f(a,b,c) : b/f(b,c,a) : c/f(c,a,b)\)
▸ Incident Central Lines5 lines

{11, 126}, {30, 511}, {37, 100}, {75, 149}, {104, 1296}

▸ Functional Relations1 relation

  • X2805 = isogonal conjugate of X2721

Notes¶

Let Ia be the reflection of X1 in the perpendicular bisector of BC, and define Ib and Ic cyclically. X2805 = X524-of-IaIbIc; IaIbIc is the inner Garcia triangle. (Randy Hutson, February 10, 2016)
