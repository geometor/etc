X850¶

BARYCENTRIC MULTIPLIER FOR KIEPERT HYPERBOLA

The barycentric product of X850 and the circumcircle is the Kiepert hyperbola.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {99, 476}, {2, 647}

Notes¶

Let P1 and P2 be the two points on the de Longchamps line whose trilinear polars are parallel to the de Longchamps line. P1 and P2 lie on the Kiepert hyperbola and circle {X2, X98, X99}}. X850 is the barycentric product P1*P2. (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
