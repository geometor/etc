X2076¶

INVERSE-IN-CIRCUMCIRCLE OF X39

Let T1 be the circumcevian triangle of the 1st Brocard point, and let T2 be the circumcevian triangle of the 2nd Brocard point. Let VAVBVC be the vertex triangle of T1 and T2, and let \(S_{A}\)\(S_{B}\)\(S_{C}\) be the side triangle of T1 and T2. The lines VA\(S_{A}\), VB\(S_{B}\), VC\(S_{C}\) concur in X2076.

Constructed Elements

  • Circle: Circumcircle (circumcircle) — Passes through vertices A, B, C; centered at X3 CIRCUMCENTER

  • Central Lines: {3, 39}

  • Related Centers: X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X39 (BROCARD MIDPOINT)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = \sin A - 2 \cos A \sin 2\omega\)
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-2.785481148478\)
