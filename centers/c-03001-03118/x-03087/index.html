X3087¶

INTERSECTION OF LINES X2X_95 AND X4X_6

Let A'B'C' be the anti-Euler triangle. X3087 is the radical center of the circumcircles of triangles A'BC, B'CA, C'AB. (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
