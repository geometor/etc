X3005¶

(DE LONGCHAMPS LINE)∩(LEMOINE AXIS)

Let L denote the line at infinity. Then (Lemoine axis) = (isogonal conjugate of isotomic conjugate of L) and (de Longchamps line) = (isotomic conjugate of isogonal conjugate of L), and X3005 = (Lemoine axis)∩(de Longchamps line). (Randy Hutson, February 10, 2016)
