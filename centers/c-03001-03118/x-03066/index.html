X3066¶

2nd LEMOINE HOMOTHETIC CENTER

This is a correction for trilinears given in TCCT, p. 188.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {6, 110}, {25, 182}

  • Related Centers: X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X110 (FOCUS OF KIEPERT PARABOLA), X25 (HOMOTHETIC CENTER OF ORTHIC AND TANGENTIAL TRIANGLES), X182 (MIDPOINT OF BROCARD DIAMETER)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = a(a^{2} + 3b^{2} - c^{2})(a^{2} - b^{2} + 3c^{2})\)
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(0.950789516177\)
▸ Incident Central Lines7 lines

{2, 1350}, {3, 373}, {6, 110}, {25, 182}, {51, 394}, {107, 458}, {125, 381}

▸ Functional Relations1 relation

  • X3066 = barycentric product of vertices of submedial triangle

Notes¶

Suppose X is a point, with isogonal conjugate \(X^{- 1}\). It is well known that the pedal triangle of X is homothetic to the antipedal triangle of \(X^{- 1}\). The Lemoine homothetic center, X1285, is the homothetic center when X = X6, and X3066 is the homothetic center when X = X2. See also X1285. (Peter Moses, Dec. 7, 2005)

In general if X =\(x : y : z\) (trilinears), then the homothetic center is given by

a(x + y cos C)(x + z cos B) : b(y + z cos A)(y + x cos C): c(z + x cos B)(z + y cos A).
