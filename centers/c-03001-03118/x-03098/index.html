X3098¶

INTERSECTION OF LINES X3X_6 AND X69X_74

Let AA1A2, BB1B2, CC1C2 be the circumcircle-inscribed equilateral triangles used in the construction of the Trinh triangle. Let A' be the midpoint of A1 and A2, and define B' and C' cyclically. Triangle A'B'C' is homothetic to ABC at X3 and perspective to the Trinh triangle at X3098.
