Notes¶

Let A2B2C2 be the 2nd Brocard triangle. Let A' = inverse-in-Brocard circle of A, and define B' and C' cyclically. Let A2' = inverse-in-circumcircle of A2, and define B2' and C2' cyclically. Let A″ = B'B2'∩C'C2', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X3094. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)

Let A'B'C' be the 3rd Brocard triangle. Then X3094 is the radical center of the circumcircles of A'BC, B'CA, C'AB. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)

Let D = P(1), E = U(1), F = P(2), G = U(2). Let D' = X4-of-EFG, E' = X4-of-DFG, F' = X4-of-DEG, G' = X4-of-DEF. Then D’E’F'G' is a cyclic quadrilateral whose circumcenter is X3094. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)
