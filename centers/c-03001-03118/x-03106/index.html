X3106¶

CENTER OF 1st BROCARD EQUILATERAL TRIANGLE

X3106 is the center of the equilateral triangle introduced at X3104.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3104, 3107}

  • Related Centers: X3104 (INNER VERTEX OF 1st BROCARD EQUILATERAL TRIANGLE), X3107 (CENTER OF 2nd BROCARD EQUILATERAL TRIANGLE)

Trilinears: \(\cos(A + \omega - \pi /3) : \cos(B + \omega - \pi /3) : \cos(C + \omega - \pi /3)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(2.561540583378\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates2 forms

  • Trilinears: \(\sin(A + \omega + \pi /6) : \sin(B + \omega + \pi /6) : \sin(C + \omega + \pi /6)\)

  • Trilinears: \(bc \cos^{2} A - a^{2}\cos B \cos C + \sqrt{3}(ab \sin B + ac \sin C) : :\)

▸ Incident Central Lines5 lines

{3, 6}, {13, 262}, {14, 2782}, {18, 76}, {194, 627}

▸ Functional Relations5 relations

  • X3106 = midpoint of X3104 and X3107

  • X3106 = reflection of Xi in Xj for these (i,j): (3105, 3107), (3107, 39)

  • X3106 = inverse-in-2nd-Brocard-circle of X62

  • X3106 = X13-of-X3PU(1)

  • X3106 = X14-of-X6PU(1)

Notes¶

The locus of the 2nd Isogonic Center in a Brocard porism (triangles sharing circumcircle and Brocard inellipse with ABC) is a circle with center X3106. (Randy Hutson, August 29, 2018)
