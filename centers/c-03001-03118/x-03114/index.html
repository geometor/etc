Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the 3rd Brocard triangle. Let La be the line through A' parallel to BC, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A″ = Lb∩Lc, B″ = Lc∩La, C″ = La∩Lb. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X3114. (Randy Hutson, August 29, 2018)
