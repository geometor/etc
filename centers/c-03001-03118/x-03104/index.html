X3104¶

INNER VERTEX OF 1st BROCARD EQUILATERAL TRIANGLE

There are two 60-rotations of the 1st Brocard point about the 2nd; X3104 is the one closer to X3.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 6}, {14, 76}

  • Related Centers: X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X14 (2nd ISOGONIC CENTER), X76 (3rd BROCARD POINT)

Barycentrics: \(af(a,b,c) : bf(b,c,a) : cf(c,a,b)\)
