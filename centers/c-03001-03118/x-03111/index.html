X3111¶

ORTHOGONAL PROJECTION OF X2 ON BROCARD AXIS

X3111 = X99-of-X2-Brocard triangle

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 6}, {2, 512}

  • Related Centers: X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X2 (CENTROID), X512 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X99)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = a^{3}(b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2})(b^{4} + c^{4} - 4b^{2}c^{2}) + ab^{2}c^{2}(b^{4} + c^{4})\)

Notes¶

Let MaMbMc be the medial triangle. Let A″ be the reflection of X115 in line MbMc, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. X3111 is the centroid of (degenerate) triangle A″B″C″.
