Notes¶

Let P be a point in the plane of ABC and not on the circumcircle. Let (OA) be the circle tangent to the circumcircle at A and passing through P. Let A' be the antipode of A in (OA). Let LA be the tangent to (OA) at A'. Define LB and LC cyclically. Let TA = LB∩LC, and define TB, TC cyclically. Triangle TATBTC is homothetic to the tangential triangle. When P = X1, the center of homothety is X3145. (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
