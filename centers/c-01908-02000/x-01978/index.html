X1978¶

TRILINEAR PRODUCT OF PU(41)

As the trilinear product of Steiner circumellipse antipodes, X1978 lies on conic {{A, B, C,X668,X789}} with center X6376 and perspector X75. (Randy Hutson, July 11, 2019)
