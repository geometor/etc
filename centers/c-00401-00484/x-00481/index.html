X481¶

1st EPPSTEIN POINT

Let S be the inner Soddy circle and \(S_{A}\), \(S_{B}\), \(S_{C}\) the Soddy circles tangent to S. Let Ia = S∩\(S_{A}\), Ea = \(S_{B}\)∩\(S_{C}\), and determine Ib, Ic, Eb, Ec cyclically. Then X481 is the point of concurrence of lines IA-to-EA, IB-to-EB, IC-to-EC.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {1, 7}, {226, 485}

  • Related Centers: X79 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X35), X482 (2nd EPPSTEIN POINT), X1 (INCENTER), X7 (GERGONNE POINT), X226 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X65), X485 (VECTEN POINT)

Trilinears: \(1 - 2 \sec A/2 \cos B/2 \cos C/2 : :\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-0.019071250304\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(1 - 4(\operatorname{area})/[a(b + c - a)] : :\) (E. Brisse, 3/20/01)

▸ Linear Combinations3 combinations

  • X481 = \(s\) X1 - \((r + 4R)\) X7

  • X481 = A / \(R_{a}\) + B / \(R_{b}\) + C / \(R_{c}\) - X1 / \(r\), where \(R_{a}\), \(R_{b}\), \(R_{c}\) = radii of Soddy circles

  • X481 = A / \(R_{a}\) + B / \(R_{b}\) + C / \(R_{c}\) + X175 / \(R_{s}'\), where \(R_{a}\), \(R_{b}\), \(R_{c}\) = radii of Soddy circles, and \(R_{s}'\) = radius of outer Soddy circle

▸ Incident Central Lines3 lines

{1, 7}, {174, 1127}, {226, 485}

▸ Functional Relations6 relations

  • X481 = X79-Ceva conjugate of X482

  • X481 = Kosnita(X175,X1) point; see X54

  • X481 = Kosnita(X175,X7) point

  • X481 = Kosnita(X176,X175) point

  • X481 = {X7,X176}-harmonic conjugate of X1373

  • X481 = 2nd-outer-Soddy-isogonal conjugate of X32058

Notes¶

David Eppstein, “Tangent spheres and triangle centers,”American Mathematical Monthly, 108 (2001) 63-66.

Let S' be the outer Soddy center, X175. X481 is the point of concurrence of the Soddy lines of BS'C, CS'A, and AS'B. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)

Let La be the line tangent to the outer Soddy circle at the touchpoint with the A-Soddy circle. Define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A' = Lb∩Lc and define B' and C’cyclically. Triangle A'B'C', here introduced as the outer Soddy tangential triangle, is perspective to ABC, and the perspector is X481. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)
