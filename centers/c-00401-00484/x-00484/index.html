X484¶

1st EVANS PERSPECTOR

X484 is the perspector of the excentral triangle and the triangle RefaRefbRefc, where Refa is the reflection of vertex A in sideline BC and Refb, Refc are determined cyclically. (Lawrence Evans, 10/22/98)

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {1, 3}, {12, 79}

  • Related Centers: X1 (INCENTER), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X12 ({X), X79 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X35)

Trilinears: \(1 + 2(\cos A - \cos B - \cos C) : 1 + 2(\cos B - \cos C - \cos A) : 1 + 2(\cos C - \cos A - \cos B)\)
Barycentrics: \(a[1 + 2(\cos A - \cos B - \cos C)] : b[1 + 2(\cos B - \cos C - \cos A)] : c[1 + 2(\cos C - \cos A - \cos B)]\)
