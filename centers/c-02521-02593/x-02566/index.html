X2566¶

INSIMILICENTER(NINE-POINT CIRCLE, 2nd BROCARD CIRCLE)

X2566 is the insimilicenter(nine-point circle, 2nd Brocard circle).

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {2, 3} (Euler line), {11, 2564}

  • Related Centers: X2 (CENTROID), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X11 (FEUERBACH POINT), X2564 (INSIMILICENTER)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = \cos A + e \cos(B - C)\), where \(e = (1 - 4 \sin^{2}\omega )^{1/2}\)
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(2.990306557406\)

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X2566 has Shinagawa coefficients \((e + 1, e - 1)\).
