X2575¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1114

As the isogonal conjugate of a point on the circumcircle, X2575 lies on the line at infinity.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {30, 511}, {74, 1113}

  • Related Centers: X1114 (2nd EULER-LINE-CIRCUMCIRCLE INTERSECTION), X30 (EULER INFINITY POINT), X511 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X98), X74 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF EULER INFINITY POINT), X1113 (1st EULER-LINE-CIRCUMCIRCLE INTERSECTION)

Trilinears: \(1/f(a,b,c) : 1/f(b,c,a) : 1/f(c,a,b)\), where f(a,b,c) = X1114
Barycentrics: \(a/f(a,b,c) : b/f(b,c,a) : c/f(c,a,b)\)

X2575 lies on the Simson quartic (Q101).
