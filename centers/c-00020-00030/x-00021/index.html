X21¶

SCHIFFLER POINT

Write I for the incenter; the Euler lines of the four triangles IBC, ICA, IAB, and ABC concur in X21. This configuration extends to Kirikami-Schiffler points and generalizations found by Peter Moses, as introduced just before X3648.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {2, 3} (Euler line), {1, 31}

  • Related Centers: X1 (INCENTER), X2 (CENTROID), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X31 (2nd POWER POINT)

Trilinears: \(1/(\cos B + \cos C) : 1/(\cos C + \cos A) : 1/(\cos A + \cos B)\)
Barycentrics: \(a/(\cos B + \cos C) : b/(\cos C + \cos A) : c/(\cos A + \cos B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(3.177166860315\)

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X21 has Shinagawa coefficients \((\$aS_{A}\$, abc - \$aS_{A}\$)\).

The name of this point honors Kurt Schiffler (../bstud/schiffler.html).

Let IaIbIc be the incentral triangle of ABC, and let LA be the reflection of line IbIc in line BC; define LB and LC cyclically. The triangle formed by the lines LA, LB, LC is perspective to ABC, and the perspector is X21. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)

Let A'B'C' be the 2nd circumperp triangle. Let A″ be the cevapoint of B' and C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, and CC″ concur in X21. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

Let EuaEubEuc be the Feuerbach triangle. Let La be the tangent to the nine-point circle at Eua, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A″ be the isogonal conjugate of the trilinear pole of La, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. Let A*B*C* be the circumcevian triangle, wrt A″B″C″, of X1. The lines AA*, BB*, CC* concur in X21. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

Let A'B'C' be the 2nd circumperp triangle. Let A″ be the trilinear product B'*C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. Then A″, B″, C″ are collinear on line X36X238 (the trilinear polar of X81). The lines A'A″, B'B″, C'C″ concur in X21. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

Let Oa be the reflection of the A-excircle in the perpendicular bisector of BC, and define Ob, Oc cyclically. Then X21 is the radical center of Oa, Ob, Oc. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

Let Ab, Ac, Bc, Ba, Ca, Cb be as in the construction of the Conway circle (see http://mathworld.wolfram.com/ConwayCircle.html). Let Oa be the circumcircle of AAbAc, and define Ob, Oc cyclically. X21 is the radical center of Oa, Ob, Oc; see also X8 and X274. (Randy Hutson, April 9, 2016)

Let JaJbJc be the excentral triangle. X21 is the radical center of the circles O(3,4) of triangles JaBC, JbCA, JcAB. (Randy Hutson, July 31 2018)

Lev Emelyanov and Tatiana Emelyanova, A note on the Schiffler point, :cite:`Forum Geometricorum <forum-geometricorum>` 3 (2003) pages 113-116.

See Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna, On a Conic Through Twelve Notable Points, Int. J. Adv. Math. and Mech. 7(2) (2019) 1-15.

In the plane of a triangle ABC, let

  • A' = A-excenter, and define B' and C' cyclically

  • A″ = A'X3∩BC, and define B″ and C″ cyclically.

The lines AA″, BB″,CC″ concur in X21. (Yuda Chen, April 13, 2022)

X21 is the {X2,X3}-harmonic conjugate of X404.
