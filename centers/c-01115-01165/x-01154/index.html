X1154¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1141

X1154 is the isogonal conjugate of X1141.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {5, 51}, {3, 54}

  • Related Centers: X1141 (GIBERT POINT), X5 (NINE-POINT CENTER), X51 (CENTROID OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X54 (KOSNITA POINT)

Trilinears: \((1 - 4 \cos^{2} A)\cos(B - C) : (1 - 4 \cos^{2} B)\cos(C - A) : (1 - 4 \cos^{2} C)\cos(A - B)\)
Barycentrics: \(a(1 - 4 \cos^{2} A)\cos(B - C) : b(1 - 4 \cos^{2} B)\cos(C - A) : c(1 - 4 \cos^{2} C)\cos(A - B)\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Barycentrics: \(a^{2}[(a^{2} - b^{2} - c^{2})^{2} - b^{2}c^{2}](b^{4} + c^{4} - a^{2}b^{2} - a^{2}c^{2} - 2b^{2}c^{2}) : :\)

Notes¶

As the isogonal conjugate of a point on the circumcircle, X1154 lies on the line at infinity; X1154 is, in fact, the point where the Euler line of the orthic triangle meets the line at infinity (Bernard Gibert, Hyacinthos 1498, September 25, 2000).
