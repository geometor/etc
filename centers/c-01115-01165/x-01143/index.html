X1143¶

2nd MALFATTI-RABINOWITZ POINT

Along each side of ABC there is a segment that is a common tangent to two of the three Malfatti circles of ABC. Let A', B', C' be the midpoints of these respective segments. Then triangle A'B'C' isperspective to ABC, and the perspector is X1143. (Stanley Rabinowitz, #4611, 12/29/01) For coordinates, see Paul Yiu, #4615, 12/30/01, and

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {8, 177}, {174, 175}

  • Related Centers: X1489 (3rd STEVANOVIC POINT), X2 (CENTROID), X8 (NAGEL POINT), X177 (1st MID-ARC POINT), X174 (YFF CENTER OF CONGRUENCE), X175 (ISOPERIMETRIC POINT)

Trilinears: \(\csc A \tan A/4 : \csc B \tan B/4 : \csc C \tan C/4\)
Barycentrics: \(\tan A/4 : \tan B/4 : \tan C/4\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(0.982123961231\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Barycentrics: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = (b + c - a)^{1/2}[2b^{1/2}c^{1/2} - (a + b + c)^{1/2}(b + c - a)^{1/2}]\)

X1143 lies on the cubic K200.

▸ Functional Relations3 relations

Notes¶

Milorad R. Stevanovic, “Triangle Centers Associated with the Malfatti Circles,” :cite:`Forum Geometricorum <forum-geometricorum>` 3 (2003) 83-93.

Let A' be the touchpoint of the line BC and the incircle of the triangle BCI, where I = incenter of ABC. Define B' and C' cyclically. The line AA', BB', CC' concur in X1143. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)
