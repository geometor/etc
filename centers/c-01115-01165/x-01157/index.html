X1157¶

INVERSE-IN-CIRCUMCIRCLE OF X54

For any point X, let XA be the reflection of X in sideline BC, and define XB and XC cyclically. Then X1157 is the unique point X for which the lines AXA, BXB, CXC concur on the circumcircle; the point of concurrence is X1141.

Constructed Elements

  • Circle: Circumcircle (circumcircle) — Passes through vertices A, B, C; centered at X3 CIRCUMCENTER

  • Central Lines: {3, 54}

  • Related Centers: X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X54 (KOSNITA POINT)

Trilinears: \(a[(a^{2} - b^{2})^{2} - c^{2}(a^{2} + b^{2})][(a^{2} - c^{2})^{2} - b^{2}(c^{2} + a^{2})]U(a,b,c)\), where \(U(a,b,c) = a^{6} - b^{6} - c^{6} + 3a^{2}(b^{4} + c^{4} - a^{2}b^{2} - a^{2}c^{2}) + b^{2}c^{2}(b^{2} + c^{2}) - a^{2}b^{2}c^{2}\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(4.612380768446\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(4 \cos A + \cos 3A \sec A \sec(B - C) : :\)

X1157 lies on the Neuberg cubic.

Notes¶

X1157 is the tangential of X3 on the Neuberg cubic.

Let RefaRefbRefc be the reflection triangle. The circumcircles of ARefbRefc, BRefcRefa, CRefaRefb (i.e., the Yiu circles) concur in X1157. (Randy Hutson, July 20, 2016)
