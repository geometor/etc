X1118¶

1st HATZIPOLAKIS PERSPECTOR

Let A'B'C' be the intouch triangle of ABC. Let CA be the point other than C' in which the perpendicular to BC from C' meets the incircle, let BA be the point other than B' in which the perpendicular to BC from B' meets the incircle, and let A0 be the point of intersection of lines BCA and CBA. Define B0 and C0 cyclically. Then triangle A0B0C0 is perspective to ABC, and the perspector is X1118. (Antreas Hatzipolakis, #5321, 4/30/02)

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {4, 65}, {28, 56}

  • Related Centers: X1259 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1118), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X65 (ORTHOCENTER OF THE INTOUCH TRIANGLE), X28 (CEVAPOINT OF X19 AND X25), X56 (EXSIMILICENTER)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = (1 - \cos A)/\cos^{2} A\)
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(0.055842400756\)

X1118 lies on the hyperbola {{A, B, C,X4,X19}}.
