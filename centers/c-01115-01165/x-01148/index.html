X1148¶

YIU-HATZIPOLAKIS POINT

Suppose LA, LB, LC are lines through a point P, respectively perpendicular to sidelines BC, CA, AB. Let AB be the point where LA meets AB, and let AC be the point where LA meets AC. Define BC, BA, CA, CB cyclically. Then X1148 is the point P, which satisfies

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {4, 65}, {46, 243}

  • Related Centers: X1 (INCENTER), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X65 (ORTHOCENTER OF THE INTOUCH TRIANGLE), X46 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X1), X243 (-LINE CONJUGATE OF X73)

Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = (\sec B + \sec C - \sec A) \sec A\)
Barycentrics: \((\sin A)f(A,B,C) : (\sin B)f(B,C,A) : (\sin C)f(C,A,B)\)
