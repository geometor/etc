X1156¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1155

X1156 is the isogonal conjugate of X1155.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {144, 149}

  • Related Centers: X144 (ANTICOMPLEMENT OF X7), X149 (REFLECTION OF X20 IN X104)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = 1/[(b - c)^{2} + a(b + c - 2a)]\)
Barycentrics: \(af(a,b,c) : bf(b,c,a) : cf(c,a,b)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(4.542109263484\)

X1156 lies on the Darboux septic.

Notes¶

Let Bc be the reflection of B in the internal angle bisector of angle C, and let Cb be the reflection of C in the internal angle bisector of angle B. Let Γa be the circle that passes through A, Bc, Cb. Let A' be the pole of BC, with respect to Γa and define B ‘and C' cyclically. Then X1156 is the perspector of A'B'C' and ABC, and X35445 is the perspector of A'B'C' and the excentral triangle. (Angel Montesdeoca, January 13, 2021)
