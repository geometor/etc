X(252) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(143)¶

Let HA be the reflection of X(4) in the Euler line of BCX(4), and define HB and HC cyclically. The lines AHA, BHB, CHC concur in X(252). (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
Trilinears: \(f(A,B,C) : f(B,C,A) : f(C,A,B)\), where \(f(A,B,C) = \sec(B - C)/[1 - 2 \cos(2A)]\)
Barycentrics: (sin A)f(A, B, C) : (sin B)f(B, C, A) : (sin C) f(C, A, B) Let A'B'C' be the orthic triangle. Let OA be the circle with center A passing through A'. Let A″ be the intersection, other than A', of OA and the nine-point circle. Define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X(252). (Randy Hutson, June 7, 2019)
