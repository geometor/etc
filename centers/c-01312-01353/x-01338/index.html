X1338¶

2nd WERNAU POINT

Let A'BC be the internal equilateral triangle on side BC, and define CB'A and AC'B cyclically. Let (AB'C') be the circle passing through the points A, B', C', and define (BC'A') and (CA'B') cyclically. The three circles concur in X1338. For details, see X1337.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {4, 617}, {1157, 1337}

  • Related Centers: X3480 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1338), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X617 (ANTICOMPLEMENT OF X14), X1157 (INVERSE-IN-CIRCUMCIRCLE OF X54), X1337 (1st WERNAU POINT)

Trilinears: \(f(a,b,c) : f(b,c,a) : f(c,a,b)\), where \(f(a,b,c) = a[x_{1} - 2Dx_{2}/\sqrt{3}]\), where \(D = \operatorname{area}(ABC)\), \(x_{1}\), \(x_{2}\) are as at X1337.
Barycentrics: \(g(a,b,c) : g(b,c,a) : g(c,a,b)\), where \(g(a,b,c) = a^{2}(2DU + 3^{1/2}VW)/(4D - 3^{1/2}a^{2})\), D, U, V, W as at X1337; see Hyacinthos #8874
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-6.939133376623\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Barycentrics: a^2*(2*(-a^2+b^2+c^2)*S+\(\sqrt{3}\)*(a^2-c^2+b^2)*(a^2-b^2+c^2))*(\(\sqrt{3}\)*b^2-2*S)*(\(\sqrt{3}\)*c^2-2*S) : : (César Lozada, December 15, 2019)
