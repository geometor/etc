X30¶

EULER INFINITY POINT

X30 is the point of intersection of the Euler line and the line at infinity. Thus, each of the lines listed below is parallel to the Euler line.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {2, 3} (Euler line), {13, 15}

  • Related Centers: X74 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF EULER INFINITY POINT), X2 (CENTROID), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X13 (1st ISOGONIC CENTER), X15 (1st ISODYNAMIC POINT)

Trilinears: \(\cos A - 2 \cos B \cos C : \cos B - 2 \cos C \cos A : \cos C - 2 \cos A \cos B\)
Barycentrics: \(g(a,b,c) : g(b,c,a) : g(c,a,b)\), where \(g(a,b,c) = 2a^{4} - (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2} - a^{2}(b^{2} + c^{2})\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates4 forms

  • Trilinears: \(bc[2a^{4} - (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2} - a^{2}(b^{2} + c^{2})] : :\)

  • Trilinears: \(2 \sec A - \sec B \sec C : :\)

  • Trilinears: \(\sin B \sin C - 3 \cos B \cos C : :\)

  • Barycentrics: \(S^{2} - 3 S_{B} S_{C} : :\)

▸ Linear Combinations1 combination

  • X30 = X2 - X3; if (i) and Xj are on the Euler line, then X30 = Xi - Xj

X30 lies on the Neuberg cubic, the Darboux quintic.

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X30 has Shinagawa coefficients \((1, -3)\).

Let A'B'C' be the reflection triangle. Let A″ be the cevapoint of B' and C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ are parallel to the Euler line, and therefore concur in X30. (Randy Hutson, December 10, 2016)
