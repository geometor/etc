Notes¶

Let A' be the midpoint between A and X7, and define B' and C' cyclically; the triangle A'B'C' is homothetic to the medial triangle, and the center of homothety is X142. (Randy Hutson, 9/23/2011)

Let A' be the intersection of these three lines:

  1. through midpoint of CA perpendicular to BX1

  2. through midpoint of AB perpendicular to CX1

  3. through midpoint of AX1 perpendicular to BC.

Define B' and C' cyclically. Then X142 = X6-of-A'B'C'. The triangle A'B'C' is the complement of the excentral triangle, and also the extraversion triangle of X10. (Randy Hutson, September 14, 2016)
