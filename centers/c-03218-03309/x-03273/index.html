X3273¶

PERSPECTOR OF TRIANGLES M1M2M3 AND J1J2J3

X3273 is the perspector of triangles m1m2m3 and j1j2j3.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {16, 358}, {357, 1136}

  • Related Centers: X3604 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X3273), X16 (2nd ISODYNAMIC POINT), X358 (2nd MORLEY-TAYLOR-MARR CENTER), X357 (1st MORLEY-TAYLOR-MARR CENTER), X1136 (5th MORLEY-TAYLOR-MARR CENTER)

Trilinears: \(\sin(A/3 + \pi /3) : :\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(1.627111702456\)
▸ Incident Central Lines3 lines

{16, 358}, {356, 3272}, {357, 1136}

▸ Functional Relations3 relations

  • X3273 = crosssum of X1135 and X3274

  • X3273 = isogonal conjugate of X3604

  • X3273 = perspector of ABC and unary cofactor triangle of 3rd Morley triangle

Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the 1st Morley triangle. Let La be the line through A' parallel to BC, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A″ = Lb∩Lc, B″ = Lc∩La, C″ = La∩Lb. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X3273. (Randy Hutson, July 20, 2016)
