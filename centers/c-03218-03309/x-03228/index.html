X3228¶

SS(a → bc) OF X111

X3228 = X1645-cross conjugate of X512

▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: for X3228 are obtained by applying the symbolic substitution (a,b,c) → (bc,ca,ab) to trilinear coordinates of X111. As a symbolic substitution, this mapping takes circumconics to circumconics. In particular, it maps circumcircle onto the Steiner circumellipse, which passes through X3228.

X3228 lies on the Steiner circumellipse.
