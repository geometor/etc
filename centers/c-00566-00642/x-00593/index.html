X593¶

1st HATZIPOLAKIS-YIU POINT

Let O(A) be the circle tangent to line BC and to the circumcircle of triangle ABC at vertex A. Let AB and AC be where O(A) meets lines AB and AC, respectively. Let L(A) be the line joining AB and AC, and define L(B) and L(C) cyclically. Let A' be where L(B) and L(C) meet, and define B' and C' cyclically. Then triangle A'B'C' is homothetic to triangle ABC, and the center of homothety is X593. See Antreas Hatzipolakis, Paul Yiu, Hyacinthos #2070.
