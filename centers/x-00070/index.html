X(70) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(26)¶

X(70) = isogonal conjugate of X(26)
Trilinears: \(bc/[b^{2} \cos 2B + c^{2} \cos 2C - a^{2} \cos 2A] : :\)
Barycentrics: \(1/(a^{8} - 2 a^{6} (b^{2} + c^{2}) + 2 a^{2} (b^{6} + c^{6}) - (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2} (b^{4} + c^{4})) : :\)
