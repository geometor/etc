X1020¶

POINT ALGEDI

X1020 = crosssum of X650 and X652

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {1, 185}, {108, 109}

  • Related Centers: X1021 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1020), X1 (INCENTER), X185 (NAGEL POINT OF THE ORTHIC TRIANGLE), X108 (Ψ(CIRCUMCENTER, INCENTER)), X109 (Ψ(INCENTER, CIRCUMCENTER))

Trilinears: \((\cos B + \cos C)/(\cos B - \cos C) : :\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(0.063021389965\)
▸ Incident Central Lines9 lines

{1, 185}, {57, 1086}, {101, 651}, {108, 109}, {190, 658}, {269, 292}, {347, 573}, {648, 1021}, {653, 2637}

▸ Functional Relations5 relations

  • X1020 = isogonal conjugate of X1021

  • X1020 = crosssum of X650 and X652

  • X1020 = trilinear pole of line X42X65

  • X1020 = crosspoint of X651 and X653

  • X1020 = cevapoint of Jerabek hyperbola intercepts of antiorthic axis

Notes¶

Let X be a point on the Euler line. Let P and U be the 1st and 2nd bicentrics of X. As X varies, the bicentric sum of P and U trace the line X42X65, of which X1020 is the trilinear pole. (Randy Hutson, March 25, 2016)
