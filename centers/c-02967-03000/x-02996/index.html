X2996¶

CD(X25)

X2996 = cevapoint of X115 and X525

Notes¶

X2996 is the trilinear pole of the line X523X4885, this line being the complement of orthic axis; the line is also the polar of X20 wrt {circumcircle, nine-point circle}-inverter). Also, let \(R_{a}\) be the radical trace of the A-McCay circle and A-Neuberg circle, and define \(R_{b}\) and \(R_{c}\) cyclically. Then the triangle RaRbRc is perspective to ABC at X2996. (Randy Hutson, October 13, 2015)
