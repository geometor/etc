X1885¶

ZOSMA TRANSFORM OF X774

X1885 = crosspoint of X4 and X1105

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {2, 3} (Euler line), {389, 974}

  • Related Centers: X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X1105 (CEVAPOINT OF X3 AND X4), X2 (CENTROID), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X389 (CENTER OF THE TAYLOR CIRCLE), X974 (2nd EHRMANN POINT)

Trilinears: (y + z) sec A : (z + x) sec B : (x + y) sec C,\(x : y : z\) = X774
Barycentrics: \((2 a^{6} - 3 a^{4} (b^{2} + c^{2}) + 8 a^{2} b^{2} c^{2} + (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2} (b^{2} + c^{2})) / (b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2}) : :\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-14.398038489980\)
▸ Incident Central Lines9 lines

▸ Functional Relations7 relations

  • X1885 = anticomplement of X31829

  • X1885 = crosspoint of X4 and X1105

  • X1885 = crosssum of X3 and X185

  • X1885 = intersection of tangents to Hatzipolakis-Lozada hyperbola at X4 and X185

  • X1885 = crosspoint, wrt orthic triangle, of X4 and X185

  • X1885 = X20-of-anti-Ara-triangle

  • X1885 = X3057-of-orthic-triangle if ABC is acute

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X1885 has Shinagawa coefficients \((F, E - 3F)\).

Let A'B'C' be the orthic triangle. X1885 is the radical center of the 2nd Droz-Farny circles of triangles AB'C', BC'A', CA'B'. (Randy Hutson, July 31 2018)
