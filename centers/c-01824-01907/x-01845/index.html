X1845¶

ZOSMA TRANSFORM OF X80

X1845 = crosssum of X3 and X1807

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {34, 46}, {4, 80}

  • Related Centers: X34 (-BETH CONJUGATE OF X4), X46 (-CEVA CONJUGATE OF X1), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X80 (REFLECTION OF INCENTER IN FEUERBACH POINT)

Trilinears: \((y + z) \sec A : (z + x) \sec B : (x + y) \sec C\), where \(x : y : z\) = X80
Barycentrics: \((y + z) \tan A : (z + x) \tan B : (x + y) \tan C\), where \(x : y : z\) = X80
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-0.979433056755\)

Notes¶

The following ten points lie on a circle: Xi for i = 11, 36, 65, 80, 108, 759, 1354, 1845, 2588, 2589. (Chris Van Tienhoven, Hyacinthos, January 4, 2011)

Let A'B'C' be the orthic triangle. Let A″ be the A-excenter of AB'C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines A'A″, B'B″, C'C″ concur in X1845. (Randy Hutson, January 29, 2018)
