X1824–X1907¶

Zosma Transforms

Isogonal conjugates of the inverse Mimosa transforms (Z(X)) of classical triangle centers.

X1824–X1907 (Zosma Transforms) — 16 representative centers (from 84 in collection) constructed on acute reference triangle \((a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)\) with unit baseline AB = \(1\).¶

Group Preamble — Zosma Transforms 1824-1907 (Zozma transforms)

The Zosma transform of a point X =\(x : y : z\) is the isogonal conjugate of the inverse Mimosa transform of X, given by trilinears
(y + z) sec A : (z + x) sec B : (x + y)sec C.

(Zosma is another star name.)
