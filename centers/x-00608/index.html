X(608) = ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X(345)¶

X(608) is the vertex conjugate of the foci of the inconic that is the polar conjugate of the isotomic conjugate of the incircle. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)
Trilinears: \(a(1 - \sec A) : b(1 - \sec B) : c(1 - \sec c)\)
Barycentrics: \(a^{2}(1 - \sec A) : b^{2}(1 - \sec B) : c^{2}(1 - \sec C)\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(-0.053535797359\)
▸ Alternate Coordinates1 form

  • Trilinears: \(\sin A - \tan A : :\)

Notes¶

X(608) is the {X(6),X(19)}-harmonic conjugate of X(607).
