X1111¶

TRILINEAR SQUARE OF X514

Let A7B7C7 and A8B8C8 be the Gemini triangles 7 and 8. Let A' be the trilinear product A7*A8 and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X1111. (Randy Hutson, November 30, 2018)

Notes¶
