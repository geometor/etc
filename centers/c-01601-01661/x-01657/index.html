X1657¶

{X3,X4}-HARMONIC CONJUGATE OF X1656

X1657 is the {X3,X4}-harmonic conjugate of X1656.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 4} (Euler line)

  • Related Centers: X1656 (INTERSECTION OF EULER LINE AND LINEX), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X4 (ORTHOCENTER)

Trilinears: \(3 \cos A - 4 \cos B \cos C : :\)
Barycentrics: \(5a^{4} - 2b^{4} - 2c^{4} - 3a^{2}b^{2} - 3a^{2}c^{2} + 4b^{2}c^{2} : :\)
Search Value (6, 9, 13): \(31.700327504442\)
▸ Linear Combinations1 combination

  • X1657 = \(3\) X381 - \(2\) X382

▸ Incident Central Lines4 lines

▸ Functional Relations6 relations

  • X1657 = reflection of Xi in Xj for these (i,j): (3, 20), (4, 550), (382, 3)

  • X1657 = complement of X33703

  • X1657 = {X381,X382}-harmonic conjugate of X5076

  • X1657 = Ehrmann-mid-to-ABC similarity image of X382

  • X1657 = circumcircle inverse of X34152

  • X1657 = endo-homothetic center of Ehrmann mid-triangle and ABC-X3 reflections triangle; the homothetic center is X382

Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X1657 has Shinagawa coefficients \((3, -7)\).

Let La be the polar of X3 wrt the A-power circle, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Let A' = Lb∩Lc, B' = Lc∩La, C' = La∩Lb. Triangle A'B'C' is homothetic to ABC, and the orthocenter of A'B'C' is X1657. (Randy Hutson, December 2, 2017)

Let OA be the circle centered at the A-vertex of the Ehrmann mid-triangle and passing through A; define OB and OC cyclically. X1657 is the radical center of OA, OB, OC. (Randy Hutson, August 28, 2020)
