X321¶

ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF X81

X321 is the isotomic conjugate of X81.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {42, 1215}

  • Related Centers: X42 (CROSSPOINT OF INCENTER AND SYMMEDIAN POINT), X1215 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X1178)

Trilinears: \((b + c)b^{2}c^{2} : :\)
Barycentrics: \(bc(b + c) : :\)

Notes¶

Let A26B26C26 be Gemini triangle 26. Let A' be the perspector of conic {A, B, C,B26,C26}}, and define B' and C' cyclically. The lines AA', BB', CC' concur in X321. (Randy Hutson, January 15, 2019)
