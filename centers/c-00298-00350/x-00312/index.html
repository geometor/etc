X312¶

ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF X57

X312 is the isotomic conjugate of X57.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {2, 37}, {29, 33}

  • Related Centers: X57 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X9), X2 (CENTROID), X37 (CROSSPOINT OF INCENTER AND CENTROID), X29 (CEVAPOINT OF INCENTER AND ORTHOCENTER), X33 (PERSPECTOR OF THE ORTHIC AND INTANGENTS TRIANGLES)
