X318¶

ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF X77

X318 is the isotomic conjugate of X77.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {4, 8}, {29, 33}

  • Related Centers: X77 (ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X33), X4 (ORTHOCENTER), X8 (NAGEL POINT), X29 (CEVAPOINT OF INCENTER AND ORTHOCENTER), X33 (PERSPECTOR OF THE ORTHIC AND INTANGENTS TRIANGLES)

Notes¶
