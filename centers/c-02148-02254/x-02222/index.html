Notes¶

  • A″ = X110-of-JaBC, and define B″ and C″ cyclically

  • (Oa) = circle with diameter AA″, and define (Ob) and (Oc) cyclically

The circles (Oa), (Ob), (Oc), and the circumcircle concur in X2222. See Hyacinthos 24369. (Antreas Hatzipolakis and Angel Montesdeoca, July 12, 2016)
