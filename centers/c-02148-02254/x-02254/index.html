Notes¶

The tangent to the nine-point circle at the point where it is tangent to the incircle (X11, the Feuerbach point) is here named the Feuerbach tangent line. It is line X11X244. The inverse of the incircle in the excircles radical circle is a circle tangent to the Apollonius circle at X3030. The line tangent to the Apollonius circle at X3030 is here named the Apollonius tangent line. It is line X2254X3030. X2254 is the intersection of the Feuerbach tangent line and the Apollonius tangent line. (Randy Hutson, January 15, 2019)
