Notes¶

Let IaIbIc be the cevian triangle of I (incenter) and let A″ be the intersection of the perpendicular bisector of AIa and the line through Ia perpendicular line to BC; note that A″(lies on AX3). Define B″ and C″ cyclically. The finite fixed point of the affine transformation that carries ABC onto IaIbIc is X2197, and the finite fixed point of the affine transformation that carries IaIbIc onto A″B″C″ is X2269. (Angel Montesdeoca, December 7, 2021)
