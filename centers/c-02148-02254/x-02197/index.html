Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the cevian triangle of I (incenter) and let A″ be the intersection of the perpendicular bisector of AA' and the line through A' perpendicular line to BC; note that A″(lies on AX3). Define B″ and C″ cyclically. The finite fixed point of the affine transformation that carries ABC onto A'B'C' is X2197, and the finite fixed point of the affine transformation that carries A'B'C' onto A″B″C″ is X2269. (Angel Montesdeoca, December 7, 2021)
