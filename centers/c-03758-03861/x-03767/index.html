X3767¶

X3053com[T(a², a² - c²)]

X3767 is the center of the inellipse that is the barycentric square of the orthic axis. The Brianchon point (perspector) of the inellipse is X393. (Randy Hutson, October 15, 2018)

Constructed Elements

  • Derived Triangle: Medial Triangle △MaMbMc — vertices Ma, Mb, Mc (Formed by the midpoints of sides BC, CA, AB)

  • Central Lines: {2, 3926}

  • Related Centers: X2 (CENTROID), X3926 (com[INVERSE(n(TANGENTIAL TRIANGLE))])

Barycentrics: \(a^{4} + (b^{2} - c^{2})^{2} : b^{4} + (c^{2} - a^{2})^{2} : c^{4} + (a^{2} - b^{2})^{2}\)
