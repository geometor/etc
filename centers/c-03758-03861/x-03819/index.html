X3819¶

X549com(2nd EULER TRIANGLE)

X3819 = complement of X51

Constructed Elements

  • Derived Triangle: Medial Triangle △MaMbMc — vertices Ma, Mb, Mc (Formed by the midpoints of sides BC, CA, AB)

  • Central Lines: {2, 51}

  • Related Centers: X2 (CENTROID), X51 (CENTROID OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE)

Barycentrics: \(a^{2}(b^{4} + c^{4} - a^{2}b^{2} - a^{2}c^{2} + 4b^{2}c^{2}) : :\)

Notes¶

Let MaMbMc be the medial triangle. Let Ab and Ac be the circumcircle intercepts of line MbMc. Define Bc and Ca cyclically, and define Ba and Cb cyclically. X3819 is the centroid of AbAcBcBaCaCb. (Randy Hutson, July 31 2018)
