Notes¶

As a point on the Euler line, X1593 has Shinagawa coefficients \((F, E - F)\).

In the plane of a triangle ABC, let

  • Γ = circumcircle

  • DEF = medial triangle

  • E' = Γ-antipode of E

    1. = circle that passes through E,F,E'

  • A' = center of (A), and define B' and C' cyclically.

Then the triangles ABC and A'B'C' are homothetic, and their homothetic center is X1593. (Angel Montesdeoca, February 14, 2023)
