X519¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X106

As the isogonal conjugate of a point on the circumcircle, X519 lies on the line at infinity.

Notes¶

Let IaIbIc be the incentral triangle. Let A″ be the reflection of A in Ia, and define B″ and C″ cyclically. Let A* be the cevapoint of B″ and C″, and define B* and C* cyclically. The lines AA*, BB*, CC* concur in X519. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)

Let A'B'C' be triangle T(-2,1). Let A″ be the trilinear product B'*C', and define B″ and C″ cyclically. The lines AA″, BB″, CC″ concur in X519. The lines A'A″, B'B″, C'C″ concur in X8. (Randy Hutson, December 26, 2015)
