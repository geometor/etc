X511¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X98

As the isogonal conjugate of a point on the circumcircle, X511 lies on the line at infinity.

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {3, 6}, {2, 51}

  • Related Centers: X98 (TARRY POINT), X3 (CIRCUMCENTER), X6 (SYMMEDIAN POINT), X2 (CENTROID), X51 (CENTROID OF ORTHIC TRIANGLE)

Barycentrics: \(\sin A \cos(A + \omega ) : \sin B \cos(B + \omega ) : \sin C \cos(C + \omega )\)

Notes¶

Let L denote the line having trilinears of X511 as coefficients. Then L is the line passing through X6 perpendicular to the Euler line.

X511 is the perspector of triangle ABC and the tangential triangle of the hyperbola {A, B, C, X2, and X110}}.
