X530¶

DIRECTION OF VECTOR AX + BX + CX, where X = X13

Let A' be the nine-point center of BCX14, and define B' and C' cyclically. A'B'C' is an equilateral triangle concyclic with X2 and X14 (inscribed in the 2nd Hutson circle). The isogonal (and isotomic) conjugate of X2 wrt A'B'C' is X530. (Randy Hutson, February 10, 2016)

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {2, 13}, {99, 299}

  • Related Centers: X2378 (CIR(X530)), X2 (CENTROID), X13 (1st ISOGONIC CENTER), X99 (STEINER POINT), X299 (ISOTOMIC CONJUGATE OF 2nd ISOGONIC CENTER)

Trilinears: \((2ax - by - cz)/a : :\), where \(x = x(A,B,C) = \csc(A + \pi /3)\), \(y = x(B,C,A)\), \(z = x(C,A,B)\)

X530 lies on the line at infinity.
