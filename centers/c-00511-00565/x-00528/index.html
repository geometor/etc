X528¶

DIRECTION OF VECTOR AX + BX + CX, where X = X11

X528 = X519-Hirst inverse of X522

Constructed Elements

  • Central Lines: {2, 11}, {9, 80}

  • Related Centers: X2 (CENTROID), X11 (FEUERBACH POINT), X9 (MITTENPUNKT), X80 (REFLECTION OF INCENTER IN FEUERBACH POINT)

Trilinears: \((2ax - by - cz)/a\), where \(x = x(A,B,C) = 1 - \cos(B-C)\), \(y = x(B,C,A)\), \(z = x(C,A,B)\)

X528 lies on the line at infinity.
