X512¶

ISOGONAL CONJUGATE OF X99

X512 is the point in which the line of the 1st and 2nd Brocard points meets the line at infinity.

Notes¶

Let A'B'C' be the 1st Brocard triangle. Let La be the reflection of B'C' in the internal angle bisector of vertex angle A, and define Lb and Lc cyclically. Then the lines La, Lb, Lc are parallel, and they concur in X512. (Randy Hutson, September 5, 2015)
