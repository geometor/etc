geometor.etc.ops¶

Exact symbolic geometric construction operators for geometor.etc using geometor.model.

Every operator accepts (model, A, B, C, ...) or (model, P, Q, ...) where points are exact sympy.geometry.Point instances registered in model over \(\mathbb{Q}\), \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d})\), or \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\phi})\).

Canonical Principles of Geometry snake_case constructors (incenter(), centroid(), circumcenter(), orthocenter(), nine_point_center(), symmedian_point(), gergonne_point(), nagel_point(), mittenpunkt(), spieker_center(), feuerbach_point(), fermat_point(), etc.) are provided alongside legacy *_of aliases and Kimberling X_n concordance metadata.

Construct one of the 4 canonical exact symbolic reference triangles in model.

Parameters:

  • model – Target geometor.model.Model.

  • preset – Canonical preset key ("heronian", "right", "kepler", "kimberling") or a SymbolicTriangle.

  • unit_baseline – When True, normalizes side \(AB = 1\). Defaults to False so Heronian \((15, 13, 14)\) and Right \((3, 4, 5)\) preserve exact integer vertex and center coordinates.

  • show_guides – When True, includes Euclid I.22 compass guide circles centered at \(A\) and \(B\).
