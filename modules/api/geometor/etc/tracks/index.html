geometor.etc.tracks¶

Generalized 5-Track Tabular Book Codex generator for GEOMETOR ETC centers.

Splits each center with a full symbolic plate suite (Collection 1 X_1–X_19 and any future multi-plate center) into five fast, focused, tabular Book Codex tracks:

  • index.rst (Overview): Identity strip, enunciation, primary viewer, ordered Homogeneous Coordinates table, Four-Triangle Symbolic Invariants table, Heronian Coordinate Decomposition table, Constructed Elements table, and Linear Combinations table.

  • lines.rst (Lines): Central Lines viewer (4-triangle tabs), Principal Central Lines table with exact Cartesian equations and spreads, incident curves/cubics, and full line catalog.

  • relations.rst (Relations): Relations viewer, Canonical Algebraic Operators table, and categorized Functional Relations tables (Unary, Binary, Perspectors/Derived Triangles).

  • notes.rst (Notes): Construction viewer, structured metric/excenter identities where present, geometric constructions, historical citations, and companion topic guides.

  • code.rst (Code): Construction viewer (4-triangle tabs) alongside exact symbolic model.py.

Zero center-specific hardcoding; every table derives from CenterSymbolicRegistry, CenterRelationsBundle, the exact symbolic Model, and parsed ETC apparatus records.
