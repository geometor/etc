geometor.etc.ingest¶

Ingest raw HTML volumes of the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC).

Splits ETC.html and ETCPart2.html..`ETCPart27.html` into: - Individual center HTML files (x-00001.html .. x-53144.html) in output_folder - Inter-center group preambles (preambles/p-XXXX-x-YYYYY.html and preambles.json) - Part 1 introductory essays (intro/00-header.html .. intro/09-combos.html and intros.json)
