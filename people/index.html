Searchable Prosopographical Directory

Angel Montesdeoca

Spanish geometer and contributor of numerous triangle centers, perspectors, and conic constructions in the ETC.

Record

Antreas Hatzipolakis

Founder of the Hyacinthos, Anopolis, and Euclid geometry research forums; proposer of hundreds of geometric configurations in the ETC.

Record

Bernard Gibert

Creator of Cubics in the Triangle Plane (CTC), cataloging and analyzing over 1,200 triangle cubics (K001–K1200+) referenced throughout the ETC.

Record

Cesar Lozada

Author of the Extended Glossary, Alphabetical Index of Terms, and Index of Triangles Referenced in ETC; contributor of thousands of triangle centers.

Record

Christian Heinrich Von Nagel

German geometer who studied the Nagel point X8 (perspector of the extouch triangle) and Nagel line in 1836.

Record

Clark Kimberling

Mathematician at the University of Evansville; founder and creator of the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC).

Record

Cyril Parry

British geometer who discovered the Parry point X111, the Parry circle, and the Parry reflection property.

Record

Dan Reznik

Researcher on Poncelet porisms, locus invariants, and dynamic triangle center loci in the ETC.

Record

Dao Thanh Oai

Geometer and discoverer of generalizations of the Simson line, Napoleon configuration, and perspective triangles in the ETC.

Record

Darij Grinberg

Mathematician and early contributor to Hyacinthos and the ETC on barycentric coordinates, pedal triangles, and circle power identities.

Record

Dasari Naga Vijay Krishna

Indian mathematician contributing synthetic reflection and circle constructions in the ETC.

Record

Emile Lemoine

French mathematician and co-founder of modern triangle geometry; studied the symmedian point X6 (Lemoine point) and Lemoine circles.

Record

Eric Weisstein

Creator of MathWorld, providing extensive cross-linked articles and illustrations for triangle centers and central circles.

Record

Ernst Wilhelm Grebe

German mathematician who investigated the symmedian point X6 (Grebe point) in 1847.

Record

Evangelista Torricelli

Italian physicist and mathematician who solved Fermat’s problem via equilateral triangles erected on the sides of ABC, locating X13 and X14.

Record

Floor Van Lamoen

Dutch mathematician and triangle geometer, discoverer of the van Lamoen circle and numerous cevapoint and perspectivity constructions in the ETC.

Record

Gaston Darboux

French geometer after whom the Darboux cubic (K004) and Darboux point X20 (with De Longchamps) are named.

Record

Gohier De Longchamps

French mathematician who studied the De Longchamps point X20 (reflection of the orthocenter in the circumcenter) and De Longchamps line.

Record

Henri Brocard

French mathematician and co-founder of modern triangle geometry; namesake of the Brocard points, Brocard angle ω, Brocard axis, Brocard circle, and Brocard triangles.

Record

John Casey

Irish mathematician and Euclidean geometer celebrated for Casey’s theorem and foundational treatises on the modern geometry of the triangle and circle.

Record

John Horton Conway

Mathematician who introduced Conway triangle notation (S, S_A, S_B, S_C, S_ω) and the Conway circle in triangle geometry.

Record

Joseph Diaz Gergonne

French geometer after whom the Gergonne point X7 (perspector of the intouch triangle) and Gergonne line are named.

Record

Joseph Neuberg

Luxembourger-Belgian mathematician, co-founder of modern triangle geometry, discoverer of the Neuberg cubic (K001) and Neuberg circles.

Record

Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach

German geometer who proved in 1822 that the nine-point circle is tangent to the incircle at the Feuerbach point X11 and to the three excircles.

Record

Kiminari Shinagawa

Geometer who introduced the Shinagawa coefficients (G, H) for triangle centers on the Euler line.

Record

Kurt Hofstadter

Cognitive scientist and geometer who discovered the Hofstadter points, including the Hofstadter zero point X359 and one point X360.

Record

Leonhard Euler

Swiss mathematician who proved in 1765 that the orthocenter X4, circumcenter X3, centroid X2, and nine-point center X5 are collinear on the Euler line.

Record

Paul Yiu

Professor of Mathematics at Florida Atlantic University, founder of Forum Geometricorum, and pioneer of modern barycentric triangle geometry.

Record

Peter Moses

Computational geometer and principal collaborator on the ETC, discoverer of thousands of barycentric combinations, coordinates, and circle/cubic properties.

Record

Peter Yff

Geometer who discovered the Yff center of congruence X174, Yff parabolic point X190, and Yff central triangles.

Record

Pierre De Fermat

French mathematician who posed the problem of finding the point X13 minimizing the sum of distances |AX| + |BX| + |CX| to the vertices of ABC.

Record

Randy Hutson

Prolific contributor to the ETC, specializing in synthetic constructions, perspective triangles, radical centers, and conjugate relationships.

Record

Stanley Rabinowitz

Mathematician and author of ‘Location of Triangle Centers Relative to the Incircle and Circumcircle’ in the ETC.

Record

Theodor Spieker

German geometer who discovered the Spieker center X10 (incenter of the medial triangle and center of mass of the perimeter of ABC).
